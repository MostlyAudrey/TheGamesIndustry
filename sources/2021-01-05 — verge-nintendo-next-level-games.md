# Nintendo to acquire Luigi's Mansion developer Next Level Games

Source: The Verge (2021-01-05)

Next Level Games was already a second-party partner (only making games for Nintendo platforms).
Nintendo said acquisition would "help secure internal development resources and boost collaboration."
