# id Software workers unionize with CWA — multiple outlets

**December 12, 2025.** A strong majority of 165 workers at Microsoft subsidiary id Software (~185 total employees) voted in favor of union representation with CWA Local 6215, forming a wall-to-wall union covering developers, artists, programmers, and more. Microsoft recognized the union per its stated labor principles.

The organizing effort kicked into high gear following Microsoft's 2024 closure of several Bethesda studios. Key issues included AI protections, remote work guarantees, and benefits. The ZeniMax-specific neutrality agreement was still in effect (valid until May 2026), enabling open organizing.
