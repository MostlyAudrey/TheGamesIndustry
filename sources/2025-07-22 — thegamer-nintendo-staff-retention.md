# While Xbox And PlayStation Have Regular Layoffs, Nintendo Has Actually Grown And Holds Onto Staff For At Least A Decade

Source: TheGamer (2025-07-22)
URL: https://www.thegamer.com/nintendo-no-layoffs-staff-retention-growth-2025/

Key data points:
- Nintendo Japan turnover rate: 1.9%
- Nintendo America turnover rate: 5.1%
- Average employee tenure Japan: 14.4 years
- Average employee tenure US: ~10 years
- 100% of Japan and Australia employees report regular career growth conversations
- 87% of US employees report regular career growth conversations
- Gender balance Japan: 2,270 male, 692 female
- Gender balance Australia: 50 male, 49 female (near parity)
- Near 100% return rate after parental leave
