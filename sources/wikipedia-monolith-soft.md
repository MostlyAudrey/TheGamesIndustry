Key data:
- Founded: October 1, 1999, Tokyo, Japan
- Founders: Hirohide Sugiura, Tetsuya Takahashi, Yasuyuki Honne
- HQ: Meguro, Tokyo
- 3 locations (Meguro, Shinagawa, Kyoto)
- Employees: 344 (2025)
- Parent: Nintendo (2007–present)
- Best known for Xenoblade series
- Notable for promoting comfortable working environment with little to no overtime
