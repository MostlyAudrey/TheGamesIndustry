# Tencent confirms leadership shakeup, denies mass layoffs across its gaming division

Reports on Oct 29, 2024 that Tencent denied rumors of mass layoffs at TiMi Studio Group and LightSpeed Studios, while confirming leadership changes. Team Kaiju (LA-based TiMi studio) had closed in 2023.
