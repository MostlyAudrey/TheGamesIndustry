# Indiana Jones and the Great Circle — MachineGames, launched Dec 2024

MachineGames' Indiana Jones and the Great Circle was released December 9, 2024 on PC and Xbox Series X|S. Developed in partnership with Lucasfilm, the game had been in development since before Microsoft's acquisition of ZeniMax (2021). It was published by Bethesda Softworks and executive produced by Todd Howard.

The game was widely regarded as a critical success and one of Xbox's strongest exclusives of 2024.
