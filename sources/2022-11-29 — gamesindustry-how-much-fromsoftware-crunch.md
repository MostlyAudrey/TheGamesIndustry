# 2022-11-29 — GamesIndustry.biz: How much does From Software crunch?

GamesIndustry.biz interviewed multiple anonymous current and former FromSoftware employees. Found department-dependent crunch cycles (2-3 months before release), below-average pay (¥3.41M/year avg), and a 10pm curfew rule. FromSoftware did not respond to comment requests. No union presence identified.
