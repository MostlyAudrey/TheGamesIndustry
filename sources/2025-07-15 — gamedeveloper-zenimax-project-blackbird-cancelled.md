# Project Blackbird cancelled at ZeniMax Online Studios — July 2025

**July 2, 2025.** ZeniMax Online Studios' unannounced MMORPG codenamed Project Blackbird was cancelled amid Microsoft Gaming layoffs. Project Blackbird was ZOS's first new MMORPG since The Elder Scrolls Online launched in 2014.

ZeniMax Online Studios founder and president Matt Firor subsequently resigned, stating that the cancellation of Project Blackbird — "the game he had waited his entire career to create" — was the reason.

The cancellation was part of the broader Microsoft 9,000 layoffs (July 2025) that impacted ZeniMax. ZOS ultimately lost 275 employees over a 12-month span (July 2025 to July 2026), representing 60%+ of the studio's staff.

Some laid-off developers formed Sackbird Studios in October 2025.
