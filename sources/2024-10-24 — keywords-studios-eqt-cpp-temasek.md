# Source — Keywords Studios: EQT + CPP + Temasek acquisition complete

Fetched 2026-10-15, HTTP 200, full text extracted. Key corroborated facts:

- Investor group **led by EQT**, with **Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (CPP Investments)** and **Temasek**, **completed** its acquisition of **Keywords Studios** on **Oct 24, 2024**; shares ceased trading on AIM, delisted from LSE.
- **Keywords Studios**: "leading international video games service provider," global gaming services market leader; AIM-listed since **July 2013**.
- **Bertrand Bodson**, CEO, quoted: "This is a significant milestone for Keywords Studios as we start the next phase of our growth journey alongside our new partners."
- **EQT** investing from its **BPEA Private Equity Fund VIII**.
- **Janice Leow** (EQT, Partner / Head of Private Capital Southeast Asia) commented on behalf of the consortium.

## Amount
- ~**$2.8B** per PitchBook (2024-11-05) and the earlier FT report (2024-05-23, "£2.2bn" unsolicited bid). CVC not involved here. Treat **~$2.8B** as the deal value.
