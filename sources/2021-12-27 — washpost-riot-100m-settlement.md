# Riot Games agrees to pay $100 million gender discrimination settlement

Riot Games agreed to pay $100 million to settle a 2018 gender-based discrimination class-action suit with California state agencies (DFEH, DLSE) and current/former women employees. Settlement covered 1,548 women. Included workplace reforms: third-party monitoring for 3 years, pay transparency, limiting implicit bias in hiring. Court gave preliminary approval July 2022, final approval September 2023.
