# Bungie's layoffs reportedly amounted to around 100 staff

> Published 2023-10-31. Fetched 2026-10-18. Verifies: ~100 employees (8% of ~1,200 staff) laid off October 2023.

Bungie's October 2023 layoffs amounted to roughly 100 staff, or about 8% of the developer's workforce. The layoffs came amid Destiny 2's declining performance following the Lightfall expansion's reception and a delay to The Final Shape expansion.
