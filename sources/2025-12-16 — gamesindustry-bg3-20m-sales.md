# Baldur's Gate 3 — 20 Million Copies Sold

GamesIndustry.biz reporting on Bloomberg interview with Swen Vincke, Dec 2025. Key points:

- 15M copies by end of 2024, 20M by Dec 2025 (+5M in 2025)
- Larian has 530 staff across 7 locations (Europe, North America, Asia)
- Working on new Divinity title (announced at The Game Awards 2025)
- Using generative AI for internal presentations/concept art only — no AI content in games
- Tencent minority stake described as "essential" but non-controlling
- Cancelled second D&D project ("hearts of the developers" weren't in it)
