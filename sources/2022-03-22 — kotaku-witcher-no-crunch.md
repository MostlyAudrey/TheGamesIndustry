# Kotaku — Witcher game director promises no crunch

CDPR co-founder Marcin Iwiński apologized after Cyberpunk 2077 launch and re-committed to limiting crunch. Jason Slama, game director for the next Witcher title (announced March 2022), publicly promised "not on my watch" when asked about crunch. Article details the company's history of breaking crunch promises dating back to The Witcher 3 and Cyberpunk 2077.
