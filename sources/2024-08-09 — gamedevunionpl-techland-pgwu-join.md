# Techland employees join Polish Gamedev Workers Union

The Polish Gamedev Workers Union (PGWU) announced on August 9, 2024 that Techland employees had joined the union. This is the same union originally formed at CD Projekt RED in October 2023, open to all Polish game industry workers. The union operates under OZZ Inicjatywa Pracownicza (Workers' Initiative), an independent union federation. Techland's inclusion represents the union's expansion beyond its founding studio.
