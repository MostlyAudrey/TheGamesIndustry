GamesRadar+ reported that 6 months after CEO Strauss Zelnick said "we didn't shutter those studios," Take-Two confirmed through a representative that both Intercept Games and Roll7 were shut down prior to the Private Division sale. The article noted Zelnick had previously denied studio closures when initially reported.
