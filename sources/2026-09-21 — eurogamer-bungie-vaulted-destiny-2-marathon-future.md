# Bungie is freeing vaulted Destiny 2 campaigns after Marathon "did not reach as many players as we had hoped"

> Published 2026-09-21. Fetched 2026-10-18. Verifies: Bungie reverses Destiny 2 end; un-vaulting old campaigns; Marathon underperformed; Poria Torkan and Josh Deane new leadership; studio restructuring.

Bungie announced it is un-vaulting Destiny 2 campaigns, locations, and raids. Studio head Poria Torkan and product head Josh Deane acknowledged: "We lost trust, we lost incredible colleagues, and we lost sight of what we should be prioritizing most, our players." Marathon "did not reach as many players as we had hoped" and Bungie plans to evolve it beyond extraction shooter genre constraints. Permanent PvE mode and social space coming December 2026.
