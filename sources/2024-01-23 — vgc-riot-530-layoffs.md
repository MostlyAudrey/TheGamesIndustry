# Riot confirms 530 layoffs

Riot confirmed cutting ~530 roles globally (~11% of workforce). Biggest impact was to teams outside core development. Statement: "This isn't to appease shareholders or to hit a quarterly earnings number—it's a necessity." Core focus: League of Legends, Valorant, Teamfight Tactics, Wild Rift. LoR downsized due to "financial challenges since launch." Riot Forge ended.
