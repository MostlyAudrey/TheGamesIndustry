# New Activision QA union is the largest in the video game industry — CNBC (March 9, 2024)

**~600 QA testers at Activision in CA, TX, and MN form Activision Quality Assurance United-CWA — the largest certified video game union in the US at formation.** The vote was 390-8 in favor. Microsoft remained neutral under the 2022 labor neutrality agreement. The union sought higher wages and career opportunities for QA staff.
