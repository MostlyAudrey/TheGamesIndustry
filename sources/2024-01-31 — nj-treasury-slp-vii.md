# Source — NJ Treasury: SLP VII investment review

Fetched 2026-10-15, HTTP 200 (PDF). The **NJ public pension** (NJ Retirement System) was considering an investment in **Silver Lake Partners VII** at its **2024-01-31** Investment Council meeting.

## Significance
This is a **named public pension LP** for the Silver Lake SLP VII fund. Public pension funds are the primary pocket-book target (state workers' money).
