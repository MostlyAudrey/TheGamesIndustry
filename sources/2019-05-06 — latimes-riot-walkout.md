# Riot Games workers walk out over forced arbitration

More than 200 Riot Games employees walked out of the LA headquarters on May 6, 2019 to protest the company's handling of two sexual discrimination lawsuits and its policy of forced arbitration. The walkout was reportedly the first in the video game industry. Riot later agreed to allow new hires to waive forced arbitration for sexual harassment claims but maintained the policy for existing employees.
