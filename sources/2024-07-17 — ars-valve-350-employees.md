Data from a poorly redacted document in Wolfire's antitrust lawsuit against Valve, covering 2003-2021. First noticed by SteamDB's Pavel Djundik. The Verge captured the raw numbers from a table labeled "Employee Headcount and Gross Pay Data, 2003-2021."

Key data points:
- Total employees: 78 (2003) → 351 (2012) → hovered 325-375 through 2021
- Steam department: peaked at 142 (2015), fell to 79 (2021)
- Total gross pay: ~$300M (2011 inflation-adj) → ~$450M (2021)
- Mean gross pay per employee: ~$483K (2011) rising significantly by 2021
- Hardware department: started at 3 employees (2011), 12% of headcount by 2021
- Valve estimated $6.5B annual revenue (2021, per Microsoft estimate)
