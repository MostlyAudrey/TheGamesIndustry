# Sony is investigating an alleged ransomware attack on Insomniac

**Source:** The Verge, Dec 12, 2023. Fetched 2026-10-18.

Key facts:
- Rhysida ransomware group claims breach of Insomniac Games
- Sony confirms investigation
- Stolen data includes employee personal info and Spider-Man voice actor data
- 1.3M+ files claimed stolen
- Threat to leak data if ransom not paid
