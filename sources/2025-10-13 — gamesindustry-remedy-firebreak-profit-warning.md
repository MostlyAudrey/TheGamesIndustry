# Remedy adjusts FY25 outlook following weak sales of FBC: Firebreak

Remedy issued a profit warning on October 10, 2025 after FBC: Firebreak (released June 17, 2025) failed to meet internal sales targets. The company took a €14.9M non-cash impairment. Q3 2025 operating loss was €16.4M, compared to operating profit of €2.4M in Q3 2024. Revenue declined 32% year-over-year in Q3 2025.
