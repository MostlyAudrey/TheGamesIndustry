Wolfire Studios originally filed April 2021, dismissed Nov 2021, refiled May 2022. Dark Catt filed June 2021. Cases consolidated July 2022. Class certified by US District Judge Jamal N. Whitehead on Nov 25, 2024, covering ~32,000 entities. The class covers developers/publishers who paid commission to Valve (the 30% Steam cut) on or after Jan 28, 2017.
