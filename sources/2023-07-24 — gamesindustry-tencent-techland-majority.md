# Tencent to become majority shareholder of Techland

Tencent announced on July 24, 2023 that it would acquire a majority stake in Polish developer Techland. Founder and CEO Paweł Marchewka stated the studio would maintain creative independence and control over its brands. The deal was the largest M&A transaction in Poland in 2023.
