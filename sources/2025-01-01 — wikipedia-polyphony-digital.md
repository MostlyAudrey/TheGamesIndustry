# Polyphony Digital — Wikipedia

**Source:** Wikipedia. Fetched 2026-10-18.

Key facts:
- Founded 1994 as Polys Entertainment
- Renamed Polyphony Digital 1998
- Internal Sony studio from inception
- Gran Turismo series developer
- Led by Kazunori Yamauchi
- Based in Tokyo, Japan
- ~200-300 employees
