# Riot lays off 2XKO developers less than three weeks after release

Riot laid off ~80 employees from the 2XKO development team (~half of team) less than three weeks after the fighting game's January 20, 2026 launch. Statement: "Overall momentum hasn't reached the level needed to support a team of this size." Affected employees offered opportunities elsewhere in Riot or minimum 6 months severance.
