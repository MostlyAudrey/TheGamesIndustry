# Riot Games "eliminates" 32 League of Legends roles

Riot confirmed 32 employees laid off — 27 from the League of Legends team and 5 from publishing. Announced by Marc Merrill, described as part of preparing LoL for its "next phase." Follows the larger 530 layoffs in January 2024.
