# Riot Games $100 million settlement final

Riot Games $100 million settlement — largest of its kind in California Department of Civil Rights history. Covered 1,548 women. Final approval granted September 2023. Included independent expert analysis of pay/hiring/promotion practices, 3-year monitoring.
