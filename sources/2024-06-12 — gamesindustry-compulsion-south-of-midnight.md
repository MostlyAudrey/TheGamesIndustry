# GamesIndustry.biz — Compulsion Games South of Midnight

Company profile and reporting on Compulsion Games, the Montreal-based studio founded in 2009 by Guillaume Provost. Studio was acquired by Microsoft in June 2018 as part of Xbox Game Studios. Known for Contrast (2013), We Happy Few (2016-2019), and South of Midnight (revealed 2023, gameplay debut 2024, released 2025).
