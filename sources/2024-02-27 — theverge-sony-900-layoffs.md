# Sony is laying off 900 PlayStation employees

**Source:** The Verge, Feb 27, 2024. Fetched 2026-10-17.

Key facts from article:
- Sony laying off ~900 PlayStation employees (~8% of global headcount)
- Impact across Insomniac Games, Naughty Dog, Guerrilla Games, Firesprite
- London Studio closing entirely
- Affects Americas, EMEA, Japan, and APAC regions
- Jim Ryan memo published: 8% reduction, London Studio full closure, Firesprite reductions
- US employees notified same day; UK entered collective consultation
- Japan "next career support program"
- PS5 sales target miss cited as preceding context
