# Riot Games cuts more than 500 jobs

Riot eliminated 530 roles (~11% of workforce). Biggest impact to teams outside core development. Core live games (LoL, Valorant, TFT, Wild Rift) prioritized. Project L (2XKO) still in development. Legends of Runeterra team reduced and refocused on PvE. Riot Forge publishing label shut down after Bandle Tale release. Severance: min 6 months salary.
