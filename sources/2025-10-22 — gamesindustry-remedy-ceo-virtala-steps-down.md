# Remedy CEO Tero Virtala steps down after nine years

Tero Virtala stepped down as CEO of Remedy Entertainment with immediate effect on October 22, 2025, following the FBC: Firebreak profit warning. Markus Mäki, co-founder and board chairman (23.44% holder), was appointed interim CEO. Board member Henri Österlund became new chairman. Mäki had been on the board since 1995.
