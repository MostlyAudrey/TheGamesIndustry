# Ubisoft spins out new unit for Assassin's Creed and other games, Tencent to take $1.25 billion stake

Reports Ubisoft creating new gaming subsidiary Vantage Studios with Tencent investing €1.16B ($1.25B) for a minority stake. Subsidiary holds Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, Rainbow Six Siege IP.
