# Bankier.pl — CD Projekt RED union formation

Employees of CD Projekt established Związek Pracowników Branży Gier (Game Industry Workers Union), the first union in the Polish gaming industry. It operates under the nationwide OZZ Inicjatywa Pracownicza (Workers' Initiative), not as a single-company union.
