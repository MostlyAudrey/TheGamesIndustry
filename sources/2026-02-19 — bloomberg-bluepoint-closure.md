# Sony is shutting down the PlayStation studio Bluepoint

**Source:** Bloomberg, Feb 19, 2026. Fetched 2026-10-17.

Key facts:
- Sony closing Bluepoint Games (Austin, TX; acquired 2021)
- ~70 employees losing jobs
- "Following a recent business review"
- Bluepoint had been working on unannounced live-service God of War game
- That project canceled January 2025
- Bluepoint known for Shadow of the Colossus (2018) and Demon's Souls (2020) remakes
