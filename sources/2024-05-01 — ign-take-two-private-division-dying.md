IGN reported that Take-Two was shuttering Intercept Games (Kerbal Space Program 2) and Roll7 (OlliOlli World, Rollerdrome), confirmed by WARN Act notice. The closures were part of a larger move to sell off or shut down Private Division. Most PD staff were laid off at the end of April 2024. A small crew remained to support No Rest for the Wicked, Tales of the Shire, and an unannounced Game Freak project. Two publishing deals (Bloober Team, One More Level) were backed out of.
