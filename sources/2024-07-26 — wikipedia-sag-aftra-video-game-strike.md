Wikipedia documents the 2024 SAG-AFTRA video game strike, which began July 26, 2024 and included Take-Two Interactive as one of the targeted publishers alongside Activision, Electronic Arts, Epic Games, Warner Bros. Games, and others. The strike centered on AI protections. A tentative agreement was reached June 9, 2025, strike suspended June 11, 2025, ratified July 9, 2025.
