# Every PlayStation Studio That's Closed Since The PS5 Launched

**Source:** Insider Gaming, Mar 25, 2026. Fetched 2026-10-17.

Comprehensive list of 8 first-party studio closures since PS5 launch (2020):

1. **2020 — Manchester Studio**: VR-focused; formed 2015, never shipped a game.
2. **2021 — Japan Studio**: Restructured around Team ASOBI; effectively closed. Developed Ape Escape, Shadow of the Colossus.
3. **2023 — PixelOpus**: Concrete Genie dev; didn't meet strategic objectives.
4. **2024 — London Studio**: Closed in Feb 2024 layoffs. Known for SingStar, Blood & Truth.
5. **2024 — Firewalk Studios**: Oct 2024. Concord developer; ~170 jobs lost.
6. **2024 — Neon Koi**: Oct 2024. Mobile dev (formerly Savage Game Studios); ~40 laid off.
7. **2026 — Bluepoint Games**: Feb 2026. Remake specialist; ~70 jobs. Live-service God of War canceled.
8. **2026 — Dark Outlaw Games**: Most recent closure. Led by Treyarch veteran Jason Blundell; never announced a game.
