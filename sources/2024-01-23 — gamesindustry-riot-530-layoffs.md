# Riot lays off 530 staff, Riot Forge shutting down

Covers the Jan 2024 layoffs of 530 staff (11% workforce). CEO Dylan Jadeja said company was "without a sharp enough focus" and had "too many things underway." Riot Forge shutting down after Bandle Tale. LoR refocused to PvE. Also references the Jan 2023 layoffs of 46 staff as part of "strategic shifts." Severance minimum 6 months salary + cash bonus.
