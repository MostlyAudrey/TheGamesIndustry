# Frogwares — Wikipedia Summary

Frogwares is a Ukrainian developer, Kyiv HQ with Dublin subsidiary. ~80+ employees. Known for Sherlock Holmes adventure series and The Sinking City. Survived Russian invasion starting Feb 2022 — staff joined humanitarian/volunteer efforts, hybrid model, removed games from Russia, received Epic MegaGrant to relocate.
