# Techland cancels two games mid-development, confirms no layoffs

Techland cancelled two unannounced games after recording record financial losses of PLN 135M ($37.4M) in 2024, following losses of $25M in 2023. The studio confirmed to Eurogamer that "none of the reported changes have resulted in staff reductions," with affected developers reassigned to remaining projects including Dying Light: The Beast (upcoming 2025) and a larger AAA open-world fantasy RPG described as the studio's "biggest project to date."
