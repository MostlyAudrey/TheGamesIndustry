# Krafton acquires Tango Gameworks — August 2024

**August 12, 2024.** South Korean publisher Krafton announced it had acquired Tango Gameworks and the Hi-Fi Rush IP from Microsoft. The deal had gone into effect on August 1, 2024. Approximately 50 of the original ~105 staff members were brought back to Krafton's Japan subsidiary.

The acquisition saved Tango Gameworks after Microsoft had announced its closure three months earlier (May 7, 2024) as part of Bethesda/ZeniMax studio cuts.
