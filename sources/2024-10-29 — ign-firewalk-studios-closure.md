# Firewalk Studios Officially Closing Following Concord's Disastrous Release

**Source:** IGN, Oct 29, 2024. Fetched 2026-10-17.

Key facts:
- Firewalk Studios (Bellevue, WA) shut down by Sony
- Concord released Aug 2024, pulled from stores weeks later, refunds offered
- Director Ryan Ellis stepped down after Concord taken offline
- Part of 18-month ownership by Sony
- ~170 jobs lost (per Insider Gaming)
- Announced same day as Neon Koi closure
