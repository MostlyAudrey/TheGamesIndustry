# Dreams and LittleBigPlanet studio Media Molecule is reportedly laying off 15-20% of staff

**Source:** VGC, Oct 24, 2023. Fetched 2026-10-18.

Key facts:
- Media Molecule laying off 15-20% of staff (~20 people)
- Headcount from 135 to 115
- Strategic shift; moving from Dreams support to new project
- Sony confirmed "significant strategic changes"
