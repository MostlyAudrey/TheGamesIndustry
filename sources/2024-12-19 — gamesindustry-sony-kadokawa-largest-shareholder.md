# 2024-12-19 — GamesIndustry.biz: Sony to become largest shareholder of FromSoftware parent Kadokawa

Sony invested ¥50 billion (~$318M) for 12M new Kadokawa shares, bringing its stake to ~10% and making it the largest shareholder. Follows earlier speculation about a full acquisition. Joint projects include anime, live-action adaptations of Kadokawa IP, and expanded game publishing.
