# 2026-06-08 — Automaton West: Kadokawa investigated for freelancer treatment

Japan Fair Trade Commission found Kadokawa violated Freelance Protection Act (Act on Ensuring Proper Transactions Involving Specified Entrusted Business Operators). Freelance writers, stylists, illustrators worked via verbal orders without written contracts. Delayed payments. Corrective recommendation issued.
