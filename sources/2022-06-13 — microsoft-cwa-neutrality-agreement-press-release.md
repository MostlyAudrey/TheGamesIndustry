# CWA, Microsoft announce labor neutrality agreement (June 13, 2022)

**Microsoft and CWA sign a labor neutrality agreement that would apply at Activision Blizzard after the acquisition closes.** The agreement commits Microsoft to neutrality in organizing campaigns, streamlined access to card-check elections, and expedited arbitration for disputes. CWA President Chris Shelton said the agreement addresses CWA's concerns and the union formally supported the acquisition's approval.
