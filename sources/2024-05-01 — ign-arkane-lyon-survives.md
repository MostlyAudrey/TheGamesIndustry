# Arkane Lyon survives Microsoft studio closures — IGN

**May 7, 2024.** IGN reported that Microsoft had closed Arkane Austin, Tango Gameworks, and Alpha Dog Games as part of a "reprioritization of titles and resources." Arkane Lyon, which was working on Marvel's Blade (announced at The Game Awards 2023), survived the cull. Roundhouse Studios was folded into ZeniMax Online Studios.

Arkane Lyon studio director Dinga Bakaba called the closure of sister studio Arkane Austin a "fucking gut stab" on social media, confirming Lyon was safe.
