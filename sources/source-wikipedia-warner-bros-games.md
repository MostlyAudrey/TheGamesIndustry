Key details from article:
- Founded as Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment on January 14, 2004
- Renamed to Warner Bros. Games in 2021
- Parent changed multiple times — under Skydance Interactive as of 2026
- Subsidiaries include Rocksteady, NetherRealm, Avalanche, TT Games, WB Games Boston, WB Games Montréal
- David Haddad exited as WB Games chief in January 2025
- Monolith Productions closed February 2025 alongside Player First Games and WB Games San Diego
