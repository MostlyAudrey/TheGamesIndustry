# An Important Update about Riot's Future

CEO Dylan Jadeja announced the elimination of ~530 roles globally (~11% of workforce), mainly impacting teams outside core development. Riot Forge to be sunset after Bandle Tale release. Legends of Runeterra team reduced and refocused on PvE mode "Path of Champions." Severance: minimum 6 months salary + 1x 2023 APB bonus + health benefits + equity treatment + career support.

Layer context: Riot had doubled headcount since 2019 and costs were "unsustainable."
