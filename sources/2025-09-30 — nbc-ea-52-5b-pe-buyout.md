# Source — NBC News: EA $52.5B PE buyout

Fetched 2026-10-15, HTTP 200, full text extracted. Key corroborated facts (all date-stamped where the article gives a date):

- EA acquired for **$52.5B** by **Silver Lake Partners + Saudi PIF + Affinity Partners**; **$210/share**; "largest-ever buyout funded by private-equity firms."
- **PIF** was already the largest insider stakeholder and will **roll over its existing 9.9% stake**.
- **Jared Kushner** runs **Affinity Partners** (CEO of Affinity).
- **Andrew Wilson** — EA CEO since **2013** — will remain in the role.
- HQ stays in **Redwood City, CA**.
- Deal **expected to close Q1 2027**; needs shareholder approval.
- **EA ended March (2025) with 14,500 employees**; laid off "several hundred" in May; "~5% of its workforce" jettisoned in 2024.
- **EA annual revenue** stagnant last 3 fiscal years: **$7.4B–$7.6B**.
- **Silver Lake** prior tech buyouts: **Skype ($1.9B, 2009)** and **Dell ($24.9B, 2013)**.
- Analyst **Andrew Marok** (Raymond James): PIF "very active player in the video gaming market since 2022," taking minority stakes in most scaled public gaming publishers, plus outright purchases of **ESL, FACEIT, Scopely**; scaling its arm **Savvy Gaming Group**.

## Key quotes (exact)
- Kushner: "Electronic Arts is an extraordinary company with a world-class management team and a bold vision for the future."
- Marok: "The Saudi PIF has been a very active player in the video gaming market since 2022… The PIF has made its intentions to scale its gaming arm, Savvy Gaming Group, clear, and the EA deal would represent the biggest such move to date by some distance."

## Caveats
- "could become the largest-ever" (article's hedged framing) — treat as superlative-at-announcement, not final.
