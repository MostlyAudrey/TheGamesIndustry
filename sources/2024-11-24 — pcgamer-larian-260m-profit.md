# Larian $260M Profit on Baldur's Gate 3 Success

PC Gamer reporting on Larian's 2023 financial results filed in Belgium. Key points:

- **Revenue:** €427 million (~$447M) in 2023, up from ~€30M in 2022
- **Profit:** €249 million pre-tax (~$260M)
- **2022 loss:** €7.6 million
- **Ownership:** Majority-owned by Swen Vincke and his wife; Tencent holds minority stake
- Larian remains independent — "largest independent studio alongside Valve"
