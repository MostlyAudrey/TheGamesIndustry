# Days Gone Developer Hit with Layoffs After Cancelling Live Service Game

**Source:** Bloomberg (via PlayStation LifeStyle), June 10, 2025. Fetched 2026-10-17.

Key facts:
- Bend Studio laid off ~30% of staff (~40 people)
- Followed cancellation of unannounced live-service project
- Studio working on new project; Sony says it remains "confident in its future"
- Bluepoint's live-service God of War also canceled around same time
- Sony shifting away from live-service games strategy
