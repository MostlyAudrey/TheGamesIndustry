# Source — LA Times: EA ~$55B take-private

Fetched 2026-10-15, HTTP 200, full text extracted. Key corroborated facts:

- EA acquired for **~$55B**, "biggest leveraged buyout in history," backed by **Saudi PIF + Silver Lake + Affinity Partners**; **$210/share**, a **25% premium** over the **$168.32** close (Thursday before announcement).
- Announced **Monday 2025-09-29** (story date 2025-09-29); "expected to close next year."
- **Wilson** CEO since **2013** stays; HQ **Redwood City**.
- **May 2025**: EA closed "several hundred positions," including **~100 jobs at L.A.-based Respawn**; **2024**: planned to cut **5%** of workforce.
- **Q1 FY2025** (ended June 30): **$1.67B** net revenue, **+1%** YoY.
- **PIF** involved with mobile gaming, including **Culver City-based Scopely**; **Scopely acquired Niantic's games portfolio in March** (incl. *Pokémon Go*).
- Saudi Arabia plans to use **PIF to invest $38B** in gaming by **2030** (citing Bloomberg).
- **Michael Pachter** (Wedbush): EA "ripe to be taken over," "a good win for EA shareholders."
- Prior context: **Activision Blizzard acquired by Microsoft for nearly $69B in 2023.**

## Key quotes (exact)
- Wilson: "Together with our partners, we will create transformative experiences to inspire generations to come."
- Pachter: "They're a company ripe to be taken over… It's a good win for EA shareholders."

## Note
LA Times headline figure is **~$55B**; NBC/AP figure is **$52.5B**. Both cited; the deal is commonly referenced by both. Treat "$52.5–55B" as the range, $210/share as the firm number.
