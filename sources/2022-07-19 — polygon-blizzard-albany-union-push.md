# Blizzard Albany QA workers announce union push — Polygon (July 19, 2022)

**QA workers at Blizzard Albany (formerly Vicarious Visions) announce union formation as Game Workers Alliance Albany-CWA — the second union at an Activision Blizzard subsidiary.** The ~20-member QA unit filed for an NLRB election after asking for voluntary recognition. The studio had been merged into Blizzard Entertainment from Vicarious Visions in April 2022.
