# Bungie is laying off 220 employees and moving others to PlayStation

> Published 2024-07-31. Fetched 2026-10-18. Verifies: 220 laid off (17% of workforce); 155 to Sony; ~40 spun into new studio; Bungie "running in the red"; 850 remaining staff.

Bungie CEO Pete Parsons announced 220 roles eliminated (17% of workforce). Another 155 employees being integrated into SIE. Combined with the 100 laid off Oct 2023, Bungie lost ~40% of workforce in 12 months. Parsons cited over-expansion, "quality miss" on Lightfall, and the studio "beginning running in the red." Bungie had ~850 remaining staff. One incubation project spun out as new Sony studio.
