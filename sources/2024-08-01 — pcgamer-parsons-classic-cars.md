# Former Bungie devs show fury over layoffs after CEO spent $2.4M on vintage cars

> Published 2024-08-01. Fetched 2026-10-18. Verifies: Pete Parsons spent $2.4M+ on classic cars; employee frustration over pay vs executive spending.

Parsons allegedly spent at least $2,414,550 on vintage cars in the two years following Sony's acquisition. Former community manager Liana Ruppert alleged Parsons bragged about expensive car purchases the Friday before the first layoffs while she discussed not having enough money for groceries.
