# Sony officially closes $3.6 billion acquisition of Bungie

> Published 2022-07-15. Fetched 2026-10-18. Verifies: Sony acquisition of Bungie closed July 15, 2022 for $3.6B; Bungie remains independent subsidiary, multi-platform.

Sony Interactive Entertainment completed its acquisition of Bungie on July 15, 2022. The deal was announced January 31, 2022 valued at $3.6 billion. Bungie CEO Pete Parsons stated Bungie would "continue to independently publish and creatively develop our games" and would remain a multi-platform studio.
