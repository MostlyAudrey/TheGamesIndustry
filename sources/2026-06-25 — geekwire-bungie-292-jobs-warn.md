# Filing: Sony cuts 292 jobs at Bungie in Bellevue following end of Destiny 2 development

> Published 2026-06-25. Fetched 2026-10-18. Verifies: 292 layoffs (WARN filing) June 2026; Destany 2 development ended; studio reorganization; Justin Truman out, Poria Torkan in.

Bungie cut 292 jobs in Bellevue as the Sony-owned studio winds down Destiny 2 and focuses on future projects. Washington State WARN filing listed 292 affected workers with separations starting July 9, 2026. Sony confirmed layoffs cut "most of the Destiny team and some Marathon team members."
