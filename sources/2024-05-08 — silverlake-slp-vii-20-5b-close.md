# Source — Silver Lake: SLP VII closes $20.5B

Fetched 2026-10-15, HTTP 200, full text extracted. Key corroborated facts:

- **Silver Lake Partners VII** final close at **$20.5B** capital commitments, **2024-05-08** (Menlo Park + New York).
- **Aggregate raised over past 5 years: $47B.**
- **Combined AUM: ~$102bn.**
- **Portfolio: ~517,000 employees** globally; ~$258B annual revenue; >$1T cumulative enterprise value.
- **21% net-of-fee return** over the past 15 years (flagship funds).
- **~$20B distributions** to investors since 2023, anchored by **VMware → Broadcom**.
- **Other deals (past year):** **Software AG (~$2.6B)**, **Qualtrics ($12.5B take-private)**, **Vantage Data Centers ($6.4B with DigitalBridge)**, **Endeavor ($13B equity / $25B EV take-private)**.
- **LPs (as of 2024-05-08):** "public and corporate pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, insurance companies, endowments, foundations, funds of funds, family offices, technology industry leaders and individual investors across the Americas, Asia-Pacific, and EMEA."

## Key quotes (exact)
- Egon Durban & Greg Mondre (Co-CEOs): "We are deeply grateful to each of our investors, new and returning, for the confidence they place in Silver Lake."
