# The Verge — Paradox CBA

Covers Paradox Interactive signing a CBA with two Swedish unions: Unionen (white-collar workers) and SACO (professionals with academic degrees). Over 200 members combined at Paradox. Notes Swedish labor model: unions at company level, bargaining for binding agreements.
