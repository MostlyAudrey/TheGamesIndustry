PC Gamer reported Take-Two confirmed sale of Private Division to an unnamed buyer. The sale included "substantially all" of Private Division's live and unreleased titles. Take-Two continued to support No Rest for the Wicked. CEO Strauss Zelnick stated "the titles, though big, were not big in the context of our core intellectual properties at 2K and Rockstar... We are top-ten hit makers around here." Take-Two also confirmed Intercept Games and Roll7 had been closed.
