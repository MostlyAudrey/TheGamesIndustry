# The Last of Us Online Canceled as Naughty Dog Focuses on Single-Player Games

**Source:** IGN, Dec 4, 2024. Fetched 2026-10-18.

Key facts:
- Naughty Dog officially cancels The Last of Us Online
- Neil Druckmann announced the decision
- 100+ developers reassigned
- Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet announced same day
- Decision framed as needing "unsustainable" resources for ongoing live service development
- No additional layoffs from cancellation
