## Capital flow observations

1. **Concentration at the top:** Bitkraft ($1.05B) and March ($1.65B+) control the bulk of institutional capital targeting games among this set.
2. **Early-stage specialists:** LVP, The Games Fund, and Konvoy serve the seed/Series A gap, with LVP having the most storied track record (Supercell, Unity).
3. **Developer-friendly alternatives:** Kowloon Nights' project-based model is distinct from traditional VC — non-dilutive, revenue-share, hands-off development.
4. **Eastern Europe bridge:** The Games Fund uniquely bridges US capital with Eastern European game development talent (Mundfish/Atomic Heart).
5. **LA media-entertainment nexus:** Powerhouse Capital connects gaming to the broader LA media/entertainment ecosystem.
