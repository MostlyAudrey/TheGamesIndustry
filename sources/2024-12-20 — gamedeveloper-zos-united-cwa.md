# ZOS United-CWA formed at ZeniMax Online Studios — December 2024

**December 12, 2024.** 461 workers at ZeniMax Online Studios (developer of The Elder Scrolls Online) unionized under the ZOS United-CWA banner. The unit includes web developers, designers, engineers, and graphic artists. Workers are primarily based in Maryland (Hunt Valley) with remote workers across the US.

Microsoft voluntarily recognized the union. This was the second union at ZOS: ZeniMax Workers United-CWA (QA testers, ~300 members) had organized in January 2023.
