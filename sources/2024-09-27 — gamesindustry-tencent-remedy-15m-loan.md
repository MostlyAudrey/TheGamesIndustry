# Tencent loans €15m to Remedy

Remedy entered into a €15 million unsecured convertible loan agreement with Tencent on September 27, 2024. The loan carries 8% annual non-cash interest with a fixed conversion rate of €27.2 per share, a 63.86% premium on closing price. If converted, Tencent could gain up to 811,100 new Remedy shares, representing approximately 5.98% of all shares, further deepening its ownership.
