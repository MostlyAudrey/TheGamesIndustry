# Tencent paid over $1.5 billion to acquire 67% of Techland

Navigator Capital and Fordata reported that Tencent acquired a 67% controlling stake in Techland. The deal was valued at approximately PLN 6.3 billion ($1.5-1.6B). Techland retained full ownership of its IP (Dying Light, Call of Juarez franchises). Founder Paweł Marchewka remained CEO. This was the largest technology M&A transaction in Polish history in 2023/2024.
