# Source — CVC/Haveli acquire Jagex

Fetched 2026-10-15, HTTP 200, full text extracted. Key corroborated facts:

- **CVC Capital Partners (Fund VIII) + Haveli Investments** agreed to acquire **Jagex** (announced **Feb 9, 2024**); terms not disclosed.
- **Jagex**: HQ **Cambridge, UK**, **>700 employees** worldwide; flagship **RuneScape** has **>$1.5B lifetime revenue** over 23 years and **320M+ player accounts**; acquired **Pipeworks** and **Gamepires** in 2022.
- **CVC**: HQ **Luxembourg**, **>€188bn assets managed**; sports portfolio (Gujarat Titans, LaLiga, LFP, Premiership Rugby, WTA).
- **Haveli Investments**: **Austin-based** PE firm; Gaming Fund has invested in **Omeda Studios (Predecessor)**, **Behaviour Interactive (Dead by Daylight, Montreal)**, and **Candivore (Match Masters, Tel Aviv)**.
- **Jagex was acquired by Carlyle in 2021.**
- **Phil Mansell**, CEO of Jagex, quoted welcoming CVC + Haveli.

## Note
Jagex is **UK-headquartered** (Cambridge). Outside the US/CA core, but the **Haveli Gaming Fund** (Austin, US) and its US/CA-adjacent portfolio (Behaviour Interactive, Montreal) make this a useful capital edge. Flagged as US/CA-adjacent, not core.
