# Activision Blizzard subsidiary wins union vote — The Verge (May 23, 2022)

**Raven Software QA workers win NLRB election 19-2, forming Game Workers Alliance-CWA — the first union at Activision Blizzard.** The victory followed a five-week strike triggered by the layoff of 12 QA testers in December 2021. Activision attempted to block the vote through multiple legal maneuvers but was denied by the NLRB.
