# Tencent raises its stake in Remedy to 14%

Tencent increased its indirect shareholding in Remedy Entertainment to 2,005,716 shares, corresponding to 14.80 percent of Remedy's shares and voting rights, via its Image Frame Investment financing vehicle. The filing was made on April 26, 2024. Tencent originally acquired 3.8% of Remedy shares in May 2021 and increased to 5.01% by November 2022.
