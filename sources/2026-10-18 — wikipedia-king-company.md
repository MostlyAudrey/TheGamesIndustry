# King — Wikipedia Summary

King.com Ltd is Swedish social game developer/publisher. Candy Crush Saga. Acquired Feb 2016 by Activision Blizzard for $5.9B. Now part of Xbox since Oct 2023. ~2,000 employees as of 2017. President Todd Green. 10 studios/offices globally.
