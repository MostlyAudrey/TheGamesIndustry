# PlayStation's annual headcount has declined for the first time since Sony began reporting it

**Source:** Game File, June 13, 2025. Fetched 2026-10-17.

Key facts:
- Sony G&NS (PlayStation) had **12,100 employees** as of March 31, 2025
- Down from **12,700** a year prior — a decline of ~600
- First headcount decline since Sony began publicizing video game workforce size in 2020
- Data quietly updated on Sony's investor site June 3, 2025
- Comes amid two years of very visible layoffs
- Some observers expected more dramatic decline given the scale of layoffs
