# PlayStation's headcount decline through 2025

**Source:** Game File, Jun 13, 2025. Fetched 2026-10-18.

Key facts:
- SIE Game & Network Services headcount: 12,100 as of March 31, 2025
- Down from 12,700 in March 2024
- Net decline of ~600 official employees
- Does not include contractor reductions
- Headcount has been steadily declining since Feb 2024 layoffs
