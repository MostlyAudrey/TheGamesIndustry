# King Union Formation — IGN

Stockholm employees formed a "union club" (Fackklubb) with Swedish Unionen in October 2024. Grew from ~10 members initially to 217 by January 2025. Catalyst was removal of private doctor benefit after Microsoft acquisition. Union club push for CBA.
