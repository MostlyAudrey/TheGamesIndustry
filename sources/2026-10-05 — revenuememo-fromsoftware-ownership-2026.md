# 2026-10-05 — RevenueMemo: Who owns FromSoftware?

FromSoftware ownership as of late 2026: Kadokawa 69.66%, Tencent (Sixjoy HK) 16.25%, Sony (SIE) 14.09%. At the parent level, Sony is Kadokawa's largest shareholder (~10%), followed by Tencent (~7.97%) and Oasis Management (~8.86% as of June 2026).
