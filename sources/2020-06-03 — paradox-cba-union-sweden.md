# Paradox Interactive — CBA with Unionen and SACO

Paradox Interactive announced it would sign a collective bargaining agreement with Unionen and SACO, effective for all Paradox studios across Sweden. The agreement formalizes policies around parental leave, sick leave, benefits, and sets industry standards for game developers in Sweden.
