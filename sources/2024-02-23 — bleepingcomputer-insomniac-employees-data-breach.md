# Insomniac Games alerts employees hit by ransomware data breach

**Source:** Bleeping Computer, Feb 23, 2024. Fetched 2026-10-18.

Key facts:
- Sony confirmed to affected employees that their data was exposed
- Over 1.3 million files stolen from Insomniac's network
- Employee names, addresses, SSNs, passport data compromised
- Sony offered identity theft monitoring services to affected staff
- Ongoing threat from leaked data
