### Sony Interactive Entertainment — ⚠️ Net negative; no union presence
- Feb 2024: ~900 (~8%) — full org [[orgs/Sony Interactive Entertainment]]
- Oct 2024: Firewalk (~170) + Neon Koi (~40) closures (Concord post-mortem)
- 2025: Bend Studio ~30% (~40)
- 2026: Bluepoint closure (~70), Dark Outlaw closure
- **Total tracked: ~1,220+** | Studio closures: 8 since 2020 | Headcount: 12,700→12,100
- Edges: No CWA/UVW union presence; CWA filed ULP over leafletting
- See [[events/2024-02-27 — SIE 900 layoffs (Feb 2024)]]

### Riot Games — ⚠️ Net negative; no formal union
- Jan 2024: 530 (~11%) — core teams (LoL, Valorant, TFT) protected; Riot Forge shuttered; LoR gutted
- Feb 2026: ~80 (2XKO team, post-launch) + 12 publishing
- Oct 2024: 32 (League + publishing)
- Jan 2023: 46 (HR, support, esports)
- **Total tracked: ~700+** | Net: headcount ~4,800→~4,270 (2024 round alone)
- Labor: [[events/2019-05-06 — Riot Games employee walkout]]; [[events/2021-12-27 — Riot Games 100M settlement]] ($100M gender discrimination settlement, CA record)
- Union: No formal union certified; LCS Players Association (employer-funded) raised NLRA questions
- See [[orgs/Riot Games]]

### Epic Games — ⚠️ Net negative; Bandcamp unionized (now divested)
- Sep 2023: 830 (~16%) — Mediatonic hit; Bandcamp divested (half of 118 staff dismissed)
- Mar 2026: ~1,000+ (~20%) — Fortnite engagement decline
- **Total tracked: ~1,830+**
- Union: Bandcamp United formed Mar 2023 (OPEIU, won 31-7), parent Epic accused of union-busting (denied); Union divested with Bandcamp sale to Songtradr
- NLRB complaint 2022: Epic refused to hire labor advocate
- See [[orgs/Epic Games]]

### Ubisoft — ⚠️ Net negative; French unions active; vicious anti-union pattern
- Headcount: 20,729 (Sep 2022) → ~16,590 (Mar 2026) = **~4,100+ reduction**
- Direct layoffs: **~1,100+** across 10+ rounds; 6 studio closures (SF, Osaka, Leamington, Halifax, Winnipeg, Belgrade)
- Tencent bailout: €1.16B for 25% of Vantage Studios (Nov 2025)
- Stock: down ~93% from peak
- French unions: STJV led 3 major strikes (Oct 2024, Oct 2025, Feb 2026 w/ 5 unions → 1,200 strikers)
- Canada: Halifax union certified (CWA Canada, Dec 2025) — **studio closed 3 weeks later**; Montreal petitioning
- See [[orgs/Ubisoft]], [[events/2026-01-07 — Ubisoft Halifax closure]]
- RQ: ask whether the Halifax closure was retaliation; pattern is conspicuous

### Take-Two Interactive — ⚠️ Net negative; largest holdout without a union
- Apr 2024: 579 (5% workforce) — largest T2 layoff ever
- 2023-2024: Roll7 + Intercept Games closed; Private Division sold (most staff already laid off)
- **Total tracked: ~609+** | Headcount: ~12,900 (FY2023) → ~11,600 (FY2024); recovered to ~12,909 via Gearbox acquisition (+800)
- Union: **No union anywhere across 2K, Rockstar, Zynga, or Gearbox** — largest non-union Western publisher
- SAG-AFTRA strike (2024-2025) covered T2 as struck publisher
- See [[orgs/Take-Two Interactive]]

### Embracer Group — ⚠️ The restructuring that redrew the map
- Trigger: $2B Savvy Games Group deal collapsed May 2023
- Restructuring (Jun 2023-May 2024): **4,532 layoffs**, 44 studio closures (16 named + 28 unnamed), 80 games canceled
- Peak headcount: ~15,701 (2023) → ~5,000 (Aug 2026) — **one-third remaining**
- Key names lost: Volition (Saints Row), Free Radical (TimeSplitters), Pieces Interactive (Alone in the Dark), Piranha Bytes (Gothic/Elex), Saber Interactive (sold $247M), Gearbox (sold to Take-Two)
- Corporate split: Embracer + Fellowship + Coffee Stain + Asmodee
- Key sales: Easybrain ($1.2B) post-split
- Union: European works councils / sectoral bargaining provided consultation but didn't prevent mass cuts
- See [[orgs/Embracer Group]]

### Microsoft Gaming — ⚠️ Net negative; ~4,000+ CWA unionized (most in NA gaming)
- Jan 2024: 1,900 (~9% of gaming division; primarily Activision Blizzard)
- Jul 2026: 3,200 (1,600 immediate + 1,600 phased through 2027; CWA members impacted)
- Union: Largest unionized workforce under a major US publisher. 4,000+ CWA members across Blizzard, ZeniMax, Activision. Sep 2026: 1,900 Blizzard workers won first CBA with recall rights, severance formula, AI language
- See [[orgs/Microsoft Gaming]], [[events/2026-09 — Blizzard workers first contract]]
- Activision QA United-CWA (400 members) — **strike authorized Oct 2026**, no contract after 2.5 years
