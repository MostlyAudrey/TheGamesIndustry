title: "Helldivers 2 becomes unexpected mega-hit"
date: 2024-02-08
publisher: Sony / Arrowhead Game Studios
confidence: verified
---

Arrowhead Game Studios' Helldivers 2 launched on February 8, 2024 for PS5 and PC. The cooperative shooter became Sony's fastest-selling PC game and a cultural phenomenon, selling ~12M copies in its first year. Required major server infrastructure scaling. Pushed Arrowhead from ~80 to ~158 employees.

**Labor significance:** Success has allowed Arrowhead to grow significantly with no layoffs. Swedish labor law provides strong protections.
