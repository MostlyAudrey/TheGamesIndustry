4A Games released Metro Exodus on February 15, 2019 — the third entry in the Metro series. The game was a critical and commercial success. The studio, co-founded by veterans of the original Metro 2033 game, operates from both Kyiv, Ukraine and Sliema, Malta.

**Labor significance:** During the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine, 4A Games (being a Ukrainian studio with founders who previously worked in Russia) had to navigate significant operational disruptions. The Malta office provided continuity. No layoffs reported during wartime. No union.
