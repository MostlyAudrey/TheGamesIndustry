# Riot Games 530 layoffs (Jan 2024)

CEO Dylan Jadeja announced elimination of ~530 roles (~11% of workforce). Severance: minimum 6 months salary + 1x APB bonus + health benefits + equity. Riot Forge publishing label sunset (after Bandle Tale release). Legends of Runeterra team reduced and refocused on PvE "Path of Champions." Core LoL, Valorant, TFT, Wild Rift teams continued investment. Laid groundwork for 2024-2026 restructuring trajectory.
