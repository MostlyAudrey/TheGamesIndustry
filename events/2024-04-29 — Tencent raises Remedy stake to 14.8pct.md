# Tencent raises Remedy Entertainment stake to 14.8%

Tencent increased its indirect shareholding in Remedy Entertainment from 5.01% (Nov 2022) to 14.80% via its Image Frame Investment vehicle. Tencent originally acquired 3.8% in May 2021 from Accendo Capital. The stake increase was disclosed via a regulatory filing on April 26, 2024.

In September 2024, Tencent further deepened its involvement with a €15M convertible loan agreement (8% annual non-cash interest, conversion rate €27.20/share), which if fully converted would represent an additional 5.98% of shares, potentially bringing Tencent's total ownership above 20%.
