# Remedy FBC: Firebreak profit warning, CEO steps down

Remedy's FBC: Firebreak, released June 17, 2025, underperformed. On October 10, 2025, Remedy issued a profit warning: €14.9M non-cash impairment on the game, Q3 2025 operating loss of €16.4M (vs €2.4M profit in Q3 2024), 32% Y/Y revenue decline in Q3. On October 22, 2025, CEO Tero Virtala (CEO since 2016) stepped down with immediate effect; co-founder Markus Mäki (23.44% owner) appointed interim CEO. No layoffs announced, but development resources were shifted from Firebreak to other projects including Max Payne remakes and Control 2.
