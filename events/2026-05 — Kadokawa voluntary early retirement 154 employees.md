# 2026-05 — Kadokawa voluntary early retirement program

Kadokawa launched an early retirement program targeting employees aged 45 and over as part of organizational restructuring amid falling profits. 154 employees accepted voluntary redundancy. ¥5.4 billion (~$33.6M) recorded as extraordinary loss for severance. The program aimed for a "leaner" organizational structure.
