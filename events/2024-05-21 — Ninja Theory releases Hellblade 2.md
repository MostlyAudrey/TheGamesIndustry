# Ninja Theory Releases Hellblade II

Senua's Saga: Hellblade II launched on May 21, 2024 for Xbox Series X|S, Windows, and Steam. The game was published by Xbox Game Studios and was available day one on Xbox Game Pass.

## Context

- Sequel to Hellblade: Senua's Sacrifice (2017)
- Highly acclaimed for audiovisual presentation and performance by Melina Juergens
- Sales performance mixed at retail (#37 on full-dollar chart May 2024 per Circana), but stronger on Game Pass (#12 in MAU)
- Ninja Theory reported £22m turnover and £5m profit in post-launch financials
