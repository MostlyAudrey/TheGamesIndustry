# Krafton Acquires Tango Gameworks

Krafton acquired Tango Gameworks (developer of Hi-Fi Rush, The Evil Within) and the Hi-Fi Rush IP from Microsoft, saving the Japanese studio from closure after Microsoft announced its shutdown. The deal took effect August 1, 2024 and was announced August 12. Approximately 50 of the original 105 staff were brought back. This was Krafton's 13th game development studio and was part of an aggressive M&A strategy that included 13 studio investments in 9 months.
