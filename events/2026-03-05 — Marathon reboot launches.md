# Marathon reboot launches March 5, 2026

Bungie's Marathon reboot — an extraction shooter based on the 1990s franchise — launched on March 5, 2026, on PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S.

## Launch performance

- **All-time Steam peak**: ~88,337 concurrent players
- **Price**: $27.99–$39.99
- **Genre**: PvPvE extraction shooter

By September 2026, Marathon had lost ~96% of its Steam peak player count, with ~1,300 concurrent players at reporting time. Bungie acknowledged the game "did not reach as many players as we had hoped."
