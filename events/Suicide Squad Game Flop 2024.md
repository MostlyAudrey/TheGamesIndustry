# Event: Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League Flop (2024)

## Event Summary

Rocksteady's Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League, released on February 2, 2024, was one of the biggest commercial failures in recent gaming history. The live-service looter-shooter, which spent 7 years in development, cost Warner Bros. $200 million in impairment charges.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2017 | Development begins (after WB directs Rocksteady to make a Suicide Squad game) |
| Aug 2020 | Officially announced; controversy over tone/setting (killing Justice League) |
| 2020–2023 | Multiple delays; studio struggles with unfamiliar genre |
| Dec 2022 | Co-founders Jamie Walker and Sefton Hill leave Rocksteady |
| Feb 2, 2024 | Game launches to mixed/negative reviews; poor sales |
| May 9, 2024 | WB discloses $200M impairment charge tied to the game |

| Late 2024 | More Rocksteady layoffs (programming, art) |
| 2024–2025 | Post-launch support seasons released to minimal player base |
| 2025 | WB ends post-launch support early |

## Why It Failed

1. **Tone-deaf concept:** Players objected to killing beloved Batman characters (Justice League) that Rocksteady spent a decade building
2. **Unwanted genre:** A live-service looter-shooter when the market was saturated with similar games (Destiny, The Division, Anthem precedent)
3. **Troubled development:** Studio had no experience in the genre; struggled for 7 years with constant reworks
4. **Co-founder departure:** Loss of Sefton Hill and Jamie Walker mid-development was a major creative blow
5. **Competitive landscape:** Released in a year packed with major titles; also competing with Fortnite, Destiny 2 for live-service attention
6. **Technical issues:** Server problems, bugs, unsatisfying gameplay loop

## Financial Detail

- $200M impairment charge confirmed by WB (May 2024)
- Approximately $100M+ in development costs
- Estimated ~$100M in revenue vs ~$300M+ investment
- Largest single-game loss in WB Games history
- The flop contributed directly to WB Games restructuring and studio closures

## Aftermath

- Rocksteady massively reduced: from ~250 to 214 employees
- Co-founders formed Hundred Star Games (2024)
- WB moved away from live-service strategy
- WB Games refocused on single-player blockbusters (Hogwarts Legacy model)
- Directly cited as a reason for WB Games' 2024–2025 restructuring
