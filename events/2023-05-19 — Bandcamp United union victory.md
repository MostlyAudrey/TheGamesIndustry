# Bandcamp United union victory at Epic Games

**First union formed under Epic Games.** Bandcamp United won NLRB election 31-7 on May 19, 2023.

- Union announced formation March 16, 2023, seeking NLRB election.
- Prior to vote, Epic and Bandcamp CEO Ethan Diamond were accused of union busting tactics (delaying NLRB process). Diamond refuted claims, said he was "following the NLRB's process and timeline."
- Joint statement after vote: both sides committed to good-faith bargaining.
- Bandcamp was acquired by Epic Games in March 2022.
