# Unity 1,800 layoffs (Jan 2024) — 25% of workforce

**Unity's largest layoff ever.** Announced Jan 8, 2024 via regulatory filing and internal company memo. The cuts were framed as the continuation of a "company reset" after the Runtime Fee crisis and Riccitiello's resignation.

## Key details
- **1,800 jobs eliminated** — 25% of then-current workforce (~7,200)
- Fifth round of layoffs since June 2022
- Completed by end of Q1 2024
- Stock rose ~5% on announcement (market reward for cost-cutting)
- Company said it could not "reasonably estimate" total severance costs
- $205M estimated total cost of layoffs for the fiscal year (per Game Developer, Feb 2025)
- Followed earlier "comprehensive assessment" of product portfolio announced in Nov 2023

## CEO context
- James Whitehurst (interim CEO since Oct 2023) presided over this round
- Matthew Bromberg was appointed permanent CEO four months later (May 15, 2024)
