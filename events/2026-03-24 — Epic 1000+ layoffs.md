# Epic Games 1,000+ layoffs — second major wave

**Second major layoff round in three years.** Epic Games laid off 1,000+ employees on March 24, 2026 (~20% of workforce).

- CEO Tim Sweeney attributed cuts to a "downturn" in Fortnite engagement starting in 2025.
- Sweeney announced $500M in identified cost savings across the business.
- Sweeney specifically stated: "the layoffs aren't related to AI."
- Promised "major cuts to keep the company funded" would put Epic "in a more stable place."
- Followed the Sep 2023 round of 830 layoffs.
