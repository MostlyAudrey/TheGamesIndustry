# 2025-12 — id Software unionizes with CWA

**December 12, 2025.** 165 workers at id Software (~185 total employees) formed a wall-to-wall union with CWA Local 6215. Microsoft recognized the union per its labor principles. The union covers developers, artists, programmers, and other roles.

The organizing effort began ~18 months prior and accelerated after Microsoft's 2024 closure of several Bethesda studios. Key issues driving the unionization included AI protections, remote work guarantees, and benefits security — with workers citing the recent layoffs at ZeniMax Online Studios as motivation.

The ZeniMax-specific neutrality agreement (valid until May 2026) enabled workers to organize without the kind of pushback typical in other campaigns.
