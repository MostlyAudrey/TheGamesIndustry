# 2024-10-06 — 343 Industries rebrands as Halo Studios

**343 Industries officially rebranded to Halo Studios** at the Halo World Championships on October 6, 2024. The studio announced it was shifting all future Halo development from the proprietary Slipspace Engine to Unreal Engine 5, with multiple new Halo games in development. The rebrand followed years of struggles with Halo Infinite's development and a leadership exodus including studio GM Bonnie Ross (departed September 2022) and veteran Joseph Staten (departed 2023). The new leadership team consists of Pierre Hintze (CEO), Bryan Koski (GM), and Elizabeth Van Wyck (COO). The studio also showcased "Project Foundry," a UE5 tech demo.
