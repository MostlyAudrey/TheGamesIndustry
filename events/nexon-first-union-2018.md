# Nexon Forms Korea's First Game Industry Union

On September 3, 2018, employees of Nexon Korea officially formed the first labor union in the Korean video game industry at a major publisher. Named "Starting Point" (시작점), the union organized under the Korean Confederation of Trade Unions (KCTU).

The union stated its mission was to "act as a tow truck to improve labor conditions in the gaming industry as a whole," citing poor labor conditions in Korean gaming despite the industry's economic importance.
