# Event — Ubisoft Creative Houses restructure (Jan 2026)

**What:** Yves Guillemot announced a "major organizational reset" dividing Ubisoft into 5 genre-focused Creative Houses. Six games cancelled, seven delayed. Targeting €1.25B in fixed cost reductions by 2028.

**When:** Announced January 21, 2026.

**Cancelled games:** Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time remake (publicly named), plus three unannounced new IP projects, a mobile game, and Splinter Cell VR (unannounced). Tom Clancy's The Division: Heartland also cancelled separately.

**Structure:** Creative Houses:
1. Massive Entertainment (The Division, Ghost Recon, Splinter Cell)
2-5. Other genre-focused divisions

**Cost target:** €200M in cuts by March 2028, on top of €100M already achieved.

**Stock impact:** Shares dropped 34% in a single day — largest one-day drop ever. Market cap fell to €600-850M (93% decline from 2018 peak).
