# Frogwares During Russian Invasion (2022-present)

Frogwares was directly impacted by the Russian invasion of Ukraine on February 24, 2022. Staff split between military service, humanitarian aid, and continued development. The studio adopted a hybrid model, removed games from Russia, received Epic MegaGrant for relocation, and released Sherlock Holmes: The Awakened (April 2023) developed entirely during the war.
