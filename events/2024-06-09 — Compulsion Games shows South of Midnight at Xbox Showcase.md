# Compulsion Games shows South of Midnight at Xbox Showcase

During the Xbox Games Showcase on June 9, 2024, Compulsion Games debuted the first gameplay footage of **South of Midnight**, a third-person action-adventure game set in the American Deep South with a dark fantasy aesthetic. The game was revealed at the 2023 Xbox Showcase with a cinematic trailer; the 2024 showcase showed gameplay demonstrating the protagonist Hazel's magical weaving abilities and encounters with creatures inspired by Southern folklore.

## Context

- South of Midnight was Compulsion's first new IP since the Microsoft acquisition
- The game launched on April 8, 2025 for Xbox Series X|S and PC
- Development employed approximately 80-100 staff at Compulsion's Montreal studio
- The game won Game of the Year and Experience of the Year at the Games for Change Awards 2026
- In July 2026, Compulsion Games spun out from Xbox as an independent studio
