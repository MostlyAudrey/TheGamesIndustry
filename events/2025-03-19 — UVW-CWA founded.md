# UVW-CWA founded

**United Videogame Workers-CWA Local 9433** launched on March 19, 2025 at the Game Developers Conference in San Francisco. The first direct-join, industry-wide video game union in North America, organized in partnership with the Communications Workers of America (CWA) and the American Federation of Musicians (AFM).

**Model:** Pre-majority unionism (direct-join). Workers join as individuals, regardless of studio, employment status, or location (US + Canada). Traditional NLRB certification is not required for members to begin building power.

**Context:** Builds off Game Workers Unite (founded 2018 at GDC) and CODE-CWA, which has organized 6,500+ tech and video game workers since 2020.

**Launch demands:** Petition for dignity and job security for all video game workers, particularly those facing layoffs.
