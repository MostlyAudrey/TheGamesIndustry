# Capcom salary increases (March 2024)

Capcom announced a comprehensive salary increase package in March 2024, bucking the industry-wide layoff trend.

## Details
- **All employees:** average 5% salary increase in fiscal year 2024 (ending March 31, 2025)
- **New graduate hires:** Starting salary raised from ¥235,000/month (~$1,570) to ¥300,000/month (~$2,000) — approximately 28% increase — effective fiscal year 2025
- **One-time special payment:** For current employees and those hired before March 31, 2024
- **HR reforms:** Restructuring HR operations, appointing a Chief HR Officer, implementing a bonus system, and employee stock-based compensation system

## Context
- Announced amid industry-wide layoffs exceeding 10,500 in 2023 and 7,500+ in early 2024
- Part of a broader pattern of Japanese publishers raising wages instead of cutting jobs
- Capcom had also enacted base salary increases in 2022
