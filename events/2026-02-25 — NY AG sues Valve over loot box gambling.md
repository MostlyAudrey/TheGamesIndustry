New York Attorney General Letitia James sued Valve Corporation on Feb 25, 2026 (Index No. 450952/2026, NY Supreme Court), alleging that Valve's loot box mechanics in games like Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2 constitute illegal gambling under Article I, Section 9 of the NY Constitution and Penal Law §§ 225.05 and 225.10 (promoting gambling). The suit claims Valve made billions of dollars from the practice and that its skin trading ecosystem enables unlicensed third-party gambling sites. Valve responded by comparing loot boxes to baseball cards and blind boxes, calling the lawsuit disappointing.

In 2025, Valve had made changes to its skin economy that reportedly wiped out over $1B in skin values, causing a CS2 skin market crash.
