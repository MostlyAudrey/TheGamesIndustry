# Matthew Bromberg appointed Unity CEO

Unity appointed Matthew Bromberg as Chief Executive Officer, President, and a member of the Board of Directors, effective May 15, 2024. Bromberg succeeded James Whitehurst, who had served as interim CEO since October 2023.

## Background
- Former COO of Zynga (2019-2024)
- Former SVP of Strategy and Operations at Electronic Arts
- Previously held roles at Guitar Center, Bertelsmann

## Key actions as CEO
- Cancelled Runtime Fee (Sep 12, 2024)
- Implemented further layoffs (Feb 2025)
- Shifted strategy toward "fidelity for ubiquity," live services, generative AI
- Raised subscription prices for larger customers
