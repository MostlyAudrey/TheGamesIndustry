# Riot Games employee walkout (May 6, 2019)

More than 150-200 employees walked out of Riot's LA headquarters to protest forced arbitration in sexual discrimination and harassment cases. Reported as the first employee walkout in the video game industry. Followed a Kotaku exposé on Riot's "bro culture." Riot initially maintained forced arbitration for existing employees but later allowed new hires to opt out for sexual harassment claims.
