# Riot Games 32 layoffs (Oct 2024)

Second wave of layoffs in 2024. Announced by co-founder Marc Merrill. 27 roles cut from League of Legends team, 5 from publishing side. Part of evolving LoL for "next phase." Combined with January 2024 cuts, total ~562 layoffs for the year.
