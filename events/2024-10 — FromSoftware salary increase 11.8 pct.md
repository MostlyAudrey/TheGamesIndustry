# 2024-10 — FromSoftware announces ~11.8% base salary increase

FromSoftware raised base salaries by approximately 11.8%. Starting salary for new graduates increased from ¥260,000 to ¥300,000/month. First significant wage adjustment following years of public criticism over below-industry pay revealed in 2022 reports. Miyazaki also stated mass layoffs "won't happen at FromSoftware as long as this company is my responsibility."
