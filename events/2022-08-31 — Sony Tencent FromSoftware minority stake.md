# 2022-08-31 — Sony and Tencent acquire combined 30.34% stake in FromSoftware

FromSoftware issued new shares via third-party allotment. Tencent subsidiary Sixjoy Hong Kong acquired 16.25%, Sony Interactive Entertainment acquired 14.09%. Kadokawa Corporation retained majority ownership (~69.66%). Deal valued at approximately ¥36.4 billion (~$259 million).

**Significance:** First time external investors held significant minority stakes in FromSoftware. Gave Sony and Tencent board-level influence over one of the most valuable independent game studios in Japan, following the massive commercial success of Elden Ring (released Feb 2022, >20M copies).
