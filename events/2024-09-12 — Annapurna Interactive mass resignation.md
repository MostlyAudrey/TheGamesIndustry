# Annapurna Interactive mass resignation

The entire Annapurna Interactive publishing team (~25 employees) resigned en masse on September 12, 2024, after negotiations to spin off the gaming division into an independent entity broke down with Annapurna Pictures owner Megan Ellison.

## Impact
- Nathan Gary (president) and entire staff walked out.
- Annapurna Pictures retained the IP and brand; rebuilt publishing team from scratch.
- Former Epic Games executive Hector Sanchez took over Oct 2024.
- Annapurna later expanded: acquired mm2, co-published *Control 2*, built film/TV pipeline.

## Significance
One of the most dramatic labor events in games publishing — an entire division resigned at once over structural disagreement with ownership.
