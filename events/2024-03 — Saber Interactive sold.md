Saber Interactive was sold to Beacon Interactive, a company owned by Saber co-founder Matthew Karch, for $247 million. The deal included Saber's satellite studios and 2,950 employees (21% of Embracer's workforce), including all staff in Russia. Some studios (4A Games, Aspyr, Beamdog, Tripwire Interactive, Zen Studios) remained with Embracer with a future option that was later canceled. [[sources/2024-03-14 — vgc-embracer-saber-sale]]
