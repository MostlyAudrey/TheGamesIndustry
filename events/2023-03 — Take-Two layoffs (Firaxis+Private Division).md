# 2023-03 — Take-Two layoffs (Firaxis + Private Division)

In **March 2023**, Take-Two confirmed layoffs affecting **30 employees at Firaxis Games** (developer of *Civilization* and *Marvel's Midnight Suns*) and an unspecified number at publishing label **Private Division**. The company cited "exponential growth in recent years" as context for the restructuring.
