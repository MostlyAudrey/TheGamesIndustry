# Nexon Workforce Reallocation 2026

At a Capital Market Briefing in Tokyo on March 31, 2026, Nexon Co. announced it was considering workforce redeployment as part of a broader efficiency drive while ruling out layoffs or forced restructuring. This marked a contrast to Krafton's voluntary resignation program and NCSoft's restructuring layoffs among major Korean game publishers.
