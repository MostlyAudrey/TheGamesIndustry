# Bungie layoffs July 2024 — 220 positions (17% of workforce)

Bungie announced 220 job cuts (17% of workforce) on July 31, 2024.

## Key numbers

- **Total laid off**: 220
- **Transferred to Sony**: ~155 employees moved into other SIE departments
- **Spun into new studio**: ~40 staff forming a new Sony studio (action game, new science-fantasy universe)
- **Remaining Bungie headcount**: ~850
- **Cumulative since Oct 2023**: ~40% of total workforce lost in 12 months

## CEO statement

Parsons wrote: "We were overly ambitious, our financial safety margins were subsequently exceeded, and we began running in the red."

## Controversy

Following the announcement, it was revealed Parsons spent at least $2.4M on vintage cars since the Sony acquisition. Former employees reported Parsons bragging about car purchases before layoffs while staff discussed struggling to afford groceries.

## Aftermath

- Combined with Oct 2023 layoffs, Bungie lost ~40% of workforce in 12 months
- Staff and former employees called for unionization on social media
- SAG-AFTRA and CWA issued statements
