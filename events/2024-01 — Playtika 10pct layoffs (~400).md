Playtika cut approximately 10% of its workforce (~300-400 employees) in January 2024. The cuts targeted primarily Central and Eastern European offices; the company's ~1,100 Israeli employees were largely left untouched. Later in 2024, Playtika eliminated CRO and COO roles.
