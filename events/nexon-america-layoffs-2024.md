# Nexon America Layoffs 2024

Nexon America laid off a "small number" of staff as part of a restructuring announced earlier in September 2024. The layoffs accompanied plans to launch a new Western Development Group in Los Angeles. Nexon stated the job cuts were part of a "hyperlocalization" strategy for major Western markets, not related to any specific game's performance.
