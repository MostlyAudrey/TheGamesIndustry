# NCSoft Corporate Split and Layoffs

NCSoft announced a sweeping restructuring including a corporate split-off into four wholly-owned subsidiaries (Studio X, Studio Y, Studio Z, NC AI), layoffs of an undisclosed number of employees, and cancellation of multiple projects including Project M and Battle Crush. A voluntary retirement program was offered. Co-CEOs cited deteriorating financial conditions and risk of becoming a "chronically loss-making company." Shareholders approved the plan on Nov 28, 2024; effective Feb 1, 2025.
