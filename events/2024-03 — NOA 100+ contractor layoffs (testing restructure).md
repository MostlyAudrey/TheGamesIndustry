# Nintendo of America contractor layoffs (March 2024)

Nintendo of America laid off over 100 contractors in its Product Testing division, as reported by Kotaku and confirmed by a Nintendo spokesperson. The layoffs came as part of a reorganization to "drive greater global integration in game development efforts." A "significant number" of full-time positions were reportedly created, although those roles would move workers out of software testing.

## Context
- Nintendo had been the last of the "Big Three" platform holders to conduct layoffs in 2024, after Sony (~900 in Feb 2024) and Microsoft (1,900 in Jan 2024).
- Several contractors told Kotaku they had not recently tested any major first-party titles and had no access to the then-unannounced Switch 2.
- NOA contractors had previously made claims of harmful work culture, pay inequity, and sexism/harassment (reported by Kotaku in August 2022).
- NOA had been taken to task in 2022 for allegedly firing employees for discussing unionization efforts.
