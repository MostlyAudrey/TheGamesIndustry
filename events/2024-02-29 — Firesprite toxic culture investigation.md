# Firesprite toxic culture investigation (Eurogamer)

**February 29, 2024.** Eurogamer published an investigation into Firesprite revealing a chaotic and toxic work environment.

## Key findings
- **Crunch culture**: After Sony's acquisition, crunch was implemented to finish Horizon Call of the Mountain
- **Toxic environment**: Staff described a "sinking ship" atmosphere with poor management decisions
- **High-profile exits**: Senior developers were leaving the studio in waves
- **Communication breakdown**: Staff reported not being informed about the studio's direction or their job security
- **Cancellation**: Twisted Metal live-service game was cancelled as part of the same SIE restructuring

## Context
- The investigation began before the Feb 2024 layoffs were announced
- Firesprite was among the hardest-hit studios in SIE's 900-person layoff wave
- Several senior staff, including the creative director of Horizon Call of the Mountain, were laid off
- Founder Graeme Ankers departed later in 2024
