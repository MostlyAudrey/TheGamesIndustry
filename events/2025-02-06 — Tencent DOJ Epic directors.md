# Tencent DOJ antitrust action — Epic Games directors

The US Department of Justice announced that Tencent removed two directors from Epic Games' board and relinquished its right to unilaterally appoint directors or observers, after the Antitrust Division raised concerns that the dual directorships violated Section 8 of the Clayton Act. Tencent's stake in Epic was ~28% as of 2026 (down from ~40% initial).
