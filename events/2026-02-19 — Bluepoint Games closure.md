# Bluepoint Games closure

**February 2026.** Sony shut down Bluepoint Games (Austin, TX) — the acclaimed studio behind the Shadow of the Colossus (2018) and Demon's Souls (2020) remakes.

## Key details
- Acquired by Sony in **2021**
- Was working on **unannounced live-service God of War game**
- That project was **canceled in January 2025** as Sony retreated from live-service
- Studio never recovered from cancellation
- ~70 jobs lost
- Third Sony-owned studio closed in two years (after Neon Koi and Firewalk)

## Industry reaction
- Widely reported as a "new low for PlayStation" (Polygon)
- Seen as emblematic of SIE's failed acquisition and live-service strategy
- 60% of Sony's post-2019 studio acquisitions experienced layoffs or shutdown
