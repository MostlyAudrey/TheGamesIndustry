# Bend Studio 30% layoffs

**June 2025.** Sony's Bend Studio (Days Gone developer, Oregon) laid off ~30% of staff (~40 people).

Cancellation of an unannounced live-service project — part of Sony's broader retreat from the live-service games push following Concord's failure.

Sony confirmed the studio is working on a new project and remains "confident in its future."

## Context
- Bluepoint's live-service God of War also canceled around same time (Jan 2025)
- Following Concord ($200-400M flop), SIE scaled back live-service ambitions
- Bend Studio survived this round but operations significantly reduced
