# Krafton AI-First Transformation

In October 2025, Krafton announced it was becoming an "AI-first company," planning to invest over 130 billion won ($88M) in agentic AI infrastructure beginning in 2026. On November 4, 2025 earnings call, CFO Bae Dong-geun announced a company-wide hiring freeze except for AI and original IP roles. On November 12, Krafton launched a voluntary resignation program offering tenure-based buyouts. The program was widely criticized as a de facto layoff plan during record profitability ($717M quarterly profit).
