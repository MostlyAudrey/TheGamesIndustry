# NetEase lays off Marvel Rivals Seattle design team (Feb 2025)

NetEase Games confirmed it laid off the Marvel Rivals design team based in Seattle, Washington on February 19, 2025. Game director Thaddeus Sasser announced on LinkedIn that his entire team was laid off after delivering "an incredibly successful new franchise for NetEase." A level designer also confirmed being impacted.

NetEase's official statement said the cuts were for "organizational reasons" and to "optimize development efficiency." The core development team remains in Guangzhou, China, led by Lead Producer Weicong Wu and Game Creative Director Guangyun Chen. The layoffs came hours before NetEase revealed Rivals had 40 million players and generated an estimated $136M in its first month.

This was part of a broader pullback under CEO William Ding, who Bloomberg reported had shifted to prioritizing "evergreen, mass-market" hits and pulled back from international investments.
