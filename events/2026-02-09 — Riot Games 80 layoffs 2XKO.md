# Riot Games ~80 layoffs (2XKO team, Feb 2026)

Riot laid off ~80 employees (~half the dev team) from the 2XKO fighting game team less than three weeks after the game's full launch (Jan 20, 2026). 2XKO had an extended early access period before full launch. Riot pledged to continue supporting the game with a smaller "focused team."
