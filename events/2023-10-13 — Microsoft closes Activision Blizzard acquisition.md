# 2023-10-13 — Microsoft closes Activision Blizzard acquisition ($68.7B)

**The largest acquisition in video game history closed.** Microsoft completed its $68.7 billion all-cash acquisition of Activision Blizzard on October 13, 2023, after 20 months of regulatory review. The deal brought Activision Publishing, Blizzard Entertainment, and King Digital under Microsoft Gaming alongside Xbox Game Studios and ZeniMax Media/Bethesda.

Regulatory hurdles included:
- FTC lawsuit seeking to block the deal (lost in federal court July 2023)
- UK CMA initial block (resolved through restructured deal terms)
- EU conditional approval

The closing of the deal activated the Microsoft-CWA Labor Neutrality Agreement 60 days later, enabling the largest unionization wave in US video game history.
