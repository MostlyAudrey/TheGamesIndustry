# Microsoft Acquires inXile Entertainment

At X018 on November 10, 2018, Microsoft announced the acquisition of inXile Entertainment alongside Obsidian Entertainment. inXile, founded by Brian Fargo in 2002, was known for the Wasteland series, Torment: Tides of Numenera, and The Bard's Tale IV.

## Context

- Founded by Interplay co-founder Brian Fargo
- Known for reviving classic CRPG franchises
- Post-acquisition: Wasteland 3 (2020), Clockwork Revolution (announced but no release date)
- Operates studios in Tustin, CA and New Orleans, LA
- Relatively small, stable team (~80-100 estimated)
