# CD Projekt RED employees form first Polish game industry union

Employees of CD Projekt RED announced the formation of the **Związek Pracowników Branży Gier** (Game Industry Workers Union), the first labor union in the Polish video game industry. The union operates under the nationwide **Ogólnopolski Związek Zawodowy Inicjatywa Pracownicza (OZZ IP)** rather than as a single-company union, allowing all Polish game industry workers to join.

**Key details:**
- Monthly membership fee: 16 PLN
- Management reportedly did not react negatively to the union's formation
- The union became public knowledge once documentation was submitted to HR
- Focus on educating young workers about their labor rights under Polish law

**Context:** The union formed shortly before the release of Cyberpunk 2077: Phantom Liberty (September 2023), during which CDPR publicly committed to "no mandatory crunch" — a sharp reversal from the mandatory six-day weeks ordered during Cyberpunk 2077's original launch (September-December 2020).
