# Pete Parsons steps down as Bungie CEO

Pete Parsons announced his departure as Bungie studio head on August 21, 2025, after years of criticism over:

- Multiple layoff rounds (2023, 2024)
- The $2.4M classic car collection revealed shortly after the 2024 layoffs
- Bungie's financial struggles after the Sony acquisition

Justin Truman, a Bungie veteran, succeeded Parsons as studio head.
