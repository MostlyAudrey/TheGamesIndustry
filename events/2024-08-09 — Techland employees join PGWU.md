# Techland employees join Polish Gamedev Workers Union

On August 9, 2024, the Polish Gamedev Workers Union (PGWU) announced that Techland employees had formally joined the union. The PGWU was originally founded at CD Projekt RED in October 2023 and is open to all Polish game industry workers. Techland is listed as an official Inter-workplace Commission member alongside CD Projekt, 11 bit studios, Flying Wild Hog, Product Madness, and Lionbridge. This represents the expansion of the PGWU beyond its founding studio, suggesting growing labor organizing in the Polish game industry.
