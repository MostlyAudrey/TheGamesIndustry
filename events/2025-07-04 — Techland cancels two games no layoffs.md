# Techland cancels two unannounced games, reports record losses

Techland cancelled two unannounced projects mid-development in July 2025. The studio recorded record financial losses of PLN 135M ($37.4M) in 2024 and $25M in 2023. Despite the cancellations, Techland confirmed to Eurogamer and GamesIndustry.biz that no layoffs occurred — affected developers were reassigned to the remaining project pipeline including Dying Light: The Beast and an unannounced AAA open-world fantasy RPG described as the studio's "biggest project to date."
