# Neople Union Strike Threat

The labor union of Neople (Nexon subsidiary that developed Dungeon & Fighter) threatened to take industrial action after wage negotiations ended in failure. The union pointed out that Neople generated 3.8 trillion won ($2.7B) in operating profit between 2019-2023 — over 70% of all Nexon Group affiliates' profits — yet was offered wage increases similar to less profitable affiliates. Management countered that the union demanded 9.24% wage increase despite an 11% drop in annual operating profit.
