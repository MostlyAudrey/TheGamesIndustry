# Nintendo of America NLRB complaint + settlement (2022)

Former Nintendo of America QA worker MacKenzie Clifton filed an NLRB complaint against Nintendo of America and staffing firm Aston Carter, alleging they were discharged for engaging in protected union activities. Clifton stated they had asked about unionization during a company-wide Q&A with NOA president Doug Bowser.

## Timeline
- **2022-04-15:** Complaint filed with NLRB (Region 19, Seattle)
- **2022-04-19:** Axios broke the story of the complaint
- **2022-04-20:** Nintendo responded, denied knowledge of union activity, stated termination was for disclosing confidential information
- **2022-10-13:** Nintendo and Aston Carter settled with Clifton for $25,910 in back pay, damages, and interest. Nintendo was required to post a notice informing workers of their right to "form, join or assist a union."

## Significance
- First documented union-related labor complaint against Nintendo in the US
- Settlement required Nintendo to post worker-rights notice — standard NLRB term but rare for the company
- Led to broader reporting on NOA's contractor treatment, culminating in the August 2022 Kotaku investigation
