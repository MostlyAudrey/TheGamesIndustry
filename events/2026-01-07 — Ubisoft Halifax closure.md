# Event — Ubisoft Halifax closed (Jan 2026)

**What:** Ubisoft closed its Halifax studio, laying off 71 workers, just weeks after the studio voted 74% to unionize with CWA Canada.

**When:** January 7, 2026.

**Controversy:** Ubisoft said the closure was part of ongoing cost-cutting. CWA Canada argued it was retaliation for unionization, which is illegal under Canadian labor law. The union is pursuing legal recourse and demanding Ubisoft disclose its reasoning.

**History:** The studio started as Longtail Studios (founded by Ubisoft co-founder Gérard Guillemot), was partially absorbed in 2010, and fully acquired by Ubisoft in 2015. It had previously attempted to unionize in 2008 (documented by IGN).
