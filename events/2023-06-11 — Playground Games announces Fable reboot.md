# Playground Games Announces Fable Reboot

At the June 11, 2023 Xbox Games Showcase, Playground Games unveiled the first in-game trailer for the Fable reboot. The trailer featured Richard Ayoade as a character named "Dave." No release date was provided at the time. The Fable reboot was first announced in July 2020 alongside the Xbox Series X/S console reveals.

## Context

- Developed by Playground Games (Forza Horizon studio), their first RPG
- First mainline Fable game since Fable III (2010)
- Built on the ForzaTech engine
- Initial announcement: July 2020 at Xbox Games Showcase
- In-game trailer reveal: June 11, 2023
