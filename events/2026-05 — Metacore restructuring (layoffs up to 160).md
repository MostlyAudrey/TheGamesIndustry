# Metacore restructuring (May–June 2026)

On **May 5, 2026**, Metacore (Merge Mansion developer) announced a restructuring plan that would cut **up to 160 roles in Finland** and review operations in Germany and Sweden.

The announcement came simultaneously with Supercell's plan to **fully acquire Metacore**. Supercell was already a major shareholder, having invested **€180M** in the company.

On **June 22, 2026**, Metacore confirmed the cuts: **159 job losses in Finland** and **full closure of its Germany and Sweden offices**.

Metacore employed approximately **280 people** at the time, meaning the cuts eliminated **more than half** the workforce. The company had previously acquired Supercell's canceled game *Everdale* (January 2023) but canceled its reboot 18 months later.

Supercell CEO Ilkka Paananen said Merge Mansion's "best days are still ahead" with Supercell's live operations expertise. The acquisition remained in process as of June 2026.
