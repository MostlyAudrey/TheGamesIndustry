# Bungie September 2026: Destiny 2 reversal, Marathon roadmap

On September 21, 2026, under new studio head Poria Torkan and product head Josh Deane, Bungie issued a major strategic update:

## Destiny 2 resurrection

- Reversed the decision to end Destiny 2 live service
- Announced un-vaulting of previously removed campaigns, locations, and raids
- "Destiny is foundational to Bungie's future"
- No new games to announce but "not done with Destiny"

## Marathon admission

- "Did not reach as many players as we had hoped"
- Plan to "evolve it beyond the genre constraints" of extraction shooter
- Permanent PvE mode and social space in development (December 2026 target)

## Leadership tone

Torkan and Deane: "We lost trust, we lost incredible colleagues, and we lost sight of what we should be prioritizing most, our players."

## Context

Earlier that year (June 2026), Bungie laid off at least 292 employees, including most of the Destiny 2 team. Sony took a $765M writedown.
