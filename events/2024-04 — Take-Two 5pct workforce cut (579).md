# 2024-04-16 — Take-Two 5% workforce layoff (579)

Take-Two Interactive announced a cost-reduction plan on **April 16, 2024**, laying off **5% of its workforce** (approximately **579 employees**). The company also cancelled several unannounced projects. The layoffs were company-wide, affecting publishing labels **2K**, **Rockstar Games**, and **Zynga**.

## Details
- **Layoff count:** 579 (5% of workforce)
- **Date:** 2024-04-16
- **Scope:** Company-wide across all Take-Two labels
- **Cuts:** Also cancelled multiple unannounced projects
- **Total employees before cut:** approximately 11,600
