# Microsoft Acquires Double Fine Productions

At E3 2019 on June 9, 2019, Microsoft announced the acquisition of Double Fine Productions, the San Francisco-based studio founded by Tim Schafer in 2000. The acquisition was notable as Double Fine had been a prominent independent developer known for Psychonauts, Brütal Legend, and Broken Age.

## Context

- Studio founder Tim Schafer remained as studio head
- Acquisition ensured completion of Psychonauts 2 (released August 2021)
- Retained independent culture within Xbox Game Studios
- Released 22-hour PsychOdyssey documentary series (2023) with 90-minute update (2024)
- Stable headcount; no known layoffs since acquisition
