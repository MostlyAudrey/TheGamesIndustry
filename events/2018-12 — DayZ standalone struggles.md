DayZ, originally a mod for Arma 2, became a standalone survival game developed by Bohemia Interactive. Released in Early Access in December 2013. The game's development was prolonged and controversial, with a split between creator Dean Hall (who left in 2014) and Bohemia.

**Labor significance:** Bohemia is based in Prague, Czech Republic. Czech labor law provides standard protections. No union reported. Bohemia has maintained stable employment (~400+ employees) through DayZ and Arma Reforger.
