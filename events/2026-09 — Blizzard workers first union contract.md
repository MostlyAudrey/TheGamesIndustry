# 2026-09 — Blizzard workers win first union contract

**Largest union contract victory in North American video game history.** ~1,900 workers across 6 Blizzard Entertainment bargaining units won their first CBA in early September 2026. Key terms: 14-month recall rights across all Blizzard units, severance (4 wks + 1 wk/6 months tenure, max 39 wks), just cause, grievance/arbitration, remote work guarantee for existing remote workers (~25%), AI bargaining language (company must discuss/evaluate/bargain over AI impact), outsourcing protections, successorship clause, paid May 1 holiday. After this win, Activision QA United-CWA still lacked a first contract, authorizing a strike in October 2026.
