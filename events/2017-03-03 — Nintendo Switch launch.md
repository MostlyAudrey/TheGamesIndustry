# Nintendo Switch Launch (March 3, 2017)

**Nintendo's seventh major home console (hybrid handheld/home system).** Launched globally on March 3, 2017 at $299.99 USD, following a January 2017 reveal event.

## Context

The Switch launched after the commercial failure of the Wii U (~13.5 million units lifetime), which had left Nintendo in a precarious  position. Under president Tatsumi Kimishima, the company consolidated its software divisions into EPD and bet the company on a hybrid console design.

## Launch details

- **Launch date:** March 3, 2017 (USA, Canada, Europe, Australia, Japan, Hong Kong)
- **Launch price:** $299.99 USD / ¥29,980 JPY
- **Launch titles:** The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild (launch window), 1-2-Switch, Super Bomberman R, Skyrim (later)

## Commercial impact

- Sold **2.74 million units in its first month** (March 2017)
- Sold **over 146 million units lifetime** (as of 2026) — third best-selling console of all time behind PlayStation 2 and Nintendo DS
- Revived Nintendo's hardware business after Wii U failure
- **Sustained profitability throughout its lifecycle**, enabling Nintendo's no-layoff labor model
- Each major first-party software release (Mario Odyssey 2017, Smash Ultimate 2018, Animal Crossing 2020, Tears of the Kingdom 2023) drove sustained hardware sales

## Labor significance

The Switch's unprecedented commercial success is a key factor in Nintendo's ability to maintain zero layoffs of permanent employees during the 2022-2026 industry downturn. While Sony and Microsoft conducted mass layoffs, Nintendo remained profitable enough to protect all permanent roles and even increase wages.
