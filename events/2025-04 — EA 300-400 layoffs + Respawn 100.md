# EA 300-400 elimination; Respawn projects canceled

**Second major EA layoff wave of 2025.** EA eliminated 300-400 positions company-wide (Bloomberg report), with ~100 at Respawn Entertainment.

Respawn stated it was 'stepping away' from two early-stage incubation projects:
- **'R7'** — an Apex Legends extraction shooter (per Bloomberg)
- An unnamed second project

Respawn described the cuts as 'targeted team adjustments' on Apex Legends and Star Wars Jedi teams, offering 'new opportunities within EA' to affected workers.

This came against a backdrop of EA citing the 'underperformance' of Dragon Age: The Veilguard and EA Sports FC 25, and EA's accelerating embrace of generative AI.

**Context:** This follows the 2024 5% cut (~670 positions) and marks ~1,000 positions eliminated from EA since the start of 2024, all before the $52.5B buyout closes.
