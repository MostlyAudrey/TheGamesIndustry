# Unity Runtime Fee announcement and CEO crisis (Sep-Oct 2023)

## Timeline

### Sep 12, 2023 — Runtime Fee announced
Unity announced a "Runtime Fee" charging developers a per-install fee on Unity games above certain revenue/install thresholds. The fee would apply retroactively to existing titles and was calculated per new install, not per sale.

### Sep 12-22, 2023 — Immediate backlash
- Major indie developers publicly condemned the policy
- A consortium of mobile developers enacted a boycott of Unity Ads monetization
- Unity partially revised the fee structure (higher thresholds, no retroactive application on older Unity versions)

### Oct 9, 2023 — Riccitiello resigns
John Riccitiello stepped down as CEO, president, chairman, and board member "effective immediately." James M. Whitehurst (former Red Hat CEO) appointed interim CEO, president, and board member. Roelof Botha (Sequoia Capital partner) became chairman.

### Sep 12, 2024 — Runtime Fee canceled
New CEO Matthew Bromberg announced the full cancellation of the Runtime Fee "effective immediately," replacing it with subscription price increases for larger customers.

## Significance
- Riccitiello's 9-year tenure ended by a single pricing misstep
- The crisis directly triggered the "company reset" that drove the massive Jan 2024 layoff round
- Developer trust severely damaged; migration to Godot/Unreal Engine increased
- Canceled one year later under Bromberg, after the damage was already done
