# 2022-01-18 — Microsoft announces $68.7B Activision Blizzard acquisition

**The largest acquisition in video game history.** Microsoft announced its intent to acquire Activision Blizzard for $68.7 billion on January 18, 2022. The deal closed October 13, 2023 after regulatory review. A key condition: Microsoft signed a Labor Neutrality Agreement with CWA, which CWA supported in exchange for neutrality — enabling the largest unionization wave in US game industry history. The deal brought Activision, Blizzard, and King under Microsoft Gaming alongside Xbox Game Studios and ZeniMax/Bethesda.
