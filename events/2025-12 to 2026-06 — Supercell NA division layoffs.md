# Supercell North American division layoffs (2025–2026)

Beginning around **late 2025**, Supercell quietly reduced headcount in its North American division. The cuts became public in **June 2026** when MobileGamer.biz reported on layoffs affecting key staff.

**Key facts:**
- **Steve Desilets**, co-founder of Supercell's US studio, announced on LinkedIn that his team's game project had been **canceled** and the **team disbanded**.
- Desilets departed after 35-year career.
- Supercell has not publicly commented on the layoffs or their extent.
- Affected roles reportedly included **marketing, data analytics, and administrative functions** (per layoff tracking site interviewpal, confidence: single-source).
- These represent the **first documented direct layoffs** in Supercell's history as a company (contrasting with its prior reputation for no-layoff culture).

The cuts affected Supercell's smallest regional office: North America (137 employees, 5.8% of global workforce as of Q1 2026). The cancellation of the US project leaves the future of Supercell's San Francisco studio uncertain.
