# Event — Ubisoft France-wide 3-day strike (Feb 2026)

**What:** Five French unions coordinated a three-day strike across Ubisoft's French and Italian (Milan) studios, protesting restructuring plans, mass layoffs, and a mandatory 5-day return-to-office mandate.

**When:** February 10-12, 2026.

**Who:** Organized by CFE-CGC, CGT, Printemps Écologique, Solidaires Informatique, and STJV (Syndicat des Travailleureuses du Jeu Vidéo).

**Turnout:** Ubisoft disputed press reports of 1,200 strikers, claiming 538 officially declared involvement.

**Demands:**
- End to mass layoffs and cost-cutting plans
- Reversal of 5-day RTO mandate
- Yves Guillemot's resignation

**Response:** Guillemot did not resign. Ubisoft issued statements about "open and constructive dialogue." Stock had fallen 39% after restructuring announcement.
