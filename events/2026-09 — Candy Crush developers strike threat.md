# Candy Crush Developers Strike Threat (Sep 2026)

Candy Crush developers at King Stockholm called for a strike in September 2026 after collective bargaining agreement negotiations stalled. King management had not entered into a CBA since the union club formed in October 2024.

Notably, Blizzard Entertainment ratified its first union contracts the same week, creating a contrast between the two Microsoft Gaming subsidiaries.
