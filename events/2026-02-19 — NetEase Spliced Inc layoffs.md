# NetEase Spliced Inc layoffs (Feb 2026)

NetEase-owned studio Spliced Inc, founded in 2023 as a remote-first AAA studio with an explicit anti-crunch structure emphasizing work-life balance, flexible hours, and realistic project scope, commenced redundancy proceedings. LinkedIn posts from employees confirmed the studio began informing workers their roles were at risk.

The UK branch was confirmed affected; it was unclear whether US and Canadian branches would also be impacted. The layoffs followed NetEase's broader restructuring under CEO William Ding.
