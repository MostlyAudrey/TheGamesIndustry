# Nexon Union Rally 2023

In October 2023, approximately 600 unionized Nexon employees held a demonstration over project cancellations, restructuring, and job security concerns. The protest followed several months of unrest as Nexon suspended projects and reorganized teams. Unionized workers highlighted the lack of job security when projects end in Korea's game industry.
