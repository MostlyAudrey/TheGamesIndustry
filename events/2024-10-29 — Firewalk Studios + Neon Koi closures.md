# Firewalk Studios and Neon Koi closures

**Two Sony-owned studios shut down on the same day** — Oct 29, 2024.

## Firewalk Studios
- Based in Bellevue, WA; acquired by Sony ~18 months prior
- Developed **Concord** — hero shooter for PS5/PC, launched Aug 2024
- Game reportedly cost **$200-400M** to develop
- Pulled from stores within weeks; full refunds issued
- Director Ryan Ellis resigned
- ~170 jobs lost

## Neon Koi
- Mobile game developer; formed when Sony acquired **Savage Game Studios** (2022) and renamed
- Never released a game under Sony ownership
- Sony tried to offer work to affected staff
- ~40 jobs lost

## Industry response
- **CWA** issued statement calling for unionization at SIE studios
- "Now more than ever, video game workers deserve a free and fair opportunity to join together to form unions"
