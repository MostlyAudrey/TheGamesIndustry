# Koei Tecmo management buyout (2009)

Following the April 2009 merger of Koei and Tecmo, Koei management orchestrated a management buyout (MBO) that took the combined company private and bought out external influence.

**Background:** Tecmo had been weakened before the merger by:
- The departure of star developer Tomonobu Itagaki (2008), who sued the company for unpaid bonuses
- Square Enix's attempt to exert influence over Tecmo during its financial distress
- Internal strife and management dysfunction

**The MBO:**
- Koei management (led by Yoichi Erikawa and family) organized a buyout of external Tecmo shareholders
- Funding came from external investors including TOSHI and other partners
- The buyout effectively removed Square Enix and other rival entities from having influence at the newly merged company
- Koei management secured majority control

**Aftermath:**
- Koei Tecmo was taken private via the MBO in 2009
- Returned to public listing on Tokyo Stock Exchange in 2010
- Management retained control, ensuring stability through the 2010s
- The company maintained its independence through subsequent industry consolidation waves

**Significance:** The MBO allowed Koei Tecmo to "purchase itself from external influence" — maintaining management control and avoiding the fate of other Japanese publishers absorbed into larger entities (e.g., Square Enix absorbing Tecmo's rivals, or FromSoftware falling under Kadokawa).
