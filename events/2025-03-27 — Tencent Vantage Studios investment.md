# Tencent Vantage Studios investment announced

Tencent invested €1.16 billion ($1.25 billion) for a 25% stake in Vantage Studios, a new Ubisoft subsidiary holding Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, and Rainbow Six Siege IP. Tencent also serves as adviser. Deal completed November 2025.
