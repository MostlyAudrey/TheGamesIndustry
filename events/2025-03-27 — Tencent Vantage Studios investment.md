# Event — Tencent Vantage Studios investment (Mar-Nov 2025)

**What:** Ubisoft spun out its flagship franchises (Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, Rainbow Six) into a new subsidiary called **Vantage Studios**. Tencent invested **€1.16B ($1.25B)** for a **25% stake**, valuing the subsidiary at ~**€4B**.

**When:** Announced March 27, 2025. Closed November 21, 2025.

**Leadership:** Charlie Guillemot and Christophe Derennes lead Vantage Studios.

**Terms:**
- Tencent must hold stake for at least 5 years unless Ubisoft loses majority control
- Ubisoft required to maintain control of Vantage for at least 2 years
- Vantage Studios licenses IP from the remaining Ubisoft entity

**Context:** Tencent previously held 9.99% of Ubisoft (as of Wikipedia). The Guillemot family retains 14% of the overall company. This investment was widely described as a bailout that kept the Guillemot family in control.
