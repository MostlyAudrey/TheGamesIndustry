# 2026-10-16 — Activision QA United authorizes strike

**Activision QA United-CWA voted to authorize a strike over stalled contract negotiations.** After 2.5 years without a first contract, workforce declined from ~600 to ~400 while workload remained constant. Key issues: wages, staffing levels, contractor use, AI automation safeguards. Strike authorized in the Modern Warfare 4 launch window, giving the union leverage. Next bargaining scheduled Oct 21-22, 2026. The stark contrast with Blizzard's first contract win (Sep 2026) highlighted Microsoft's differing labor strategies across its gaming divisions.
