# 2024-03-08 — Activision QA United-CWA formed (largest game union)

**The largest certified video game union in US history at formation — ~600 QA testers across three states.** Activision Quality Assurance United-CWA was formed on March 8, 2024, with a 390-8 vote in favor. The unit covers QA workers at Activision studios in Austin, TX; Eden Prairie, MN; and El Segundo, CA.

This was the first union organized under the Microsoft-CWA Labor Neutrality Agreement that took effect after the acquisition closed. Microsoft stated it "maintained our commitment to remain neutral during the organizing campaign."

Despite the historic formation, the union has been unable to secure a first contract after 2.5 years of bargaining. By October 2026, the workforce had shrunk from 600 to 400 through attrition without replacement. The union authorized a strike on October 16, 2026 — coordinated to coincide with the Modern Warfare 4 launch window.
