# NLRB labor complaint against Epic Games

**Labor complaint filed with NLRB against Epic Games** (April 2022), alleging the company refused to hire a former Activision Blizzard and Apple employee because of her union organizing / labor advocacy work with the Apple Together movement.

- Complainant disclosed labor advocacy on Epic's Request for Outside Activity form after 4 interview rounds.
- Job offer withdrawn 2 days later.
- Epic spokesperson Elka Looks said advocacy work was known from the start and did not factor; candidate scored lower.
- NLRB investigated; outcome not confirmed in available sources.
