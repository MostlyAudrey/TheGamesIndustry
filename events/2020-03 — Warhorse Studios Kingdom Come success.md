Warhorse Studios' Kingdom Come: Deliverance, a historically accurate medieval RPG, launched February 13, 2018. Despite mixed reception on difficulty and pacing, it sold 6M+ copies by 2024, making it one of the most successful Czech games ever. A sequel, Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, was announced in April 2024 for early 2025 release.

**Labor significance:** Warhorse is based in Prague. Founder Daniel Vávra has been outspoken about game development practices. No union reported. No layoffs. Studio has grown from ~100 to ~250 employees.
