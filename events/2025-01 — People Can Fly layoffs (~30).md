# People Can Fly layoffs (~30)

~30-40 roles cut (~12% of ~300 workforce) at Polish developer People Can Fly, citing market conditions and project reset. Project Victoria (AAA action-adventure published by Sony) was paused, then cancelled. Studio shifted from co-development to self-publishing strategy.
