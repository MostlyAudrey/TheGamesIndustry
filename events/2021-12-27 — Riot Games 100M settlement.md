# Riot Games $100M gender discrimination settlement

Global settlement with California DFEH, DLSE, and private plaintiffs resolving 2018 class action. Largest gender discrimination settlement in California civil rights history. Covered 1,548 women. Included workplace reforms: 3-year independent monitoring, pay transparency, revisions to hiring/promotion practices. Preliminary approval July 2022; final approval September 2023. Initial $10M settlement (2019) was rejected as inadequate.
