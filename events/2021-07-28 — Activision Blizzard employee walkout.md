# 2021-07-28 — Activision Blizzard employee walkout

**Thousands of Activision Blizzard employees walked out in protest** following the California DFEH lawsuit alleging systemic sexual harassment, discrimination, and unequal pay. A Better ABK — the employee activist group — organized the walkout. The company's initial response was widely condemned as dismissive. The walkout presaged a multi-year organizing wave that under the Microsoft Labor Neutrality Agreement resulted in the largest unionized workforce in US game history (~4,000+ CWA members).
