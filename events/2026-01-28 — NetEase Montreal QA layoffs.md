# NetEase Montreal QA staff layoffs (Jan 2026)

In January 2026, NetEase shed several roles from its Montréal studio, primarily affecting QA staff. While not formally confirmed by NetEase, multiple former employees posted on LinkedIn confirming redundancies, including:

- Scott Killingsworth (QA project manager)
- Stefano Magnabosco (senior QA lead)
- Pavlina Rahneva (senior QA specialist)

The layoffs followed several closures at the end of 2025, including T-Minus Zero Entertainment, Bad Brain Game Studios, and Fantastic Pixel Castle.
