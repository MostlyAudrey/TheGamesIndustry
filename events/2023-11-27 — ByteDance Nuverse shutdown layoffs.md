# ByteDance Nuverse Gaming Wind-Down (Nov 2023)

ByteDance announced sudden retreat from gaming on November 27, 2023. Nuverse brand wound down. ~1,000 employees laid off; ~3,000 affected. Moonton explicitly retained. Unreleased games cancelled. One of the most dramatic corporate gaming retreats of 2023.
