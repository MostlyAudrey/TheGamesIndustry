# Microsoft Acquires Obsidian Entertainment

At X018 on November 10, 2018, Microsoft announced the acquisition of Obsidian Entertainment, bringing the veteran RPG developer into Xbox Game Studios. Obsidian, founded in 2003 by former Black Isle Studios staff, was known for Fallout: New Vegas, South Park: The Stick of Truth, and Pillars of Eternity.

## Context

- Obsidian had experienced significant financial struggles prior to acquisition
- CEO Feargus Urquhart remained in leadership post-acquisition
- Post-acquisition titles: Grounded (2020), Pentiment (2022), The Outer Worlds 2 (announced), Avowed (2025)
- In September 2026, Obsidian was transferred from Xbox Game Studios to ZeniMax reporting structure
