# 2022-11 — FromSoftware crunch reports following Elden Ring success

GamesIndustry.biz investigation revealed department-dependent crunch (2-3 months before release), average salary ¥3.41M ($24,660), overtime at half hourly rate after midnight. Employee satisfaction rating 2.6/5 on Career Connection. FromSoftware had 349 employees.

**Labor findings:**
- Crunch varies by department; some report no OT, others report 2-3 months of early mornings + overtime
- 10pm curfew rule; most staff leave by 9pm "90% of the time"
- Average salary ¥3.41M ($24,660) — significantly below industry average and below Atlus comparable (~¥5.2M)
- New graduate base rate: ¥220,000/month
- No response from FromSoftware to comment requests
- No union presence identified
