Microsoft announced the acquisition of Mojang (Minecraft developer) for $2.5 billion on September 15, 2014, closing November 6, 2014. Markus "Notch" Persson, the creator of Minecraft, left the company. Microsoft has continued to expand Minecraft across all platforms including PlayStation and Nintendo.

**Labor significance:** Mojang operates under Swedish labor law with strong protections. No major labor disputes. Minecraft's sustainability has provided stable employment for ~600 employees. No union reported.
