# Tencent Lightspeed LA layoffs

Tencent-owned Lightspeed LA (US division of Lightspeed Studios) laid off approximately 68-80 developers after the game Last Sentinel reportedly ended development following 6 years and hundreds of millions in spending. The studio shifted its creative and development direction.
