# 2024-01-25 — Microsoft Gaming 1,900 layoffs (Activision Blizzard)

**First major layoff after the $69B Activision Blizzard acquisition.** 1,900 workers (~9% of Microsoft Gaming division) were laid off, primarily from Activision Blizzard. Blizzard president Mike Ybarra departed as part of this round. CWA confirmed that its represented members (ZeniMax, Raven, Blizzard Albany) were not impacted. The layoffs struck hardest at Activision and Blizzard, while Xbox and ZeniMax were relatively spared. Johanna Faries was named new Blizzard president days later.
