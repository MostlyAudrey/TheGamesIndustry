# 2026-07 — Microsoft 3,200 gaming layoffs hit Bethesda/ZeniMax

**Major restructuring of ZeniMax/Bethesda under Microsoft's broader 3,200 gaming layoff announcement.** Losses included 166 at ZeniMax Media (MD), 22 at BGS Austin (WARN filing), 136 at id Software (Texas WARN), and ZOS lost 275 employees between July 2025 and July 2026 cumulatively (60%+ staff decline at ZOS). CWA reported 440+ unionized members affected across Bethesda and ZeniMax. Bloomberg reported ZeniMax will focus on biggest franchises going forward. CWA-organized walkouts and strikes began July 15.
