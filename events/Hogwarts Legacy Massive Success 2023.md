# Event: Hogwarts Legacy Massive Success (2023)

## Event Summary

Hogwarts Legacy, developed by Avalanche Software and published by Portkey Games (both WB Games subsidiaries), was the best-selling game of 2023 worldwide. It became one of the best-selling video games of all time, selling over 40 million copies by December 2025.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| Sept 2020 | Announced at PlayStation 5 Showcase; named "Star of the Evening" |
| Feb 2021 | Game director Troy Leavitt resigns amid social media controversy |
| Feb 10, 2023 | Game released on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC |
| May 5, 2023 | Released on PS4, Xbox One |
| Nov 14, 2023 | Released on Nintendo Switch |
| Dec 2023 | Sold 22M+ copies; best-selling game of the year |
| Oct 2024 | Sales reached 30M copies |
| March 2025 | Sales reached 34M copies |
| Dec 2025 | Sales reached 40M copies |

## Key Details

- **Sales milestones:**
  - 22M copies sold in 2023
  - Best-selling game in the US for 2023 (first non-CoD/Rockstar title since 2008's Rock Band)
  - Best-selling game worldwide for 2023
  - Passed $1B in revenue within its first year
  - By Dec 2025: 40M copies sold, making it one of the best-selling games ever
- **Critical reception:** Generally positive reviews (84 Metacritic on PS5)
- **Audience:** Broader than typical AAA games; attracted both Harry Potter fans and general RPG fans
- **Platform reach:** Released on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC, PS4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2

## Why It Succeeded

1. **Powerful IP:** Harry Potter is one of the most valuable entertainment franchises globally
2. **First open-world Harry Potter game:** Fans had waited decades for this concept
3. **Quality execution:** Avalanche delivered a polished, immersive experience
4. **Broad appeal:** Accessible RPG that appealed to casual and hardcore audiences
5. **Cross-gen release:** Reached players on all major platforms
6. **Strong marketing:** WB maximized the IP's promotional power
7. **Cultural moment:** Both controversy (J.K. Rowling trans rights stance) and nostalgia drove attention despite (or because of) the discourse

## Business Impact on WB

- Hogwarts Legacy was the financial highlight of WB Games in the 2023–2025 period
- Proved the value of single-player, story-driven games vs. live-service
- Directly influenced WB Games' refocus on core IPs (Harry Potter mentioned alongside DC, Mortal Kombat, Game of Thrones)
- Made Avalanche Software the most valuable studio in WB's portfolio
- A rock-solid foundation for WB Games' future amid the Suicide Squad and MultiVersus disasters
