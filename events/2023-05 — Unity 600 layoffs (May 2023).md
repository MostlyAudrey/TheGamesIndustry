# Unity 600 layoffs (May 2023)

Second round of layoffs at Unity. Announced in a company filing as an 8% workforce reduction. Unity characterized this as necessary for "long-term and profitable growth." Preceded by January 2023 round of 284 cuts and June 2022 round of ~225.

## Context
- Stock had fallen significantly from 2021 peak (~$160 to ~$30 range)
- Company under mounting pressure to show profitability
- Riccitiello still CEO at this point
