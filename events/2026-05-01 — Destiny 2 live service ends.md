# Destiny 2 live service ends

In May 2026, Bungie announced that the June 2026 "Monument of Triumph" update would be the final planned live-service content update for Destiny 2. The studio ended active development on the 2017 title to focus resources on Marathon.

This decision was later reversed in September 2026 under new leadership.
