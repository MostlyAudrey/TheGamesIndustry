# 2026-09 — Xbox transfers Halo franchise and Rare to Activision

**Xbox restructured internal studio assignments.** Microsoft transferred Halo franchise development, World's Edge, and Rare from Xbox Game Studios to Activision Publishing. Activision is now tasked with developing the next Halo title. The move signals a consolidation of major IPs under Activision's Call of Duty-focused development engine and may indicate Activision's position as the lead development powerhouse within Microsoft Gaming.
