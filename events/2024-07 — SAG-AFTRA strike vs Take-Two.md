# 2024-2025 — SAG-AFTRA strike against Take-Two

**SAG-AFTRA** initiated a strike against **Take-Two Interactive** and other major video game publishers (Activision, Electronic Arts, Epic Games, Warner Bros. Games) on **July 26, 2024**, over AI protections in the Interactive Media Agreement. Take-Two was listed by name as a struck company.

A tentative agreement was reached **June 9, 2025**; the strike was suspended **June 11, 2025** and ratified **July 9, 2025**.
