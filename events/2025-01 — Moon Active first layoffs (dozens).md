Moon Active conducted its first known layoff round in January 2025, cutting "dozens" of staff (reports of 50-100). The cuts primarily affected the Lithuanian studio Melsoft (acquired 2024), with staff in multiple other countries also impacted. The layoffs occurred despite Moon Active reporting record revenues exceeding $2 billion.
