Take-Two Interactive acquired Gearbox Entertainment and placed it under the 2K label. Gearbox retained Borderlands, Homeworld, Risk of Rain, Brothers in Arms, Duke Nukem IPs. Embracer retained Gearbox Publishing San Francisco (renamed Arc Games), Cryptic Studios, Lost Boys Interactive, and Captured Dimensions. [[sources/2024-05-23 — eurogamer-human-cost-embracer]]
