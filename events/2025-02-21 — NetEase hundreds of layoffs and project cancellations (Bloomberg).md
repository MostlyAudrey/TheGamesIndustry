# NetEase hundreds of layoffs and project cancellations (Feb 2025)

Per Bloomberg report published February 21, 2025, NetEase founder and CEO William Ding spent the prior year focusing on a "smaller portfolio" of titles, pulling back on international investments. The game division laid off hundreds of developers and canceled dozens of projects.

Affected Western studios:
- **Jar of Sparks**: Paused operations after funding pulled
- **Worlds Untold**: Paused operations after funding pulled (~60 devs combined with Jar of Sparks)
- **Ouka Studios**: Layoffs before Visions of Mana's release (2024)
- **Skybox Labs**: Affected by pullback

Ding reportedly hired recent finance graduates to directly report to him and lead/supervise units within the game division. He allegedly tried to cancel Marvel Rivals before release to avoid Disney licensing fees. NetEase argued the adjustments did not constitute a "wide scale layoff."
