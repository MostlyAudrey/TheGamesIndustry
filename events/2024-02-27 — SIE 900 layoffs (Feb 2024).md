# SIE PlayStation 900 layoffs (Feb 2024)

**Largest single layoff event in Sony Interactive Entertainment history.** Announced Feb 27, 2024 by outgoing SIE president Jim Ryan.

## Key details
- **~900 jobs eliminated** (~8% of SIE's then ~11,250 workforce)
- **London Studio closed entirely** after ~20 years of operation (SingStar, Blood & Truth, PSVR titles)
- **Firesprite** (Liverpool, UK) — reductions, never recovered
- **Insomniac Games** (Burbank, CA) — reductions
- **Naughty Dog** (Santa Monica, CA) — reductions; ~25 contract devs laid off per reports
- **Guerrilla Games** (Amsterdam, NL) — reductions
- US employees notified same day; UK entered collective consultation period
- London Studio closure completed by May 2024

## Preceding context
- Sony missed PS5 sales target (Feb 2024 earnings), stock fell $10B
- Near-decade low gaming margins reported
- Cost of AAA game development rising

## CEO transition context
- Jim Ryan retired March 2024
- Hiroki Totoki interim CEO; co-CEO arrangement June 2024
- Hermen Hulst (PlayStation Studios head) later elevated
