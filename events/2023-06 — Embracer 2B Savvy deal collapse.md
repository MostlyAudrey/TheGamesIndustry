title: "Embracer Group's $2 billion Savvy Gaming deal collapses"

confidence: verified
---

On May 24, 2023, Embracer Group announced that a previously signed $2 billion development partnership with Savvy Gaming Group (backed by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund) had collapsed. The deal had been central to Embracer's financial projections and acquisition strategy.

**Consequences:** Embracer's stock crashed ~40% in a single day. The company announced an immediate restructuring program including studio closures, project cancellations, and mass layoffs. This triggered the unraveling of the Embracer acquisition empire:
- Free Radical Design closed (Dec 2023)
- Volition closed (Aug 2023)
- Sold Saber Interactive for ~$500M (Mar 2024)
- Sold Gearbox to Take-Two for $460M (May 2024)
- Split into three independent companies (Apr 2024)
