# Epic Games 830 layoffs + Bandcamp divestiture

**Mass layoff and restructuring at Epic Games announced Sep 28, 2023.**

- **830 employees laid off** (16% of workforce). Also ~250 leaving through Bandcamp and SuperAwesome divestitures.
- **Bandcamp sold** to Songtradr (music licensing platform). Bandcamp had 118 employees; 60 received offers from Songtradr, 58 did not (half dismissed).
- **SuperAwesome spun off** — ad business became independent under Kate O'Loughlin; Kids Web Services (KWS) remained at Epic.
- **Mediatonic (Fall Guys) hit hard** — Bloomberg reported studio was hit "very hard" but remained open. Layoffs across IT, marketing, software engineering, character art, gameplay programming, level design, production, and narrative staff.
- Sweeney memo: "two-thirds of the layoffs were in teams outside of core development."
