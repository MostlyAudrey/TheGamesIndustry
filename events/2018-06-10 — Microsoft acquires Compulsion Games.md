# Microsoft Acquires Compulsion Games

At E3 2018 on June 10, 2018, Xbox head Phil Spencer announced that Microsoft had acquired Compulsion Games, making it part of Microsoft Studios (now Xbox Game Studios). The announcement was made alongside acquisitions of Ninja Theory, Playground Games, and Undead Labs as part of a major first-party studio expansion.

## Context

- Compulsion Games founded in 2009 by Guillaume Provost in Montreal, Quebec
- First title: Contrast (2013), a puzzle-platformer
- Second title: We Happy Few (early access 2016, full release August 2019), a survival-action game set in a dystopian 1960s England
- Acquisition ensured studio resources to complete We Happy Few and begin work on next project
- Studio was reportedly stable post-acquisition; no known layoffs through 2024
- South of Midnight announced at Xbox Showcase 2023, gameplay debuted June 2024
- In July 2026, Compulsion Games spun out from Xbox as an independent studio, retaining rights to Contrast, We Happy Few, and South of Midnight
