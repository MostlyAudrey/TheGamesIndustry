# 2024-11 — Private Division sale

Take-Two sold its indie publishing label **Private Division** to an unnamed buyer in **November 2024**. The deal included "substantially all" of Private Division's live and unreleased titles. Take-Two retained support for **No Rest for the Wicked** (Moon Studios).

CEO Strauss Zelnick stated the label's hits "were not big in the context of our core intellectual properties at 2K and Rockstar."
