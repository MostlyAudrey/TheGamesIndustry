# Event: WB Games Restructuring / Layoffs 2023–2024

## Event Summary

As part of Warner Bros. Discovery's massive debt-driven cost-cutting, the games division underwent significant restructuring across 2023 and 2024. Multiple studios faced layoffs, project cancellations, and strategic refocusing.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2023 | TT Games cancels multiple Lego projects (Lego Disney, "Project Cosmic," others) |
| 2023 | NetherRealm's Mortal Kombat: Onslaught mobile game announced then flounders |
| Sept 2024 | Rocksteady QA team reduced by 50% |
| July 2024 | NetherRealm mobile division shut down (~50 layoffs) |
| Late 2024 | Rocksteady programming and artist layoffs |
| Oct 2024 | Mortal Kombat: Onslaught shut down |
| Nov 2024 | David Zaslav announces WB Games will focus on 4 core IPs |
| Dec 12, 2024 | WBD splits into two divisions; games division under Streaming & Studios |
| Jan 2025 | David Haddad (head of WB Games) exits |
| Feb 25, 2025 | Monolith Productions, Player First Games, WB Games San Diego closed |
| June 2025 | WB Games formally restructured: focus on Harry Potter, Mortal Kombat, Game of Thrones, DC |

## Layoff Totals (Games Division)

- Rocksteady: ~50+ (multiple rounds in 2024)
- NetherRealm: ~50 (mobile division, July 2024)
- Monolith Productions: ~300 (closed, Feb 2025)
- Player First Games: ~50+ (closed, Feb 2025)
- WB Games San Diego: Unknown (closed, Feb 2025)
- **Total games division job losses: ~500+**

## Strategic Impact

- WB Games shifted from a broad publisher with many studios to a leaner operation focused on 4 proven IPs
- The restructuring essentially killed the experimental/live-service strategy that produced Suicide Squad and MultiVersus
- Single-player, story-driven games (like Hogwarts Legacy) were vindicated as the preferred strategy
- Unionization discussions at WB studios reportedly stalled due to layoffs and instability
