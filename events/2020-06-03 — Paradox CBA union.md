# Paradox Interactive signs CBA with Unionen and SACO

Paradox Interactive signed a **collective bargaining agreement** with two Swedish labor unions: **Unionen** (white-collar workers union) and **SACO** (union for professionals with academic degrees). Over 200 combined members at Paradox. The CBA locks in company policies around parental leave, sick leave, and benefits so they cannot be changed unilaterally.

Paradox was one of the first major games publishers in Sweden to formalize a CBA, though the Swedish labor model — with most employees belonging to a union and bargaining agreements at company level — had long been the norm.
