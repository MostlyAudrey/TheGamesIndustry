# 2022-06-13 — Microsoft-CWA Labor Neutrality Agreement signed

**The agreement that enabled the largest unionization wave in US video game history.** Microsoft and CWA signed a labor neutrality agreement on June 13, 2022. The agreement committed Microsoft to neutrality when Activision Blizzard employees expressed interest in unionizing, provided streamlined access to card-check elections, communication rights, privacy protections, and expedited arbitration for disputes.

CWA President Chris Shelton stated the agreement "addresses CWA's previous concerns regarding the acquisition" and CWA formally supported regulatory approval. The agreement was set to apply at Activision Blizzard 60 days after the Microsoft acquisition closed (which happened in October 2023). It expired in Fall 2025, after which labor relations deteriorated significantly — most notably visible in Activision QA United's strike authorization in October 2026 while Blizzard workers had already secured a contract.

Five key provisions:
1. Microsoft will take a neutral approach when employees express interest in joining a union
2. Employees can easily communicate with other employees and union representatives
3. Streamlined, technology-supported process for choosing whether to join a union
4. Confidentiality and privacy of choice
5. Expedited arbitration process for disputes
