# Event — Ubisoft kills XDefiant, closes San Francisco and Osaka studios (Dec 2024)

**What:** Ubisoft discontinued free-to-play shooter XDefiant (launched May 2024), closed San Francisco and Osaka production studios, and ramped down Sydney operations.

**When:** December 3-4, 2024. XDefiant servers shut down June 2025.

**Who:** Ubisoft San Francisco (143 jobs), Ubisoft Osaka (~134 combined with Sydney ramp-down).

**Total impact:** ~277 jobs.

## Context
- XDefiant had been in development for years but failed to meet expectations after a May 2024 launch
- San Francisco was XDefiant's lead studio
- Osaka provided support; Sydney was ramped down alongside
