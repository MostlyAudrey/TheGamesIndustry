# Nintendo Switch OLED Model Launch + Tears of the Kingdom

A two-part milestone in the Switch lifecycle:

## Part 1: Switch OLED Model (October 8, 2021)

- **Nintendo Switch – OLED Model** launched October 8, 2021 at $349.99 USD
- Featured a 7-inch OLED screen, wider adjustable stand, enhanced audio, dock with wired LAN port
- New white and neon red/blue color variants
- Mid-generation refresh that extended Switch sales momentum

## Part 2: The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom (May 12, 2023)

- Released May 12, 2023 as direct sequel to Breath of the Wild
- Developed by Nintendo EPD Production Group No. 3 (Eiji Aonuma, Hidemaro Fujibayashi)
- **Sold over 10 million copies in its first 3 days** — fastest-selling Zelda game ever
- **Over 21 million copies sold lifetime** (as of 2026)
- Simultaneous launch with Tears of the Kingdom edition Switch OLED
- Widely praised; multiple Game of the Year awards

## Significance

TotK's record-breaking launch demonstrated the continued strength of the Switch platform entering its seventh year. The game's success contributed to Nintendo maintaining its policy of no permanent employee layoffs during a period when much of the industry was contracting.
