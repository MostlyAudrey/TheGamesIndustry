# Event — EA take-private (agreed 2025-09-29)

**What:** Electronic Arts agreed to be taken private in a **~$52.5B** (NBC/AP) / **~$55B** (LA Times) all-cash deal.

**Who:**
- **[[orgs/Silver Lake Partners]]** (Redwood City, CA)
- **[[orgs/Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia]]** (rolls over its 9.9% stake)
- **[[orgs/Affinity Partners]]** (Miami, FL, Kushner)

**When:** announced **2025-09-29**; **expected to close Q1 2027**; pending shareholder + regulatory approval.

**Why it matters for the map:**
- This is the **single biggest PE transaction in gaming history** — the anchor for the "money is connected" thread.
- **US/CA labor surface:** ~14,500 employees (as of end of March 2025), including BioWare, Maxis, EA Sports, Respawn.
- **PIF's Savvy platform** is the other major US/CA-relevant capital cluster (Scopely, ESL FACEIT).
