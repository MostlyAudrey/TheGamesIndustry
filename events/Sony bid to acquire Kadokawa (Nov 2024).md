# Sony bid to acquire Kadokawa (Nov 2024)

In November 2024, Sony made an acquisition offer for Kadokawa Corporation, the Tokyo-based media conglomerate and majority owner of FromSoftware (69.66%). The bid was widely reported as being motivated primarily by Sony's desire to gain full control of FromSoftware, following the massive success of Elden Ring (25M+ units).

**Key details:**
- Sony approached Kadokawa with an acquisition proposal in November 2024
- Kadokawa's board evaluated the offer throughout November
- By December 2024, the negotiations resulted in a strategic capital alliance instead of a full acquisition
- On December 19, 2024, Sony announced a ¥50 billion ($318M) investment for ~10% of Kadokawa, making Sony the largest shareholder — but not an acquirer
- Multiple reports indicated Kadokawa's leadership preferred independence over full acquisition

**Significance:** The Sony-Kadokawa bid was one of the most consequential acquisition attempts in the Japanese gaming industry in 2024-2025. It demonstrated:
1. Sony's strategic interest in FromSoftware specifically (not just Kadokawa broadly)
2. The rising valuations of Japanese game developers after Elden Ring's success
3. The difficulty of M&A in Japan's keiretsu-influenced corporate landscape
4. Tencent's parallel stake-building in Kadokawa (increasing from 6.86% to 7.97% in March 2025) as a counterweight to Sony's influence
