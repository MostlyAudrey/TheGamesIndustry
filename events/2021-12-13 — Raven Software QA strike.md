# 2021-12-13 — Raven Software QA strike

**The strike that started the ABK union wave.** Activision laid off ~12 QA testers from Raven Software in early December 2021. The surviving ~40 QA workers walked out in protest, triggering a union drive through Game Workers Alliance-CWA. The union won its election 19-2 in May 2022. Raven QA eventually ratified their first contract in August 2025 — the first union contract at any Activision Blizzard studio. This strike directly set the precedent for the 2022 Microsoft Labor Neutrality Agreement and the entire organizing wave that followed.
