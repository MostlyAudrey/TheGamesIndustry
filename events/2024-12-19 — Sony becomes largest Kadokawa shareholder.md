# 2024-12-19 — Sony becomes largest shareholder of Kadokawa Corporation

Sony invested ¥50 billion (~$318M) to acquire 12,054,100 new Kadokawa shares. Deal closed January 7, 2025. Sony's stake rose to ~10%, making it Kadokawa's largest single shareholder. Agreement includes strategic alliance for co-producing anime, live-action adaptations of Kadokawa IP, and expanded game publishing/distribution. Sony already owned 14.09% of FromSoftware directly.

**Significance:** Came after a month of speculation that Sony might fully acquire Kadokawa. The minority stake signals strategic alignment without full acquisition. Sony now has influence at both Kadokawa (parent, as largest shareholder) and FromSoftware (subsidiary, 14.09% direct stake).
