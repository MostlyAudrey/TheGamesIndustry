Embracer Group announced a large-scale restructuring program focused on cost reduction, comprising layoffs, studio closures, divestments, and project cancellations. Initially targeted to run until March 2024 but continued beyond. By November 2023: 904 employees laid off (~5% workforce), 15 projects written down, debt reduced from $2B to $1.5B. By May 2024: 4,532 employees lost, 44 studios closed, 80 projects canceled.
