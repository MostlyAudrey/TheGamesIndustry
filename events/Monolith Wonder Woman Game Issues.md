# Event: Monolith Wonder Woman Game Issues (2021–2025)

## Event Summary

Monolith Productions' Wonder Woman game was announced at The Game Awards in December 2021 but was plagued by development struggles, WB Games restructuring, and ultimately cancelled in February 2025 when Warner Bros. closed the studio.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| Dec 2021 | Announced at The Game Awards; set to use Nemesis System |
| 2021–2023 | Early development; initial work on open-world Wonder Woman with Nemesis |
| 2023 | WB Games starts reviewing projects amid cost-cutting; Wonder Woman reportedly paused at times |
| 2024 | Development continues but with reduced scope and resources |
| Nov 2024 | Zaslav announces refocus on 4 IPs — it's unclear if Wonder Woman (DC) is included |
| Feb 2025 | Wonder Woman still not shown publicly since 2021; no release date |
| Feb 25, 2025 | WB Games cancels Wonder Woman, closes Monolith Productions |
| Feb 2025 | ~300 employees lose their jobs |

## Development Issues

1. **Unproven concept:** Wonder Woman had never had a successful AAA video game. Translating the Nemesis System (known for orcs/brutes in Middle-earth) to a superhero setting was unproven.
2. **WB turmoil:** The game's development coincided exactly with WBD's debt crisis, multiple restructuring rounds, and leadership changes.
3. **No public progress:** From announcement in Dec 2021 to cancellation in Feb 2025, WB showed zero gameplay footage or trailers. This is unusual for a major AAA title and suggests the game was never in a demo-ready state.
4. **Leadership instability:** David Haddad (head of WB Games) departed Jan 2025, which may have been the final blow.
5. **Nemesis System patent:** WB holds a controversial patent on the Nemesis System (expiring in 2035ish), limiting its use to WB games. The Wonder Woman game was supposed to showcase the system in a new context.

## Cancellation

The cancellation was announced alongside the closures of Monolith Productions, Player First Games (MultiVersus), and WB Games San Diego on February 25, 2025. Bloomberg broke the news. The closure was framed as part of WB's strategic shift to focus on a smaller number of core franchises.

## Impact

- ~300 jobs lost at Monolith (Seattle-area)
- The Nemesis System remains locked in WB's vault with uncertain future
- Middle-earth game franchise is on hold indefinitely
- WB lost one of its most historically significant studios
- First-party single-player development capacity significantly reduced

## Post-Cancellation

The Wonder Woman IP remains with Warner Bros. / DC. No other studio has been announced as taking over development. WB's new strategy (June 2025) names DC as a core IP, but the specific games in development are unclear.
