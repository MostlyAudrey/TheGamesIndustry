# Bungie June 2026 mass layoffs

On June 25, 2026, Bungie confirmed a major layoff round — the largest in the studio's history.

## Key numbers

- **WA WARN filing**: 292 affected workers (Bellevue HQ)
- **Reported total**: Estimates up to ~400 (~50% of remaining ~800 roles)
- **Separations start**: July 9, 2026
- **Scope**: "Most of the Destiny team and some Marathon team members"

## Leadership change

- **Justin Truman out**: Replaced Parsons only ~10 months earlier (Aug 2025)
- **Poria Torkan in**: Former VP of Operations, now studio head
- Context: Co-founder Jason Jones was reportedly among those laid off

## Sony context

- Sony recorded a $765M impairment on the Bungie acquisition
- The writedown acknowledged Bungie had underperformed against financial targets justifying the $3.6B purchase price
