US District Judge Jamal N. Whitehead certified the consolidated antitrust lawsuit (Wolfire + Dark Catt) as a class action on Nov 25, 2024. The class covers approximately 32,000 entities that sold games on Steam or paid commissions to Valve since Jan 28, 2017. The Ninth Circuit declined to hear Valve's appeal in January 2025. The case is ongoing as of October 2026, with Wolfire seeking summary judgment and the class action estimated to potentially cover billions in damages.

Timeline:
- Apr 2021: Wolfire files original antitrust suit
- Nov 2021: Suit dismissed
- May 2022: Wolfire refiles with updated arguments
- Jun 2021: Dark Catt files separate suit
- Jul 2022: Cases consolidated
- Nov 25, 2024: Class certified by Judge Whitehead
- Jan 2025: Ninth Circuit declines Valve's appeal
