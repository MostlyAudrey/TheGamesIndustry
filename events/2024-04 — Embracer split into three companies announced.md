Embracer Group announced plans to split into three standalone publicly traded companies: (1) Embracer — remaining segment with THQ Nordic, Deca Games, Plaion, Tripwire Interactive; (2) Fellowship Entertainment — Tolkien IP, Tomb Raider, Crystal Dynamics, Eidos-Montréal, Warhorse Studios, 4A Games, Dark Horse Media; (3) Coffee Stain Group — indie/community studios. Asmodee Group (board games) was later spun off separately in February 2025. The split was to be completed within two years. Wingefors owned all via new holding company Embracer AB.
