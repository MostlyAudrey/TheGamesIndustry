# HoYoverse unannounced layoffs (Western branches, Jan 2025)

Unconfirmed reports emerged in January 2025 that HoYoverse conducted unannounced layoffs primarily affecting recruitment staff and writers working on Genshin Impact at Western branches. Singapore operations were reportedly largely untouched, as was miHoYo China.

Revelio Labs data corroborates a broader hiring slowdown: miHoYo's hiring velocity dropped from 53 new roles/month in 2024 to 18/month in 2025, and further to 15/month in 2026. Active job postings rose 17.4% in 2026 but new postings per month cooled from 86 in 2023 to 15 in 2026, suggesting significant deceleration.
