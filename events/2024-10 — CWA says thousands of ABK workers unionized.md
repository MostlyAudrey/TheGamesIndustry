# 2024-10 — CWA says thousands of ABK workers unionized under Microsoft

**CWA announces that thousands of Activision Blizzard King workers have unionized under Microsoft ownership.** By October 2024, CWA had organized multiple bargaining units across ABK including:

- Raven Software QA (GWA-CWA, ~34 members, May 2022)
- Blizzard Albany QA (GWA Albany-CWA, ~20 members, Dec 2022)
- ZeniMax Workers United-CWA (~300 members, Jan 2023)
- Activision QA United-CWA (~600 members, Mar 2024)
- World of Warcraft wall-to-wall (WoWGG-CWA, ~500 members, Jul 2024)

Combined with ongoing organizing at Blizzard (Overwatch, Diablo, Story & Franchise, Platform & Technology), the total would grow to ~4,000+ CWA-represented Microsoft gaming workers by late 2025. This represented the largest unionized workforce at a major US game publisher.
