# 2024-05 — Take-Two studio closures (Roll7 + Intercept Games)

Take-Two Interactive shut down two studios under its Private Division label:

- **Intercept Games** (Seattle, WA) — developer of *Kerbal Space Program 2*; closed June 28, 2024 per Washington State WARN Act notice
- **Roll7** (UK) — developer of *OlliOlli World* and *Rollerdrome*; wound down with a small team remaining temporarily

CEO Strauss Zelnick initially denied the closures in May 2024, saying "we didn't shutter those studios," but Take-Two later confirmed both were indeed closed prior to the sale of Private Division in November 2024.
