# 2026-06 — Japan Fair Trade Commission finds Kadokawa violated Freelance Protection Act

Japan Fair Trade Commission (JFTC) found Kadokawa violated the Act on Ensuring Proper Transactions Involving Specified Entrusted Business Operators (Freelance Protection Act). Freelance writers, stylists, illustrators commissioned through verbal work orders without written contracts. Delayed payments. Corrective recommendation issued. Kadokawa LifeDesign subsidiary also cited for subcontractor payment violations (Nov 2024). Glovision, a Kadokawa Group subsidiary, also sanctioned for freelance-law violations (Dec 2025).

**Significance:** Points to systemic labor practices issues at Kadokawa Group, particularly in its publishing and content creation divisions, affecting freelance workers who form a significant part of Japan's creative economy.
