# BioWare restructuring — headcount collapse

Two-part labor event at BioWare under EA ownership:

- **2023-08:** ~50 employees laid off, including veteran writer Mary Kirby (all Dragon Age games), technical director Jon Renish, and other senior devs (as of 2023-08-24, [[sources/2025-01-30 — pcgamer-bioware-veterans-laid-off]])
- **2025-01:** ~24+ laid off; headcount dropped from >200 during Dragon Age: The Veilguard development to <100 employees; EA blog claimed staff were 'being reassigned to other EA teams'; employees immediately posted on social media that they had been laid off; senior Dragon Age and Mass Effect devs confirmed lost (as of 2025-02-02, [[sources/2025-02-02 — pcgamer-bioware-under-100]]; as of 2025-04-29, [[sources/2025-04-29 — gamedeveloper-respawn-hundreds-layoffs]])

**Net effect:** BioWare is now a fraction of its former size — <100 employees focused on the next Mass Effect game.
