# 2022-12-02 — Blizzard Albany QA union wins NLRB election

**Second certified union at Activision Blizzard — and the first under the Blizzard Entertainment banner.** QA workers at Blizzard Albany (formerly Vicarious Visions) voted unanimously 14-0 to unionize as Albany Game Workers Alliance-CWA. Activision Blizzard attempted to delay the vote count by filing a motion to impound ballots, but the NLRB denied the request. The following day, ballots were opened revealing all 14 in favor.

The unit covers ~20 QA testers at the Albany, NY studio. Blizzard Albany was originally founded as Vicarious Visions in 1991 (known for Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy and Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 1+2) and was merged into Blizzard Entertainment in April 2022.
