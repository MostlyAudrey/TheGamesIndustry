# Bungie layoffs October 2023

Bungie laid off approximately 100 employees (~8% of its then-1,200-person workforce) on October 30, 2023.

## Context

- **Trigger**: Destiny 2 revenue fell 45% below projections for 2023; poor reception of the Lightfall expansion; general economic slowdown
- **Impact**: Two game releases were delayed — The Final Shape expansion and the Marathon reboot
- **Headcount before**: ~1,200–1,400
- **Headcount after**: ~1,100
- **CEO response**: Pete Parsons blamed over-ambition and the "quality miss" of Lightfall
- Note: These layoffs came just ~15 months after Sony's $3.6B acquisition closed (July 2022)
