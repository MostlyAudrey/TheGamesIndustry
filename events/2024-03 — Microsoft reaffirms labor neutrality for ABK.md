# 2024-03 — Microsoft reaffirms labor neutrality for ABK unions

**Microsoft formally reaffirms its commitment to labor neutrality** as the Activision QA United-CWA union organized under the framework of the 2022 agreement. Microsoft lawyer Amy Pannoni stated: "We maintained our commitment to remain neutral during the organizing campaign, and following this vote."

The 2022 agreement expired in Fall 2025. This renewal/reaffirmation period (early 2024) represented the peak cooperative period between Microsoft and CWA. After the agreement expired, labor relations deteriorated — most notably in the Activision QA United contract impasse and strike authorization.
