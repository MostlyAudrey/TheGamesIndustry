# Naughty Dog cancels The Last of Us Online

**December 4, 2024.** Naughty Dog officially announced the cancellation of The Last of Us Online (formerly known as Factions).

## Key details
- Neil Druckmann announced the cancellation in a public statement
- 100+ developers had been working on the project
- Affected staff were reassigned to Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet (announced at same time)
- No additional layoffs from the cancellation itself
- The decision was framed as a choice between becoming a "live-service studio" or continuing single-player focus

## Preceding context
- Originally conceived as The Last of Us Part II's Factions multiplayer mode
- Expanded into a standalone project after 2020
- Development problems reported since 2021
- Project was "on ice" since October 2023 (contractor layoffs)
- Sony was in the process of retreating from live-service (Concord launched and died Aug-Oct 2024)

## Significance
- Marked the end of Naughty Dog's live-service ambitions
- The cancellation came the same day the studio announced Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet
- No additional layoffs, but the project had consumed years of development resources
- Part of Sony's broader live-service strategy retreat following Concord's failure
