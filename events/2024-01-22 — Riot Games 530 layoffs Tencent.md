# Riot Games 530 layoffs

Tencent's wholly owned subsidiary Riot Games announced layoffs of 530 employees (~11% of staff globally). CEO Dylan Jadeja cited the company having too many projects and too little focus. Riot Forge publishing label was shut down, and Legends of Runeterra was significantly downsized.
