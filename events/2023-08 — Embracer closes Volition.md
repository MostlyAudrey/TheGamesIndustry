---
title: "Embracer Group closes Volition (Saints Row studio)"
date: 2023-08-31
publisher: Embracer Group
confidence: verified
---

Embracer Group announced the immediate closure of Volition, the studio behind Saints Row and Red Faction, on August 31, 2023. The closure came after the poorly received Saints Row (2022) reboot and as part of Embracer's restructuring program. Approximately 150 employees were laid off.

Volition had been acquired by Embracer in 2018 as part of the Koch Media (Plaion) group. Founded in 1993 (originally Parallax Software, then Outrage Entertainment, then THQ's Volition), the studio had a 30-year history.
