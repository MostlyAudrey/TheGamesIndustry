Playtika announced a layoff of approximately 500 employees (15% of its global workforce) in January 2026, to be executed during Q1. CEO Robert Antokol cited changing "market reality" and a strategy to "leverage AI and automation to do more with less." The company recognized $12-15 million in restructuring costs.
