# Epic Games announces major AI pivot

**Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney announced a "huge pivot" toward AI game development** on October 12, 2026, months after laying off 1,000+ employees.

- Announced via post on X (Twitter).
- Described as a major AI development push / retooling teams for AI.
- First reported result of AI acceleration was a delay to a Star Wars Fortnite collaboration (per Aftermath).
- The March 2026 layoff memo had specifically stated the cuts were "not related to AI."
