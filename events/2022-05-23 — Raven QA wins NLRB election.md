# 2022-05-23 — Raven QA wins NLRB election (first ABK union)

**First union certified at an Activision Blizzard subsidiary.** Raven Software QA workers voted 19-2 (86% support) to unionize as Game Workers Alliance-CWA. The election was held after a months-long campaign where Activision attempted to block the union through department restructuring, refusal of voluntary recognition, and NLRB petitions to expand the voting unit. The NLRB found evidence that Activision violated labor laws during the campaign.

The victory directly enabled the Microsoft-CWA Labor Neutrality Agreement signed weeks later and inspired the entire organizing wave that followed — Blizzard Albany (Dec 2022), Activision QA United (Mar 2024), and wall-to-wall Blizzard unions (2024-2025).
