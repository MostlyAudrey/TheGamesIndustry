# King Stockholm Union Formation (Oct 2024)

**Candy Crush developer King's Stockholm employees formed a union club (Fackklubb) with Swedish Unionen in October 2024.** Started with ~9-10 members, grew to 217 by January 2025 after King management removed an on-site private doctor benefit.

As of September 2026, King has not entered into a collective bargaining agreement, leading to strike authorization by Candy Crush developers.
