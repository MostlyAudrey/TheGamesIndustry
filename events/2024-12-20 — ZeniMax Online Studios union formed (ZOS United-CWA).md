# 2024-12 — ZeniMax Online Studios union formed (ZOS United-CWA)

**December 12, 2024.** 461 workers at ZeniMax Online Studios (developer of The Elder Scrolls Online) announced the formation of ZOS United-CWA. The wall-to-wall unit covers web developers, designers, engineers, and graphic artists. Microsoft voluntarily recognized the union.

This was the second union at ZOS — ZeniMax Workers United-CWA (QA testers, ~300 members) had organized in January 2023.
