# Tencent/Sony acquire FromSoftware minority stake

Tencent subsidiary Sixjoy Hong Kong acquired 16.25% and Sony Interactive Entertainment acquired 14.09% of FromSoftware through a third-party allotment of new shares. Kadokawa Corporation retains majority ownership at 69.66%.
