Hello Games' No Man's Sky launched August 9, 2016 to massive hype followed by disastrous reception. Missing features, server issues, and what was perceived as misleading marketing led to review bombs and refunds. Hello Games founder Sean Murray was criticized for promising features that didn't ship.

**Turnaround (2016–2025):** Over 8 years, Hello Games released 25+ major free updates — Foundation, Path Finder, Atlas Rises, NEXT, Beyond, Origins, Sentinel, Endurance, Waypoint, Fractal, Singularity, Echoes, Omega, Orbital, Worlds Part I, Worlds Part II, and more. Each added significant content. The game's reputation recovered remarkably.

**Labor significance:** Hello Games maintained a tiny team (~15-25 people) throughout the entire 8-year turnaround. No layoffs. No union. The studio's resilience and commitment to free updates is a rare story in the industry.
