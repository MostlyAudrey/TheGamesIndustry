# Nexon MapleStory Loot Box Fine

The Korea Fair Trade Commission fined Nexon 11.6 billion won ($8.9M) — a record penalty — for secretly altering the drop rates of coveted items in MapleStory's gacha/cube system without informing players. The manipulation occurred over multiple years before discovery. Following the fine, a class action lawsuit was filed by over 1,000 players seeking damages.
