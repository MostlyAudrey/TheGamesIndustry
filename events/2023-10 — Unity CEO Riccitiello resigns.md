# John Riccitiello resigns as Unity CEO

John Riccitiello stepped down as CEO, president, chairman, and board member of Unity effective immediately on October 9, 2023. His resignation came less than a month after the disastrous Runtime Fee announcement.

## Leadership changes
- Riccitiello: CEO since 2014; resigned all positions
- James M. Whitehurst: appointed interim CEO, president, board member (former Red Hat CEO)
- Roelof Botha: replaced Riccitiello as chairman (Sequoia Capital partner)
- Matthew Bromberg: appointed permanent CEO effective May 15, 2024 (former Zynga COO, EA SVP)

## Significance
- Riccitiello had led Unity since 2014, overseeing its 2020 IPO
- His forced exit marked a turning point in Unity's trajectory
- The leadership vacuum accelerated the aggressive cost-cutting that followed
