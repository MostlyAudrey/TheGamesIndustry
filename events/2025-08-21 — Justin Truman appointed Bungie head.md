# Justin Truman succeeds Pete Parsons as Bungie studio head

Justin Truman took over as Bungie studio head in August 2025 after Pete Parsons stepped down following years of criticism.

Tenure: August 2025 – June 2026
Duration: ~10 months

Truman's brief tenure was marked by continued struggles: Marathon's delayed release, ongoing financial pressure from Sony, and ultimately the largest layoff round in Bungie history.
