# SAG-AFTRA 'Do Not Work' order — Mega Man: Dual Override (March 2026)

Performer union SAG-AFTRA issued a "Do Not Work" order for Mega Man: Dual Override, a Capcom-licensed mobile title being developed by Australian studio No Reply Games. The order was issued because Capcom refused to provide union terms for the project.

## Details
- SAG-AFTRA instructed its members to avoid working on the title
- Capcom would not sign a union contract for the project
- The title was a Capcom-licensed mobile game, not a first-party Capcom release
- Australian developer No Reply Games was caught in the middle of the dispute

## Significance
- Capcom's most significant union-related event in the 2022-2026 period
- Indicates Capcom's resistance to union terms when working with US performers
- Follows broader SAG-AFTRA pattern of targeting non-union game productions
- Not an internal worker organizing effort — this is a performer union pressuring a licensor
