# Paradox cancels Life by You, shuts Tectonic studio

Paradox Interactive cancelled **Life by You**, its Sims-like life simulation game, and shuttered the internal studio **Paradox Tectonic** in Berkeley, California. All 24 employees were affected. The studio was led by **Rod Humble**, formerly lead designer on The Sims 2 and The Sims 3. Life by You had been in early access and had already been delayed multiple times before the cancellation.
