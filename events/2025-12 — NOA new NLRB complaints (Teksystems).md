# New NLRB complaints against Nintendo of America (2025-2026)

Two new labor complaints were filed against Nintendo of America and contracting firm Teksystems, alleging violations of the National Labor Relations Act. The charges were filed on December 17, 2025, and January 6, 2026.

## Allegations
- Section 8(a)(4) — "Discharge (including Layoff and Refusal to Hire)"
- Section 8(a)(1) — "Concerted Activities (Retaliation, Discharge, Discipline)"

## Context
- Filed just over three years after the 2022 settlement with MacKenzie Clifton
- Involves a different contracting firm (Teksystems vs. Aston Carter in 2022), suggesting structural issues rather than vendor-specific
- Specific circumstances not publicly detailed as of January 2026
- Comes amid concerns about Nintendo outsourcing customer service overseas ahead of Switch 2 holiday season
