# Nintendo Acquires Next Level Games

**Nintendo announced on January 5, 2021 that it would acquire Next Level Games, the Vancouver-based developer of Luigi's Mansion 3 and the Mario Strikers series.** The acquisition completed March 1, 2021 (per Nintendo's announcement) or March 31, 2021 (per Nintendo's 2021 annual report).

## Details

- **Target:** Next Level Games Inc., Vancouver, Canada
- **Buyer:** Nintendo Co., Ltd.
- **Structure:** 100% share acquisition
- **Price:** Undisclosed
- **Pre-deal status:** Next Level Games had been a second-party partner since 2014, developing exclusively for Nintendo platforms
- **Motivation (Nintendo):** "Securing internal development resources," "boost collaboration," "improvement in development speed and quality"
- **Motivation (NLG):** Shareholder directors (employees and owner-directors) wanted to sell their shares; studio explored potential sale transactions

## Significance

This was one of the very few studio acquisitions Nintendo has ever conducted. The company historically prefers internal development (EPD) and second-party partnerships over M&A. The Next Level Games acquisition (along with the full buyout of Monolith Soft shares completed December 2024) signals a slight shift toward securing external development capacity.

## Post-acquisition

- Co-founder Douglas Tronsgard retired by March 2022
- Studio released Mario Strikers: Battle League (June 2022) as first post-acquisition title
- No layoffs or restructuring since acquisition
- Studio maintains its Vancouver headquarters and leadership
- As of 2026, studio has 154 employees with no reported labor issues
