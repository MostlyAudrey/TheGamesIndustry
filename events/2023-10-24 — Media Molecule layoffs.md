# Media Molecule layoffs

**October 2023.** Media Molecule laid off ~20 staff (~15% of workforce), reducing headcount from 135 to 115.

## Key details
- Sony confirmed the layoffs were due to "significant strategic changes"
- The end of Dreams live service support (announced earlier in 2023) triggered the restructuring
- The studio was transitioning from supporting Dreams to working on a new project
- Reports indicated Media Molecule could have been a candidate for closure but was spared

## Timeline
- January 2023: Dreams live service support ended
- October 2023: ~20 staff laid off
- March 2024: Studio confirmed the layoffs affected planned community and curation support for Dreams
