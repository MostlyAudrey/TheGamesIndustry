# Tencent completes Grinding Gear Games full acquisition

Tencent completed full acquisition of Grinding Gear Games, developer of Path of Exile and Path of Exile 2, after founders Arik Olofsson, Jonathan Rogers, and Chris Wilson sold their remaining shares.
