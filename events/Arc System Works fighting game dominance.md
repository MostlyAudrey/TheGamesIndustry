# Arc System Works fighting game dominance (2018-2026)

Arc System Works rose from a niche fighting game developer to the genre's defining studio between 2018-2026, driven by technical innovation and a string of critically and commercially successful releases.

**The turning point: Dragon Ball FighterZ (2018)**

Dragon Ball FighterZ (published by Bandai Namco) was a watershed moment. Released January 2018, the game:
- Sold 10M+ units worldwide — a record for Arc System Works and one of the best-selling fighting games ever
- Won Best Fighting Game at The Game Awards 2018
- Set new visual standards for anime fighting games with Arc System Works' proprietary 3D cel-shading
- Introduced a generation of Dragon Ball fans to the fighting game genre

**Guilty Gear Strive (2021) — Genre reinvention**

Guilty Gear Strive represented another milestone:
- 2.5M+ units sold (Guilty Gear's previous peak was ~500K)
- Credited with revitalizing the fighting game genre through:
  - Industry-leading rollback netcode (a major technical standard for online play)
  - Simplified but deep mechanics accessible to new players
  - Streamlined presentation with spectacular visual effects
  - Cross-platform play implementation
- Established Arc System Works as the genre's technical leader

**Expansion arc (2021-2026)**

Arc System Works leveraged its newfound dominance for a series of high-profile projects:
- Granblue Fantasy: Versus (2020) and Rising (2023) — with Cygames
- DNF Duel (2022) — with Neople/Nexon
- Guilty Gear Strive continued DLC support through Season 4+
- International studio expansion (Arc System Works America 2018, Arc System Works Europe 2022)
- Arc World Championship (AWC) established as premier Fighting Game Community (FGC) circuit

**Market position (as of 2026)**

Arc System Works is the leading independent fighting game developer, holding an estimated 30-40% of the fighting game genre market share behind Capcom's Street Fighter series. The studio's unique position:
- Independent (no parent company, no major external investors)
- Technical leader in online infrastructure and visual presentation
- Preferred partner for anime-IP fighting game adaptations
- Consistent release cadence with multiple concurrent projects

**Significance:** Arc System Works demonstrated that a mid-tier Japanese studio could achieve global dominance in a specific genre through technical excellence and strong IP partnerships — without being acquired or taking outside investment.
