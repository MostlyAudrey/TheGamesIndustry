A poorly redacted document in Wolfire's antitrust lawsuit against Valve leaked detailed headcount and payroll data covering 2003-2021. First noticed by SteamDB's Pavel Djundik. The data showed Valve operated with approximately 350 employees and generated ~$6.5B in revenue (2021), with mean gross pay per employee rising from $483K (2011) to approximately $1.3M (2021). The Steam department had shrunk from a peak of 142 (2015) to just 79 (2021). The Games department represented a majority of headcount.
