# Unity 265 layoffs + Weta Digital split (Nov 2023)

Unity announced 265 job cuts (3.8% of workforce), with the vast majority (256) coming from the Weta Digital division that Unity had acquired for $1.63 billion in 2021. Unity simultaneously terminated its agreement with Weta FX.

This was the fourth round of layoffs since July 2022, occurring just two months after Riccitiello's resignation and two months before the massive 1,800 layoff round.
