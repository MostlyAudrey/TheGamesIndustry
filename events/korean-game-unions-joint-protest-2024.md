# Cross-Company Union Protest

Labor unions from Nexon, NCSOFT, and Netmarble jointly protested restructuring across the Korean game industry. NCSOFT's union demanded retraction of the spinoff plan. Neople (Nexon subsidiary) threatened strike action. Netmarble employees formed a new union, joining workers at seven other Korean gaming firms already organized.
