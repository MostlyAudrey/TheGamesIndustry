IO Interactive, the Danish developer of the Hitman series, completed a management buyout from Square Enix in June 2017. Square Enix had announced in May 2017 that it was divesting the studio due to disappointing financial results. IO Interactive retained the Hitman IP — a rare outcome in publisher-studio splits.

The buyout allowed IO Interactive to operate as an independent studio, self-publishing the Hitman World of Assassination trilogy. The studio has since grown from ~200 to ~500+ employees and announced a James Bond 007 game (Project 007).

**Labor significance:** No layoffs or union reported. Danish labor law provides strong protections. The independence deal is notable for preserving the IP with the developer rather than the publisher.
