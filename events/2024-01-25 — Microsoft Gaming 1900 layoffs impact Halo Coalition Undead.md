# 2024-01-25 — Microsoft Gaming 1,900 layoffs impact first-party studios

**The first major layoff wave under Microsoft Gaming after the $69B Activision Blizzard acquisition.** 1,900 workers (~9% of Microsoft Gaming division) were laid off. While primarily targeting Activision Blizzard roles, first-party Xbox Game Studios were also affected. Halo Studios (then 343 Industries) saw "over 60" staff cut in the related January 2023 Microsoft layoffs, and additional reductions in the 1,900 wave. The Coalition was largely spared in this round. Undead Labs saw some impact. CWA-represented members (ZeniMax QA, Raven, Blizzard Albany) were not impacted per CWA statement.

## Impact by studio
| Studio | Impact | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Halo Studios (343 Industries) | Affected | Layoffs continued from Jan 2023 cuts; studio already weakened by leadership departures |
| The Coalition | Largely spared | Considered a "flagship" first-party studio; active Gears development |
| Undead Labs | Partial impact | Some roles cut; State of Decay 3 development continued |
| Activision/Blizzard | Heaviest impact | Primary target of the 1,900 cuts; Blizzard president Mike Ybarra departed |
