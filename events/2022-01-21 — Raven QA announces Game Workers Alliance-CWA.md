# 2022-01-21 — Raven Software QA announces Game Workers Alliance-CWA

**The first union at Activision Blizzard.** After a five-week strike triggered by the layoff of ~12 QA testers in December 2021, the remaining Raven Software QA workers formed the Game Workers Alliance (GWA) in partnership with CWA. Activision Blizzard refused to voluntarily recognize the union, and the workers filed for an NLRB election on January 25, 2022. The union won its election 19-2 on May 23, 2022 — making it the first certified union at Activision Blizzard and the second formal video game union in the US.

This union drive directly set the precedent for the Microsoft-CWA Labor Neutrality Agreement (June 2022) and the entire organizing wave that followed under Microsoft ownership.
