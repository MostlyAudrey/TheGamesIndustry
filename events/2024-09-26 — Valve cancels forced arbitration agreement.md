Valve canceled its forced arbitration clause in the Steam Subscriber Agreement on Sept 26, 2024. The decision came after a law firm and litigation funder organized a mass arbitration campaign targeting Valve. Valve had accused the law firm of "extortion." By removing the arbitration clause, Valve chose to face class actions in court rather than deal with thousands of individual arbitration claims.
