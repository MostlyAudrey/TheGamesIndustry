# Unity Feb 2025 layoffs — sixth wave

Another round of layoffs at Unity, described as the sixth wave since 2022. Employees were notified via 5AM email. The entire Behavior team (builds NPC scripting tools) was eliminated.

## CEO memo details
Matthew Bromberg's leaked internal memo acknowledged:
- "Exhaustion associated with prior changes that haven't delivered promised results"
- Product/engineering teams "stretched across too many products"
- New focus on "fidelity for ubiquity" (cross-platform optimization over high-end graphics)
- Investing in stability and technical debt reduction
- Live services and generative AI as priorities
- Integrating Unity Ads, LevelPlay, and Tapjoy into core Runtime

## Impact
- Number of affected employees undisclosed
- Sixth mass layoff wave since 2022
- Follows 1,800 cuts in Jan 2024, 265 in Nov 2023, 600 in May 2023, 284 in Jan 2023, ~225 in Jun 2022
- Exodus to Godot and other engines continues
