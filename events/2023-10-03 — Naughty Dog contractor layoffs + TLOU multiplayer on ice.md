# Naughty Dog contractor layoffs + TLOU multiplayer on ice

**October 2023.** Naughty Dog laid off at least 25 contract developers working on the troubled The Last of Us multiplayer spin-off (project codenamed Factions/Online). Multiple outlets reported the studio was putting the project "on ice" and ending contracts early.

## Key details
- ~25 contract developers laid off
- The Last of Us multiplayer project described as "on ice"
- Sony declined to confirm the layoffs publicly but did not deny them
- The studio stated it was "still working on the project" but at reduced capacity
- By December 2024, the project was officially cancelled

## Preceding context
- The multiplayer game was first conceived as part of The Last of Us Part II's Factions mode
- Expanded into a standalone project after 2020
- Development had reportedly struggled for years
- Sony was pushing live-service games across studios (2022-2023)
