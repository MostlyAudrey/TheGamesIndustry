# 2024-05 — Microsoft closes Arkane Austin, Tango Gameworks, Alpha Dog; absorbs Roundhouse

**Closure of three ZeniMax studios and absorption of a fourth.** Announced May 7, 2024 as part of Microsoft Gaming restructuring. Tango Gameworks was later saved when PUBG publisher Krafton acquired the studio in August 2024.
