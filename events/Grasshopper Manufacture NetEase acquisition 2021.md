# NetEase acquires Grasshopper Manufacture (October 2021)

NetEase acquired Grasshopper Manufacture from Marvelous Inc. in October 2021, marking the Chinese internet giant's first major Japanese game developer acquisition.

**Key details:**
- Grasshopper was acquired from Marvelous Inc. (which had held the studio since 2013 via GungHo)
- Founder Goichi Suda ("Suda51") remained as CEO
- NetEase publicly committed to preserving Grasshopper's creative autonomy
- The transaction value was not disclosed but was reported as sizeable for a boutique studio

**Suda51's statement:** "This is not a so-called 'absorption' merger, but rather that [Grasshopper] will continue to operate normally as an organization while being under the umbrella of NetEase. There is absolutely no change in the creative side."

**Context:** The acquisition was part of NetEase's broader Japanese studio investment strategy:
- Grasshopper Manufacture (2021) — Suda51's cult hit studio
- Nagoshi Studio (2022) — founded by former Sega/RGG Studio head Toshihiro Nagoshi
- GPTRACK50 (2022) — founded by former Capcom producer Hiroyuki Kobayashi
- Bokeh Game Studio (2022 investment) — founded by former Sony Japan Studio's Keiichiro Toyama
- Quantic Dream (2022, partial) — though French, part of the same acquisition wave

**Post-acquisition status (2021-2026):**
- Grasshopper has not released a major new title since the acquisition
- A next-gen game was announced as in development in 2024
- Studio has maintained ~50-person headcount and creative independence as promised

**Significance:** Grasshopper was NetEase's first Japanese studio acquisition, launching a wave of Chinese investment in Japanese game development talent that continued through 2022-2023.
