# Event — Ubisoft Halifax union certified (Dec 2025)

**What:** Ubisoft Halifax workers voted 74% to unionize with CWA Canada Local 30111 — creating the **first unionized Ubisoft studio in North America**.

**When:** Certified December 18, 2025.

**Who:** ~60 workers at Ubisoft Halifax (formerly Longtail Studios), including producers, designers, artists, testers, and researchers.

**Structure:** Wall-to-wall union under CWA Canada Local 30111, which also covers Bethesda Game Studios workers.

**Aftermath:** Studio was closed ~3 weeks later on January 7, 2026 (71 positions eliminated). CWA Canada is pursuing legal recourse, demanding information about the timing.
