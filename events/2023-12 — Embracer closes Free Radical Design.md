---
title: "Embracer Group closes Free Radical Design (TimeSplitters)"
date: 2023-12-11
publisher: Embracer Group
confidence: verified
---

Embracer Group closed Free Radical Design, a UK-based studio that had been re-established in 2021 to revive the TimeSplitters franchise. The closure was part of Embracer's restructuring program following the Savvy deal collapse. Approximately 80 employees were laid off. The TimeSplitters IP was retained by Embracer.

Free Radical Design was originally founded in 1999 by ex-Rare employees and had created the TimeSplitters series. It was acquired by Crytek (2007), then closed (2009). The team was only briefly reassembled before being shut down again.
