# 2025-03 — Tencent increases Kadokawa stake to 7.97%

Tencent acquired an additional 1.11% of Kadokawa shares (from 6.86% to 7.97%). Part of ongoing "capital and business alliance" strategy. Motivations include access to Kadokawa's extensive anime, manga, and light novel IP library.
