# Brian Bulatao — Chief Administrative Officer, Activision Blizzard

**Chief Administrative Officer of Activision Blizzard from 2021 to 2023.** An unusual hire for a gaming company — Bulatao brought a national security and government background to ABK's post-DFEH reform efforts. His role encompassed HR transformation, compliance overhaul, and corporate operations.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** Business and operations roles in private sector.
- **2017-2018:** Chief Operating Officer of the CIA — appointed by Director Mike Pompeo. Managed agency-wide operations and administration.
- **2018-2019:** Nominated by President Trump as Under Secretary of State for Management — confirmed by Senate 92-5 in May 2019.
- **2021:** Joined Activision Blizzard as CAO — a key hire in the wake of the DFEH lawsuit. Tasked with rebuilding the company's compliance and HR infrastructure.
- **2023:** Departed following the Microsoft acquisition.

## Controversy and context

Bulatao's appointment at ABK was controversial — critics questioned whether a former CIA and Trump administration official was the right person to lead cultural reform at a company accused of systemic harassment. Supporters pointed to his extensive management experience at massive organizations.
