# Shuji Utsumi

Shuji Utsumi (born April 19, 1961) is a Japanese business executive and video game producer who has been President and COO of [[orgs/Sega]] since April 2024, also serving as CEO of Sega of America and Sega Europe.

## Career Arc

- Joined Sony in 1994 as Vice President, involved in the launch of PlayStation in North America
- Joined Sega in 1996 as Senior Vice President, oversaw the launch of the Sega Dreamcast in North America
- Involved in the creation of Kingdom Hearts at Disney Interactive
- Co-founded Q Entertainment with Tetsuya Mizuguchi
- Returned to Sega in a senior leadership capacity around 2023
- Appointed President and COO of Sega (April 2024)
- Also serves as CEO of Sega of America and Sega Europe
- Over 30-year career in the games industry

## Key Decisions

- Leading Sega's transmedia push and global expansion strategy
- Overseeing Sega's "super game" initiative and revival of classic IPs
- Driving the company's strategy in an era of Japanese games resurgence
- Has spoken about the risks of over-relying on nostalgia

## Labor Context

- Sega as a company has had labor relations challenges typical of Japanese game companies
- Sega's Western studios have had various restructuring events
- No direct labor controversies personally associated
