# Neil Druckmann — Co-President, Naughty Dog

**The most prominent AAA writer-director of the 2010s.** Druckmann co-created The Last of Us (one of the most acclaimed game series of all time), co-wrote Uncharted 4, and co-created the HBO television adaptation. He is now studio head of Naughty Dog.

## Career arc

- **2003:** Internship at Naughty Dog while studying computer science at Carnegie Mellon's Entertainment Technology Center.
- **2004–2005:** Programmer on Jak 3 and Jak X: Combat Racing.
- **2007:** Designer on Uncharted: Drake's Fortune.
- **2009:** Co-lead game designer and co-writer on Uncharted 2: Among Thieves.
- **2013:** Co-writer and creative director of The Last of Us — one of the highest-rated games of all time (Metacritic 95). Won over 200 Game of the Year awards.
- **2014:** Wrote The Last of Us: Left Behind DLC — critically acclaimed prequel.
- **2016:** Co-writer on Uncharted 4: A Thief's End.
- **2018–2020:** Writer, creative director on The Last of Us Part II — polarizing, critically acclaimed, won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2020.
- **2020:** Promoted to Co-President of Naughty Dog alongside Alison Mori and Christian Gyrling.
- **2023:** Co-created and co-wrote HBO's The Last of Us with Craig Mazin — a critically acclaimed TV series, nominated for and winning multiple Emmys.
- **2024:** Became Studio Head and Head of Creative. Announced Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet — a new sci-fi IP.
- **2025–2026:** Working on Intergalactic and overseeing Naughty Dog's next projects.

- **Narrative-focused design:** Druckmann's games prioritize character-driven emotion and morally complex storytelling over gameplay-centric design.
- **The HBO adaptation:** The Last of Us TV series demonstrated that game-to-screen adaptations could succeed critically. Its success (viewership, Emmy noms) helped legitimize games as source material.
- **Creative ambition:** The Last of Us Part II's narrative — about the cycle of violence, revenge, and empathy — took risks that most AAA games would not attempt.

## Labour context

- Naughty Dog has been criticized for crunch culture. The Last of Us Part II reportedly involved extensive overtime, including a period of 12-hour, 6-day weeks.
- Druckmann has acknowledged Naughty Dog's intense work culture: "We are definitely trying to address [crunch]. I'm not going to say we've figured it out, but we're more aware than ever."
- In 2020, Naughty Dog released a statement acknowledging the studio "should have done better" on work-life balance during Part II's development.
- No union presence at Naughty Dog.
- Druckmann's leadership of Naughty Dog coincides with [[people/Bruce Straley]]'s departure (Straley was co-director of The Last of Us and left Naughty Dog in 2017).
- The industry has criticized Naughty Dog as one of the remaining "crunch culture" studios in the post-2020 era, alongside [[orgs/Rockstar Games]].
