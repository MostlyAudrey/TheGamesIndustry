# Nate Mitchell

**Role:** Co-founder, Oculus VR  
**Orgs:** [[Oculus VR]], [[Meta]] (formerly Facebook), [[Scaleform]]

## Career Arc

Nate Mitchell is an American virtual reality pioneer and co-founder of **Oculus VR**. He joined the Oculus founding team very early alongside Brendan Iribe, Palmer Luckey, and Michael Antonov. Mitchell and Iribe had previously co-founded **Scaleform**, a gaming UI software company sold to Autodesk in 2011, with the team transitioning directly from Scaleform to Oculus.

At Oculus, Mitchell served initially as VP of Product, became Head of Rift in 2016, and later Head of VR Product at Facebook (after the $3B acquisition in 2014). He was a prominent spokesperson for VR technology and the Oculus brand. In August 2019, Mitchell left Oculus after seven years, becoming the last co-founder to depart Facebook's VR division.

## Key Decisions

- **Joined Oculus founding team:** Transitioned from Scaleform to Oculus early in its formation.
- **Led Rift product development:** Oversaw the creation of the first consumer VR headset.
- **Stayed through Facebook acquisition integration:** Navigated the cultural and operational integration of Oculus into Facebook.
- **Departed in 2019:** Left as the last co-founder, marking the end of Oculus's founding era.

## Labor Context

Mitchell's tenure spans the transition from startup to corporate integration, a period that saw significant tension between Oculus's original hacker culture and Facebook's corporate environment. The departure of all Oculus co-founders over time reflects the challenges of startup-to-corporate transitions in gaming/tech.
