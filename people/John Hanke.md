# John Hanke

**Role:** Founder & former CEO, Niantic (now Executive Chairman)  
**Orgs:** [[Niantic]], [[Google]], [[Niantic Spatial]]

## Career Arc

John Hanke is an American entrepreneur and the founder of **Niantic Inc.**, originally an internal startup at Google. He earned his MBA from UC Berkeley Haas in 1996. Before Niantic, Hanke founded Keyhole, a geospatial visualization company that Google acquired in 2004 and turned into **Google Earth**. He went on to lead Google's Geo division before spinning out Niantic in 2015.

Niantic created *Ingress* (2013), *Pokémon GO* (2016), and *Harry Potter: Wizards Unite* (2019). *Pokémon GO* became a global phenomenon with over 630M downloads, pioneering augmented reality mobile gaming. In March 2026, Hanke transitioned from CEO to Executive Chairman, as Niantic split to form **Niantic Spatial**, a geospatial AI company.

## Key Decisions

- **Created Ingress as a Google internal project:** Built the foundation for location-based AR gaming.
- **Spun Niantic out of Google (2015):** Secured independence and $30M from Google, Nintendo, and Pokémon Company.
- **Partnered with Nintendo/The Pokémon Company (2016):** Launched *Pokémon GO*, which became a historic cultural phenomenon.
- **AR-first design philosophy:** Pioneered real-world, location-based mobile gaming at massive scale.
- **Sold games business (2025–2026):** Transitioned Niantic's games division and focused on geospatial AI.

## Labor Context

Niantic's labor history includes notable tensions with its player community over COVID-era changes and feature rollbacks. Internally, the company faced challenges around work-life balance during *Pokémon GO*'s explosive launch. The 2025–2026 restructuring that sold the games business affected many game developers. Niantic's reliance on live events created a workforce with intense seasonal peaks.
