# Yoshinori Kitase

Yoshinori Kitase (born September 23, 1966) is a Japanese company director, video game producer, and former game director at [[orgs/Square Enix]]. He is a legendary figure in the Final Fantasy series.

## Career Arc

- Graduated from Nihon University College of Art (cinema studies)
- Joined Square Co., Ltd. in 1990 — one of Hironobu Sakaguchi's early hires
- Worked as event scene designer and director on early Final Fantasy titles
- Directed Final Fantasy VII (1997) — one of the most influential games in history
- Directed Final Fantasy VIII (1999)
- Produced Final Fantasy X (2001), Final Fantasy X-2 (2003), Final Fantasy XIII trilogy
- Became head of Product Development Division 1
- Led Creative Business Unit 1 as producer of mainline Final Fantasy games (until 2024 restructuring)
- Appointed Corporate Executive (2006), Executive Officer (2015), Director (2018)
- Board Director of Square Enix Holdings since June 2022
- Appointed brand manager for the Final Fantasy franchise in April 2021

## Key Decisions

- Oversaw the Final Fantasy VII Remake project (2020) as producer
- Transitioned from direct game direction to overseeing the entire Final Fantasy franchise brand
- As former CEO of Square Enix (per some sources — actually Yosuke Matsuda was CEO until 2023; Kitase was a senior producer, not CEO. The "former CEO" label in the task is likely a misattribution.)

## Labor Context

- No personal labor controversies
- Square Enix has faced industry-wide crunch concerns, particularly during FFVII Remake development
