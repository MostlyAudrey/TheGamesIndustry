# Markus Persson (Notch) — Creator of Minecraft

**The developer who built the best-selling video game in history.** Markus "Notch" Persson's Minecraft has sold over 300 million copies and defined a genre (sandbox survival crafting). His sale of Mojang to Microsoft for $2.5 billion (2014) remains one of the largest exits in video game history.

## Career arc

- **2000s:** Self-taught programmer. Worked at King.com (now King, the Candy Crush company) as a game developer.
- **2009:** Developed Minecraft Classic in a single weekend. Released the alpha/pre-alpha version online — it went viral.
- **2010:** Founded Mojang Studios in Stockholm. Minecraft sales exploded through alpha and beta phases.
- **2011:** Official retail launch of Minecraft. Persson stepped back from lead design, handing creative control to Jens Bergensten (Jeb).
- **2014 (November):** Microsoft acquired Mojang for $2.5 billion. Persson left the company.
- **2015–present:** Created Rubberbrain (later relaunched as Bitshift Entertainment). Public controversies over social media posts regarding race, gender, and politics. Microsoft distanced itself from Persson, removing his name from Minecraft (except the end credits).
- Claimed in 2024 to be working on a spiritual sequel to Minecraft called "Levers and Chests" but no release has materialized.

- **Early access pioneer:** Minecraft's alpha/beta model generated enormous revenue before "full release," proving that unfinished games could be commercially viable.
- **Open development:** Persson released frequent updates during alpha/beta, engaging the community in feature design.
- **IP sale:** The $2.5B sale to Microsoft was one of the largest game studio acquisitions at the time, making Persson a billionaire at age 35.

## Labour context

- Mojang under Persson was a ~70-person studio with a famously flat organizational structure and unconventional culture.
- No union activity at Mojang; studio culture was described as idiosyncratic rather than exploitative.
- Post-Microsoft, Mojang has operated as a normal subsidiary with no reported mass layoffs.
- Persson has become increasingly controversial. The Minecraft community and Microsoft have actively distanced from his views.
- Persson's post-Mojang statements on Twitter were described as "explicitly racist, homophobic, transphobic and generally hideous" (Forbes, 2024).
