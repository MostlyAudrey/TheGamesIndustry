# Masaru Kawaguchi

Masaru Kawaguchi is the CEO, President, and Representative Director of [[orgs/Bandai Namco]] Holdings, taking the role in April 2021 under a new corporate structure.

## Career Arc

- Spent career at Bandai and Bandai Namco; served as Executive Vice President and Director before becoming President
- Became President and Representative Director of Bandai Namco Entertainment Unit in 2021
- Elevated to President and Representative Director of Bandai Namco Holdings Inc. (the parent company)
- Appointed CEO of the holding company in 2022
- Age ~64 as of 2025 (per GlobalData)
- Oversaw the integration of Bandai Namco's business units

## Key Decisions

- Led the 2021 restructuring that merged Bandai Namco's business units
- Pushed for stronger IP management across Bandai Namco's anime and game properties
- Oversees Bandai Namco's entertainment business including games (Tekken, Elden Ring, Dark Souls publishing), toys, and anime
- Navigated the company through the post-pandemic entertainment landscape

## Labor Context

- Bandai Namco has faced reports of poor working conditions and low pay at some studios
- Bandai Namco Online (subsidiary) was involved in controversies around game shutdowns
- No direct personal labor controversies
