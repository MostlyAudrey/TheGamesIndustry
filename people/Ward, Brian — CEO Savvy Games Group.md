# Brian Ward — CEO, Savvy Games Group

Brian Ward is the Chief Executive Officer of Savvy Games Group, the PIF subsidiary executing Saudi Arabia's $38 billion gaming industry investment plan. He oversees a portfolio including Scopely (mobile games), ESL FACEIT Group (esports), Steer Studios (game development), and the Niantic games portfolio (Pokémon Go, Monster Hunter Now, Pikmin Bloom).

## Career arc

- **Background:** Gaming and media executive with deep experience in mobile gaming, esports, and game publishing before joining Savvy.
- **Savvy Games Group (2023–present):** CEO overseeing all PIF gaming operations. Reports to chairman Yasir Al-Rumayyan and the PIF board. Tasked with making Saudi Arabia a global gaming hub by 2030.
- **Key operational achievements:** Led Savvy through the Scopely acquisition ($4.9B), the Niantic games portfolio acquisition, and the ongoing integration of Savvy's multiple gaming properties into a coherent operating platform. Manages 3,500+ employees across 29 locations globally.
