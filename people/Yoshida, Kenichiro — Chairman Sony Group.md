# Kenichiro Yoshida — Executive Chairman, Sony Group Corporation

Japanese corporate executive who led Sony through its transformation from a struggling electronics conglomerate into a global entertainment powerhouse. Architect of Sony's pivot to content and IP-driven growth.

## Career arc

- **1983:** Joins Sony after University of Tokyo (B.A. Economics).
- **2000–2013:** Leads So-net (Sony ISP subsidiary), taking it public in 2005.
- **2013:** Returns to Sony as Deputy CFO.
- **2014:** Promoted to CFO; engineers financial restructuring that returns Sony to profitability.
- **2018:** Becomes President and CEO of Sony Group Corporation.
- **2025:** Becomes Executive Chairman; Hiroki Totoki becomes President and CEO.

## Key decisions and strategic direction

Yoshida shifted Sony's focus from hardware-centric consumer electronics to entertainment IP — games, music, anime, and film. Under his leadership, Sony:

- Deepened PlayStation investment through studio acquisitions (Bungie, Insomniac, Bluepoint, Housemarque)
- Acquired a strategic stake in Kadokawa Corporation (FromSoftware parent)
- Expanded PlayStation to PC with first-party ports
- Pushed Sony into anime via Funimation/Crunchyroll
- Created the dual-CEO structure at SIE with Hulst (Studio) and Nishino (Platform)

## Labour context

Yoshida operated at the Sony Group level, not directly managing SIE day-to-day. However, the 900-person SIE layoff (Feb 2024), Bungie layoffs, and Firewalk/Neon Koi closures happened under his group oversight. No documented anti-union activity at the group level.
