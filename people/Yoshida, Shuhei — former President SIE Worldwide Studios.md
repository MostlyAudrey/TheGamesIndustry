# Shuhei Yoshida — Former President, SIE Worldwide Studios

The heart and soul of PlayStation for three decades. Yoshida was part of the original PlayStation project from 1993, rose to lead Worldwide Studios through the PS3 and PS4 generations, and ended his career championing indie developers. His 2026 revelation that he was pushed out for refusing Jim Ryan's demands added a sharp coda to a beloved career.

## Career arc

- **1986:** Joins Sony corporate strategy.
- **1993:** Joins the secret PlayStation project — first non-engineer hire.
- **1996–2000:** Producer on iconic PlayStation titles.
- **2000:** VP at SCE America.
- **2008:** President of SCE Worldwide Studios.
- **2019:** Steps down as Worldwide Studios President; heads PlayStation Indies.
- **2025:** Retires from Sony.
- **2026:** Reveals in interview he was fired from Worldwide Studios for saying no to Jim Ryan.

## Legacy and impact

Yoshida was the public face of PlayStation game development for over a decade. Known for his genuine enthusiasm, active Twitter presence, and close relationships with developers. Under his leadership, Worldwide Studios produced some of the most acclaimed games of the PS3 and PS4 generations. His switch to indies in 2019 signaled Sony's growing commitment to smaller creative projects.

## Labour context

Yoshida's era was before the wave of mass layoffs that hit the industry in 2023–2026. No major labour controversies occurred under his leadership. He is widely liked by developers across the industry.
