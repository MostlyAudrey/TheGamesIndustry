# Greg Joswiak

**Role:** SVP Worldwide Marketing, Apple  
**Orgs:** [[Apple]]

## Career Arc

Gregory "Joz" Joswiak (born October 1963) is an American business executive and Senior Vice President of Worldwide Marketing at **Apple Inc.**. He graduated from the University of Michigan with a BS in Computer Engineering in 1986 and joined Apple shortly after, beginning what has become a 40+ year career at the company.

Joswiak held various product marketing roles before becoming VP of Apple Product Marketing, managing teams responsible for Apple's entire product lineup. In August 2020, he succeeded Phil Schiller as SVP of Worldwide Marketing when Schiller became Apple Fellow. Joswiak is a regular presence at Apple product launches and keynotes.

## Key Decisions

- **Succeeded Phil Schiller (2020):** Took over Apple's worldwide marketing organization.
- **iPhone, iPad product marketing:** Led product marketing across the most important gaming platforms.
- **Apple's gaming narrative:** Shaped how Apple positions its devices for gaming — particularly the iPhone as a mobile gaming platform and the Mac's expansion into AAA gaming via Apple Silicon.

## Labor Context

Joswiak's marketing leadership directly influences how Apple positions itself to mobile game developers and players. Apple's push into AAA gaming on Mac/Apple Silicon has implications for which games reach Apple users. As App Store policies remain under regulatory scrutiny, Joswiak helps shape Apple's public narrative on gaming.
