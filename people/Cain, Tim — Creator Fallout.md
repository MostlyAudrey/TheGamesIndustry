# Tim Cain — Creator of Fallout

**The creator of one of the most influential RPGs ever made.** Tim Cain created the original Fallout (1997), co-founded Troika Games, and co-created The Outer Worlds. He is one of the most respected designers in Western RPG development.

## Career arc

- **Early 1990s:** Worked at Interplay Entertainment as a programmer.
- **1994–1997:** Built a game engine for a sci-fi RPG in his spare time. The engine became the basis for Fallout — a post-apocalyptic RPG that he produced, lead-programmed, and co-designed.
- **1997 (October):** Fallout released to critical acclaim. Iconic for its 1950s retro-future aesthetic, deep character customization (SPECIAL system), and moral choices.
- **1998:** Left Interplay with Leonard Boyarsky and Jason Anderson to co-found Troika Games.
- **2001:** Troika's first game: Arcanum: Of Steamworks and Magick Obscura (steampunk fantasy RPG).
- **2004:** Troika's final game: Vampire: The Masquerade - Bloodlines — a cult classic RPG that was notoriously buggy on release but beloved by fans.
- **2005:** Troika closed due to financial difficulties caused by publisher funding gaps and the commercial underperformance of Bloodlines.
- **2008:** Joined Obsidian Entertainment.
- **2016–2019:** Co-led The Outer Worlds with [[people/Leonard Boyarsky]] — a spiritual successor to Fallout: New Vegas in tone and design.
- **2019 (October):** The Outer Worlds released to positive reviews.
- **2025–2026:** The Outer Worlds 2 in development at Obsidian (now Microsoft-owned).

- **The SPECIAL system:** Cain created Fallout's character system (Strength, Perception, Endurance, Charisma, Intelligence, Agility, Luck) — a licensed adaptation of GURPS that became iconic.
- **Post-apocalyptic setting:** The Fallout universe — 1950s retro-futurism meets nuclear apocalypse — defined the visual identity of post-nuclear RPGs.
- **Troika's legacy:** Though Troika only shipped three games, Arcanum, Vampire: Bloodlines, and the cancelled post-apocalyptic game all maintain cult followings.

## Labour context

- Troika Games operated as a small independent studio dependent on publisher funding. The income gap between Fallout (commercial hit) and Bloodlines (commercial disappointment) forced Troika's closure — a classic example of indie studio precarity.
- Obsidian Entertainment (before Microsoft acquisition) was repeatedly in financial difficulty, surviving on work-for-hire contracts and Kickstarter campaigns.
- Cain has spoken about the financial instability of independent game development in interviews and on his YouTube channel.
- No union presence at any of Cain's employers. Cain has been relatively quiet on labor organizing.
