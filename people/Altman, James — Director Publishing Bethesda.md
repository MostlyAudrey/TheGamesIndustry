# James Altman — Director of Publishing Operations, Bethesda Softworks

**Director of Publishing Operations at Bethesda Softworks.** The son of ZeniMax Media founder and CEO Robert A. Altman and actress Lynda Carter. James Altman works on the publishing side of Bethesda, with a focus on Asian market operations.

## Background

- **Education:** Graduated from George Washington University Law School.
- **Family:** Son of Robert A. Altman (ZeniMax founder/chairman/CEO) and Lynda Carter (Wonder Woman actress, singer).
- **Role:** Director of Publishing Operations at Bethesda Softworks — managing business operations, particularly in Asian markets.
- **Post-acquisition:** Following his father's death in February 2021 and Microsoft's acquisition of ZeniMax in March 2021, Altman's role and family involvement in the company changed significantly.
