# Hideaki Nishino — President and CEO, Sony Interactive Entertainment

Japanese technology executive who rose through Sony's consumer electronics and network services divisions to lead PlayStation. After a brief co-CEO structure with Hermen Hulst in 2024, Nishino became the sole President and CEO of Sony Interactive Entertainment in 2025, responsible for both platform and studio operations.

## Career arc

- **2000:** Joins Sony Corporation.
- **2001–2005:** Sony Ericsson Mobile Communications — product planning.
- **2005–2015:** Returns to Sony; various roles across VAIO, network services, and home entertainment.
- **2015–2019:** Key architect of PlayStation Network evolution and PlayStation Plus subscription model.
- **2019:** Appointed SVP, Platform Experience — helped define PS5 architecture, UI, and ecosystem.
- **2024:** CEO, SIE Platform Business Group (hardware, platform, network services, peripherals).
- **2025:** Appointed President and CEO of Sony Interactive Entertainment (solo CEO) and Business CEO, Officer in charge of Game & Network Services Business, Sony Group Corporation.

## Role and responsibilities

As SIE CEO, Nishino oversees:
- PlayStation hardware platforms (PS5, PS5 Pro, next-gen)
- PlayStation Network and online services
- PlayStation Plus subscription
- Third-party relations and publishing
- The Platform Business Group
- After 2025, also Studio Business Group (absorbing Hulst's division)

## Labour context

Nishino's career has been platform-side rather than studio-side. No documented labour controversies. As SIE CEO, inherits the labour relations landscape of first-party studios with no union presence.
