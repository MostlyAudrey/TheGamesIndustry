# Neil Young — former EA executive, founder ngmoco

Neil Young is a video game executive best known for founding ngmoco, the pioneering iPhone game publisher acquired by DeNA for $400 million. He spent over a decade at Electronic Arts rising to VP, running Maxis, EA Los Angeles, and EA's Blueprint division before leaving to pursue mobile gaming.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** Joined Electronic Arts and rose through management ranks. Oversaw game development across multiple EA studios.
- **EA executive (circa 1997–2008):** Managed Maxis (SimCity, The Sims), EA Los Angeles (Command & Conquer series), and EA's Blueprint division focused on new IP and technology incubation. Worked closely with Bing Gordon and other EA leadership.
- **ngmoco (2008–2010):** Founded as a mobile-first game publisher focusing on iPhone/iOS. Titles included Rolando, Eliminate Pro, and the Plus+ social gaming network. Backed by KPCB's iFund ($40.6M total). Rapidly grew to millions of users.
- **DeNA (2010–2016):** Stayed on after the $400M acquisition as head of DeNA's Western operations, overseeing studios in Vancouver, Santiago, Stockholm, and Amsterdam.
- **Post-DeNA:** Turned to blockchain gaming investment and advisory roles. Connected to Game Closure, n3twork, and other blockchain gaming ventures.

## Key relationships

- Worked under Bing Gordon at EA — later Gordon joined ngmoco's board
- Co-founded ngmoco with Bob Stevenson (former EA exec) and Alan Yu
- Reported to Frank Gibeau (EA Games label president) before departure
- Joined by Simon Jeffery (former Sega of America / LucasArts exec) at ngmoco
