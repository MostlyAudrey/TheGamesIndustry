# Alan Hartman — Former Head of Xbox Game Studios; Co-Founder, Turn 10 Studios

**Head of Xbox Game Studios from November 2023 to October 2024.** A nearly 30-year Microsoft veteran, Hartman co-founded Turn 10 Studios and built the Forza Motorsport franchise before ascending to lead all of Xbox Game Studios.

## Career arc

- **Mid-1990s:** Started at Digital Anvil (Chris Roberts' studio) as a studio manager — worked on Freelancer and Brute Force.
- **2001:** Following Microsoft's dissolution of Digital Anvil, became a founding member of Turn 10 Studios.
- **2005:** Promoted to head of Turn 10 — oversaw the Forza Motorsport franchise for nearly 20 years, including Forza Motorsport (2005) through Forza Motorsport (2023).
- **2023-11:** Elevated to head of Xbox Game Studios after Matt Booty moved to oversee ZeniMax. Hartman inherited management of ~15 first-party studios.
- **2024-10:** Announced retirement. [[people/Craig Duncan]] succeeded him as head of Xbox Game Studios.

## Key contributions

- **Forza franchise:** Built Turn 10 into one of the premier racing game developers in the world. The Forza Motorsport series consistently delivered high-quality simulation racing exclusive to Xbox.
- **Turn 10 legacy:** The studio was merged into Playground Games in 2026 (after his retirement), marking the end of Turn 10 as an independent entity.
- **XGS brief tenure:** As XGS head, he oversaw the launch of several key titles and managed the early integration of ABK studios into the overall Microsoft Gaming studio structure.
