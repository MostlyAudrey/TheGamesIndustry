# Humam Sakhnini — M&A architect at Activision Blizzard

Humam Sakhnini is a Palestinian-American executive who served as the M&A and corporate strategy architect of Activision Blizzard's transformation, including the $5.9B King acquisition and the $68.7B Microsoft sale. He is currently CEO of Discord.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BA in Economics, University of Western Ontario; MA in Economics, Queen's University; MBA, Yale School of Management.
- **Investment banking:** Nesbitt Burns; Department of Finance Canada.
- **McKinsey & Company (2000s):** Eight years as partner advising media/tech companies on strategy, M&A, and market entry.
- **Activision Blizzard (2009–2023):** Joined as EVP Corporate Strategy. Rose to Chief Strategy & Talent Officer. Led the King acquisition ($5.9B, 2016) — the single largest mobile gaming acquisition at the time. Joined King as CFO/CSO, later President (2019–2022). Returned to ABK as Vice Chairman to oversee Microsoft acquisition integration and closing. Departed December 2023.
- **Discord (2025–present):** CEO. Taking the gaming-adjacent communications platform toward public markets. 
- **Board service:** Yale School of Management Center for Customer Insights.

## Key relationships

- Worked directly for Bobby Kotick for 12 years — Kotick called him "instrumental" in both King and Blizzard acquisitions
- Partnered with Riccardo Zacconi (King co-founder) and Sebastian Knutsson
- Succeeded by Tjodolf Sommestad as King president
- At Discord: replaced Jason Citron (founder); works with board member Mitch Lasky
