# James M. Whitehurst

Interim CEO of Unity Technologies from October 2023 to May 2024. A career enterprise software executive who bridged the gap between the Riccitiello crisis era and Bromberg's permanent leadership.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BS in Computer Science and Economics from Rice University (1989); MBA from Harvard Business School.
- **Boston Consulting Group:** Partner.
- **Delta Air Lines:** Six years, latterly as COO.
- **Red Hat (2008–2020):** President and CEO. Led the open-source enterprise software company through its $34B acquisition by IBM in 2019. Stayed on as president through 2020.
- **2023–2024:** Interim CEO of Unity Technologies. Appointed after Riccitiello's resignation.
- **Current:** Unity board chairman; advisor to Silver Lake (private equity).

## Key relationships

- Appointed by Unity board (Roelof Botha, Sequoia)
- Replaced by Matthew Bromberg as permanent CEO
- Works with Silver Lake — the PE firm that has invested heavily in the game industry (Embracer)
- Former colleague: Marc Andreessen (board connections via Silver Lake / Red Hat era)
