# Yasir Al-Rumayyan — Governor, PIF

Yasir bin Othman Al-Rumayyan is the Governor (Managing Director) of the Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia — the $930B sovereign wealth fund that is the most powerful capital force in the global video game industry. He also chairs Saudi Aramco, Savvy Games Group, and Newcastle United.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BS in Accounting from King Faisal University (1993); Harvard Business School executive course (2007).
- **Saudi Hollandi Bank (early career):** Head of international brokerage.
- **Capital Markets Authority:** Head of securities listings for Saudi Arabia's financial regulator.
- **Saudi Fransi Capital (2011–2015):** CEO of the investment banking arm of Banque Saudi Fransi. Also served on the board of the Saudi Stock Exchange (Tadawul).
- **PIF (2015–present):** Appointed Governor in September 2015. Transformed PIF from a passive holding company into the world's most active sovereign wealth fund in gaming and entertainment. Assets grew from ~$150B to $930B+.
- **Savvy Games Group (2022–present):** Chairs the PIF subsidiary with a $38B gaming investment mandate. Oversees Savvy's portfolio including Scopely (acquired 2023), ESL FACEIT Group, Steer Studios, and the Niantic games portfolio (Pokémon Go, et al., acquired 2025).
- **Key board seats:** Saudi Aramco (Chairman), SoftBank Group, Savvy Games Group, Newcastle United (Chairman), Riyadh Air (Chairman), LIV Golf, Neom.
- **EA take-private (2025):** PIF was the lead LP in the Silver Lake consortium, deepening the Kingdom's direct exposure to Western AAA gaming.

## Key relationships

- Close ally of Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman — his rise paralleled MbS's consolidation of power
- Works alongside Brian Ward (CEO, Savvy Games Group)
- EA deal partners: Egon Durban (Silver Lake), Mike Bingle (Silver Lake)
- Connected to Masayoshi Son (SoftBank), Patrick Pouyanné (TotalEnergies), Elon Musk (via Uber board)
- Brother-in-law Mazin Al-Kahmous heads Saudi Arabia's Oversight and Anti-Corruption Authority
