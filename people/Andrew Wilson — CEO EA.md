# Andrew Wilson — CEO, Electronic Arts

**CEO of EA since 2013.** The anchor leader of the map. Under his tenure, EA went from a $7.4–7.6B stagnant-revenue public company to a **~$52.5B PE take-private** (agreed 2025-09-29).

## Tenure ledger (big decisions, date-stamped)
- **2013** — Became EA CEO.
- **2024** — EA planned to **cut 5% of its workforce**.
- **2025-05** — Closed **several hundred positions**, including **~100 at Respawn (L.A.)**, as part of "long-term strategic priorities."
- **2025-09-29** — Agreed to the **take-private** in the **~$52.5B** (NBC/AP) / **~$55B** (LA Times) deal.
- **Revenue context:** stagnant **$7.4–7.6B** over the last 3 fiscal years; **Q1 FY2025 $1.67B net revenue** (+1% YoY).
