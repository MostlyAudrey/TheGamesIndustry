# Emma Kinema

**Founding organizer of game worker labor movement.** Emma Kinema worked as a QA tester before co-founding Game Workers Unite (GWU) at the 2018 Game Developers Conference. She was hired by the CWA in January 2020 to lead its first-ever initiative to organize video game and tech workers — CODE-CWA.

## Career

- **Late 2010s:** QA tester in video game industry
- **2018:** Co-founded Game Workers Unite — grassroots labor advocacy group targeting game industry
- **2020-01:** Hired by CWA as full-time staff to lead CODE-CWA campaign

## Key role in gaming labor

Kinema is one of the most important figures in the history of video game unionization:
- **Game Workers Unite:** Founded the first major grassroots organization dedicated to game industry unionization
- **CODE-CWA:** First dedicated union campaign for video game and tech workers in the United States
- **Organizing record:** CODE-CWA organized thousands of workers across dozens of workplaces including Raven Software's Game Workers Alliance (first union at a major US game publisher), ZeniMax Workers United (first Microsoft union), and the massive Activision QA United drive
