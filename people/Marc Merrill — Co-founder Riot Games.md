# Marc Merrill

Marc "Tryndamere" Merrill is the co-founder, co-chairman, and Chief Product Officer of [[orgs/Riot Games]]. Along with Brandon Beck, he co-founded Riot in 2006 driven by a vision to revolutionize game development by putting players at the center of everything.

## Career Arc

- Attended University of Southern California (1998-2002)
- Worked as an analyst at U.S. Bank before founding Riot
- Co-founded Riot Games in 2006 with Brandon Beck
- Co-led the development and launch of League of Legends (2009)
- Served as co-CEO alongside Brandon Beck until 2017
- Transitioned to Chief Product Officer role, overseeing product direction and vision across Riot's portfolio
- In-game namesake champion "Tryndamere" appears in League of Legends
- Emmy-winning Arcane series was produced under his and Beck's leadership

## Key Decisions

- Co-creator of League of Legends; drove gameplay vision and community engagement
- Championed the free-to-play, regularly updated live service model
- Led the transition from single-game company to multi-game publisher including Valorant, Teamfight Tactics, and new fighting game projects
- Advocates for "player-focused" development philosophy

## Labor Context

- As co-founder, was part of leadership that established Riot's original culture, which was later criticized
- Maintained active community presence, including on Reddit forums
- Co-chairman during the 2018-2021 workplace crisis period
- Riot remains non-union
- Has spoken about improving company culture post-crisis
