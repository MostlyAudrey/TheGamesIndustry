# Mark Pincus
Internet entrepreneur who founded Zynga, the social gaming giant that defined Facebook-era casual games. Despite Zynga's decline, its acquisition by Take-Two for $12.7B validated the mobile gaming market.

## Career arc
- **2007:** Founded Zynga. Created FarmVille, CityVille, Words With Friends.
- **2011:** Zynga IPO. Peak valuation ~$10B.
- **2013:** Stepped down as CEO; returned briefly in 2015.
- **2022:** Take-Two acquired Zynga for $12.7B.
