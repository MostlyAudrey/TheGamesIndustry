# Cory Barlog — God of War Director

**The creative force who reinvented a flagship franchise.** Barlog directed the original God of War (2005), God of War II (2007), and returned to direct the 2018 God of War reboot — one of the most critically acclaimed games of the generation.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** Worked in film and visual effects before entering game development.
- **2005:** Directed God of War at Santa Monica Studio — established Kratos as a PlayStation icon.
- **2007:** Directed God of War II — widely considered one of the best action games ever made.
- **2008:** Left Sony for the film industry. Worked at an animation studio and on HBO's Game of Thrones.
- **2013:** Returned to Santa Monica Studio.
- **2014–2017:** Led the development of God of War (2018), a full franchise reinvention — shifting from Greek to Norse mythology, introducing a new combat system (the Leviathan Axe), and reframing Kratos as a father.
- **2018 (April):** God of War (2018) released to universal acclaim. Won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2018.
- **2022:** God of War: Ragnarök — conclusion of the Norse saga. Also critically acclaimed, commercially massive.
- **2024–2026:** Working on the next God of War project. Barlog is now Creative Director of Game Development at Santa Monica Studio, overseeing the studio's creative direction.

- **One-shot camera:** God of War (2018) used a continuous "one-shot" camera technique (no cuts from title screen to end credits) — a bold narrative choice that influenced other games.
- **Narrative reinvention:** Turning Kratos from a rage-filled antihero to a nuanced father figure is one of the most successful character transformations in gaming history.
- **The "Dad of War" phenomenon:** The father-son relationship between Kratos and Atreus resonated broadly with audiences and critics.

## Labour context

- **Open about crunch:** Barlog is one of the few AAA directors who has publicly discussed the personal cost of game development. A video of his young daughter crying "Daddy, when are you done with the video game?" went viral during God of War (2018)'s production.
- He has stated that the industry needs to do better about work-life balance and that crunch is not sustainable.
- Santa Monica Studio is a Sony first-party studio with professional management structures, but like virtually all AAA studios, it has no union representation.
- Barlog has publicly expressed support for the idea of better working conditions while not specifically endorsing unionization.
- The 2023–2024 industry layoff wave affected Sony (including 900+ SIE layoffs, the closure of [[orgs/London Studio]]) but Santa Monica Studio was reportedly spared from major cuts.
