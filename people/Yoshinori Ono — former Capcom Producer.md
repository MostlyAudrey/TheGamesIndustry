# Yoshinori Ono

Yoshinori Ono is a Japanese video game producer and former brand manager, best known as the driving force behind the Street Fighter revival at [[orgs/Capcom]]. He joined Capcom in 1993 and left the company in 2020.

## Career Arc

- Joined Capcom in 1993 as a sound producer and composer
- Worked on sound for various Capcom titles including Street Fighter II
- Became producer for the Street Fighter franchise
- Led the development of Street Fighter IV (2008), which revived the fighting game genre
- Produced Street Fighter V (2016) and its ongoing content updates
- Also produced Marvel vs. Capcom 3, Street Fighter X Tekken, and Darkstalkers Resurrection
- Became brand manager for Capcom's fighting games
- Left Capcom in 2020 under reportedly strained circumstances
- His departure was connected to Street Fighter V's mixed reception and internal conflicts
- After Capcom, joined del.icio.us (a game development company)

## Key Decisions

- Drove the revival of Street Fighter IV — a landmark fighting game that saved the genre
- Known for his flamboyant public persona ("Ono-san") and strong community engagement
- His departure was widely covered and symbolized the end of an era at Capcom

## Labor Context

- No direct labor controversies
- Left Capcom during a period of internal restructuring
- Eurogamer's "The Rise and Collapse of Yoshinori Ono" documented the pressures he faced
