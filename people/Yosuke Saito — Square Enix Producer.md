# Yosuke Saito

Yosuke Saito is a Japanese video game producer, director, and executive at [[orgs/Square Enix]]. He is a Board Member, Executive Officer, and Executive Producer, known for his work on the NieR series and Dragon Quest X Online.

## Career Arc

- Currently serves as Member of the Board, Executive Officer, and Executive Producer at Square Enix
- Previously Director of Square Enix Creative Business Unit II: Division 4 (under the old structure)
- Now leads Creative Studio 4 under the 2024 restructuring
- Studio head Takeshi Nozue works alongside him

## Notable Games

- Producer on the NieR series (NieR Replicant, NieR: Automata) — collaborated with Yoko Taro and PlatinumGames
- Producer on Dragon Quest X Online
- Has worked on various other Square Enix titles
- Known for close collaboration with external studios and IP partnerships

## Key Decisions

- Championed the NieR franchise, which grew from a cult hit into one of Square Enix's most beloved series
- Advocated for cross-media and external studio collaborations
- Continues to drive Square Enix's RPG portfolio

## Labor Context

- No major labor controversies personally associated
- Japanese game industry generally non-union
