# Todd Howard — Director and Executive Producer, Bethesda Game Studios

**The creative face of Bethesda Game Studios for over 30 years.** Howard is one of the few game developers whose name carries the same weight as the brands he works on. As the director of The Elder Scrolls, Fallout, and Starfield, he has shaped the open-world RPG genre more than any other living designer.

## Career arc

- **1994:** Joined Bethesda — started as a producer on The Elder Scrolls: Arena.
- **1995:** Project leader on Daggerfall — the ambitious sequel that laid the groundwork for the series' future.
- **2002:** Lead on Morrowind — the game that transformed The Elder Scrolls from a cult series to an industry force.
- **2006:** Executive producer on Oblivion — brought voice acting, Radiant AI, and cinematic presentation to the series.
- **2008:** Game director on Fallout 3 — revived Fallout as a first-person open-world RPG and established Bethesda's ownership of the franchise.
- **2011:** Director on Skyrim — the defining game of his career. Won numerous Game of the Year awards, sold 60M+ copies, and remains a cultural phenomenon.
- **2015:** Executive producer on Fallout 4 — simplified RPG mechanics but massive commercial success.
- **2018:** Executive producer on Fallout 76 — the studio's first multiplayer game; critically panned at launch but improved over time.
- **2023:** Director on Starfield — Bethesda's first new IP since 1998's The Elder Scrolls: Redguard. Met with mixed reviews but strong sales.
- **Present:** Working on The Elder Scrolls VI and ongoing Starfield development.

## Creative philosophy

Howard is known for:
- **"Big, handcrafted worlds"** — emphasis on environmental storytelling and player freedom
- **Bethesda Game Engine (Creation Engine)** — a heavily modified version of the Gamebryo engine, upgraded for each title
- **Modding support** — Bethesda games have historically embraced the modding community
- **"16 times the detail"** — his enthusiastic public presentations have become internet memes

## Post-Microsoft acquisition

After Microsoft's acquisition of ZeniMax (2021), Howard remained in his creative role at Bethesda Game Studios. Starfield was the first Howard-directed game released under Microsoft ownership. The game's console exclusivity to Xbox/PC was a significant strategic move for Microsoft Gaming.
