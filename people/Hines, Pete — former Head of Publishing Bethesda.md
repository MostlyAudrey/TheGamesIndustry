# Pete Hines — Former Head of Publishing, Bethesda Softworks

**The public face of Bethesda for 24 years (1999-2023).** As SVP of Global Marketing and later Head of Publishing, Hines was the most recognizable Bethesda executive after Todd Howard. His retirement after Starfield's launch marked the end of an era.

## Career arc

- **1999:** Joined Bethesda Softworks as SVP of Global Marketing and Communications — the company was still a small operation.
- **2000s:** Oversaw marketing for the breakout success of The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind and the Bethesda-published lineup.
- **2008-2015:** The golden era — marketing campaigns for Fallout 3, Skyrim, Fallout 4, and the launch of id Software titles under Bethesda publishing.
- **2022-10:** Promoted to Head of Publishing — overseeing all publishing operations at Bethesda Softworks.
- **2023-10:** Announced retirement, effective by end of 2023.

## Communication style

Hines was known for his candid, often humorous social media presence and willingness to engage directly with fans. He was the primary conduit between Bethesda and its community, delivering news about delays, announcements, and franchise updates. His departure was seen as a significant loss of institutional knowledge.

## Post-Microsoft era

Hines' retirement came after Microsoft's ZeniMax acquisition was fully integrated. His final major project was the marketing campaign for Starfield — Bethesda's biggest launch under Microsoft ownership.
