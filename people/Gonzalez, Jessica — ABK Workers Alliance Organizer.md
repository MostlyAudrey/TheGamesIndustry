# Jessica Gonzalez

**Grassroots organizer who sparked unionization at Activision Blizzard.** Jessica Gonzalez (born 1992 or 1993) worked as a senior test analyst at Activision Blizzard before becoming one of the most prominent worker advocates in the video game industry.

## Career

- **Pre-2021:** Senior test analyst at Activision Blizzard (QA department)
- **2021:** Founded A Better ABK — internal worker group at Activision Blizzard, focused on improving workplace culture and advocating for unionization
- **2021:** Co-founded ABK Workers Alliance — solidarity union for Activision Blizzard workers
- **2021-12:** Left Activision Blizzard as employee
- **2022:** Joined CODE-CWA as full-time organizer
- **2022:** Co-founded Game Workers Alliance — Raven Software QA union (CWA affiliate)
- **2022:** Was organizing support for workers during the DFEH lawsuit fallout

## Key role in gaming labor

Gonzalez was a key figure in the earliest stage of the wave of game worker unionization:
- **A Better ABK** (2021): First major internal worker advocacy group at Activision Blizzard, predating formal unionization
- **ABK Workers Alliance:** Broad solidarity union for workers across Activision Blizzard
- **Game Workers Alliance:** Co-founded the Raven Software QA union that won the first union certification at a major US game publisher
- **Transition to CODE-CWA:** Brought grassroots organizing expertise to CWA's professional campaign
