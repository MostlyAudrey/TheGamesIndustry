# Tony Chen (Chen Yidan)

Note: The task lists "Tony Chen" as Vice President of Tencent Games. There is no current "Tony Chen" at that title. The closest Tencent figures are: **Chen Yidan (Charles Chen)**, co-founder of Tencent and Advisor Emeritus, and **Zhang Zhidong (Tony Zhang)**, co-founder and former CTO. Neither currently serves as VP of Tencent Games. This file covers Chen Yidan, who is likely the intended "Tony Chen."

## Career Arc

- Co-founded [[orgs/Tencent]] in 1998 alongside Ma Huateng, Zhang Zhidong, Xu Chenye, and Zeng Liqing
- Served as Chief Administrative Officer and oversaw HR, legal, and administrative functions
- Stepped down from daily management in 2013 and transitioned to Advisor Emeritus
- Founder and Honorary Chairman of the Tencent Charity Foundation
- Has focused primarily on philanthropy through the Yidan Prize for education research

## Tencent Games Connection

Not directly involved in Tencent Games operations or strategy. Chen Yidan's role was in corporate administration and later philanthropy.

## Labor Context

- No personal labor controversies
- Part of founding team that established Tencent's corporate culture, which has faced scrutiny over "996" work culture
