# Chris Shelton

**CWA President who drove historic game worker organizing.** Christopher M. Shelton grew up in the Bronx, began working for New York Telephone in 1968 as an outside technician, and rose through union ranks over five decades.

## Career

- **1968:** Started at New York Telephone as outside technician; joined CWA
- **1988:** Began working full-time for the national CWA union
- **2005:** Elected vice-president of CWA District 1
- **2015:** Elected CWA President — succeeded Larry Cohen
- **2016:** Led 40,000-member Verizon strike
- **2020:** Launched CODE-CWA to organize tech and game workers
- **2022-06-13:** Negotiated Microsoft labor neutrality agreement — a first for the games industry
- **2023:** Retired

## Key role in gaming labor

Under Shelton's leadership, CWA made its largest-ever push into video game and tech worker organizing:
- **CODE-CWA** (2020): Dedicated campaign to organize digital employees, hiring Emma Kinema and Wes McEnany as lead organizers
- **Microsoft neutrality agreement** (2022): Unprecedented deal that removed company opposition to unionization at Microsoft gaming subsidiaries
- **Legacy:** Set the stage for all subsequent Microsoft/CWA game unions (ZeniMax, Activision QA, Blizzard coalition, etc.)
