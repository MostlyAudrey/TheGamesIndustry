# Jonathan Lai — a16z Games Lead

Jonathan Lai is a partner at Andreessen Horowitz overseeing the firm's dedicated Games fund — totaling **>$1.2B** across two funds. He is the primary architect of a16z's aggressive push into gaming venture capital, making it one of the most active gaming VCs in Silicon Valley.

## Career arc

- **Background:** Previously an investor and operator in gaming and consumer tech. Holds deep expertise in game economies, Web3/gaming convergence, and live-service monetization.
- **a16z (2021–present):** Joined as partner focused on gaming. Led the firm to raise Games Fund One ($600M, 2022) and Games Fund Two ($600M, 2024). Created the a16z Games Speedrun accelerator program.
- **Key investments:** Mythical Games (blockchain gaming infrastructure), Bonfire Studios (founded by ex-Blizzard developers), and other portfolio companies across US/Canada.
- **Thesis:** Games as platforms, creator economies in gaming, Web3/gaming intersection, and the rise of user-generated content games.

## Key relationships

- Works alongside Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz (founding partners)
- Oversees a16z's gaming team alongside other consumer partners
- Invests alongside Troy Kirwin (a16z games partner in London)
- Portfolio relationships with Mythical Games, Bonfire Studios CEOs
