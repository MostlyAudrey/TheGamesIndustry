# Strauss Zelnick — CEO, Take-Two Interactive

American businessman and lawyer. Founder/CEO of ZMC (private equity), Chairman/CEO of Take-Two Interactive (Rockstar, 2K, Zynga), former chairman of CBS Corporation. One of the most powerful crossover figures between traditional media, private equity, and gaming.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BA from Wesleyan University; MBA and JD from Harvard University.
- **Early career:** Worked at 20th Century Fox, then President/CEO of BMG Entertainment.
- **Crystal Dynamics:** President and CEO — took the studio through its Tomb Raider era.
- **ZMC (1999–present):** Founded media-focused private equity firm.
- **Take-Two (2007–present):** Chairman (2007), Executive Chairman (2008), CEO (2011–present).
- **CBS Corporation:** Former chairman — media network overlap.

## Key relationships

- Reports to Take-Two board (which he chairs)
- Works directly with Sam Houser (Rockstar president) and studio heads across 2K
- ZMC portfolio includes media companies — potential cross-industry connections
- Oversaw Zynga acquisition integration — absorbed Zynga leadership
