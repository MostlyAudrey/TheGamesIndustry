# Sarah Bond — President, Xbox

**President of Xbox since November 2023.** Bond oversees Xbox as a platform and ecosystem — including hardware, devices, player experiences, platform engineering, business strategy, and developer partnerships. She is the first Black woman to serve as president of a major gaming platform company.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BA in Molecular Biology from Williams College; MBA from Harvard Business School.
- **Early career:** Management consultant at McKinsey & Company; strategy roles at T-Mobile and The Wonderful Company.
- **2018:** Joined Microsoft as Corporate Vice President for Gaming Partnerships and Business Development.
- **2020s:** Rose to CVP of Game Creator Experience & Ecosystem, Xbox — leading developer tools, platform services, and game content partnerships.
- **2023-11:** Named President of Xbox, reporting to Phil Spencer, following the Activision Blizzard acquisition restructure.
- **2024-2026:** Led platform integration of Activision Blizzard's games and services into Xbox ecosystem.

## Key responsibilities

As President of Xbox, Bond manages:
- Xbox hardware and devices (consoles, accessories)
- Player and creator experiences (store, services, features)
- Platform engineering and infrastructure
- Strategy, business planning, data and analytics
- Business development and partnerships

## Labor context

Bond's role focuses on platform operations rather than studio management. While she does not directly oversee unionized workforces, her platform decisions (store policies, revenue sharing, developer agreements) shape the conditions in which unionized and non-union studios operate. She has been described internally as a pragmatic operator focused on execution and growth.

## Post-Microsoft departure

In 2026, Bond departed Microsoft and joined the board of Tyson Foods, suggesting a transition out of gaming leadership. She currently serves as a board director at Tyson Foods while listed as "Former President, Xbox."
