# David Ko

**Role:** Former COO, Zynga  
**Orgs:** [[Zynga]], [[Yahoo!]], [[Rally Health]], [[Ripple Health Group]]

## Career Arc

David Ko is an American technology executive who served as COO of Zynga. He began his career at **Yahoo!**, where he spent 10 years in senior executive roles, including SVP of Yahoo!'s audience, mobile, and local businesses.

He joined Zynga and was promoted to COO in November 2012, overseeing strategy, planning, and operations. He helped take Zynga public in December 2011 — at the time, the largest social gaming company in the world with 100M+ users. Ko championed Zynga's push into mobile and the transformation of its Facebook-oriented franchises (*FarmVille*, *Mafia Wars*) into multiplatform experiences.

After Zynga, Ko moved into healthcare technology as President and COO at **Rally Health**, and later co-founded **Ripple Health Group**, serving as its CEO.

## Key Decisions

- **Championed Zynga's mobile transformation:** Led the company's pivot from Facebook-first to multiplatform.
- **Helped take Zynga public:** Played a key operational role in the 2011 IPO.
- **Transitioned to health tech:** Applied gaming engagement principles to healthcare technology.

## Labor Context

Ko was part of Zynga's executive team during its most turbulent period — the post-IPO stock decline, restructuring, and layoffs. His shift from gaming to health tech reflects a broader pattern of gaming executives applying engagement mechanics to other industries.
