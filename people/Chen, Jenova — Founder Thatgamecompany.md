# Jenova Chen — Founder, Thatgamecompany

**The most influential voice in "art games" and emotional game design.** Chen (born Xinhan Chen, known professionally as Jenova Chen) founded Thatgamecompany and created Flow, Flower, and Journey — the latter routinely named one of the greatest games of all time.

## Career arc

- **2000 (education):** Bachelor's in computer science, minor in digital art/design at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
- **2005 (US):** Master's from USC's Interactive Media Division. Created Cloud and Flow as student projects.
- **2006:** Worked briefly at Maxis (Spore). Co-founded Thatgamecompany with Kellee Santiago.
- **2006–2007:** Signed exclusive three-game deal with Sony for PSN releases.
- **2007:** Released Flow on PSN.
- **2009:** Released Flower — critically acclaimed meditative game.
- **2012:** Released Journey — breakthrough critical success, 30+ Game of the Year awards, Grammy nomination for score.
- **2019:** Released Sky: Children of the Light on iOS (later Android, Switch, PC). Live-service social MMO exploring emotional connection.
- **Advisor** to Annapurna Interactive.
- **Ongoing:** Sky continues active development. Chen has discussed future Thatgamecompany projects.

## Key games

| Game | Year | Platform | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cloud | 2005 | PC (student) | Emotional exploration in game form |
| Flow | 2006 | PS3/PSP | Fluid motion, physics-based gameplay |
| Flower | 2009 | PS3/PS4 | Emotional narrative through environment |
| Journey | 2012 | PS3/PS4/PC | Landmark emotional game; GOTY awards; Grammy nomination |
| Sky: Children of the Light | 2019 | iOS/Android/Switch/PC | Live-service emotional MMO |

- **Art-game pioneer:** Chen's work argues that games can evoke emotional depth comparable to film and literature — a thesis that influenced the entire "indie boom" of the 2010s.
- **Minimalist design:** Journey's anonymous multiplayer (players meet strangers whose identity is unknown) was a groundbreaking design choice.
- **Live-service without exploitation:** Sky monetizes through cosmetic purchases in a way Chen has argued is ethical — no gambling mechanics (loot boxes), no pay-to-win.

## Labour context

- Thatgamecompany is a small, independent studio with roughly 30–40 staff.
- The studio is headquartered in Los Angeles (no union presence).
- Chen has advocated for sustainable game development practices. The studio avoided the worst of industry-wide 2023–2024 layoffs.
- Sky: Children of the Light's live-service model creates ongoing employment, contrasting with AAA studios that hire temporarily for a single title.
