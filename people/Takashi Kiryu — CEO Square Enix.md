# Takashi Kiryu

Takashi Kiryu (born June 20, 1975) is the President and Representative Director of [[orgs/Square Enix|Square Enix Holdings]], becoming the company's CEO in 2023. He represents a new generation of leadership, having joined the company only in 2020 from outside the game development business.

## Career Arc

- Career background outside Square Enix; previously held executive roles at other companies
- Joined Square Enix Holdings in June 2020 as General Manager of the Corporate Planning Division
- Appointed Chief Strategy Officer in April 2021
- Became President and Representative Director (CEO) in June 2023, succeeding Yosuke Matsuda
- An unconventional choice — Kiryu is a corporate strategist rather than a game developer, signalling Square Enix's focus on business restructuring and efficiency

## Key Decisions

- Led the restructuring of Square Enix's development operations in 2024, moving from the Creative Business Unit structure to a new Creative Studio structure
- Announced a shift toward multiplatform releases (ending PlayStation exclusivity periods)
- Focused on improving profit margins and operational efficiency
- Oversaw the integration of acquired studios
- Navigated the company's evolving relationship with Microsoft and Nintendo platforms

## Labor Context

- Part of leadership during a period of restructuring and organizational change at Square Enix
- No major labor controversies personally associated
- Japanese game industry generally non-union
- Square Enix has faced some crunch-related concerns in the past
