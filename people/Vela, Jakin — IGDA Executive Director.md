# Jakin Vela

**Current Executive Director of the International Game Developers Association (IGDA),** the world's largest nonprofit membership organization for video game developers.

## Career

- **2022–present:** IGDA Executive Director
- Successor to Renée Gittins (2019–2022) and Jennifer MacLean (2017–2019)

## Key role in gaming labor

As IGDA ED during 2022–2026, Vela leads the organization through:
- The largest wave of unionization in game industry history
- Record layoffs (10,500 in 2023; 14,600 in 2024)
- Microsoft/CWA neutrality agreement implementation
- Industry-wide restructuring and studio closures
- IGDA's role: Research, advocacy, and professional support — not collective bargaining (non-union organization)
