# Swen Vincke

Belgian video game designer and programmer. Founder and CEO of Larian Studios (1996–present). Most famous for Baldur's Gate 3 (2023), one of the most critically and commercially successful RPGs ever made.

Career:
- Founded Larian Studios in 1996 in Ghent, Belgium
- Self-funded for years, making small fantasy RPGs
- Created Divinity series (Divine Divinity 2002, Divinity: Original Sin 2014)
- Divinity: Original Sin 2 (2017) was a breakthrough critical hit
- Baldur's Gate 3 (2023) sold 20M+ copies, won hundreds of GOTY awards
- Larian grew to 530+ employees across 7 global studios

## Labor context

Vincke is notable for his vocal commitment to ethical game development:
- No layoffs in Larian's history — rare in AAA
- Consistent anti-crunch messaging (though BG3 still had some crunch periods)
- No union presence (European labor protections cover most Larian sites)
- Rejected publisher acquisition offers to maintain independence
- Walked away from D&D license after BG3 to return to own Divinity IP
- Tencent owns a small, non-controlling minority stake

## Industry influence

Vincke is widely respected as an independent voice in AAA game development. His success with BG3 demonstrated that deep, complex RPGs can find massive audiences. His public stances on ethical development, crunch, and publisher relationships have made him a counterweight to industry consolidation trends.
