# Frank Gibeau

**Role:** CEO, Zynga (since 2020); Former EVP, EA Mobile  
**Orgs:** [[Zynga]], [[Electronic Arts]], [[Take-Two Interactive]], [[Hasbro]] (board)

## Career Arc

Frank Gibeau is a mobile, PC, and console gaming veteran with 25+ years in interactive entertainment. He spent over two decades at **Electronic Arts**, most recently as Executive Vice President of EA Mobile, leading strategy, product development, and publishing for mobile platforms. At EA, he also oversaw the EA Partners program and several major franchise teams.

Gibeau joined **Zynga** in 2020 as CEO. In May 2022, he led Zynga through its pivotal $12.7B combination with **Take-Two Interactive**, positioning Zynga as Take-Two's mobile division. He also serves on the board of Hasbro.

## Key Decisions

- **Led EA Mobile:** Oversaw the mobile strategy for EA's portfolio of titles.
- **Joined Zynga as CEO (2020):** Took the helm to revitalize and grow Zynga's mobile portfolio.
- **Orchestrated Take-Two acquisition (2022):** Led Zynga's $12.7B sale to Take-Two, creating a major mobile+console gaming powerhouse.
- **Built Zynga's live services:** Invested in live-ops and franchise management for mature mobile titles.

## Labor Context

Gibeau took over Zynga during a period of industry consolidation and mobile gaming maturation. His role in the Take-Two acquisition affected thousands of employees across studios, typical of gaming M&A. The mobile gaming workforce under Gibeau has faced ongoing industry-wide challenges around job security amid consolidation.
