# Matthew Bromberg — CEO, Unity Technologies

Current CEO and President of Unity Technologies since May 2024. Took over after the Riccitiello/Runtime Fee crisis and interim leadership of James Whitehurst.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** AOL, Major League Gaming — early roles in digital media and gaming.
- **EA:** Senior Vice President of Strategy — worked on The Sims and other EA franchises.
- **Zynga (2018–2024):** Chief Operating Officer — managed global game studios, played key role in company turnaround. Oversaw mobile game operations including Words With Friends, Zynga Poker.
- **2024:** Appointed Unity CEO and President.

## CEO context

Bromberg inherited a company in crisis — Unity's stock had declined ~60% over the prior year following the Runtime Fee backlash, developer trust was at a low, and the company had just undergone 1,800 layoffs (Jan 2024). His first major act was cancelling the Runtime Fee entirely (Sep 2024). He focused on stabilizing the core business, increasing subscription prices, and pushing deeper into non-gaming sectors (automotive, film, construction) — a strategy Riccitiello had begun.

## Key relationships

- Reports to Unity board (chaired by Roelof Botha of Sequoia Capital)
- Preceded by James Whitehurst (interim CEO after Riccitiello)
- Worked with Mark Skaggs (Zynga CPO) and other Zynga leadership
- Previously reported to Andrew Wilson at EA
