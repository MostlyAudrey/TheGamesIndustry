# Koji Kondo — Senior Officer, Nintendo EPD

The most influential video game composer in history. Kondo's music for Super Mario Bros. and The Legend of Zelda is recognized worldwide — the Super Mario Bros. theme became the first video game music entered into the U.S. National Recording Registry. He is also a Senior Officer at Nintendo EPD, making him one of the most senior creative executives at the company.

## Career arc

- **1984:** Hired by Nintendo — first dedicated composer at the company. Audio design for Punch-Out!! arcade.
- **1985:** Super Mario Bros. — composes the most iconic video game theme ever created.
- **1986:** First Legend of Zelda game — overworld and dungeon themes.
- **1988:** Super Mario Bros. 3.
- **1990:** Super Mario World.
- **1996:** Super Mario 64 — his first fully 3D game score.
- **1998:** Ocarina of Time — last game he scored alone.
- **2000s–present:** Supervises sound for Nintendo projects, mentoring younger composers.
- **2015:** Promoted to Senior Officer at Nintendo EPD.

## Musical legacy

Kondo's work defined the sound of video games for multiple generations. His approach — short, endlessly-repeating musical segments that don't cause boredom during gameplay — became the template for game music. His Latin and jazz-influenced Super Mario Bros. theme and the adventurous Zelda overworld theme are cultural landmarks.
