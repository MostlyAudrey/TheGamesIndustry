# Hermen Hulst — CEO, Sony Interactive Entertainment Studio Business Group

Dutch video game executive who co-founded Guerrilla Games and led it from a small Dutch studio to one of Europe's most acclaimed developers. Became head of Worldwide Studios/PlayStation Studios in 2019, then co-CEO of SIE in 2024, responsible for all first-party game development, IP expansion into film/TV, and PC strategy.

## Career arc

- **1995:** Internship at Ubisoft (US) — worked on Rayman marketing.
- **1996–2001:** Marketing at Philips, then management consulting at Andersen Consulting.
- **2001:** Co-founds Guerrilla Games (as Lost Boys Games); becomes Managing Director.
- **2005:** Sony acquires Guerrilla Games for an undisclosed sum.
- **2004–2013:** Leads Killzone franchise across PS2, PS3, PS4.
- **2011:** Appointed VP of Worldwide Studios under Shuhei Yoshida.
- **2017:** Launches Horizon Zero Dawn — Guerrilla's breakthrough IP.
- **2019:** Succeeds Shuhei Yoshida as Head of Worldwide Studios.
- **2022:** Horizon Forbidden West launches to acclaim.
- **2024:** Becomes CEO of SIE Studio Business Group (co-CEO with Hideaki Nishino).
- **2025:** Nishino becomes solo SIE CEO; Hulst continues running Studio Business Group.

## Key strategic moves

- Shifted PlayStation from console-sales focus to multiplatform IP: PC ports of first-party games
- Expanded PlayStation Productions film/TV adaptations (The Last of Us HBO, Uncharted film, Twisted Metal, Gran Turismo, Horizon Netflix)
- Continued aggressive studio acquisitions
- Pushed live-service games (Helldivers 2 success, Concord failure)

## Labour context

Hulst's tenure as head of first-party studios coincided with significant industry-wide layoffs. Multiple PlayStation studios experienced reductions, restructuring, and closures. The Feb 2024 SIE layoff of 900 people (8% of workforce) included the closure of London Studio entirely. Critics note the contrast between Sony's premium-priced games and repeated job cuts.
