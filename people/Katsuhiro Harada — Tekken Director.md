# Katsuhiro Harada

Katsuhiro Harada (born June 10, 1971) is a Japanese game director and producer, best known as the creator and longtime director of the Tekken fighting game series at [[orgs/Bandai Namco|Bandai Namco Entertainment]]. After 31 years, he left Bandai Namco at the end of 2025 and became CEO of SNK VS Studio in 2026.

## Career Arc

- Joined Namco in 1994 (later merged into Bandai Namco)
- Worked on the original Tekken (1994) and led the series from Tekken 3 (1997) onward
- Became the face of the Tekken franchise, known for his community engagement and social media presence
- Directed/co-produced Tekken Tag Tournament, Tekken 5, Tekken 6, Tekken 7, and Tekken 8
- Also contributed to Soulcalibur and other Namco fighting games
- Left Bandai Namco in December 2025 after 31 years
- Founded SNK VS Studio in 2026 as CEO, in partnership with SNK Corporation
- Former Tekken 8 director Kohei Ikeda joined Harada's new studio

## Key Decisions

- Drove Tekken 7 (2015) to massive success, reviving the fighting game franchise
- Pushed for cross-platform play, Western tournament integration, and aggressive DLC/crossover character strategy
- Memorable: the "Harada's Bar" series of developer communication
- Decision to leave Bandai Namco was a major industry story in 2025

## Labor Context

- No major labor controversies personally associated
- Japanese game industry generally non-union
- Bandai Namco has faced broader labor concerns
