# Mohammed bin Salman — Crown Prince of Saudi Arabia

Mohammed bin Salman Al Saud (MbS) is the de facto ruler of Saudi Arabia. As Crown Prince and Prime Minister, he is the ultimate authority behind the Kingdom's massive investment in the global video game industry through the Public Investment Fund and Savvy Games Group.

## Career arc

- **Education:** Law degree from King Saud University (graduated second in class). Brief private sector work before becoming advisor to his father.
- **Rise to power (2015–2017):** Appointed Defense Minister and Deputy Crown Prince in 2015. Led Saudi intervention in Yemen. Elevated to Crown Prince in June 2017 after deposing his cousin Muhammad bin Nayef.
- **Consolidation (2017–2019):** Led sweeping purge of competing Saudi elites, detaining hundreds in the Ritz-Carlton. Consolidated control over PIF, Saudi Aramco, all security services. Seized up to $800B in assets.
- **Prime Minister (2022–present):** Appointed PM in September 2022. Launched Vision 2030 to reduce oil dependence.
- **Gaming strategy:** Under his direction, PIF has become the single most powerful capital force in gaming — with direct stakes in Nintendo, EA, Take-Two, Activision Blizzard (pre-Microsoft), and full ownership of Scopely, Savvy Games Group, and the Niantic games portfolio. The PIF's $38B gaming mandate aims to make Saudi Arabia a global gaming hub.

## Gaming industry significance

MbS's Vision 2030 identified gaming as a pillar of economic diversification. The Kingdom has deployed more state capital into the global gaming industry than any other entity in history, including:

- **Savvy Games Group:** State-owned holding company with $38B mandate
- **Scopely:** Full ownership ($4.9B, 2023)
- **Niantic games portfolio:** Including Pokémon Go (2025)
- **Nintendo:** 5%+ stake
- **EA:** Major stake; anchor LP in Silver Lake take-private
- **Embracer Group:** $1B for 8% stake (2022)
- **ESL FACEIT Group:** Full ownership of the world's largest esports company

## Key relationships

- Directly controls and directs Yasir Al-Rumayyan (PIF Governor) and Brian Ward (Savvy CEO)
- Close relationship with Donald Trump and Jared Kushner — influenced US-Saudi policy
- Egon Durban (Silver Lake) — partnered on EA deal
- Masayoshi Son (SoftBank) — PIF is largest LP in SoftBank Vision Fund
- Architect of 2026 Iran war — reshaping Middle East geopolitics
