# Leanne Loombe

**Role:** VP of External Games, Netflix  
**Orgs:** [[Netflix]], [[Riot Games]], [[Electronic Arts]]

## Career Arc

Leanne Loombe is the Vice President of External Games at Netflix, responsible for all games created by external partners and creators. Before Netflix, she created and oversaw **Riot Forge**, the external publishing division of Riot Games, which produced a variety of *League of Legends* story games with external developers.

Before Riot, Loombe worked at **Electronic Arts**, bridging her love of cars and games — likely on the *Need for Speed* franchise or similar automotive titles. She joined Netflix in 2021 and managed a portfolio of over 150 games, working with developers worldwide to bring titles to a platform that had never done gaming before.

## Key Decisions

- **Created Riot Forge:** Built Riot's external publishing program for *League of Legends* universe games.
- **Joined Netflix Games (2021):** Key hire alongside Mike Verdu to build Netflix's gaming capabilities.
- **Scaled external game portfolio:** Managed relationships with dozens of independent studios worldwide.
- **Focused on authentic IP adaptations:** Emphasized games capturing the spirit of Netflix shows and films.

## Labor Context

Loombe's role at Netflix involves managing relationships with external developers, placing her at the center of labor dynamics between platform holders and independent studios. Her experience at Riot Forge provides insight into how major IP holders collaborate with smaller developers — a critical labor relationship in modern gaming.
