# Yves Guillemot

French businessman. Co-founder and CEO of Ubisoft, one of the largest video game publishers in the world (1986–present). Born in Carentoir, France in 1960. One of five Guillemot brothers who built the company.

Career:
- Co-founded Ubisoft with four brothers in 1986
- Built Ubisoft from a small French distributor to a global publisher
- Expanded through the 2000s with internal studio growth and acquisitions
- Created major franchises: Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, Rainbow Six, Just Dance
- Fought off Vivendi hostile takeover attempt (2015–2018)
- Survived major sexual misconduct allegations against top executives (2020–2022)
- Tencent invested €300M for 49.9% stake in Guillemot holding company (2022)
- Rejected multiple buyout offers; family controls company through holding structure

## Labor context

Guillemot's tenure at Ubisoft has been marked by significant labor challenges:
- Ubisoft hit by major sexual misconduct and toxic culture allegations (2020–2021) — multiple executives removed
- STJV union formed at Ubisoft Montreal and Paris (2020s)
- 600+ employees walked out at Montreal in Feb 2025
- Massive headcount reductions: ~4,100 positions cut globally (2024–2025)
- Guillemot personally resisted unions early, later accepted STJV presence
- French labor law provides strong protections; works councils exist at French Ubisoft sites
