# Satoru Iwata — 4th President of Nintendo

Programmer, executive, and the most beloved figure in video game history. Iwata joined Nintendo as the company's first president from outside the founding Yamauchi family and led it through the triumphant DS/Wii era, refused to lay off employees during the Wii U downturn, and was developing the Nintendo Switch at the time of his death.

## Career arc

- **1980:** Part-time programmer at HAL Laboratory.
- **1982:** Full-time — 5th employee, sole programmer.
- **1993:** President of HAL Laboratory; rescues company from bankruptcy.
- **2000:** Joins Nintendo as Corporate Planning head.
- **2002:** Becomes 4th President of Nintendo.
- **2013:** CEO of Nintendo of America.
- **2015:** Dies at age 55.

## Key contributions

- **Programmer:** Wrote custom compression algorithms; contributed to Kirby, EarthBound, Pokémon, Super Smash Bros.
- **Strategist:** Blue-ocean strategy with DS (Touch Generations) and Wii (motion controls) expanded gaming to millions of non-gamers.
- **Leader:** Refused layoffs despite financial crisis — became a symbol of employee-first leadership.
- **Communicator:** Created Nintendo Direct and Iwata Asks — unprecedented transparency.
- **Visionary:** Started mobile partnership with DeNA; initiated Switch development.

## Labour legacy

Iwata's refusal to lay off employees set a philosophical foundation that continues at Nintendo. His statement — "If we reduce the number of employees for better short-term profits, employee morale will decrease" — is frequently cited as the gold standard for leadership in the game industry. Nintendo today still has no layoffs of permanent employees in Japan.
