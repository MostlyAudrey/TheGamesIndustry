# Skylar Hinnant

**Senior QA tester who helped establish Microsoft's first union.** Skylar Hinnant was a Senior QA Tester II at ZeniMax Studios in Rockville, Maryland, and one of the most visible organizers of the ZeniMax Workers United-CWA campaign.

## Career

- **Role:** Senior QA Tester II at ZeniMax Studios (Rockville, MD)
- **2022-12:** Helped organize ZeniMax Workers United union drive
- **2023-01-03:** Announced successful union vote — 300 QA workers across multiple ZeniMax studios
- **2023-06:** Gave interview to Business Insider predicting a wave of unionization across US tech and gaming

## Key role in gaming labor

Hinnant was the most frequently quoted organizer in press coverage of the ZeniMax union formation:
- First Microsoft union — landmark organizing victory
- Largest certified video game union at the time (~300 members)
- Union drive benefited from Microsoft's 2022 neutrality agreement with CWA
- Covered by CWA press release, Hollywood Reporter, Business Insider, Rock Paper Shotgun, and multiple outlets
