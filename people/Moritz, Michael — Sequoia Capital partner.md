# Sir Michael Moritz

Welsh billionaire venture capitalist, author, and philanthropist. Sequoia Capital partner for 37 years — led some of the most successful VC investments in history including Google, PayPal, YouTube, Instagram, and LinkedIn. Knighted KBE in 2013.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BA in History from Christ Church, Oxford; MBA from Wharton School (Thouron Scholar).
- **Time magazine (early 1980s):** Journalist and San Francisco Bureau Chief. Documented the development of the original Macintosh. Wrote The Little Kingdom, the first history of Apple.
- **Technologic Partners:** Co-founded technology newsletter company before joining venture capital.
- **Sequoia Capital (1986–2023):** Joined and became one of the most successful VCs in history. Key investments: Google ($12.5M for ~10%), PayPal, Yahoo!, YouTube, Instagram, Zappos, Webvan, eToys, GameFly, and others. Board seats included Google, Yahoo!, PayPal, Stripe, Klarna, Instacart.
- **Gaming investments:** While not primarily a gaming investor, Moritz led or oversaw Sequoia's early gaming deals including GameFly (game rental subscription). Sequoia under Moritz invested broadly in consumer internet that intersected gaming.
- **Sequoia Heritage:** After stepping down, focused on this wealth management fund now independent of Sequoia.
- **Philanthropy:** The Giving Pledge signatory. Donated £75M to Oxford University, $50M to Christ Church, $50M to University of Chicago, $20M to ACLU. Crankstart charity sponsors the Booker Prize. £150M pledge to National Gallery London.

## Key relationships

- Recruited to Sequoia by Don Valentine (founder)
- Co-invested with John Doerr (KPCB) on Google
- Mentored Roelof Botha — recruited Botha to Sequoia
- Worked alongside Doug Leone (Sequoia managing partner)
- Early relationship with Steve Jobs (documented Apple history) and Elon Musk
