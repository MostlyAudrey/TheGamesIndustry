# Ashley Cheng — Managing Director, Bethesda Game Studios

**Managing Director of Bethesda Game Studios since August 2020.** Cheng is the operational and production leader of Bethesda's internal development, coordinating all projects across the studio's multiple locations. He works alongside Todd Howard (executive producer) and Angela Browder (studio director) to run BGS.

## Career arc

- **2001:** Joined Bethesda Game Studios as a producer when the development unit was spun off from Bethesda Softworks.
- **2000s:** Promoted to senior producer, then production director — worked on The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind, IV: Oblivion, and Fallout 3.
- **2010s:** Production director for major BGS titles — overseeing increasingly complex production pipelines.
- **2015-2018:** Production leadership on Fallout 4 and Fallout 76.
- **2020-08:** Appointed Managing Director — the operational head of BGS, coordinating all internal development.
- **2023:** Oversaw production of Starfield — the studio's most ambitious project.
- **2024-2026:** Leading production on The Elder Scrolls VI and ongoing Starfield content development.

## Role at BGS

Cheng's role is distinct from Todd Howard's creative direction:
- **Howard** focuses on game design and creative vision
- **Cheng** manages production, pipelines, scheduling, and resource allocation
- **Angela Browder** serves as studio director (people management, operations)

This structure allows BGS to run multiple AAA projects simultaneously — a necessity in the modern development landscape.
