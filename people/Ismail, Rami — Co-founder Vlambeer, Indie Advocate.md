# Rami Ismail — Co-founder Vlambeer, The indien Agency

**One of indie gaming's most important business and industry advocates.** Ismail is the co-founder of Vlambeer (Ridiculous Fishing, Nuclear Throne) and founder of indien, a consultancy that helps independent developers navigate publishing, platform, and business challenges.

## Career arc

- **2010:** Co-founded Vlambeer with Jan Willem "JW" Nijman in the Netherlands.
- **2011:** *Super Crate Box* — free arcade platformer, cult hit.
- **2013:** *Ridiculous Fishing* — mobile hit, notable for the "cloning" controversy where another publisher released a near-identical game before Vlambeer could launch.
- **2014:** *Luftrausers* — arcade flight combat.
- **2015:** *Nuclear Throne* — top-down roguelike shooter.
- **2016:** Founded indien — originally an events/business agency, evolved into a consultancy helping indies with platform deals, contracts, and revenue terms.
- **2019–2020:** Withdrew from active Vlambeer development. Vlambeer effectively went on indefinite hiatus.
- **2020s:** indien became a significant force in indie game business — Ismail represents hundreds of developers in negotiations with platforms (Steam, Epic, Nintendo, etc.).
- Ismail also serves as a regular speaker, panelist, and media commentator on the business of independent games.

- **Indie business advocacy:** Ismail's most lasting contribution may be indien, which helps redress the power imbalance between individual developers and large platforms/publishers.
- **Clone wars:** Vlambeer's experience with Ridiculous Fishing being cloned before release became a rallying point for the industry's conversation about IP protection and platform enforcement.
- **Global perspective:** Ismail regularly highlights the challenges facing developers outside North America and Western Europe.

## Labour context

- Ismail has been a consistent voice on the economic precarity of game development.
- Through indien, he has helped developers secure better revenue terms, retain IP rights, and navigate complex platform agreements:
  - Negotiating better royalty rates across stores
  - Improving contract terms with publishers
  - Helping developers understand rights, audits, and revenue share models
- Ismail has openly discussed the difficulty of sustaining a small studio, particularly after Vlambeer's hiatus.
- He has criticized the "hustle culture" in indie development and the pressure on developers to function as their own marketing, PR, legal, and business teams.
