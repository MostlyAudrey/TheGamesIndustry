# Doug Bowser — Former President, Nintendo of America

American business executive who served as President and COO of Nintendo of America from April 2019 to December 2025. Carried the ironic distinction of sharing a name with the Mario franchise villain Bowser. His tenure was defined by the Switch era's commercial peak and an escalating labor controversy that pitted NoA against the CWA and NLRB.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** Procter & Gamble — brand management and consumer goods.
- **EA:** Joined Electronic Arts in the mid-2000s. Held senior roles in global operations, supply chain, and publishing — VP of Global Operations at EA.
- **Nintendo (2015–2019):** VP of Sales and Marketing → Senior VP of Sales and Marketing → VP of Publisher and Developer Relations.
- **2019:** Succeeded Reggie Fils-Aimé as President and COO of Nintendo of America.
- **2025:** Retired. Succeeded by NoA leadership transition.

## Labor context

Bowser's tenure was the most labor-controversial period in NoA history. The Mack Clifton firing in 2022 drew national attention — Clifton was fired after one shift for discussing unionization in a TikTok video. The NLRB issued a complaint, and Nintendo eventually settled. Multiple additional NLRB complaints and contractor layoffs followed. Unlike Microsoft (which signed a neutrality agreement with CWA) and Sony (which avoided unionization through different means), Nintendo under Bowser aggressively fought organizing efforts through its heavy contractor model.

## Key relationships

- Succeeded Reggie Fils-Aimé as NoA president
- Reported to Shuntaro Furukawa (Nintendo global president)
- Worked closely with Nintendo Co., Ltd. Kyoto-based leadership
- Former EA colleague: Andrew Wilson (current EA CEO)
