# Phil Schiller

**Role:** Apple Fellow; Former SVP Worldwide Marketing  
**Orgs:** [[Apple]], [[App Store]]

## Career Arc

Phil Schiller is an Apple executive who has been with the company since 1997. He served as Senior Vice President of Worldwide Product Marketing for decades, playing a key role in the iPod, iPhone, iPad, and Mac product lines. In 2015, he took over management of the **App Store**, making him one of the most influential figures in mobile gaming distribution.

In August 2020, Schiller was designated an **Apple Fellow**, with Greg Joswiak taking over his marketing role. As Apple Fellow, Schiller continues to oversee the App Store and lead Apple Events. His role in App Store policy has made him a central figure in the Epic Games antitrust litigation and debates about in-app purchase commissions that directly impact mobile game developers.

## Key Decisions

- **App Store stewardship (2015–present):** Oversees the rules, policies, and revenue model that defines mobile game distribution on iOS.
- **Defended App Store commission structure:** Publicly advocated for Apple's 30% cut and "walled garden" security model.
- **Banned crypto mining and certain app categories:** Set policies that shaped the mobile gaming landscape.
- **Led Apple's anti-violence stance:** Took public stands against violent content in apps.
- **Testified in Epic Games trial:** Key witness in the landmark antitrust case.

## Labor Context

Schiller's App Store policies directly affect the financial model for millions of mobile game developers. The 30% commission, restrictions on alternative payment systems, and app review guidelines have been central to labor/economic debates about platform power in mobile gaming. The Epic Games lawsuit highlighted tensions between Apple's control and developer livelihoods.
