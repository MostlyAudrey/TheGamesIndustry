# Mikko Oksanen — Unity PR and Communications Lead

**The communications executive at the center of Unity's most turbulent period.** As a senior communications figure at Unity Technologies, Mikko Oksanen has been the public-facing voice of Unity during the Runtime Fee crisis, mass layoffs, CEO transitions, and strategic reorientation.

## Career arc

- **Pre-Unity:** Background in marketing and communications across technology companies.
- **2010s:** Joined Unity Technologies, rising through the communications ranks as the company grew from a small game engine developer to a publicly-traded technology platform (IPO: 2020, NYSE: U).
- **2020:** Unity IPO. Oksanen involved in corporate communications around the public listing.
- **2022:** Unity's announcement of the ironSource merger — a transformative but controversial deal that combined Unity's engine business with the ad-tech company.
- **2023 (September):** Unity announced its Runtime Fee — a per-install fee on games built with Unity. The backlash was unprecedented: thousands of developers threatened to leave the platform. Oksanen was heavily involved in crisis communications.
- **2023 (October):** Unity abandoned the Runtime Fee in its original form (replaced by a much smaller per-install fee for high-revenue games). CEO John Riccitiello resigned. Oksanen's communications team managed the turnaround.
- **2024 (January):** Unity announced 1,800 layoffs (25% of workforce). James M. Whitehurst served as interim CEO.
- **2024–2025:** Matthew Bromberg appointed CEO. Unity restructured, refocusing on its core engine business and reducing its ad-tech ambitions.
- **2025–2026:** Unity continues as a smaller, more focused company under Bromberg. Oksanen remains communications lead.

## Key contributions

- **Crisis management:** The Runtime Fee crisis was one of the most significant developer relations disasters in game industry history. The communications response — from initial announcement through reversal and CEO departure — was a case study in crisis communications.
- **Restructuring narrative:** As Unity underwent multiple rounds of layoffs and strategic pivots, Oksanen's team managed the public narrative around the company's transformation.

## Labour context

- Unity's employee experience has been dramatically affected by the company's turbulence:
  - 1,800 layoffs in January 2024 alone.
  - Multiple rounds of smaller cuts throughout 2023–2025.
  - Office closures and SF Bay Area consolidation.
  - CEO turnover (3 CEOs in 18 months: Riccitiello → Whitehurst → Bromberg).
- No union presence at Unity.
- Oksanen has not been publicly involved in labor discussion beyond corporate communications about the layoffs.
