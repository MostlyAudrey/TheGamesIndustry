# Mike Morhaime — Blizzard Co-founder, Dreamhaven CEO

**The architect of Blizzard's golden age.** Mike Morhaime co-founded Blizzard Entertainment (Silicon & Synapse) in 1991 and led it through its most successful period — from Warcraft through World of Warcraft and Hearthstone. After departing Blizzard in 2018, he founded Dreamhaven.

## Career arc

- **1991:** With Allen Adham and Frank Pearce, founded Silicon & Synapse (later Blizzard Entertainment).
- **1992–1993:** Early games for other publishers: The Lost Vikings, Rock n' Roll Racing, Blackthorne.
- **1994:** Warcraft: Orcs & Humans — established the Warcraft universe and Blizzard's franchise model.
- **1995:** Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness — breakout hit.
- **1996:** Acquired by CUC International. Blizzard given operational autonomy.
- **1998:** Starcraft — cultural phenomenon, especially in South Korea.
- **2000–2003:** Diablo II, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos.
- **2004:** World of Warcraft (WoW) — the most successful subscription MMO in history (12M+ peak subscribers).
- **2008:** Vivendi Games merged with Activision — Activision Blizzard formed. Morhaime became CEO of Blizzard Entertainment.
- **2010–2016:** Blizzard's expansion era: Starcraft II, Diablo III, Hearthstone, Overwatch.
- **2018:** Announced he would step down as Blizzard president. Succeeded by J. Allen Brack.
- **2020:** Founded Dreamhaven, a new game development holding company in Irvine, CA.
- **2023–2024:** Dreamhaven launched internal studios: Secret Door, Moonshot Games. Hired numerous Blizzard veterans.
- **2024–2026:** Dreamhaven developing unannounced projects; positions as a developer-friendly alternative to Activision Blizzard.

- **Blizzard's franchise formula:** Under Morhaime, Blizzard became known for "polish over shipping" — famously canceling projects that didn't meet quality standards. This created both legendary quality and long development cycles.
- **WoW's transformation:** World of Warcraft changed Blizzard from a mid-sized developer into a multi-billion dollar enterprise and defined the subscription MMO market.
- **Dreamhaven vision:** Positioned as an independent studio focused on creative freedom and developer welfare — explicitly a response to the activist Blizzard's post-Morhaime reputation.

## Labour context

- Morhaime's Blizzard (pre-2018) had no union presence and was widely regarded as a desirable workplace in the industry.
- The Blizzard "golden age" coincided with a culture that would later be described as "frat house" in the California DFEH lawsuit (filed 2021).
- Morhaime has stated he was unaware of the extent of harassment and discrimination at Activision Blizzard — a claim that has been challenged.
- Under Activision (post-2018), Blizzard workers unionized heavily: ~1,900 employees in a CWA coalition, first contract ratified September 2026.
- Dreamhaven (Morhaime's new company) explicitly promotes a healthier culture, though it is too small for union comparison.
- The 2023–2024 layoff wave at Microsoft Gaming (post-Activision acquisition) affected Blizzard, with significant cuts.
