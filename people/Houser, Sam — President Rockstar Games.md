# Sam Houser

British video game producer and businessman. Co-founder and President of Rockstar Games (1998–present). The most commercially successful western game developer of his generation. Brother of Dan Houser (departed 2020).

Career:
- Joined BMG Interactive in the mid-1990s with brother Dan
- Acquired by Take-Two Interactive in 1998 — became Rockstar Games
- Led creation of the Grand Theft Auto franchise, one of the most valuable IPs in entertainment
- GTA III (2001) revolutionized open-world gaming
- GTA V (2013) — sold 200M+ copies, second best-selling game ever
- Also created Red Dead Redemption, Max Payne 3, Bully
- GTA VI announced (Dec 2023), expected release Fall 2026
- Known for intense secrecy and meticulous quality control

## Labor context

Houser's Rockstar has been at the center of industry crunch debates:
- Rockstar has a history of mandatory overtime during game releases
- GTA V and RDR2 development involved months of 60-100 hour weeks
- Dan Houser's 2018 comments about "100-hour weeks" caused controversy
- No union presence at Rockstar titles
- Rockstar has more recently emphasized work-life balance (post-RDR2)
- Take-Two's union stance has been anti-union historically
- By 2025, some Rockstar studios began indicating openness to unionization
