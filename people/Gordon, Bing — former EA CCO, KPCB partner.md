# Bing Gordon

William "Bing" Gordon is a video game executive and technology venture capitalist. He served as Chief Creative Officer of Electronic Arts for ten years (1998–2008) before joining Kleiner Perkins. He was a founding director of Audible.com and served on the board of Amazon for 14 years.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BA from Yale University (drama and literature); MBA from Stanford Graduate School of Business (1978). Acted in New York City before business school.
- **Ogilvy and Mather:** Account executive in advertising.
- **EA (1982–2008):** Joined EA as employee number ~30, serving as the company's first one-person marketing department. Worked directly on game design alongside Will Wright (SimCity), Sid Meier, and other legendary designers. Named Chief Creative Officer in 1998. Designed Sid Meier's Alpha Centauri, Sid Meier's SimGolf, and The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth. Took a faculty chair at USC's Interactive Media Division in 2005.
- **KPCB (2008–present):** Joined as venture partner. Focused on gaming, consumer, and mobile investments. Led KPCB's investment in ngmoco (iFund), Zynga, and other gaming companies.
- **Board service:** Amazon (2003–2017), Zynga (pre-IPO), Duolingo, Audible (founding director), Ngmoco.

## Key relationships

- Mentored Neil Young at EA; later joined ngmoco board as KPCB partner
- Worked alongside Trip Hawkins (EA founder) and Larry Probst as EA's early leadership
- Co-designed games with Sid Meier and Will Wright
- Connected to John Doerr (KPCB chairman) on gaming investments
