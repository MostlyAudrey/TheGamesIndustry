# Matt Cox — President, Blizzard Entertainment

**President of Blizzard Entertainment since 2024.** Cox succeeded Johanna Faries as Blizzard's president, coming from a senior leadership role on the Call of Duty franchise. He now oversees all of Blizzard's game development and publishing operations in the post-acquisition, post-first contract era.

## Career arc

- **~2012-2024:** Various leadership roles at Activision/Activision Blizzard — rose through the Call of Duty franchise.
- **Pre-presidency:** Served as EVP and GM of Call of Duty — co-leading the franchise alongside or succeeding Faries' GM role.
- **2024:** Named President of Blizzard Entertainment.
- **2024-2026:** Leading Blizzard through the post-acquisition integration, union contract implementation, and ongoing game development.

## Blizzard presidency

Cox's tenure as Blizzard president began during a transitional period:
- **Post-Faries:** Picked up where Faries left off after she was moved or departed from the role
- **Union implementation:** The Blizzard CWA coalition had recently won a first contract — Cox is responsible for executing on its terms
- **Game development:** Continued work on WoW, Diablo, Overwatch, and other Blizzard franchises
