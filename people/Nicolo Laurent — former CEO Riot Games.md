# Nicolo Laurent

Nicolo Laurent is a French-born business executive who served as CEO of [[orgs/Riot Games]] from 2017 to 2023. He joined Riot in 2009 as a regional manager and helped globalize League of Legends before rising to the top role. His tenure ended after multiple workplace scandals and a lawsuit alleging sexual harassment.

## Career Arc

- Born and educated in France; previous experience in business management
- Joined Riot Games in 2009 as one of its earliest non-founding executives
- Led Riot's international expansion, opening offices in Europe, Asia, and other regions
- Helped grow League of Legends into the largest PC game in the world
- Appointed CEO in 2017, succeeding founders Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill
- Departure announced in 2023; succeeded by Dylan Jadeja
- Served ~15 years at Riot, 7 as CEO

## Key Decisions and Scandals

- Oversaw Riot's expansion into multiple games (Valorant, Teamfight Tactics, Legends of Runeterra)
- Led through the 2018-2019 workplace culture crisis:
  - May 2018: Kotaku exposé documented "bro culture" of sexism and discrimination
  - May 2019: employee walkout over forced arbitration policies
  - August 2019: $10 million class-action settlement over gender discrimination
  - 2021: Riot's special committee found "no evidence" Laurent sexually harassed an assistant after a former employee's lawsuit
  - Lawsuit alleged harassment, discrimination, and retaliation; Riot's board cleared him
- Approved Arcane animated series (2021), which was a critical and commercial success

## Labor Context

- Central figure in Riot's workplace crisis (2018-2021)
- His leadership was criticized by employees during the walkout period
- Riot's forced arbitration policy (later ended) sparked widespread industry conversation about labor rights in gaming
- No union formation at Riot Games during his tenure; Riot remains non-union
- Riot implemented various DEI and culture reforms after the scandals
