# Craig Duncan — Former Head of Xbox Game Studios

**Head of Xbox Game Studios from October 2024 to June 2026.** Previously studio head of Rare Ltd. for 13 years. Duncan's tenure at Xbox Game Studios was brief — less than two years — ending with his departure amid broader Microsoft Gaming restructuring.

## Career arc

- **Before 2011:** Worked at various UK game studios including Midway and others in production roles.
- **2011:** Joined Rare as Studio Head — the longest-serving studio head in Rare's modern era.
- **2011-2024:** Led Rare through several phases:
  - Kinect Sports series (2011-2014)
  - Rare Replay (2015) — compilation of 30 Rare classics
  - Sea of Thieves (2018) — the studio's defining success under his leadership
  - Everwild (announced 2019, still in development as of 2026)
- **2023-2024:** Rare restructured with some layoffs (July 2025, per Xbox Game Studios Wikipedia entry).
- **2024-10:** Promoted to head of Xbox Game Studios, replacing Alan Hartman who retired.
- **2026-06:** Stepped down and left Microsoft — Eurogamer reported he "will be departing the company this week."

## Legacy

Duncan's legacy is most strongly tied to Rare — he helmed the studio during its transition from a Kinect-focused developer to a multiplatform hitmaker with Sea of Thieves. His tenure as XGS head was cut short before he could make long-term structural changes.
