# Tom Smith

**CWA's top organizing director for the tech and gaming sector.** Tom Smith serves as CWA's National Organizing Director (also referred to as Senior Director of Organizing), directing the union's resources toward building power in the technology, media, and communications sectors.

## Career

- **Role:** Directs CWA's organizing campaigns nationally
- **Sector focus:** Technology, Media, and Communications
- **Game sector involvement:** Lead strategist and spokesperson for CODE-CWA campaign
- **2022:** Active in Raven Software union battle — issued public statements on NLRB proceedings
- **2023:** Atlantic Fellow for Social Equity at LSE

## Key role in gaming labor

As the senior organizing director, Smith is the strategic lead behind CWA's gaming sector push:
- Oversaw CODE-CWA campaign organizational strategy
- Key spokesperson on Activision Blizzard labor violations during Raven Software union drive
- Advocated for company-wide (rather than craft-based) organizing model
- Represents CWA at industry events like GDC
