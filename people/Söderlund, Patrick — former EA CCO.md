# Patrick Söderlund
Swedish game developer who co-founded DICE (Battlefield), rose to EA's CCO, then left to found Embark Studios.

## Career arc
- **1992:** Co-founded DICE. Created Battlefield franchise, Frostbite engine.
- **2006:** EA acquired DICE.
- **2016:** Promoted to EA CCO.
- **2018:** Left EA to co-found Embark Studios (The Finals).
