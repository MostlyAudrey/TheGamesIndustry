# Jade Raymond

Canadian video game producer and executive. Founder of Haven Studios (owned by Sony). One of the most prominent women in the game industry, known for founding several high-profile studios. Born in Montreal, Quebec in 1975.

Career:
- Started as programmer at Ubisoft Montreal
- Associate producer on the original Assassin's Creed (2007), which made her an industry name
- Founded Ubisoft Toronto (2009) — built studio from scratch
- Left Ubisoft to join EA (2015) — founded Motive Studios in Montreal
- Motive produced Star Wars: Squadrons, worked on Dead Space remake, and upcoming Iron Man game
- Left EA for Google Stadia (2019) — founded Stadia Games and Entertainment in Montreal
- Google closed Stadia's internal studios in February 2021 — Raymond's first studio closure
- Founded Haven Studios (2021) — acquired by Sony Interactive Entertainment in 2022
- Haven developing a new original IP for PlayStation (unannounced)
- Departed Haven Studios in early 2025 (confirmed by Sony in Feb 2025)

## Labor context

Raymond's studios have generally maintained positive labor reputations:
- Haven Studios has no layoffs reported
- Motive had no major labor disputes during her tenure
- The Stadia closure was a Google corporate decision, not studio-specific
- No union presence reported at any of her studios
- She has spoken about work-life balance but specifics are limited
