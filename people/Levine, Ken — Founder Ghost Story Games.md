# Ken Levine

American video game writer and director. Founder of Irrational Games (1997) and Ghost Story Games (2017). Most famous for the BioShock series, consistently named among the greatest games of all time.

Career:
- First game: Thief: The Dark Project (1998) — co-writer at Looking Glass Studios
- Founded Irrational Games (as Braintree) in 1997
- Created System Shock 2 (1999) with Looking Glass
- Created BioShock (2007) — critical phenomenon, 30+ GOTY awards
- Led BioShock Infinite (2013) — controversial development, massive scope, heavy crunch
- Closed Irrational Games in 2014, retaining a small team
- Founded Ghost Story Games (2017) under Take-Two
- Judas announced (2022) — BioShock spiritual successor, still unreleased as of Oct 2026

## Labor context

Levine's development style has been associated with significant crunch:
- BioShock Infinite development: multiple reboots, scope creep, reported 70-100 hour weeks
- Irrational Games closure: 90+ employees laid off when studio downsized
- Ghost Story Games: deliberately small team (~20), slower output
- No union presence at Ghost Story Games
