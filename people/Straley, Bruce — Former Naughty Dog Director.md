# Bruce Straley — Former Naughty Dog Director

**The director of two of the most acclaimed games of all time — who walked away from AAA.** Straley directed The Last of Us (2013) and Uncharted 4: A Thief's End (2016) before leaving Naughty Dog in 2017, citing burnout. He later founded Wildflower Interactive.

## Career arc

- **1990s:** Early career at Western Technologies Inc (Menacer, X-Men), Pacific Softscape (Generations Lost), and Crystal Dynamics (Gex: Enter the Gecko, Gex 3).
- **1999:** Hired at Naughty Dog as an artist on Crash Team Racing (1999).
- **2001–2004:** Artist on Jak and Daxter series.
- **2007:** Co-art director on Uncharted: Drake's Fortune.
- **2009:** Game director on Uncharted 2: Among Thieves — narrative and gameplay landmark.
- **2011:** Development contributor on Uncharted 3: Drake's Deception.
- **2013:** Game director on The Last of Us — one of the most acclaimed games of all time; 200+ GOTY awards.
- **2014–2016:** Game director on Uncharted 4: A Thief's End — a massive AAA production.
- **2017:** Departed Naughty Dog after 18 years.
- **2017–2021:** Recovered from burnout; served as advisor or consultant to various game projects. Publicly discussed the unsustainable nature of AAA development.
- **2021:** Founded Wildflower Interactive, a small indie studio.
- **2025:** Announced debut game: Coven of the Chicken Foot — an adventure game.

- **The Last of Us directing:** Straley's direction shaped the game's pacing, cinematic language, and gameplay-flow, working alongside [[people/Neil Druckmann]] (creative director).
- **Uncharted 4:** A massive production; Straley directed what was then Naughty Dog's most technically ambitious game.
- **AAA exodus:** Straley's departure from Naughty Dog, along with [[people/Amy Hennig]]'s earlier exit and other senior departures, revealed the intensity of the studio's development culture.

## Labour context

- **Burnout as systemic problem:** Straley's post-Naughty Dog interviews are some of the most candid accounts of AAA burnout from a senior creative. He described the feeling of "hitting a wall" after back-to-back multi-year projects.
- **The Naughty Dog model:** The studio's "one big game at a time" approach means teams work on a single title for 4-5 years under intense pressure. This model creates exceptional quality but at human cost.
- **Transition to indie:** Straley founded Wildflower Interactive as a small indie studio, explicitly seeking a healthier, more sustainable development model.
- **Industry commentary:** Straley has been quoted in investigations about Naughty Dog's crunch practices and has advocated for healthier development practices.
- **Unionization stance:** Straley has not publicly taken a firm stance on unionization, but his critique of AAA labor practices aligns with union arguments.
