# Mike Bithell — Thomas Was Alone

**The developer who proved that simple shapes could tell an emotionally devastating story.** Mike Bithell's Thomas Was Alone (2012) is a minimalist platformer where rectangles are characters with distinct personalities and a poignant narrative arc.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** Worked as a developer on unspecified projects before going independent.
- **2012 (July):** Released Thomas Was Alone on PC (later ported to PS3, PS4, Vita, iOS, Android). The game featured only basic geometric shapes but used Danny Wallace's narration to give them rich personalities.
- **2013–2014:** Thomas Was Alone expanded to multiple platforms, becoming a significant indie hit.
- **2015:** Released Volume — a stealth-action game inspired by the Robin Hood myth, set in a dystopian tech world. Featuring Andy Serkis as the antagonist.
- **2016:** Formed Mike Bithell Games as a formal development entity.
- **2020:** Released The Solitaire Conspiracy — a narrative-driven solitaire game with a spy-thriller story.
- Bithell also co-hosts Play, Watch, Listen, a popular game industry podcast with Troy Baker, Alanah Pearce, and composer Austin Wintory.
- 2020s: Has continued to develop small-to-mid-sized games with a focus on narrative innovation.

## Key games

| Game | Year | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Thomas Was Alone | 2012 | Minimalist emotional platformer; indie milestone |
| Volume | 2015 | Stealth-action; star performances |

- **Minimalist narrative:** Thomas Was Alone proved that game storytelling didn't need realistic graphics, dialogue, or cutscenes — just good writing and design.
- **Narrative experimentation:** Each Bithell game experiments with different narrative frameworks — from the personification of shapes to spy solitaire.

## Labour context

- Mike Bithell Games is a small studio (single digits). Bithell has been open about the funding and sustainability challenges of independent development.
- Through Play, Watch, Listen, Bithell regularly discusses industry issues including crunch, unionization, diversity, and labour conditions.
