# Egon Durban — Co-CEO, Silver Lake

Egon Durban is co-Chief Executive Officer and Managing Partner of Silver Lake, the global technology private equity firm with $110B in assets under management. He is the key architect of Silver Lake's gaming and entertainment investment strategy, including the landmark $52.5B Electronic Arts take-private.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BA from University of Pennsylvania; MBA from Harvard Business School.
- **Early career:** Worked at Donaldson, Lufkin & Jenrette (DLJ) before joining Silver Lake early in its history.
- **Silver Lake (founding era–present):** Joined as one of the earliest employees; named Managing Director, then Managing Partner. Instrumental in building Silver Lake's tech PE franchise. In 2019, appointed Co-CEO alongside Greg Mondre.
- **Key deals:** 
  - **Electronic Arts ($52.5B, 2025):** Led Silver Lake consortium (with PIF/others) to take EA private — the largest LBO ever.
  - **Endeavor Group:** Silver Lake's largest single investment; backing the UFC/WWE entertainment-entertainment conglomerate with significant gaming/IP crossover.
  - **City Football Group:** $1.1B investment for 18% stake — CFG's esports and gaming operations intersect with Silver Lake's sports-tech thesis.
  - **Dell Technologies ($24.9B, 2013):** Participated in the landmark Dell buyout.
  - **Skype ($1.9B, 2009):** Silver Lake's acquisition and subsequent sale to Microsoft.
  - **TikTok USDS:** Board member for TikTok's US data security operations.
- **Current board seats:** Endeavor Group Holdings, City Football Group, TikTok USDS, G42, and others.

## Gaming and entertainment thesis

Durban has driven Silver Lake's thesis that gaming is the dominant entertainment medium. The EA take-private represents the largest single bet on gaming by any private equity firm. Silver Lake also backed Endeavor's acquisition of WWE (2023) and owns significant sports exposure through CFG.

## Key relationships

- Co-CEO with Greg Mondre (Silver Lake's other co-CEO)
- Works with Ken Hao (chairman) and Mike Bingle (vice chairman)
- Connected to Ari Emanuel (Endeavor CEO) — Silver Lake's largest portfolio relationship
- EA deal consortium partners: Yasir Al-Rumayyan (PIF), Saudi PIF, and other institutional LPs
