# Eiji Aonuma — Senior Officer, Nintendo EPD; Producer, The Legend of Zelda

The creative force behind The Legend of Zelda for over 25 years. Aonuma joined Nintendo without ever having played a video game, was recruited by Shigeru Miyamoto to work on Zelda, and went on to direct and produce the most acclaimed entries in the series — from Ocarina of Time through Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom.

## Career arc

- **1988:** Joins Nintendo, fresh from Tokyo University of the Arts.
- **1991:** Graphic artist on NES Open Tournament Golf.
- **1998:** Hired by Miyamoto to work on The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — dungeon designer.
- **2000:** Director — Majora's Mask.
- **2002:** Director — The Wind Waker.
- **2006:** Director — Twilight Princess.
- **2017:** Producer — Breath of the Wild (game of the year, 30M+ copies).
- **2023:** Producer — Tears of the Kingdom (20M+ copies).
- **2023:** Promoted to Senior Officer, Nintendo EPD.

## Key contributions

- Evolved Zelda from linear dungeon-crawler to open-air exploration
- Maintained franchise quality across 25+ years and multiple platforms
- Known for willingness to innovate while respecting tradition
- Created the 'Aonuma formula' of iteration on proven mechanics with new twists

## Labour context

Aonuma has been at Nintendo since 1988 — 38 years. No labour controversies. He is a prominent example of Japanese lifetime employment in the game industry. Like all Nintendo permanent employees, no layoff risk.
