# Steven Ma (Ma Xiaoyi)

Steven Ma (Ma Xiaoyi) is Senior Vice President of [[orgs/Tencent]], responsible for leading Tencent Games' international publishing, distribution, and strategic M&A investments. He is one of the most senior gaming-specific executives at Tencent.

## Career Arc

- Before Tencent: General Manager of the games division at OPTIC Communication Co., Ltd.
- Joined Tencent in 2007, initially in game investment and publishing roles
- Promoted to Senior Vice President in 2013
- Currently responsible for international distribution of Tencent Games, establishing and maintaining long-term business partnerships and cooperation
- Oversees Tencent's agency publishing business and strategic M&A investments in the gaming sector
- Known as a key figure in Tencent's global gaming expansion strategy

## Key Decisions

- Orchestrated investments and partnerships with major Western studios including [[orgs/Riot Games]] (League of Legends), [[orgs/Epic Games]] (Fortnite), and Bluehole/Krafton (PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds)
- Represents Tencent as a hands-on partner to portfolio studios rather than a purely financial investor
- Spoke publicly at GDC 2019 about helping developers with live operations, quality demands, and community management

## Labor Context

- No direct labor controversies personally reported
- As an architect of Tencent's global game investment strategy, his portfolio companies have faced labor scrutiny (notably Riot Games' workplace culture issues)
- No specific union or labor right positions on record
