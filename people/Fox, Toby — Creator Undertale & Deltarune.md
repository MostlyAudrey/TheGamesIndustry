# Toby Fox — Creator of Undertale & Deltarune

**The creator of one of the most beloved and influential indie games of all time.** Toby Fox's Undertale (2015) redefined the RPG genre with its pacifist/mercy mechanics, meta-narrative, and distinctive soundtrack, which Fox composed himself. Its spiritual successor Deltarune is releasing in chapters.

## Career arc

- **1990s–2000s:** Grew up in Manchester, New Hampshire. Deeply influenced by EarthBound (Mother 2). Self-taught musician; re-learned piano in middle school to play video game music.
- **2008:** Released *EarthBound Halloween Hack* (as "Radiation") — a ROM hack that gained cult status in the EarthBound fan community.
- **2010:** Joined Homestuck (Andrew Hussie's webcomic) music team, composing for one of the largest creative communities on the internet.
- **2011–2013:** Studied at Northeastern University in Boston. Conceived Undertale and began development.
- **2013 (Kickstarter):** Raised $51k (goal was $5k) — 10x the target.
- **2015 (September 15):** Released Undertale on PC. Critically acclaimed; developed almost entirely by Fox alone.
- **2018–present:** Deltarune released in chapters (Ch. 1: 2018, Ch. 2: 2021, Ch. 3–5: 2025). Known for its parallel-story relationship to Undertale.
- Influenced Japanese developers including Super Mario creator — Fox collaborated with Nintendo on content and has worked with Game Freak (Pokémon) for music contributions.

## Key games

| Game | Year | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Undertale | 2015 | Landmark indie RPG; consistently ranked among greatest games of all time |
| Deltarune | 2018–2026+ | Chapter-based spiritual successor |

- **Undertale's design:** Introduced bullet-hell shooter mechanics combined with the ability to complete the entire game without killing any enemy — a radical subversion of RPG conventions.
- **Music:** Fox composed Undertale's entire soundtrack (which he also released commercially). His music has been widely covered, remixed, and performed in concert.
- **No-combat pacifist run:** The game's morality system, where player choices permanently affect outcomes, set a new standard for narrative agency in indie games.

## Labour context

- Fox has operated as an independent developer with no formal studio structure.
- For Deltarune, Fox has assembled a small team including contracted artists, translators, and programmers — but retains all creative authority.
- Fox has not commented publicly on game industry unionization or labour organizing.
- The Undertale/Deltarune development model — crowdfunded, micro-team, creator-owned — represents a counterpoint to the AAA publisher-studio model that dominates the industry's labour discourse.
