# Tim Sweeney

American video game programmer and billionaire. Founder and CEO of Epic Games (1991–present). Creator of the Unreal Engine, the most widely used commercial game engine in the world. Born in Potomac, Maryland in 1970. Studied mechanical engineering at University of Maryland, College Park (dropped out).

Key accomplishments:
- Founded Epic Games (originally Potomac Computer Systems) in 1991
- Created Epic Games' first hit ZZT (1991)
- Led development of Unreal (1998), creating the Unreal Engine
- Unreal Engine evolved through UE3 (Gears of War), UE4, and UE5
- Launched Fortnite Battle Royale (2017) — one of the most profitable games ever
- Took on Apple with antitrust lawsuit (2020–2024) over App Store fees
- Built Epic Games Store as Steam competitor (2018)

## Labor context

Epic Games has had multiple labor controversies:
- Mass layoffs of ~830 employees (Sept 2023) — 16% of workforce, including significant Bandcamp and Mediatonic cuts
- No union presence at Epic
- Sweeney made comments critical of unionization in tech, though Epic has more recently signaled neutrality
- Epic's Fortnite creative economy has faced criticism for conditions similar to child labor debates

## Industry influence

Sweeney is one of the most influential people in gaming. His antitrust battle with Apple fundamentally challenged the App Store business model. Through Unreal Engine, he controls the underlying technology for thousands of games. He owns approximately 52% of Epic Games. Net worth estimated at $9.6B (2025).
