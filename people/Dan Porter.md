# Dan Porter

**Role:** Former CEO, OMGPOP; Founder, Overtime  
**Orgs:** [[OMGPOP]], [[Zynga]], [[Overtime]], [[William Morris Endeavor]]

## Career Arc

Daniel Jonathan Porter (born June 13, 1966) is an American Internet entrepreneur. He became CEO of **OMGPOP**, a mobile/social game studio, and oversaw the design and release of *Draw Something* — a Pictionary-style mobile game that became a viral sensation with over 100M downloads.

In 2012, Zynga acquired OMGPOP for approximately $180M at the height of *Draw Something*'s popularity. Porter left Zynga a year later in April 2013. He later served as Head of Digital at **William Morris Endeavor** (WME) and founded **Overtime**, a digital sports media company targeting Gen Z. Between ventures, he also started Tally, a financial wellness app.

## Key Decisions

- **Acquired OMGPOP:** Took over as CEO after founder Charles Forman departed, then led the studio to create *Draw Something*.
- **$180M sale to Zynga:** Closed the deal at the peak of the mobile social gaming boom, one of the highest-profile acquisitions of that era.
- **Exited Zynga quickly:** Left within a year after the acquisition, as *Draw Something*'s user base declined sharply.
- **Pivoted to sports media:** Founded Overtime, building a digital-native sports brand for younger audiences.

## Labor Context

The OMGPOP/Zynga acquisition story is a cautionary tale in mobile gaming. *Draw Something*'s rapid rise and fall exemplified the volatility of hits-driven mobile gaming. Porter's departure a year post-acquisition is often cited in discussions of talent retention challenges after studio acquisitions. The OMGPOP team was eventually affected by Zynga's broader layoffs and restructuring.
