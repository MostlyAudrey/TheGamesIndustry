# Roelof Botha

South African-American venture capitalist. CFO of PayPal during its IPO, then Sequoia Capital partner for 24 years (2003–2026), leading the firm from 2022–2025. Chairman of Unity Technologies board since October 2023. Grandson of Pik Botha, South Africa's apartheid-era foreign minister.

## Career arc

- **Education:** BSc in Actuarial Science, Economics, Statistics from University of Cape Town (valedictorian); MBA from Stanford GSB (valedictorian). McKinsey & Company analyst.
- **PayPal (2000–2003):** Director of corporate development → VP Finance → CFO. Led PayPal's 2002 IPO at age 28. Helped negotiate eBay acquisition.
- **Sequoia Capital (2003–2026):** Partner. Led investments in YouTube, Instagram, Square/Block, MongoDB, Unity. Took over US operations 2017. Became global Senior Steward (2022). Resigned as managing partner Nov 2025 after challenges. Departed entirely 2026.
- **Unity board (2023–present):** Chairman since Oct 2023. Appointed after Riccitiello's Runtime Fee resignation.
- **SpaceX board (2026–present):** Joined after departing Sequoia.

## Key relationships

- Longtime Elon Musk ally — worked at PayPal, joined SpaceX board
- Replaced Doug Leone at Sequoia; succeeded by Alfred Lin and Pat Grady
- Unity board lead — appointed and worked with Riccitiello, Whitehurst, Bromberg
- Recruited to PayPal by Elon Musk; recruited to Sequoia by Michael Moritz
