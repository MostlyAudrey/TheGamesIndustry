# Shinya Takahashi — Executive General Manager, Nintendo EPD

The highest-ranking development executive at Nintendo, Takahashi heads the Entertainment Planning & Development Division — the largest in-house game development organization in the world. He succeeded Shigeru Miyamoto as the head of Nintendo's internal software development after the 2015 reorganization merged EAD and SPD.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** Nintendo SPD (Software Planning & Development), rising to General Manager.
- **2015:** Appointed Executive General Manager of Nintendo EPD when SPD and EAD merge.
- **2020s:** Promoted to Senior Managing Executive Officer and Corporate Director at Nintendo.
- **Visible role:** Presented during Nintendo Switch Presentation (Jan 2017); regular face in Nintendo Directs.

## Role at Nintendo EPD

As Executive General Manager, Takahashi oversees:
- 10 production groups developing all major Nintendo franchises
- Co-Production Group (external partnerships with HAL, Intelligent Systems, Monolith Soft, etc.)
- Smart Device Production Group (mobile games)
- Programming, Design, and Sound Management Groups
- Senior officers: Yoshiaki Koizumi (Deputy GM), Katsuya Eguchi, Eiji Aonuma, Hisashi Nogami, Takashi Tezuka, Yoshio Sakamoto, Koji Kondo

## Labour context

Nintendo's Japanese development culture is characterized by lifetime employment, no layoffs, and long tenure. EPD has no documented unionization or labour disputes.
