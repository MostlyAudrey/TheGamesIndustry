# Hideo Kojima

Japanese video game designer, director, and producer. Founder of Kojima Productions. Most famous for the Metal Gear series, one of the most influential game franchises in history. Born in Tokyo in 1963.

Career:
- Joined Konami in 1986 as a designer
- Created Metal Gear (1987), inventing the stealth genre
- Led Metal Gear Solid (1998) — a defining game of the PS1 era
- Directed critically acclaimed sequels: MGS2 (2001), MGS3 (2004), MGS4 (2008), MGSV (2015)
- Left Konami in 2015 under controversial circumstances — canceled projects, contract dispute
- Launched Kojima Productions as independent studio in 2016
- Released Death Stranding (2019) — divisive but commercially successful
- Death Stranding 2 (2025) in partnership with Sony
- Also making a game with Xbox (OD, 2024), an action-espionage title (Physint, Sony)
- Collaborating with Jordan Peele and Hideo Kojima on film/TV projects

## Labor context

Kojima's Konami departure was marked by allegations of poor working conditions:
- Kojima's Konami team was reportedly isolated and surveilled before departure
- Konami's post-Kojima labor practices: stricter controls, reduced creative freedom
- Kojima Productions has no reported labor issues
- No union presence
- Japanese game industry has very low union density in general
