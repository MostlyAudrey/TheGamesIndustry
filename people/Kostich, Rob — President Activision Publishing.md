# Rob Kostich — President, Activision Publishing

**President of Activision Publishing since January 2019.** Kostich has spent over two decades at Activision, rising through the ranks to lead one of the largest video game publishers in the world. His portfolio includes Call of Duty, Crash Bandicoot, Tony Hawk, and other major franchises.

## Career arc

- **2004:** Joined Activision — early roles in product management, marketing, and franchise strategy.
- **2010s:** Rose to senior leadership roles overseeing publishing operations, franchise management, and commercial strategy.
- **2019-01:** Appointed President of Activision Publishing — replacing the CEO role vacated by Eric Hirshberg's departure in 2018.
- **2019-2026:** Leading Activision Publishing through the Call of Duty Warzone era, the Microsoft acquisition, and post-acquisition integration.

## Key responsibilities

As President of Activision Publishing, Kostich oversees:
- Global publishing strategy for all Activision-owned IP
- Call of Duty franchise — annual premium releases, Warzone free-to-play, Call of Duty League
- Business operations, commercial planning, and franchise management
- Studio publishing support for Activision's development teams

## Labor context

Kostich runs the publishing side, while studio development is managed through the broader Microsoft Gaming content org (Matt Booty). However, his franchise-level decisions (release timing, contract work, QA staffing) directly affect working conditions at Activision studios. The Raven Software QA union and the Activision QA United strike authorization are defining labor issues under his franchise portfolio.
