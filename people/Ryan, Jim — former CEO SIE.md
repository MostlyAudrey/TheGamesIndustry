# Jim Ryan — Former President and CEO, Sony Interactive Entertainment

British executive who led PlayStation through the PS5 generation. A 30-year Sony veteran who rose from European operations to global CEO. His tenure was marked by aggressive M&A (Bungie, Kadokawa), significant layoffs, and mixed first-party studio performance.

## Career arc

- **1994:** Joined Sony. Spent 25 years in European operations.
- **2011:** President of Sony Computer Entertainment Europe.
- **2018:** Deputy President of SIE Global.
- **2019:** Became President and CEO of Sony Interactive Entertainment.
- **2024:** Retired. Succeeded by co-CEOs Hermen Hulst and Hideaki Nishino.

## Key tenure events

Ryan's leadership coincided with the PS5 generation (2020 launch). Major decisions included Japan Studio's closure, Bungie's $3.6B acquisition, the $68.7B Microsoft-Activision response, the Concord/Firewalk disaster (shut down days after launch), and securing a strategic stake in Kadokawa (FromSoftware, Elden Ring).

## Labor context

Ryan's era saw some of Sony's largest layoffs (900 in early 2024), plus repeated Bungie cuts. Unlike Microsoft, Sony did not sign any labor neutrality agreements — no first-party Sony studio unionized during his tenure.
