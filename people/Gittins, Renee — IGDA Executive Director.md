# Renée Gittins

**Former IGDA Executive Director during the early wave of game industry labor organizing.** Gittins led the International Game Developers Association from 2019 to 2022, a period that saw significant growth in worker organizing and industry labor awareness.

## Career

- American game developer by background
- **2019:** Became IGDA Executive Director
- **2022:** Left IGDA; succeeded by Jakin Vela

## Key role in gaming labor

As head of the world's largest nonprofit membership organization for game developers, Gittins:
- Represented game developers during early unionization wave (2019–2022)
- Navigated IGDA's position on labor issues — the organization advocates for developer rights but does not engage in collective bargaining
- Served during pivotal events: Riot Games walkout (2019), California DFEH lawsuit against Activision Blizzard (2021), Raven Software union drive (2021–2022)
