# Brandon Beck

Brandon "Ryze" Beck is the co-founder and co-chairman of [[orgs/Riot Games]]. Along with Marc Merrill, he founded Riot in 2006 with the vision of creating a game company that would redefine how online games are developed, delivered, and supported for players.

## Career Arc

- Born in 1982, grew up as a lifelong gamer
- Earned degree from University of Southern California Marshall School of Business
- Worked as an analyst at Bain & Company before founding Riot
- Co-founded Riot Games in 2006 with Marc Merrill
- Self-funded the company in its early years before securing investment from [[orgs/Tencent]] in 2011
- Led Riot as co-CEO alongside Marc Merrill from 2006 to 2017
- Stepped down as CEO in 2017, handing the role to Nicolo Laurent
- Remains Co-Chairman of the Board of Directors
- His in-game namesake champion "Ryze" appears in League of Legends

## Key Decisions

- Co-created League of Legends, which launched in 2009 and became the most played PC game in the world
- Led the decision to accept Tencent investment (2011) and ultimately full acquisition
- Advocated for the "player-first" development philosophy that defined Riot's approach
- Pushed for free-to-play model with cosmetic monetization, setting industry standards
- Approved Arcane, Riot's first animated series, and the expansion into transmedia

## Labor Context

- Part of leadership during Riot's early growth phase, which established the company culture later criticized as exclusionary
- Stepped down as CEO before 2018 workplace crisis became public
- As co-chairman, was involved in the board's handling of Nicolo Laurent's misconduct allegations
- Riot remains non-union
