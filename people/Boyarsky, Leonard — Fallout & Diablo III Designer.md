# Leonard Boyarsky — Fallout, Diablo III, The Outer Worlds

**The artist and designer who gave Fallout its look, gave Diablo III its story, and co-created The Outer Worlds.** Boyarsky was art director on the original Fallout, lead world designer on Diablo III, and co-creator of The Outer Worlds.

## Career arc

- **1992:** Joined Interplay Entertainment as employee #88. Freelance work on Rags to Riches and Castles II.
- **1995:** Lead artist on Stonekeep. Met Tim Cain and Jason Anderson; the three played D&D and began discussing a game based on Cain's engine.
- **1997:** Art director on Fallout. Boyarsky set the iconic "1950s future" visual style, designed Vault Boy, and contributed to the game's darkly comic tone.
- **1998:** After Fallout 2 contributions, left Interplay with Cain and Anderson to co-found Troika Games.
- **2001:** Art director, writer, and dialog editor on Arcanum.
- **2004:** Project leader and art director on Vampire: The Masquerade - Bloodlines.
- **2005:** Troika closed. Boyarsky took a year off to recover from burnout.
- **2006–2016:** Joined Blizzard Entertainment as lead world designer on Diablo III (released 2012) and Diablo III: Reaper of Souls (2014). Key figure in expanding Diablo's lore and narrative depth.
- **2016:** Joined Obsidian Entertainment. Reunited with Tim Cain to work on a new project.
- **2019:** The Outer Worlds released. Boyarsky co-created the game's satirical corporate-dystopia setting.
- **2025–2026:** The Outer Worlds 2 in development.

- **Fallout's aesthetic:** The "retro-futuristic 1950s apocalypse" — Boyarsky's vision gave Fallout its unique visual identity that set it apart from other post-apocalyptic games.
- **Vault Boy:** Boyarsky designed the iconic mascot whose cheerful demeanor contrasts with the horrors of the wasteland.
- **Blizzard era:** At Blizzard, Boyarsky was instrumental in deepening the story and lore of the Diablo franchise — a significant shift for a series previously focused on gameplay.
- **Reuniting with Cain:** Boyarsky and Cain's partnership at Obsidian produced The Outer Worlds, proving the duo's enduring creative chemistry.

## Labour context

- **Troika's burnout:** Boyarsky took a year off after Troika's closure to recover from burnout, a common story in the indie game space.
- **Blizzard corporate culture:** At Blizzard, he worked within a well-resourced corporate environment — a contrast from Troika's indie struggle.
- **Obsidian acquisition:** Boyarsky experienced Obsidian's transition from independent studio (financial precarity) to Microsoft subsidiary (stable funding) in 2018.
- No union context.
