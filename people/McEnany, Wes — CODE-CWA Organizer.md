# Wes McEnany

**Veteran labor organizer tapped to lead CWA's tech and game worker campaign.** Wes McEnany brought deep SEIU and Fight for 15 organizing experience to the video game sector when the CWA launched CODE-CWA in January 2020.

## Career

- **Pre-2020:** Longtime SEIU organizer; leader in the Fight for $15 minimum wage campaign
- **2020-01:** Joined CWA as East Coast-based CODE-CWA organizer alongside Emma Kinema (West Coast)

## Key role in gaming labor

McEnany's role in game labor is as an organizer and strategist rather than a public-facing figure. As one of the two original CODE-CWA staffers:
- Co-architect of the campaign infrastructure that organized Raven Software, ZeniMax, Activision QA, and Blizzard unions
- Works with gaming workers across the East Coast of the United States
- Brought Fight for $15 campaign experience to game industry organizing strategy
