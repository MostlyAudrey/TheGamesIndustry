# Vince Zampella — Co-founder Respawn Entertainment

American video game designer and executive. Co-founded Infinity Ward and Respawn Entertainment. Central figure behind the Call of Duty: Modern Warfare and Titanfall franchises, and Apex Legends. His 2010 ouster from Activision triggered one of the game industry's most famous legal battles.

## Career arc

- **Early:** Worked at SegaSoft, Panasonic Interactive Media, and 2015 Inc.
- **2002:** Co-founded Infinity Ward with Jason West. Created Call of Duty series.
- **2010:** Fired by Activision; co-founded Respawn.
- **2017:** EA acquired Respawn for $455 million.
- **2020s:** Expanded oversight to Ripple Effect Studios (DICE LA).
- **2025-12-21:** Passed away at 56.
