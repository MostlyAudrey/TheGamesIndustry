# Toshihiro Nagoshi

Toshihiro Nagoshi (born June 17, 1965) is a legendary Japanese game creator best known for creating the Yakuza (Ryu Ga Gotoku) series and Super Monkey Ball. After 32 years at [[orgs/Sega]], he left in 2021 to establish Nagoshi Studio under [[orgs/NetEase]].

## Career Arc

- Joined Sega in 1989; spent his entire career at the company until 2021
- Created the Super Monkey Ball franchise (2001) and the Yakuza series (2005)
- Became Chief Creative Officer (CCO) of Sega in April 2021 as part of a corporate restructuring
- Announced departure from Sega in October 2021, after less than a year as CCO
- Established Nagoshi Studio in January 2022 as a wholly owned subsidiary of NetEase, based in Tokyo
- Brought several former Sega and Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio staff to Nagoshi Studio, including Daisuke Sato
- First game "Gang of Dragon" was shown at The Game Awards 2025
- In March 2026, reports emerged that NetEase would stop funding the game's development — indicating potential studio troubles

## Key Decisions

- Creator and longtime producer of the Yakuza series — one of Sega's most successful franchises
- Led Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio as its head
- Decision to leave Sega after 32 years marked a major shift in Japanese game development talent to Chinese-backed studios
- His move to NetEase was part of a wave of top Japanese creators joining Chinese publishers
