# Shuntaro Furukawa — Global President, Nintendo Co., Ltd.

Sixth president of Nintendo, an accountant by training who rose through the company's European finance operations to lead the company through the Switch generation. Took office in 2018 as the Switch was hitting its stride and has overseen Nintendo's expansion into mobile, film, and theme parks.

## Career arc

- **1994:** Joins Nintendo after Waseda University (Political Science and Economics).
- **1994–2004:** Accountant at Nintendo of Europe (Germany) for a decade.
- **2012:** Nintendo representative director at The Pokémon Company.
- **2015:** Senior Director, Corporate Planning.
- **2016:** Managing Executive Officer, Board of Directors; also Global Marketing.
- **2018:** President of Nintendo (June 28).

## Key achievements

- Continued Nintendo Switch momentum — became third best-selling console of all time
- Launched Switch OLED (2021) and Switch successor (2025+)
- Expanded Nintendo's IP into film: The Super Mario Bros. Movie ($1.36B global)
- Opened Super Nintendo World theme park areas (Universal Studios Japan, Hollywood, Orlando)
- Acquired Next Level Games, established Nintendo Entertainment Planning & Development
- Mobile gaming partnerships with DeNA, Niantic (Pokémon GO)

## Labour context

Furukawa's tenure has been largely controversy-free on labour. The main exception is Nintendo of America, which faced allegations of anti-union practices and contractor misclassification. In Japan, Nintendo maintains its traditional lifetime-employment culture with no documented layoffs of permanent employees.
