# Dylan Jadeja

Dylan Jadeja is the CEO of [[orgs/Riot Games]], appointed in September 2023. He is a key architect of Riot's growth and global expansion, having previously served as CFO and COO.

## Career Arc

- Before Riot: Vice President at Goldman Sachs
- Joined Riot Games in 2011 as Chief Financial Officer
- Added Chief Operating Officer responsibilities in 2014
- Played a central role in Riot's financial strategy, global office expansion, and operational scaling
- Appointed CEO in September 2023, succeeding Nicolo Laurent
- Oversees Riot's strategy as it expands from a single-game company (League of Legends) to a multi-game publisher (Valorant, Teamfight Tactics, fighting game, MMO project)

## Key Decisions

- Instrumental in Riot's growth from a small studio to thousands of employees worldwide
- Guided the financial strategy during Riot's transition to multi-game development and transmedia (Arcane)
- Led the company post-workplace-crisis, tasked with culture reform and rebuilding trust
- President of Riot Games since June 2024 according to some leadership listings

## Labor Context

- Took over after Nicolo Laurent's scandal-ridden tenure
- Has emphasized a more transparent and employee-friendly culture
- Riot remains non-union
- Oversees ongoing culture reforms from 2018-2021 crises
