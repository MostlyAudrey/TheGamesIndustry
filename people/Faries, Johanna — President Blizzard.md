# Johanna Faries — President, Blizzard Entertainment

**President of Blizzard Entertainment since January 2024.** Faries came to Blizzard from the Call of Duty franchise, where she served as GM for six years. Her appointment marked a new era for Blizzard — post-Microsoft acquisition, post-Ybarra, and in the midst of the CWA unionization wave.

## Career arc

- **~2005-2017:** 12 years at the National Football League (NFL) — cross-functional roles in marketing, club business development, and league operations. Served as VP of Club Business Development.
- **2018:** Joined Activision as SVP and General Manager of Call of Duty — responsible for one of the highest-grossing entertainment franchises in history.
- **2018-2024:** Led Call of Duty through the Warzone era, the launch of Call of Duty League esports, and significant franchise expansion.
- **2024-01:** Appointed President of Blizzard Entertainment — replacing Mike Ybarra who was laid off in the Microsoft Gaming cuts.
- **2024-2026:** Leading Blizzard through a period of union expansion, first contract negotiations, and continued game development (WoW: Midnight, Diablo IV expansion, Overwatch 2 content).

## Blizzard era

Faries' presidency at Blizzard coincides with several major developments:
- **Unionization:** The CWA organized ~1,900 Blizzard workers across multiple units
- **First contract:** The Blizzard CWA bargaining coalition ratified a first contract in September 2026
- **Game development:** Continued development of WoW: Midnight expansion (2026), Diablo IV expansions, Overwatch 2 seasonal content
- **Microsoft integration:** Blizzard operating as a division of Microsoft Gaming under Phil Spencer
