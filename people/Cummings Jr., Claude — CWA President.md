# Claude Cummings Jr.

**Current CWA President (2023–present).** First African-American to lead the 700,000-member union. Born 1951/1952 in Kashmere Gardens, Houston, Texas.

## Career

- **1973:** Began working at Southwestern Bell Telephone as a frame attendant; joined CWA
- Became aware of gender discrimination in break room policies — led successful campaign for equal break area access
- Rose through local union ranks to become local president
- **2011:** Elected CWA Vice President representing District 6
- Served on CWA Executive Board; led union's Human Rights Department
- Active in NAACP, A. Philip Randolph Institute, Coalition of Black Trade Unionists
- **2023:** Elected CWA President
- **2024:** Spoke at Democratic National Convention

## Key role in gaming labor

As current CWA president, Cummings oversees the union's growing gaming sector operations:
- Continued implementation of Microsoft neutrality agreement
- Supervised contract negotiations across Microsoft gaming unions
- Led CWA's response to industry-wide layoffs (Microsoft 3,200 layoffs Jul 2026)
- Issued statement on EA buyout — positioned CWA as active voice in gaming industry restructuring
