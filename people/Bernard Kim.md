# Bernard Kim

**Role:** CEO, Match Group (since 2022); Former President, Zynga  
**Orgs:** [[Match Group]], [[Zynga]], [[Take-Two Interactive]], [[Tinder]]

## Career Arc

Bernard Kim is a gaming and mobile executive with 20+ years of leadership experience. He served as **President of Zynga**, overseeing how the company connected the world through games. At Zynga, he was responsible for strategy, operations, and growth across the company's portfolio of mobile titles.

In May 2022, Kim left the games industry to become CEO of **Match Group**, the dating and social discovery conglomerate whose brands include Tinder, Hinge, Match, Meetic, OkCupid, and Plenty of Fish. He also serves as interim CEO of Tinder. His background in mobile engagement and gamification is directly relevant to Match Group's products, which increasingly incorporate game-like mechanics.

## Key Decisions

- **Left gaming for dating (2022):** Made a high-profile career shift from gaming to dating app leadership.
- **Led Zynga's growth:** Oversaw operations as Zynga's president during the Take-Two acquisition period.
- **Interim CEO of Tinder:** Stepped in to lead Match Group's flagship brand directly.

## Labor Context

Kim's move from gaming to dating apps highlights the converging skill sets between mobile gaming engagement mechanics and social/dating products. Match Group has faced labor scrutiny around gig-worker classification for content moderators and the psychological impact of gamified dating products.
