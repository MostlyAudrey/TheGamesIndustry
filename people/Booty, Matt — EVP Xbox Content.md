# Matt Booty — EVP and Chief Content Officer, Xbox

**Executive Vice President and Chief Content Officer of Xbox since 2024.** Booty oversees all first-party game content strategy, product development, and business operations across Xbox Game Studios, Bethesda Softworks, Activision, Blizzard Entertainment, and King. He is the single most powerful content executive in Microsoft Gaming, managing a portfolio of 30+ studios producing games for Xbox, PC, and cloud.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** Worked in video game production and studio management; known for time at Epic Games where he served as President.
- **Epic Games years:** Oversaw Unreal Engine business, Gears of War franchise development. Departure predated Fortnite's explosive growth.
- **2020:** Joined Microsoft as Corporate Vice President / Head of Xbox Game Studios, reporting to Phil Spencer.
- **2023:** As Microsoft Gaming reorganized post-ABK acquisition, Booty transitioned to overseeing ZeniMax/Bethesda subsidiaries as Matt Booty's role shifted.
- **2024:** Promoted to EVP + Chief Content Officer — expanding his oversight to ALL Microsoft Gaming first-party content (XGS + Bethesda + ABK).
- **2024-2026:** Managing integration of Activision Blizzard development teams into Microsoft's content pipeline.

## Studio portfolio

Booty's content empire includes:
- **Xbox Game Studios:** ~15 studios (343 Industries/Halo Studios, The Coalition, Playground Games, etc.)
- **Bethesda/ZeniMax:** Bethesda Game Studios, id Software, Arkane, MachineGames, ZeniMax Online
- **Activision:** Treyarch, Infinity Ward, Sledgehammer Games, Raven Software
- **Blizzard:** Blizzard teams (WoW, Overwatch, Diablo, etc.)
- **King:** Mobile game development (Candy Crush)

## Labor context

Booty sits at the intersection of Microsoft's labor relations challenges. His studios include:
- **Unionized teams:** Raven Software QA (CWA), Blizzard wall-to-wall units (CWA), ZeniMax Workers United-CWA (various Bethesda studios)
- **Non-union teams:** Most development staff at Activision Call of Duty studios, Xbox Game Studios
- **Transition tension:** The post-neutrality era has created friction between Booty's content leadership and the CWA-represented workforces
