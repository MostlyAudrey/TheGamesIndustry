# Phil Harrison

**Role:** Former VP & GM, Google Stadia  
**Orgs:** [[Google]], [[Stadia]], [[Sony]] (Sony Computer Entertainment), [[Microsoft]] (Xbox)

## Career Arc

Phil Harrison is a British video game executive who has held senior roles at three of the biggest platforms in gaming. He began at **Sony Computer Entertainment**, where he served as head of Sony's game studios and was instrumental in the PlayStation brand through multiple console generations. He later joined **Microsoft** as a senior executive on the Xbox team, leading Microsoft Game Studios.

In January 2018, Harrison joined **Google** as Vice President and General Manager to develop and launch **Google Stadia**, the company's cloud gaming platform. Stadia launched in 2019 but was shut down in early 2023 after failing to gain traction. Harrison departed Google following Stadia's closure.

## Key Decisions

- **Helped launch PlayStation platforms:** Was a key figure in multiple PlayStation console launches at Sony.
- **Led Microsoft Game Studios (2012–2015):** Oversaw first-party game development for Xbox.
- **Pioneered cloud gaming at Google (2018–2023):** Led the ambitious but ultimately unsuccessful Stadia project.
- **Stadia's shutdown (2023):** Managed the platform's closure, a major cloud gaming industry event.

## Labor Context

Harrison's career illustrates the volatility of cloud gaming. Stadia's failure affected hundreds of game developers who had built games or ported titles to the platform. The closure also displaced Google's internal Stadia team and impacted partner studios. The Stadia saga is often studied as a cautionary tale about tech giant entry into gaming without sustained commitment.
