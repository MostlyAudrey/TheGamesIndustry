# Eric Barone — ConcernedApe

**The one-person studio who built the most beloved farming simulator ever.** Eric Barone, known professionally as ConcernedApe, created Stardew Valley entirely alone over 4.5 years — programming, pixel art, music, and writing. The game has sold over 50 million copies and is the highest-rated game on Steam (per Steam250, July 2025).

## Career arc

- **2000s:** Studied computer science at University of Washington Tacoma. Unable to find a programming job after graduation.
- **2011–2016:** Self-taught art, music, and programming skills while developing Stardew Valley alone. Released February 26, 2016 on Steam.
- **2016–2019:** Handled all updates single-handedly while game grew exponentially. Published on consoles, mobile.
- **2019:** Formed a small team (Applesauce Studios) to assist with porting and updates, though Barone remains lead developer on all content.
- **2021 (announced):** *Haunted Chocolatier* — a shop-management game set in a ghostly chocolate shop. Still in development as of 2026.
- **2024–2026:** Stardew Valley continues receiving updates (1.6 released 2024). Barone announced a concert tour based on the soundtrack. Collaborated on Balatro and Magic: The Gathering card collaborations.

- **Solo development model:** Barone's decision to create everything himself — code, art, music, design — set a template for solo indie success. He continues to personally write most Stardew Valley code.
- **No publisher deal:** Self-published, avoiding the revenue share and creative control issues common with publisher-funded indies.
- **Multi-platform expansion:** Stardew Valley reached virtually every platform (PC, consoles, mobile), demonstrating the long-tail viability of well-crafted solo games.

## Labour context

- Barone's solo development period (2011–2016) involved unknown working hours; he has described it as a period of financial uncertainty supported by his then-girlfriend (now wife).
- As of 2026, Barone maintains a small team — not a traditional studio with formal HR, management layers, or union coverage.
- Barone has not been publicly associated with any union organizing or labour advocacy.
- Notably, Stardew Valley was frequently cited during 2023–2024 industry layoffs as an example of sustainable independent game development versus the AAA model of repeated mass layoffs.
