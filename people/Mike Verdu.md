# Mike Verdu

**Role:** Former VP of Games / VP of GenAI for Games, Netflix  
**Orgs:** [[Netflix]], [[Electronic Arts]], [[Kabam]], [[Facebook]], [[Oculus VR]]

## Career Arc

Mike Verdu is a veteran game executive who spearheaded Netflix's entry into gaming. His career includes stints at **Electronic Arts** (SVP of EA Mobile), **Kabam** (COO and head of studios), and **Facebook/Oculus** (VP of content for AR/VR).

Verdu joined **Netflix** in July 2021 as VP of Games, tasked with building Netflix's gaming division from scratch. He launched Netflix's mobile game service in November 2021, acquiring studios like Night School Studio, Next Games, and Boss Fight Entertainment. In 2024, amid a leadership reshuffle, Verdu stepped aside as games VP as former Epic Games exec Alain Tascan took over as President of Games. Verdu shifted to VP of GenAI for Games before leaving Netflix in March 2025.

## Key Decisions

- **Built Netflix Games from scratch:** Hired the team, set the strategy, and launched the service in 4 months.
- **Acquired game studios:** Led acquisition of Night School Studio, Next Games, and Boss Fight Entertainment.
- **Mobile-first, no-ads strategy:** Established Netflix Games as a subscriber benefit with no ads, no microtransactions.
- **Shifted to GenAI for games (2024):** Led Netflix's exploration of generative AI in game development.
- **Departed Netflix (2025):** Left after nearly 4 years as the gaming division evolved.

## Labor Context

Verdu's departure in 2025 occurred amid industry-wide restructuring and layoffs at Netflix's gaming division. The shift from startup-mode game development to a more structured corporate approach affected studio culture. Verdu's focus on GenAI before his exit reflected a broader industry debate about AI's role in game development and its impact on creative labor.
