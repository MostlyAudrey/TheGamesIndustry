# Mike Ybarra — Former President, Blizzard Entertainment

**President of Blizzard Entertainment from February 2022 to January 2024.** A 22-year Microsoft veteran who joined Blizzard during its darkest period (post-DFEH lawsuit) and led it through the Microsoft acquisition. His tenure ended when he was laid off in the post-acquisition restructuring.

## Career arc

- **~1997-2019:** 22 years at Microsoft. Key roles included:
  - Xbox platform engineering and service development
  - General manager of Xbox Live
  - Corporate Vice President of Xbox — worked directly with Phil Spencer
- **2019:** Joined Activision Blizzard as EVP and GM of Blizzard Entertainment — a homecoming of sorts given his Microsoft Xbox background.
- **2021-08:** Following J. Allen Brack's departure after the DFEH lawsuit, Ybarra and Jen Oneal were named co-leaders of Blizzard.
- **2021-11:** Jen Oneal stepped down; Ybarra became sole leader.
- **2022-02:** Officially named President of Blizzard Entertainment.
- **2023:** Oversaw the release of Diablo IV — a critical and commercial success. Blizzard also continued developing Overwatch 2 and WoW: Dragonflight.
- **2024-01:** Laid off in Microsoft Gaming's 1,900-person reduction. His departure, along with Chief Design Officer Allen Adham, marked the end of an era.

## Blizzard legacy

Ybarra was widely described as a stabilizing force at Blizzard following the DFEH crisis. His Microsoft background made him a natural bridge between Blizzard's culture and Microsoft's corporate structure. However, his tenure was short — just under two years as sole president — and he was removed before the most significant union milestones at Blizzard.
