# Tony Petitti — Former President of Sports and Entertainment, Activision Blizzard

**President of Sports and Entertainment at Activision Blizzard from August 2020 to 2022.** Petitti ran Activision's esports properties — including the Call of Duty League and Overwatch League — before returning to traditional sports as Commissioner of the Big Ten Conference.

## Career arc

- **Early career:** Sports television executive — helped launch MLB Network and served as its President and CEO.
- **MLB years:** Deputy Commissioner and COO of Major League Baseball — oversaw operations, scheduling, and broadcast.
- **2020-08:** Joined Activision Blizzard as President of Sports and Entertainment — running esports leagues.
- **2022:** Departed Activision Blizzard after ~2 years — the esports properties were underperforming financially.
- **2023-05:** Appointed Commissioner of the Big Ten Conference — one of college sports' most powerful positions.
- **2026:** Still serving as Big Ten Commissioner.

## Esports era

Petitti's tenure at ABK coincided with the height of the franchised esports model — the Overwatch League and Call of Duty League were built on expensive franchise fees and city-based team structures. Both leagues faced significant challenges, including declining viewership, team withdrawals, and questions about long-term sustainability. The leagues contracted significantly after Petitti's departure.
