# Laura Miele — President, EA Entertainment

American business executive and 30-year Electronic Arts veteran. As President of EA Entertainment, she oversees all EA studios outside of its sports divisions, making her the second most powerful person at the publisher.

## Career arc

- **Education:** University of Oregon (BS Telecommunications); USC (MBA)
- **Joined EA 1996:** Varied roles in publishing, marketing, analytics
- **EVP Global Publishing:** Oversaw EA's worldwide publishing operations
- **COO & Chief Studios Officer (2021):** Second-in-command behind Andrew Wilson
- **President, EA Entertainment (2023):** BioWare, Respawn, DICE, Criterion, Motive, and more
