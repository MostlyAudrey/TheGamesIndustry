# Amy Hennig — Creator of Uncharted

**The most acclaimed narrative designer in AAA gaming.** Hennig created the Uncharted series, wrote its first three entries, and directed Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver. Her departure from Naughty Dog and the cancellation of her Star Wars project are among the most-discussed labour stories in AAA game development.

## Career arc

- **1990s:** Started in the game industry as a writer and designer. Joined Crystal Dynamics.
- **1997–2003:** Directed Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver and its sequels. Established reputation for story-driven action-adventure games.
- **2003:** Joined Naughty Dog as a writer/designer.
- **2007:** Uncharted: Drake's Fortune on PS3 — first entry in the series, establishing the "cinematic adventure" formula.
- **2009:** Uncharted 2: Among Thieves — widely praised for its narrative, pacing, and character development. Won numerous GOTY awards.
- **2011:** Uncharted 3: Drake's Deception.
- **2014:** Departed Naughty Dog during Uncharted 4 development. Naughty Dog cited a "natural transition" but Hennig's departure was widely reported to involve creative differences over the game's direction.
- **2015:** Joined EA as part of Visceral Games. Led development of a linear Star Wars action-adventure game (codenamed Project Ragtag).
- **2017:** EA cancelled Project Ragtag and closed Visceral Games — 100+ developers laid off.
- **2019:** Joined Skydance New Media (founded by former Disney executive) to develop narrative-driven games.
- **2024:** Announced as Studio Creative Director for Paramount Games Studio — a newly formed internal studio focused on high-budget narrative games.

- **Cinematic narrative standard:** Uncharted 2 set the standard for how AAA games could deliver movie-quality storytelling while remaining interactive.
- **Character writing:** Hennig's Nathan Drake, Elena Fisher, and Victor Sullivan are among the most beloved characters in gaming.
- **The "Uncharted 4" departure:** The circumstances of Hennig's departure from Naughty Dog remain one of the most talked-about behind-the-scenes stories in AAA game development. A key figure, Bruce Straley, also left after Uncharted 4.
- **Star Wars cancellation:** The closure of Visceral Games and cancellation of her Star Wars project demonstrated how studio management decisions can override creative talent even at the highest level.

## Labour context

- **Naughty Dog departure:** Hennig's exit was part of a pattern of departures of senior Naughty Dog creatives (see [[people/Bruce Straley]]). The studio's reported culture of perfectionism, long hours, and directorial control has been documented.
- **Visceral closure:** The cancellation of Ragtag and closure of Visceral was part of EA's broader shift toward service games. ~100 staff were reassigned or laid off.
- **Gender in AAA:** Hennig has been one of the few women to hold creative director roles at major AAA studios. Her career is frequently cited in discussions of gender representation in game development leadership.
- **No union context:** Hennig has not publicly discussed unionization of game workers.
