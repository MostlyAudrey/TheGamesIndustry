# Hideki Hayakawa

Hideki Hayakawa is the President and Representative Director of [[orgs/Konami]] Digital Entertainment, a position he assumed on April 1, 2015. He is also Senior Managing Executive Officer at Konami Group Corp.

## Career Arc

- Longtime Konami executive before promotion
- Took leadership of Konami Digital Entertainment in 2015, shortly after the company's public split with Hideo Kojima
- Implemented Konami's controversial "mobile-first" strategy, pivoting focus away from AAA console games toward mobile, pachinko, and wellness businesses
- Oversaw the restructuring that saw Kojima Productions become a division under Konami's umbrella before Kojima's departure
- Has maintained Konami's sports division (eFootball, Power Pros) as well as Yu-Gi-Oh! card game

## Key Decisions

- Spearheaded the 2015 "mobile-first" strategy that de-emphasized AAA console game development
- Oversaw the controversial treatment of Hideo Kojima and the cancellation of Silent Hills (2015)
- Led Konami's pivot toward esports, pachinko machines, and fitness clubs
- Under his leadership, Konami has continued to produce Metal Gear and Castlevania pachinko machines while largely abandoning AAA game development
- Maintained strong mobile and trading card game revenue

## Labor Context

- Central figure in Konami's post-Kojima labor controversies
- Reports of poor working conditions, low morale, and strict managerial controls at Konami post-2015
- Konami was accused of "treating Kojima like a hostage" and blacklisting him from industry events
- Japanese game industry generally non-union
- Konami's reputation among core gamers suffered significantly under his leadership
