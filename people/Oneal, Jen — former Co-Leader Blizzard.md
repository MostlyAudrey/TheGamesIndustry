# Jen Oneal — Former Co-Leader, Blizzard Entertainment

**Co-Leader of Blizzard Entertainment from August 2021 to November 2021.** The first woman to lead Blizzard, Oneal's tenure lasted just three months — the shortest of any Blizzard president. Her appointment and rapid departure crystallized the challenges of reforming Blizzard's culture from within.

## Career arc

- **2003:** Joined Activision Blizzard — spent 18 years at the company.
- **Vicarious Visions:** Became head of the studio, overseeing remaster and remake projects including Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy and Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 1+2.
- **Diablo/Overwatch:** Had development oversight for Diablo II: Resurrected and franchise oversight across Diablo and Overwatch.
- **2021-08:** Appointed co-leader of Blizzard alongside Mike Ybarra — intended as a signal of cultural reform.
- **2021-11:** Stepped down — saying she was "optimistic" about Blizzard's future but decided to leave.
- **2022:** Departed Activision Blizzard entirely.

## Why it matters

Oneal's brief tenure is a key data point in understanding ABK's leadership crisis:
- Her appointment was reactive — a response to the DFEH lawsuit, not a planned succession
- She was given co-leader status with Ybarra — an awkward power-sharing arrangement
- Her departure suggested she believed meaningful change was not possible within the existing structure
- The revolving door of Blizzard leadership in 2021 (Brack → Oneal/Ybarra → Ybarra alone) exemplified the company's instability
