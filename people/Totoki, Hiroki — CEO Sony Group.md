# Hiroki Totoki — President and CEO, Sony Group Corporation

Japanese businessman who rose through Sony's financial and strategic ranks over four decades. Co-founder of Sony Bank, turnaround leader of Sony Mobile, and architect of Sony's strategy shift toward entertainment. Became Sony Group CEO in April 2025 after being hand-picked by Kenichiro Yoshida.

## Career arc

- **1987:** Joins Sony after Waseda University.
- **2001:** Co-founds Sony Bank — his first major entrepreneurial initiative within Sony.
- **2005:** Senior Managing Director, Sony Communication Network.
- **2014:** President/CEO Sony Mobile — tasked with turning around the unprofitable handset division.
- **2017:** Sony CSO — begins shaping group-wide strategy.
- **2018:** CFO — key role in Sony's financial restructuring.
- **2023:** President, COO, CFO — and Chairman of SIE.
- **2024:** Interim CEO of SIE (April–May) bridging Jim Ryan's departure to the new co-CEO structure.
- **2025:** President and CEO, Sony Group Corporation. Elevates Nishino to solo SIE CEO.

## Role at Sony Interactive Entertainment

Totoki became Chairman of SIE in October 2023 and served as interim CEO from April to May 2024 after Jim Ryan's departure. He oversaw the transition to the dual-CEO structure (Hulst/Nishino) and then to Nishino as solo CEO in 2025. He remains Chairman of SIE.

## Labour context

Totoki's tenure at SIE was transitional and strategic. No documented labour controversies. The 2024 SIE restructuring (900 layoffs, London Studio closure) occurred under his Chairmanship but was driven by Ryan's outgoing strategy.
