# Daniel Alegre — Former President and COO, Activision Blizzard

**President and COO of Activision Blizzard from 2020 to 2022.** A longtime Google executive who joined ABK during a period of intense scrutiny and departed for the Web3 space as CEO of Yuga Labs.

## Career arc

- **~1998-2014:** 16 years at Google in various senior leadership roles — including president of Google Asia-Pacific and Google partnerships.
- **2020:** Joined Activision Blizzard as President and COO — overseeing international operations, franchise commercialization, and global publishing.
- **2022-12:** Announced departure to become CEO of Yuga Labs — the Web3 company behind Bored Ape Yacht Club NFTs and the Otherside metaverse platform.
- **2023-present:** CEO of Yuga Labs — navigating the post-bubble Web3 landscape.

## ABK era

Alegre joined ABK during a period of growth but left before the full impact of the DFEH lawsuit and unionization wave reshaped the company. His role focused on international operations and franchise commercialization — the business side rather than studio management.
