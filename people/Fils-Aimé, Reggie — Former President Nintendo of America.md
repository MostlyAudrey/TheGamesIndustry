# Reggie Fils-Aimé

American businessman. Former President and COO of Nintendo of America (2006–2019). Widely recognized as the public face of Nintendo in the West during a transformative era. Born in Queens, New York in 1961 of Haitian immigrant parents.

Career:
- Career began at Procter & Gamble and other consumer packaged goods companies
- Joined Nintendo of America as EVP of Sales & Marketing in 2003
- Became President of NOA in 2006 — one of the few non-Japanese presidents of Nintendo's American division
- Presided over the Wii (2006), DS (2004), and 3DS (2011) eras
- Led NOA through the challenging Wii U (2012–2016) era
- Retired from Nintendo in April 2019
- "My body is ready" — famous E3 2004 moment
- Joined GameStop's board of directors in 2019
- Currently on the board of GameStop (resigned in 2022?), Spin Master, and other companies

## Labor context

As NOA president during a period of strong labor protections:
- No major labor disputes during his tenure
- NOA was known for good working conditions during his era
- The more serious NOA labor issues emerged post-retirement (2021-2022 sexual harassment investigation, contractor layoffs)

## Industry influence

Fils-Aimé is one of the most prominent Black executives in the gaming industry. His "Regginator" persona made him a beloved public figure. His memoir "Disrupting the Game" (2022) was well-received.
