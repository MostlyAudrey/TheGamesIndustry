# Martin Lau

Martin Lau (born 1973 in Beijing, China; grew up in Hong Kong) is the President of [[orgs/Tencent]], responsible for overseeing the company's day-to-day operations, corporate strategy, investments, mergers and acquisitions, and investor relations.

## Career Arc

- Educated at University of Michigan (B.S., Electronic Engineering) and Stanford University (M.S., Electronic Engineering)
- Worked at Goldman Sachs as a technology investment banker
- Joined Tencent in 2005 as Chief Strategy and Investment Officer
- Promoted to President in 2006 — one of the fastest executive rises in the company
- Appointed Executive Director in 2007
- Architect of Tencent's prolific investment portfolio, overseeing billions in gaming and tech acquisitions
- Described as the strategic mind behind Tencent's expansion beyond China into global gaming

## Key Decisions

- Led Tencent's landmark acquisitions and investments: full acquisition of [[orgs/Riot Games]] (2011/2015), majority stake in [[orgs/Supercell]] (2016), major stake in [[orgs/Epic Games]]
- Structured Tencent's investment thesis: buy stakes in successful foreign studios, maintain operational independence, provide publishing and distribution expertise
- Guided Tencent through regulatory challenges with Chinese government over gaming monopolies and data security

## Labor Context

- Part of senior leadership during Riot Games' workplace scandals (2018-2019 walkout, discrimination lawsuit, forced arbitration controversy)
- Tencent as parent company was criticized for lack of oversight at Riot during these events
- No direct labor controversies personally reported
