# Frontier Developments plc

**British video game developer and publisher based in Cambridge, England, founded by David Braben.** Known for the Elite Dangerous space sim, Planet Coaster/Zoo tycoon sims, F1 Manager series, and Jurassic World Evolution.

## Organisational review and layoffs (October 2023)

On October 17, 2023, Frontier announced an organisational review following "a period of disappointing financial performance and more challenging industry conditions." Key elements:

- **20% cost reduction target** through recruitment freeze, spending cuts, and redundancies
- **Exact number of layoffs undisclosed** (speculation ~100-160; Revelio Labs data shows ~140 headcount reduction from ~855 to ~715)
- **Frontier Foundry closed** — the third-party publishing arm was shut down after a strategic review
- **F1 Manager 2023 underperformance** cited as a major factor; lower-than-expected sales at launch
- Review expected to conclude by early 2024

The company reported £104.6M revenue (down 8%) with an operating loss of £26.6M for the full year ended May 31, 2023 (compared to £1.5M operating profit the year before).

## Post-layoff trajectory

Despite the cost-cutting measures, Frontier continued to release major titles:
- **Warhammer Age of Sigmar: Realms of Ruin** (November 2023) — RTS
- **Planet Coaster 2** (November 2024) — sequel to popular theme park sim
- Continued support for Elite Dangerous and Planet Zoo

By 2026, headcount had stabilised at ~728 employees per Revelio Labs.

## Union status
**No known union organizing at Frontier Developments.** No reported labor disputes.
