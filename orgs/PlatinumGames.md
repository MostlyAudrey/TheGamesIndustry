# PlatinumGames

**Japanese video game developer headquartered in Osaka.** Known for high-quality action games including Bayonetta, NieR:Automata, and Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance. Founded in 2006 by former Capcom Clover Studio staff (Hideki Kamiya, Atsushi Inaba, Tatsuya Minami).

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Studio in significant distress. Co-founders departed (Kamiya 2023, Inaba reportedly reduced role by 2024). Multiple veteran directors confirmed left by Feb 2025 — including leads of Bayonetta, NieR:Automata, and Astral Chain. Series creator Kamiya formed new studio Clovers under Capcom and is making Okami 2 — not with Platinum. CEO Inaba insists Bayonetta series will continue, but the studio has lost most of its key creative talent.

## Lost major projects
- **Scalebound (Microsoft):** Cancelled 2017 — high-profile Xbox One exclusive
- **Bayonetta 3 (2022):** Despite commercial success, development was troubled; Kamiya's departure followed
- **Babylon's Fall (2022):** Live-service game; commercially failed; servers shut down 2023
- **Sol Cresta (2022):** Niche arcade shooter; limited commercial impact
- **Project GG:** Announced 2020 as third in Kamiya's superhero trilogy; status unknown post-Kamiya departure
- **Okami sequel:** Being developed by Clovers (Kamiya's new studio, funded by Capcom/M-Two) — not Platinum

## Financial and operational concerns
- Studio reported no major hit since NieR:Automata (2017) that it directly developed
- Bayonetta 3 (2022) sold well but development was reportedly troubled
- Babylon's Fall (2022) was a critical and commercial failure
- Multiple reported project cancellations by publishers
- The studio appears to have struggled to secure major publishing deals post-2020
- By 2025-2026, the studio's future is uncertain
