# CyberConnect2

**Japanese video game developer; creators of the .hack// series and Naruto: Ultimate Ninja Storm franchise.** Headquartered in Fukuoka, Japan. Founded 1996 by Hiroshi Matsuyama. Known for anime-licensed games and original IP.

## Independence story (2021 backstory)

In 2021, CyberConnect2 made the unusual decision to go independent again. CEO Hiroshi Matsuyama spent approximately ¥20 billion of the company's cash reserves to buy back majority stakes held by existing investors. The decision reflected Matsuyama's desire to control the company's direction and prioritize original IP development over publisher-commissioned licensed work.

**Key quote from Matsuyama (2021):** "I want to create original titles. I don't want CyberConnect2 to be a work-for-hire studio forever."

This independence has allowed the studio to:
- Develop original IP (Fuga: Melodies of Steel trilogy)
- Move at its own pace without investor pressure
- Maintain headcount through project transitions
- Invest in employee mental health programs

## Founder: Hiroshi Matsuyama

Matsuyama is an unusually public-facing Japanese game executive:
- Regularly livestreams and posts on social media
- Opened Kawamura Clinic (2022) — a free mental health clinic for game industry workers
- Speaks candidly about burnout, developer welfare, and the challenges of running a studio
- Known for passionate, sometimes eccentric public presentations

## Labor overview

- **No union:** No union organizing activity documented
- **No layoffs:** No documented layoffs in the 2022-2026 period
- **Mental health focus:** Unique among Japanese studios for founder's investment in mental health infrastructure
- **Original IP pivot:** The shift from licensed work to original IP is partly driven by labor concerns — Matsuyama wanted to avoid the crunch-heavy cycles of publisher-commissioned licensed games
