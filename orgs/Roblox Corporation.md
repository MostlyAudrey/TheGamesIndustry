# Roblox Corporation

**American game platform company — San Mateo, CA. Creator of Roblox, a UGC gaming platform with 70M+ daily active users.** Public (NYSE: RBLX) since 2021. Founded 2004 by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel. 2,474 employees.

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Roblox remains a massive platform but faces growing labor scrutiny. The child labor lawsuits (filed May-June 2026) represent novel legal territory — testing whether child labor laws apply to the creator economy.

## The Roblox Economy and Labor Questions

Roblox operates a unique economic model that creates significant labor questions:

**Developer Exchange (DevEx):** Allows creators to convert Robux (platform currency) into real currency. Minimum payout threshold. Terms of service heavily favor Roblox.

**Talent Hub:** Job board where creators can hire other users (including minors) to work on experiences.

**The controversy:**
- Children as young as 11 allegedly develop games for 40+ hours/week for little/no real pay
- Roblox takes a large cut of all Robux transactions
- The platform markets itself as a "play" destination while encouraging millions of hours of unpaid or underpaid labor
- AI training on user-generated content adds another layer (lawsuit requests profits from AI training on children's content be placed in trust)

## The 2026 Child Labor Lawsuits

| Lawsuit | Filed | Plaintiffs | Key Allegations |
|---|---|---|---|
| John Doe B.D. v. Roblox | May 2026 | Minor plaintiff (11-13yo) | 40+ hr/week for 2+ years; advanced Lua scripting + game design; little/no pay through DevEx |
| Weitz & Luxenberg class action | June 2026 | Class of minor developers | Allegations of state/federal child labor law violations; "multi-billion-dollar empire on the unpaid and underpaid labor of children" |

**Legal novelty:** The suits test whether 1930s-era child labor laws apply to digital creator economies. Outcome could set precedent across the entire UGC platform industry.

## Moderator Labor Conditions

Beyond the child labor issue, Roblox relies on a large content moderation workforce:
- Mix of AI and human moderators
- Contract moderator conditions have been criticized
- Moderators review millions of pieces of user-generated content daily
- Reports of psychological harm from exposure to toxic content
- No known union organizing among moderators (unlike Meta/Facebook moderators in Kenya)

## Union Status

**No known union organizing** at Roblox Corporation. Factors:
- Silicon Valley culture with high compensation for full-time staff
- Contract moderator workforce (more typical union target) hasn't organized
- Creator workforce is atomized, global, and mostly minors — hard to organize
- Anti-union Silicon Valley employer culture
