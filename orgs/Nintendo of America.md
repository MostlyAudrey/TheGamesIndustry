# Nintendo of America

**North American publishing and operations arm of [[orgs/Nintendo]] Co., Ltd.** Headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Established 1980. Responsible for sales, marketing, localization, product testing, and customer support across North America.

## Labor overview

| Issue | Status | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Union** | None | No union organizing activity; no certification election filed |
| **NLRB complaints** | 2 filed (2022, 2025-2026) | 2022 settled ($25,910 + posting); 2025-2026 pending |
| **Contractor layoffs** | ~100+ (2024) | Product Testing reorganization |
| **Harassment claims** | Documented (2022) | Kotaku investigation — sexism, harassment, wage inequity |
| **Full-time layoffs** | None documented | No permanent employee layoffs |

## Contract structures

Nintendo of America relies heavily on contract workers for its product testing division. Key features:
- **"Red badge" system** — distinct badges identify contractors vs full-time employees
- **Lower wages:** Contractors paid less than full-time employees for equivalent work
- **Limited benefits:** Contractors not eligible for full benefits package
- **Harassment vulnerability:** Kotaku (2022) reported contractors unable to report harassment from full-time employees without retaliation risk
- **Periodic reduction:** Contractor headcount varies based on product testing cycle

## Leadership

- **Doug Bowser** (President, 2019-2026): Notable for being named Bowser while leading Nintendo — coincidence played up as running joke. Presided over the successful Switch lifecycle and early Switch 2 launch.
- **Reggie Fils-Aimé** (President, 2006-2019): Oversaw Wii and Switch launches; retired 2019.
- **Previous:** Minoru Arakawa (first president, 1980-2002), Tatsumi Kimishima (2002-2006).
