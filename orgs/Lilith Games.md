# Lilith Games

## Overview

Lilith Games is a Chinese mobile game developer and publisher founded in 2013 by Wang Xinwen. The company is known for hit mobile titles including *AFK Arena*, *Dislyte*, *Farlight 84*, and *Rise of Kingdoms*. Lilith is one of China's most valuable private game companies (unicorn, valued at ~$22 billion RMB as of 2024).

## Labor Events

### 2022–2023 layoffs
Per Chinese media outlet GameLook, Lilith Games cut staff in both 2022 and 2023. A study from July 2024 found that Lilith Games reduced its workforce by 238 people in 2023, making it one of the largest staff reductions among Chinese game companies that year. [[sources/2024-07-23 — gameworldobserver-china-layoffs-2023]]

### December 2024 layoffs (~40 employees)
In December 2024, Lilith Games laid off approximately 40 employees — roughly 40% of the ~100-person *Project Party* development team — as the project underwent rework. The company had previously cut staff in 2022 and 2023. [[sources/2024-12-04 — gamedeveloper-lilith-games-layoffs]] [[sources/2024-12-02 — gamesindustry-lilith-layoffs]]

### Labor organizing
No known union organizing activity at Lilith Games. Labor organizing in China's video game industry is effectively suppressed due to government restrictions on independent unions.
