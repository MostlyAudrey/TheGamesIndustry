# Beenox

**Canadian game developer based in Quebec City — primary Activision studio for PC porting and Call of Duty support.** Founded in 2000, acquired by Activision in 2005. Beenox has been a key support studio for the Call of Duty franchise, handling PC ports and additional development support since ~2018.

## Labor status

As of October 2026, no known union organizing has occurred at Beenox. The studio falls under Quebec labor law which differs from US labor law — unionization in Quebec requires a different process through the province's labour board. Canadian video game unions have been slower to emerge than in the US, though Quebec has seen organizing at Ubisoft Montreal (not yet unionized) and Ubisoft Halifax (certified Dec 2025, since closed).

Beenox workers may or may not be covered by the broader Activision QA bargaining unit depending on whether Quebec workers were included in the US-focused unit.

## Key games
- Studio supports Call of Duty franchise: PC ports of Black Ops Cold War, Modern Warfare II, Modern Warfare III, Black Ops 6
- Previously developed Crash Team Racing Nitro-Fueled (2019)
- Previously developed Spider-Man and X-Men games
