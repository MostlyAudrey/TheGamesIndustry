# Activision Blizzard

**The holding company formed by the 2008 merger of Activision and Vivendi Games (Blizzard).** Activision Blizzard (ABK — Activision, Blizzard, King) was the largest standalone game publisher by revenue before its $68.7B acquisition by Microsoft in 2023. The company was the center of the game industry's largest labor disputes and unionization wave.

## Structure

Activision Blizzard operated through three primary operating segments:

- **Activision Publishing** — Call of Duty franchise (annual rotation: Infinity Ward, Treyarch, Sledgehammer), plus Crash Bandicoot, Spyro. President: Rob Kostich.
- **Blizzard Entertainment** — Warcraft, Overwatch, Diablo, StarCraft, Hearthstone. President: Johanna Faries (since Feb 2024).
- **King Digital** — Candy Crush Saga, mobile games. ~2,000 employees in Stockholm. President: Tjodolf Sommestad.

## Pre-acquisition labor crisis (2021-2023)

The company faced a watershed labor crisis beginning in July 2021 when the California DFEH filed a lawsuit alleging "frat boy" culture of systemic sexual harassment, discrimination, and unequal pay. The lawsuit triggered:

- Employee walkout organized by A Better ABK (July 28, 2021)
- Departures of Blizzard president J. Allen Brack (Aug 2021) and Jen Oneal (Nov 2021)
- Raven Software QA strike and union formation (Dec 2021 — May 2022)
- Blizzard Albany QA union formation (Jul — Dec 2022)

Bobby Kotick, CEO since 1991, was named in WSJ reporting as aware of misconduct and doing little. He announced he would step down once the Microsoft acquisition closed.

## Post-acquisition labor landscape (2023-present)

After the Microsoft acquisition closed, the 2022 labor neutrality agreement activated and the unionization wave accelerated dramatically. However, outcomes have diverged sharply:

- **Blizzard** secured a historic first contract for ~1,900 workers in September 2026 with landmark AI and remote work provisions.
- **Activision QA** has been unable to secure a first contract after 2.5 years, with workforce declining from 600 to 400, and authorized a strike in October 2026.
- The neutrality agreement's expiration in Fall 2025 is cited as a key factor in this divergence.
