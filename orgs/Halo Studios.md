# Halo Studios

**Formerly 343 Industries. First-party Xbox studio founded in 2007 to manage the Halo franchise after Bungie's departure.** HQ Redmond, WA. Rebranded to Halo Studios in October 2024 as part of a strategic reset.

**Central case study in studio atrophy under Microsoft:** From a ~500+ person studio managing one of gaming's biggest franchises, Halo Studios has been reduced to roughly 20 staffers by late 2026. The studio faced repeated layoff waves (Jan 2023, Jan 2024, Sep 2026), a high-profile leadership exodus (Bonnie Ross, Joseph Staten), and ultimately lost the Halo franchise to Activision. No union organizing has been reported.
