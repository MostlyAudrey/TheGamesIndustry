# inXile Entertainment

**American video game developer with studios in Tustin, California and New Orleans, Louisiana.** Founded in 2002 by Brian Fargo, co-founder of Interplay Entertainment. Known for the Wasteland series, The Bard's Tale, and Torment: Tides of Numenera. Acquired by Microsoft in 2018.

## History

inXile Entertainment was founded by Brian Fargo in 2002, building on his legacy as co-founder of Interplay Entertainment. The studio gained prominence through the Kickstarter-funded Wasteland 2 (2014), which revived the classic CRPG franchise. Wasteland 3 (2020) was the studio's first post-acquisition release and received critical acclaim.

The studio operates two locations: Tustin, CA (main studio) and New Orleans, LA (second studio opened in 2021). The next major title is Clockwork Revolution, a steampunk-themed first-person RPG announced at the 2023 Xbox Games Showcase.

## Labor

No union organizing has been reported at inXile Entertainment (as of 2026-10-18). The small, stable team has not experienced known layoffs. As a California employer with a Louisiana second location, it is subject to state WARN Act requirements.
