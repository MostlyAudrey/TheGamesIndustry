# Tango Gameworks

**Japanese developer founded by Shinji Mikami, closed by Microsoft and saved by Krafton.** Founded 2010 in Tokyo. Acquired by ZeniMax in 2010. Known for The Evil Within series, Ghostwire: Tokyo, and the surprise hit Hi-Fi Rush (2023).

## The closure-and-rescue story

Tango Gameworks' journey is one of the most dramatic in recent gaming industry history:

1. **May 7, 2024:** Microsoft announced the closure of Tango Gameworks along with Arkane Austin and Alpha Dog Games, as part of a "reprioritization of titles and resources" at Bethesda/ZeniMax.
2. **August 1, 2024:** Krafton (PUBG publisher) acquired Tango Gameworks and the Hi-Fi Rush IP from Microsoft.
3. **August 12, 2024:** Deal announced publicly. Approximately 50 of the original ~105 staff were brought back under Krafton's Japan subsidiary.

The acquisition was Krafton's 13th game development studio and part of an aggressive M&A strategy.

## Labor status

Tango operates under Japanese labor law. There have been no union drives at the studio. The studio was not unionized under Microsoft/ZeniMax ownership.

## Key franchises

- **The Evil Within** (2014) — Survival horror directed by Shinji Mikami
- **The Evil Within 2** (2017) — Open-ended survival horror
- **Ghostwire: Tokyo** (2022) — First-person action-adventure set in Tokyo
- **Hi-Fi Rush** (2023) — Rhythm-action game; surprise announcement and launch; critical acclaim
