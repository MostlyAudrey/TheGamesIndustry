# Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc.

**Japanese video game publisher and the digital/game publishing arm of the Bandai Namco Group.** Headquartered in Minato, Tokyo. Publishes major franchises: Tekken, Gundam, Soulcalibur, Ace Combat, The Idolmaster, and serves as co-publisher for [[orgs/FromSoftware]] titles (Elden Ring, Dark Souls, Armored Core VI).

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Undergoing structural consolidation. 2024 oidashi beya scandal revealed systematic pressure-to-resign tactics at Bandai Namco Studios. BNEI headcount declining since 2023. Major group-wide restructuring announced for 2026 with Sony investment involvement.

## Corporate structure

Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc. is the **operational video game publishing subsidiary** of Bandai Namco Holdings (the consolidated conglomerate). This distinguishes BNEI from the existing [[orgs/Bandai Namco]] page which covers the parent holding company.

- **Bandai Namco Holdings Inc.** — Parent conglomerate (TYO: 7832); also covers toys, arcade, film, music
- **Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc. (BNEI)** — Digital/game publishing subsidiary (this entity)
- **Bandai Namco Studios Inc.** — Internal game development subsidiary

## Oidashi Beya (Expulsion Rooms) scandal

In October 2024, Bloomberg reported that approximately **200 out of 1,300 Bandai Namco Studios employees** were moved to "expulsion rooms" (oidashi beya) — given no tasks and pressured to resign. Nearly half (~100) had voluntarily resigned by the time of reporting. Bandai Namco denied the practice, calling it a standard reassignment process.

This followed cancellation of several game titles including One Piece, Naruto, and a Nintendo-licensed project. The oidashi beya is a well-known Japanese corporate downsizing tactic used to avoid formal layoffs.

## FromSoftware relationship
- BNEI historically co-published and co-owned Elden Ring IP with FromSoftware
- **Feb 2024:** Reports indicated Elden Ring IP rights transferred fully to FromSoftware, ending co-ownership
- Bandai Namco remains a publishing partner; FromSoftware continues to operate independently
- Bandai Namco does not own FromSoftware — FromSoftware is majority-owned by Kadokawa Corporation

## Union context
- **No evidence** of union formation or union organizing activity at BNEI or Bandai Namco in Japan during 2022-2026
- Labor practices in Japan occur within the standard enterprise union framework; no independent game developer union identified
- The oidashi beya practice is specifically designed to avoid triggering Japan's strict layoff protections
