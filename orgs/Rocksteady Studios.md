# Rocksteady Studios

## Overview

- **Full name:** Rocksteady Studios Limited
- **Location:** London, England (founded in North London, later moved)
- **Founded:** December 13, 2004
- **Parent:** Warner Bros. Games (since 2010, majority stake)
- **Founders:** Jamie Walker, Sefton Hill (both left end of 2022)
- **Key people:** Nathan Burlow (co-studio head), Darius Sadeghian (co-studio head)
- **Employees:** 214 (as of end of 2024, down from ~250 pre-layoffs)
- **Union status:** No union
- **Website:** rocksteadyltd.com

## History

Founded by Jamie Walker and Sefton Hill, former staff of Argonaut Games (which shut down in 2004). Publisher SCi Entertainment invested early, acquiring 25.1% of shares and loaning £250,000.

**Batman: Arkham era (2009–2015):** Landed the Batman license through Eidos in 2007. Arkham Asylum was a breakthrough hit, leading Warner Bros. to acquire a majority stake in 2010. Followed by Arkham City (2011) and Arkham Knight (2015), both critical and commercial successes. The Arkham trilogy is considered among the greatest superhero game series ever made.

**Post-Arkham struggles (2016–2023):** After Arkham Knight, the studio worked on a multiplayer puzzle game (codenamed "Stones") that was shelved. Warner Bros. directed them to make a Suicide Squad game instead. Development began around 2017 and took 7 years — the studio had very little experience in the live-service looter-shooter genre. Multiple postponements.

**Co-founders exit (2022):** Jamie Walker and Sefton Hill left Rocksteady at the end of 2022 after 18+ years. They formed Hundred Star Games in January 2024.

**Suicide Squad flop (2024):** Released February 2024. Warner Bros. disclosed a $200 million impairment charge by May 2024. The game was a live-service looter-shooter that failed to find an audience. WB provided post-launch support for about a year before ending it.

**Layoffs (2024):** Two rounds — first QA team reduced by half (September 2024), then programming and artist teams (late 2024). Employee count dropped to 214.

## Controversies

- **Sexual harassment allegations (2020):** The Guardian reported that more than half the female employees wrote to executives in 2018 about sexual harassment, slurs, and derogatory behavior. Rocksteady said they addressed the issues. The following day, Rocksteady posted an "unsolicited" letter from some employees disputing the claims.
- **Workplace culture:** Reports of crunch and management issues during Suicide Squad development.

## Financial

- 2024: Suicide Squad caused ~$200M loss for Warner Bros.
- The studio had largely coasted on Arkham reputation before the Suicide Squad failure.
- Co-founders Hill and Walker reportedly made tens of millions from WB acquisition over the years.

## Future

As of late 2026, Rocksteady is still operating but significantly reduced. The studio briefly worked on a Hogwarts Legacy director's cut (later cancelled). Long-term future is uncertain following the Suicide Squad debacle and WB Games restructuring.
