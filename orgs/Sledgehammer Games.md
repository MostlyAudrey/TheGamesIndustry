# Sledgehammer Games

**Call of Duty developer co-founded by Dead Space creators Glen Schofield and Michael Condrey.** Sledgehammer Games was founded in 2009 after the duo left EA Redwood Shores. The studio initially co-developed Modern Warfare 3 (2011) with Infinity Ward, then led Advanced Warfare (2014) — the first Call of Duty developed by a studio outside of the Infinity Ward/Treyarch rotation.

## Labor history

Sledgehammer Games has no known union organizing as of October 2026. Like other Activision studios, it has been part of the broader Call of Duty crunch cycle documented across the franchise. The studio experienced significant leadership turnover: co-founders Schofield and Condrey both left in 2017-2018. Condrey joined 2K Games; Schofield formed Striking Distance Studios (The Callisto Protocol) before departing that studio in 2023.

Sledgehammer was the lead developer for Call of Duty: Vanguard (2021) and co-developed Modern Warfare III (2023). The studio has approximately 400 employees.

## Key games

- Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare (2014)
- Call of Duty: WWII (2017)

- Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III (2023, co-developed)
