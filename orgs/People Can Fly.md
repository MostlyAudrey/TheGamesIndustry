# People Can Fly

Polish game developer known for *Bulletstorm*, *Gears of War: Judgment*, and *Outriders*. Has multiple studios in Warsaw, Kraków, and Newcastle (UK).

## Capital & Ownership
- Publicly traded on Warsaw Stock Exchange; Tencent is largest shareholder (~22%).
- Tencent acquired a 22% stake in 2021, with options to increase to 30% by 2025.

## Labor Record (2022–2026)

| Date | Event | Detail |
|------|-------|--------|
| Jan 2024 | Project restructuring | "Several dozen" roles cut as part of shift from co-development to own IP |
| Jan 2025 | Layoffs (~30-40 employees) | 30+ roles cut, ~12% of workforce; citing market conditions and project reset |
| Mar 2025 | Project cancellation / delayed | *Project Victoria* (AAA action-adventure, published by Sony) — development paused then cancelled; project had been in development since 2022 |

**Labor organizing:** Employees are covered by Polish labor law. PCF employees joined the [[orgs/Związek Pracowników Branży Gier]] (Polish Gamedev Workers Union) as part of cross-studio organizing. Exact timing unclear — most organizing at PCF reported by 2024.

## Headcount trajectory
- 2022: ~300 (peak)
- 2025: ~262

## Pipeline
- *Project Bifrost* (self-published, third-person action RPG — scaled back)
- Co-development agreements (unannounced titles)

## Notes
- Studio was acquired by and then split from Epic Games (2011–2015) before going public.
- *Outriders* (2021) was a commercial success but PCF did not retain the IP (owned by Square Enix).
- Studio has been struggling to establish a sustainable self-publishing pipeline since the Tencent investment.
