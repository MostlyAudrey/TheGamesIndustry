# Ubisoft Paris

French game developer, founded 1992 as one of Ubisoft's original studios. Primarily known for the Just Dance franchise and various Assassin's Creed development support. Approximately 600 employees pre-reductions.

## Labor record

| Year | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 2024 | Ubisoft global restructuring | Paris affected; department-level reductions |
| 2025 | Solidarity strike actions | Paris team participated in STJV-led actions |

## Union presence

Ubisoft Paris has **STJV** presence among its workforce. Paris employees participated in the February 2025 strike actions alongside Montreal.
