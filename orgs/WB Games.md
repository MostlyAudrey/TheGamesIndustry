# WB Games (Warner Bros. Games)

## Overview

- **Full name:** Warner Bros. Games (formerly Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment, 2004–2021)
- **Parent company:** Warner Bros. Discovery (2022–2026) → Skydance Interactive (2026–present)
- **Headquarters:** Burbank, California
- **Founded:** January 14, 2004
- **Type:** Division / Publisher
- **Website:** warnerbrosgames.com

## Key Leadership

- **David Zaslav** — President & CEO of Warner Bros. Discovery. Driving force behind restructuring and debt reduction. Announced plans to focus on four core gaming IPs (Nov 2024): Harry Potter, Game of Thrones, Mortal Kombat, DC.
- **David Haddad** — Head of WB Games, exited January 2025.
- **Gunnar Wiedenfels** — CFO of WBD, cited MultiVersus failure as additive $100M loss on top of $200M games write-down in 2024.

## Financial Context

- WBD created with **$50+ billion in debt** from the AT&T WarnerMedia/Discovery merger in 2022.
- **2023:** Hogwarts Legacy became best-selling game of the year worldwide (22M+ units by end of 2023, 40M+ by Dec 2025). Major financial bright spot.
- **2024:** Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League → $200M impairment charge. MultiVersus → additional $100M loss. Games division overall had ~$300M in losses/write-downs.
- **2024:** WBD reported games revenue decline despite Hogwarts Legacy's continued performance.
- **Restructuring (Dec 2024):** WBD split into two divisions: Streaming & Studios and Global Linear Networks.
- **June 2025:** WB Games formally restructured to focus on 4 core IPs: Harry Potter, Game of Thrones, Mortal Kombat, DC.
- **2026:** WBD acquired by Paramount Skydance.
