# Naughty Dog

**Naughty Dog** is a Santa Monica, CA-based AAA game developer and Sony's flagship first-party studio. Best known for the Uncharted and The Last of Us series. The studio went from jam studio in the 1980s to Sony's premier narrative-driven studio, but has faced growing labor challenges including crunch reports, contractor layoffs, and project cancellations.

## Ownership & Structure

- **Sony Interactive Entertainment** acquired Naughty Dog in 2001. Originally independent (founded 1984).
- Operates as a wholly owned subsidiary within PlayStation Studios.
- Co-presidents Evan Wells (retired late 2023) and Neil Druckmann. Druckmann is now sole studio head.

## Labor Timeline

### 2020 — The Last of Us Part II crunch
Widely reported crunch culture at the studio during development of The Last of Us Part II. Multiple reports described forced overtime, including developers working 12-hour days and weekends.

### October 2023 — Contractor layoffs (~25)
Naughty Dog laid off at least 25 contract developers working on the troubled The Last of Us multiplayer spin-off. The project was described as "on ice" and development was reportedly paused.

### February 2024 — SIE global layoffs
Further reductions at Naughty Dog as part of SIE's 900-person layoff wave across PlayStation Studios. Specific number at Naughty Dog undisclosed, but reported as "reductions" affecting the studio alongside Insomniac, Guerrilla, and Firesprite.

### December 2024 — The Last of Us Online cancelled
Sony and Naughty Dog officially announced the cancellation of The Last of Us Online (formerly known as Factions). The multiplayer project had been in development for years and was described by Druckmann as requiring unsustainable levels of ongoing support. 100+ developers had been working on it; many were reassigned to Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet.

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Naughty Dog as of October 2026.

Despite significant labor events (crunch, contractor layoffs, project cancellation), no public unionization drive has been reported. The studio remains non-union.

## Crunch history
- **The Last of Us (2013)**: Reports of 12-hour days in final months
- **Uncharted 4 (2016)**: Director openly acknowledged crunch
- **The Last of Us Part II (2020)**: Extensive reports of mandatory overtime including weekends
- Studio has publicly said it's working to improve work-life balance

## Edge Notes
- One of the few first-party studios with *both* major external criticism for labor practices AND no union presence
- Intergalactic represents first new IP since The Last of Us (2013)
- Neil Druckmann's rising influence at Sony — increasingly involved across PlayStation Studios creative direction
- Naughty Dog was not affected by the 2025-2026 Bend/Bluepoint layoffs
