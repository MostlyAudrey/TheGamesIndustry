# Związek Pracowników Branży Gier

**Game Industry Workers Union** — the first labor union in the Polish video game industry. Founded in **October 2023** by employees of CD Projekt RED, but open to all Polish game industry workers.

**Key facts:**
- Founded by CD Projekt RED employees in October 2023
- Operates under **OZZ Inicjatywa Pracownicza** (Ogólnopolski Związek Zawodowy Inicjatywa Pracownicza), a nationwide independent union federation
- Not limited to CDPR — any Polish game industry worker can join
- Monthly membership fee: **16 PLN** (~$4 USD)
- Management was informed after documentation was complete; reportedly no negative reaction
- Focus areas: worker education about legal rights, employment security, fair treatment, safe working conditions
- Formation timing: just before Phantom Liberty release (September 2023)
