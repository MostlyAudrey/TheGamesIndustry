## History

**Independent era (1995–2005):** Founded by four former Sculptured Software programmers. Developed games across multiple platforms for various publishers.

**Disney era (2005–2016):** Acquired by Buena Vista Games (Disney) in 2005. Developed Toy Story 3 and the Disney Infinity franchise. Disney Infinity was a major toys-to-life franchise rivaling Skylanders. However, the toys-to-life market declined sharply, and Disney closed Disney Interactive Studios in May 2016, shutting down Avalanche.

**Revival under WB (2017–present):** Warner Bros. acquired and re-opened Avalanche in January 2017, with John Blackburn returning as CEO. The studio began work on Hogwarts Legacy.

**Hogwarts Legacy (2023):** Released February 2023. Became the best-selling game of the year worldwide, selling 22M+ copies in 2023 and 40M+ by December 2025. The game revitalized the Harry Potter video game franchise and became the biggest single success in WB Games history.

## Controversies

- **Troy Leavitt controversy (2021):** Game director Troy Leavitt resigned after backlash over his social media posts supporting cultural appropriation and Gamergate. He claimed it was not the reason for his departure, and WB declined to comment.

## Financial

- Hogwarts Legacy is likely WB Games' most profitable title ever
- The success validated WB's strategy of focusing on major IP
- As of 2025, Hogwarts Legacy had sold 34M+ copies by March 2025, and 40M+ by December 2025
- Avalanche is positioned as WB's crown jewel studio

## Future

- Hogwarts Legacy sequel is widely expected, though not officially confirmed
- The studio's focus will likely remain on Harry Potter/Wizarding World games
- Portkey Games (WB's HP publishing label) works closely with Avalanche
- Named as beneficiary of WB's refocus on Harry Potter IP (June 2025 restructuring)
