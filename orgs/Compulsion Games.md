# Compulsion Games

**Independent game developer based in Montreal, Quebec, Canada.** Founded in 2009 by Guillaume Provost. Acquired by Microsoft in 2018 as part of Xbox Game Studios, Compulsion developed the cult hits Contrast and We Happy Few before creating the critically acclaimed South of Midnight (released April 2025). The studio spun out from Xbox in July 2026 as an independent entity, retaining its IP.

## History

Compulsion Games was founded in Montreal in 2009 by industry veteran Guillaume Provost. The studio's first title, Contrast (2013), was a shadow-themed puzzle-platformer published by Focus Home Interactive that gained positive reviews for its art direction and mechanics.

The studio's second game, **We Happy Few**, began development as a survival game set in a dystopian alternate-history 1960s England. It entered Steam Early Access in 2016 and was acquired by Microsoft in June 2018 at E3, alongside Ninja Theory, Playground Games, and Undead Labs. The acquisition provided resources to complete We Happy Few, which was fully released in August 2019.

## South of Midnight

Announced at the Xbox Showcase in June 2023, South of Midnight received its full gameplay debut at the Xbox Games Showcase on June 9, 2024. The game launched on April 8, 2025 for Xbox Series X|S and Windows PC.

South of Midnight is a third-person action-adventure game featuring protagonist Hazel, a young woman with magical weaving abilities, navigating a dark fantasy version of the American Deep South inspired by Southern folklore. The game received critical acclaim and won Game of the Year and Experience of the Year at the Games for Change Awards in July 2026.

## Independence (2026)

In June 2026, GamesIndustry.biz reported that Xbox was considering closing Compulsion Games, along with Double Fine and Ninja Theory reportedly at risk. The studio entered negotiations and by July 2026 had successfully spun out as an independent studio, retaining the rights to Contrast, We Happy Few, and South of Midnight.

## Labor

Compulsion Games is a small studio estimated at 80–100 employees. No union activity has been reported at the studio. No layoffs have been documented since the Microsoft acquisition. The studio has maintained a relatively stable headcount through its transitions.
