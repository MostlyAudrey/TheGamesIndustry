# MachineGames

**Swedish first-person action developer, founded 2009.** HQ Uppsala with a Sundsvall office. Acquired by ZeniMax Media in 2010. Known for revitalizing the Wolfenstein franchise and developing Indiana Jones and the Great Circle.

## Labor status

MachineGames operates under Swedish labor law, which provides strong worker protections through collective bargaining agreements (collectiveavtal). The studio has had no confirmed layoffs or union drives under Swedish collective bargaining frameworks. No US-style CWA union presence.

## Key franchises

- **Wolfenstein reboot series** — The New Order (2014), The New Colossus (2017), Youngblood (2019)
- **Indiana Jones and the Great Circle** — Released December 9, 2024; executive produced by Todd Howard

## Post-Microsoft timeline

| Date | Event | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 2021-03-09 | ZeniMax acquisition closed | MachineGames becomes part of Microsoft Gaming |
| 2024-05-07 | Sister studio closures | Arkane Austin, Tango closed but MachineGames unaffected |
| 2024-12-09 | Indiana Jones launched | Critical and commercial success; Xbox/PC exclusive |
| 2026-07 | Microsoft 3,200 layoffs | MachineGames not reported as impacted |

## Workforce

Estimated ~400 employees across Uppsala and Sundsvall offices. No public headcount from Microsoft/ZeniMax. MachineGames has not been significantly impacted by the layoff waves that hit US-based ZeniMax studios.
