# Unknown Worlds Entertainment

**San Francisco-based game developer** best known for the Subnautica series. Acquired by South Korean publisher Krafton in October 2021 for approximately $500 million upfront plus up to $250 million in performance-based earn-out payments.

## History

Founded in 2001 by Charlie Cleveland, Unknown Worlds initially developed the Natural Selection series — first-person shooter/real-time strategy hybrids built on the Half-Life and Source engines. The studio pivoted to open-world survival with Subnautica (2018), which became a breakout hit, selling millions of copies and establishing a major franchise.

## Krafton acquisition and labor dispute

The Krafton acquisition (2021) promised operational independence, but the relationship soured. After the release of Subnautica 2 (2025) triggered earn-out bonuses, Krafton attempted to install a new CEO and avoid paying contractual bonuses. Former leadership sued, and the dispute culminated in a July 2026 settlement where:

1. Krafton agreed to pay bonuses to all staff, including post-acquisition hires, in three annual installments
2. The installed CEO resigned
3. A new CEO will be sought from outside both companies

This dispute is notable as a case study in post-acquisition labor relations — the tension between a parent company's financial control and a studio's contractual independence.

## Labor profile

No union activity documented. The studio operates under standard US employment law. The bonus dispute is Unknown Worlds' most significant labor-related event.
