# Infinity Ward

**Original Call of Duty developer, best known for the Modern Warfare series.** Founded in 2002 by Vince Zampella, Jason West, and Grant Collier. Acquired by Activision in 2003 after the breakout success of the original Call of Duty. The studio has approximately 500 employees.

## Labor history

Infinity Ward has a history of leadership instability but no known union organizing. In 2010, Activision fired co-founders Zampella and West for "breaches of contract and insubordination" — triggering a mass exodus of developers who would go on to form [[orgs/Respawn Entertainment]]. The resulting legal battles lasted years.

As of October 2026, no union organizing has been reported at Infinity Ward. The studio's QA testers are covered by [[orgs/Activision QA United-CWA]] if they fall under the broader Activision QA unit, but no wall-to-wall or studio-specific union exists.

## Key games
- Call of Duty (2003)
- Call of Duty: Modern Warfare series (2007, 2009, 2011, 2019, 2022)
- Call of Duty: Warzone (2020; with Raven Software)
