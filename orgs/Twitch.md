# Twitch

> The dominant video game live-streaming platform. Subsidiary of Amazon since 2014. HQ: San Francisco, CA.

Twitch is the world's leading live-streaming platform for video games, esports, and creative content. Founded 2011, acquired by Amazon for $970 million in 2014. As of early 2024, it has undergone deep workforce cuts totaling ~900 jobs (50%+ of its workforce).

## Labor history

### March 2023 — 400 layoffs

Three days after co-founder and CEO Emmett Shear announced his departure, Twitch laid off ~400 employees. The company cited the "current macroeconomic environment" ([[sources/2024-01-10 — ign-twitch-500-layoffs-35-pct]]).

### January 2024 — 500 layoffs (35% of workforce)

In January 2024, Twitch announced it would cut approximately 500 employees, or 35% of its remaining workforce. This was the third consecutive year of layoffs at Twitch ([[sources/2024-01-10 — ign-twitch-500-layoffs-35-pct]]). Combined with the March 2023 round, this reduced Twitch's workforce to approximately 50% of its pre-2023 level.

### Crown Channel (2023)

The Crown Channel, an Amazon-backed Twitch channel that was an attempt at synergy between Amazon and Twitch featuring streaming stars and celebrities, was shut down as part of Amazon Games' November 2023 layoffs. Earlier in 2023, a Bloomberg report had claimed the channel's viewership numbers were artificially inflated ([[sources/2023-11-15 — aftermath-amazon-180-games-layoffs]]).

### Union activity

**No known union organizing** at Twitch as of 2026. Despite Twitch streamers organizing informally around platform policies and revenue shares, there is no formal union at the corporate-employee level.

## Relationship to Amazon

Twitch is part of Amazon Entertainment, a division of Amazon. Steve Boom serves as VP of Audio, Twitch, and Games at Amazon. The Crown Channel was an attempt at cross-division synergy that ultimately failed and was shut down in the Nov 2023 layoffs.
