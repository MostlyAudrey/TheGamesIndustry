# Paradox Interactive

**Swedish video game publisher, HQ Stockholm.** Best known for grand strategy games (Europa Universalis, Hearts of Iron, Crusader Kings, Victoria, Stellaris) and city-building/management sims (Cities: Skylines series). One of the few major publishers with a formal collective bargaining agreement covering its home-country workforce.

> **Note:** US + Canada only per project scope; Paradox Interactive is included as a comparison/labor example for the campaign-drama pipeline, particularly as a Swedish CBA case study.

## Labor organization (Sweden)

Paradox Interactive is notable in the games industry for voluntarily signing a **collective bargaining agreement (CBA)** with two Swedish labor unions in **June 2020**:

| Union | Type | Members at Paradox |
|---|---|---|
| **Unionen** | White-collar workers | ✓ (largest union) |
| **SACO** | Academic professionals | ✓ |

**Details of the CBA:**
- Covers all Paradox studios within **Sweden**
- Formalizes company policies on **parental leave, sick leave, and benefits** so they cannot be changed unilaterally
- Over 200 combined members at the time of signing
- Sets industry standards for Swedish game developers
- Result of the Swedish labor model where most employees belong to a union and companies negotiate binding agreements at workplace level

**Non-Swedish studios:** The CBA explicitly applies only to Swedish operations. Studios like Triumph Studios (Netherlands), Haemimont Games (Bulgaria), and Iceflake (Finland) operate under local labor laws and are not necessarily covered by Swedish union agreements.

## Layoffs & restructuring

Paradox has been relatively insulated from the 2023-2026 industry-wide layoff wave, with one notable exception:

**June 2024 — Paradox Tectonic closure:**
- **24 employees** lost jobs
- Studio in Berkeley, CA led by Rod Humble (former Sims 2/3 lead designer)
- Life by You (Sims-like game) cancelled after multiple delays
- Paradox stated the game "did not meet quality expectations"

Paradox has not undergone any mass layoff rounds comparable to Embracer, EA, Ubisoft, or Microsoft Gaming.
