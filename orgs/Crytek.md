# Crytek GmbH

**German video game developer and game engine creator, headquartered in Frankfurt.** Privately held by the Yerli brothers. Known for the Crysis series (famous for pushing PC hardware benchmarks), Hunt: Showdown extraction shooter, and the CRYENGINE middleware platform.

## Financial and restructuring history

Crytek has undergone multiple restructurings over the past decade following overexpansion:

- **2014:** Financial difficulties led to closure of Crytek UK (sold to Deep Silver, became Dambuster Studios) and unpaid wages controversy
- **2017:** Sold Crytek Black Sea (Bulgaria) to Sega Europe
- **2024 Q3:** Crysis 4 development put on hold
- **2025-02-12:** Announced 15% workforce reduction (~60 of ~400 employees)

### 2025 layoff details

In February 2025, Crytek announced layoffs affecting approximately 60 employees across development teams and shared services. Crysis 4, announced in January 2022, was put on hold after its development was paused in Q3 2024. The company attempted to shift developers from Crysis 4 to Hunt: Showdown 1896, but determined that layoffs were "inevitable to move forward."

Founder Avni Yerli stated: "Like so many of our peers, we aren't immune to the complex, unfavorable market dynamics that have hit our industry these past several years."

The company said it would offer affected employees severance packages and career assistance services.

## Current focus
Crytek is now focused entirely on **Hunt: Showdown 1896**, its PVPVE extraction shooter that launched initially in 2018 and received a major overhaul/relaunch. The company remains committed to CRYENGINE development but no longer funds major AAA single-player projects.

## Union status
**No known union organizing at Crytek.** No reported labor disputes in Germany.
