# Fishlabs

German video game developer based in Hamburg. Best known for the Galaxy on Fire series of space combat games. Initially developed mobile titles before transitioning to PC/console with Chorus (2021).

Acquired by Embracer Group in 2018 via the THQ Nordic group. In 2023, Fishlabs was affected by Embracer's restructuring, with an undisclosed number of layoffs.

## Labor record

Affected by 2023 Embracer restructuring layoffs. German labor law provides works council protections. No union presence reported.
