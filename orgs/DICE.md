# DICE (EA Digital Illusions CE)

**Stockholm-based studio, owned by [[orgs/Electronic Arts]].** Flagship developer of the **Battlefield** series. Founded 1992, acquired by EA in 2006.

## Labor context — Sweden

DICE operates under **Swedish labor law**, which provides:
- **Works council (MBL)** consultation rights on major organizational changes
- **Collective bargaining agreements** (typically with Unionen/Sveriges Ingenjörer)
- Stronger protections than US/UK equivalents (statutory severance, notice periods)
- Swedish law requires **collective redundancy consultation** when laying off 5+ employees

Despite these protections, DICE has been part of all major EA layoff rounds since 2024.

## Role in EA's Battlefield division

DICE is the lead studio of EA's Battlefield division, which also includes:
- **Criterion Games** (Guildford, UK)
- **Ripple Effect Studios** (Los Angeles, US)
- **Motive Studio** (Montreal, CA — joined April 2024)
