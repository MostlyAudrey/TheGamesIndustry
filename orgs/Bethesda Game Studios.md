# Bethesda Game Studios

**Microsoft/Xbox's flagship RPG developer — Rockville, Austin, Dallas, Montreal.** Best known for The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim (2011), Fallout 4 (2015), and Starfield (2023). Approximately 500 employees across four studios.

## Current Status (Oct 2026)

BGS is navigating post-Starfield recovery. The studio is in active development on The Elder Scrolls VI (announced 2018, still in pre-production as of 2026). The Fallout TV series (April 2024) was a massive cultural hit that drove millions of returning players to Fallout 76 and Fallout 4, demonstrating the franchise's enduring power. Starfield, while commercially successful, received mixed fan/critic reception, and BGS has continued to release expansions (Shattered Space, 2024).

## Unionization

BGS made history in 2024 as one of the first AAA development studios under Microsoft to form a wall-to-wall union:

- **July 2024:** One BGS USA formed — 241 artists, engineers, programmers, and designers across Rockville, Austin, and Dallas voluntarily recognized by Microsoft/CWA
- **August 2024:** BGS Montreal union certified under CWA Canada
- **Context:** Part of the broader ZeniMax union wave (ZeniMax Workers United in Jan 2023, ZOS United-CWA in Dec 2024, id Software union in Dec 2025)

The unions were enabled by Microsoft's CWA neutrality agreement (2022), which expired in Fall 2025. BGS's union status under the post-neutrality environment is untested.

## Fallout TV Show Effect

The Amazon Prime Fallout TV series (April 2024) was a watershed moment:
- Fallout 76 saw player counts surge by 600%+ (Steam concurrent players)
- Fallout 4 became one of the top-selling games on Steam again in 2024
- Renewed interest across the entire franchise
- Fallout 76's long-term live service model finally vindicated after rocky 2018 launch

## The Elder Scrolls VI

- Announced 2018 (E3 teaser)
- Still in pre-production as of 2026
- No release date; widely expected to be post-2028
- Will be Xbox/pass exclusive
- Under massive pressure to deliver after Starfield's mixed reception
- Creation Engine 2 (same as Starfield)
