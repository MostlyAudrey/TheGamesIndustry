# Remedy Entertainment — Games Power Map

## Overview

Remedy Entertainment is a Finnish video game developer founded in 1995, publicly traded on Nasdaq Helsinki. Known for cinematic single-player action games (Max Payne, Alan Wake, Control). It transitioned to a multi-project studio model from 2016 onward and operates from Espoo (HQ) and Stockholm.

Remedy has notably avoided mass layoffs throughout the 2023-2026 industry contraction — one of few AAA studios that maintained stable headcount. However, the failure of FBC: Firebreak in mid-2025 triggered a profit warning, a €14.9M impairment, and the departure of long-serving CEO Tero Virtala in October 2025. Co-founder Markus Mäki (largest individual shareholder at 23.44%) returned as interim CEO.

## Tencent Relationship

Tencent has steadily deepened its involvement with Remedy:
- **May 2021:** Acquired 3.8% from Accendo Capital
- **Nov 2022:** Increased to 5.01%
- **Apr 2024:** Increased to **14.80%** (2,005,716 shares) via Image Frame Investment
- **Sep 2024:** €15M convertible loan (8% interest, €27.20/share conversion price); if converted, adds ~5.98% in shares → could bring Tencent above 20%

Tencent has board observation rights as a >10% shareholder but no formal board seat reported. CEO Tero Virtala stated in 2024: "We don't see this changing anything" regarding day-to-day operations.

## Labor Profile

### Finnish Labor Context

Finland has one of the most unionized workforces in the world (~60%+ membership rate). Key features:
- **Universal collective bargaining:** Sector-wide agreements (työehtosopimus) cover minimum wages, working hours, vacation, sick pay
- **Co-determination Act (Yhteistoimintalaki / Act on Cooperation within Undertakings):** Requires employer to negotiate with employee representatives (not necessarily unions) before layoffs of 10+ workers, major restructuring, or changes to employment terms. Applies to companies with 20+ regular employees
- **No statutory minimum wage** — but sector agreements set minimums
- **Strong dismissal protection:** Employers need "proper and weighty reason" for termination (financial/production-related or individual)
- **Works councils (yhteistoimintahenkilöstö):** Elected employee reps in companies with 30+ employees

### Remedy-Specific Labor

No documented union organizing or unionization campaign at Remedy as of October 2026. Finnish game industry workers generally organize under general sector unions (e.g., **TEAM Teollisuusalojen ammattiliitto** — Industrial Union TEAM, covering technology industry employees). Remedy employees, like other Finnish tech workers, are likely covered by the **Technology Industries collective agreement** negotiated between TEAM and the Technology Industries of Finland.

No strikes, unfair labor practice charges, or public labor disputes have been reported at Remedy. The company's publicly stated anti-crunch stance (post-Alan Wake 2) and lack of layoffs may have reduced organizing pressure.

## Financial Stress Signals (2025-2026)

FBC: Firebreak's failure poses the most significant financial challenge in Remedy's history as a public company. With a net loss of €13M in FY2025 and an operating loss of €16.4M in Q3 2025 alone, the company faces potential restructuring pressure. The Max Payne remakes and Control 2 represent critical pipeline bets.
