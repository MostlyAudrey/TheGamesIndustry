# Ubisoft Entertainment SA

**French video game publisher, HQ Saint-Mandé, France.** Founded 1986 by the Guillemot brothers. Major franchises: Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, Rainbow Six, The Division, Just Dance, Rayman, Prince of Persia, Tom Clancy's, Anno, Watch Dogs.

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Active corporate collapse. Headcount down from 20,729 (Sep 2022) to 16,590 (Mar 2026, then lower after Oct 2026 cuts). Stock down ~93% from 2018 peak (€100+ → ~€3-5 range, market cap €600-850M). Record operating loss of €−1.044B in FY2026.

## Capital structure
- **Public company:** Euronext Paris: UBI, CAC Mid 60 component (was CAC 40 until 2024)
- **Guillemot family:** ~14% ownership (effective control)
- **Tencent:** 9.99% direct; additional 25% of Vantage Studios subsidiary
- **Vantage Studios:** Formed 2025, holds Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, Rainbow Six IP. Ubisoft holds 75%, Tencent 25%. Valued ~€4B.
- **Revenue (FY2026):** €1.395B (down ~17-22% YoY)
- **Operating loss (FY2026):** €−1.044B
- **Net loss (FY2026):** €−1.516B
- **Stock decline:** ~93% from 2018 peak (€100+ → ~€3-5 range)
- **Cost target:** €200M in cuts by March 2028; targeting €1.25B fixed cost reduction

## Union organizing

### France — STJV (Syndicat des Travailleurs et Travailleuses du Jeu Vidéo)
- Active since at least 2024 organizing Ubisoft workers
- **Oct 2024:** 3-day strike — 700+ across 4 French studios protesting 3-day RTO mandate
- **Oct 2025:** National strike — ~700 across 5 studios (Paris, Montpellier, Annecy, Lyon, Bordeaux)
- **Feb 2026:** Coordinated 3-day strike with 4 other unions (CFE-CGC, CGT, Printemps Écologique, Solidaires Informatique) — 1,200 workers reported; protested restructure, layoffs, 5-day RTO
- STJV also active in broader French games industry (Don't Nod, Cyanide, Quantic Dream layoffs)

### Canada — CWA Canada
- **Ubisoft Halifax:** Certified CWA Canada Local 30111 on Dec 18, 2025 — 74% yes — first Ubisoft union in North America. Studio closed Jan 7, 2026. CWA Canada pursuing legal action.
- **Ubisoft Workers of Montreal (UWM):** Informal union organizing, petitioned Oct 2026 to preserve hybrid work (300+ signatures). Supported by UVW-CWA.
- **Context:** Quebec government multimedia tax credit was $477M in 2025 (up from $310M in 2020). Ubisoft cut ~500 jobs in Montreal 2024-2025 while receiving these subsidies (per The Walrus).
- **Longtail Studios history:** The Halifax studio (as Longtail) attempted to unionize in 2008 but failed (per IGN).

## Notable labor concerns
- **Five-day RTO mandate** imposed Jan 2026 reversed remote/hybrid agreements — unions described it as "coercive control" and designed to make workers quit
- **Halifax timing:** Union certification followed by studio closure within 3 weeks
- **Creative Houses restructuring** announced without consulting works councils
- **Workers learning from press:** Unions reported being informed of major decisions "at the same time as the press"
- **GenAI investment:** Ubisoft simultaneously announced accelerated investment in GenAI while cutting thousands of jobs
- **CEO:** Yves Guillemot remains in place through Oct 2026, despite calls for resignation

## Corporate transactions and buyout rumors

### Tencent investment (completed Nov 2025)
- Ubisoft spun out Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, Rainbow Six into Vantage Studios
- Tencent invested €1.16B ($1.25B) for 25% stake
- Ubisoft retained 75% and control for at least 2 years
- Tencent must hold stake for at least 5 years unless Ubisoft loses majority
- Described as a "bailout" — the Guillemot family remains in control

### Buyout speculation context
- Guillemot family wants to retain control; Tencent previously expressed desire for more control
- Stock at ~€3-5 range (market cap €600-850M) makes full buyout theoretically possible
- No confirmed buyout bid as of Oct 2026
- Nico Laurent (former Tencent/Riot exec) joined as adviser on major franchises
- Company forecasts profitability by 2027-28, with continued losses in 2026-27
