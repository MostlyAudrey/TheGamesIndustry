# Powerhouse Capital

Los Angeles-based venture capital firm focused on media, entertainment, and interactive gaming. Founded 2019. Focuses on four pillars: Sight, Sound, Play, and Live.

**Key people:** Ron LaWeave (co-founder), Salim Mitha, Ian Doody, David Ma, Jill Royster.

**Games/Interactive portfolio:** [[orgs/Scopely]] (Monopoly Go!), [[orgs/Discord]], [[orgs/Epic Games]] (Fortnite, Unreal Engine — via secondary/syndicate).

**Other notable investments:** MasterClass, Wondery (→Amazon), The Athletic (→NYT), Calm, Epic! Kids.

**Strategy:** Sector-focused consumer tech/media VC. Leverages LA entertainment industry connections. Small lean team of 4 investment professionals. Games is one of four verticals, not the sole focus.
