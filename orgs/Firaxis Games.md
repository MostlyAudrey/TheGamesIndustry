# Firaxis Games

**Baltimore, MD-based studio, owned by [[orgs/2K Games]] / [[orgs/Take-Two Interactive]].** Founded 1996 by Sid Meier. Developer of the **Civilization** (turn-based 4X strategy) and **XCOM** (turn-based tactical) series. Known for long development cycles and post-launch content support.

## Labor events

### 2023 layoffs (March)
~30 employees laid off as part of Take-Two's first post-acquisition cost reduction. Affected both the Civilization and XCOM teams. 2K confirmed the layoffs but declined to specify which projects were impacted.

### 2025 layoffs (September)
Despite Civilization VII launching in February 2025, Firaxis laid off an undisclosed number of employees seven months later. Employees confirmed on social media, including writers and designers who worked on Civilization VII. 2K confirmed a "staff reduction" but did not disclose numbers. The layoffs came despite Take-Two forecasting record net bookings for FY2026-2027 driven by GTA VI.
