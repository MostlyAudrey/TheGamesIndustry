# Frogwares

**Ukrainian game developer — surviving and releasing games through war.** Based in Kyiv (~80 employees) with Dublin subsidiary. Best known for the Sherlock Holmes series (~7M copies) and The Sinking City. Founded in 2000 by French expatriates. CEO Waël Amr.

One of the few active game studios operating out of Ukraine during the Russian invasion. A unique case study in wartime game development.

## Wartime Development (2022-present)

When Russia invaded Ukraine on February 24, 2022:

- Staff split between active war participation, humanitarian aid, and continued development
- Those who remained adopted a hybrid model — working from bomb shelters, with frequent power outages
- Removed all games from sale in Russia and called on other companies to do the same
- Received Epic MegaGrant in May 2022 to relocate staff
- Missile attacks on energy infrastructure (Oct 2022 onward) made development a "trial"
- Released Sherlock Holmes: The Awakened in April 2023 — developed entirely during the war
- The game starts with a developer message acknowledging the wartime conditions

**The Sinking City 2** was announced March 2024 and released August 2026 — a survival horror sequel representing a new direction with more focus on combat.

## Legal Dispute with Nacon

Frogwares had a significant legal battle with publisher Nacon over The Sinking City:
- Nacon acquired Bigben Interactive (original publisher)
- Frogwares alleged Nacon hacked and illegally uploaded the game to Steam
- Frogwares asked customers not to buy the Steam version
- Frogwares eventually regained publishing rights (2023-2024)
- Result: Frogwares became self-publisher for The Sinking City

## Labor Profile

- **No union:** Small studio, Ukrainian labor context
- **No layoffs:** Remarkably stable headcount (~80) despite war
- **No crunch scandals** unlike larger studios
- **Wartime resilience:** Staff retention through the invasion
