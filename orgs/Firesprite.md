# Firesprite

**Firesprite** is a Liverpool, UK-based game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Formed by former Sony Studio Liverpool (Wipeout) staff after that studio's 2012 closure. Best known for Horizon Call of the Mountain (PSVR2).

## Ownership & Structure

- **Sony Interactive Entertainment** acquired Firesprite in 2021
- Based in Liverpool, UK
- Founded by Graeme Ankers (former Studio Liverpool developer) in 2013
- Initially worked on PSVR games before moving to AAA/VR hybrid

## Labor Timeline

### 2021-2023 — Sony acquisition and Horizon VR
Firesprite was acquired by Sony in 2021 and quickly put to work on Horizon Call of the Mountain, a PSVR2 launch title. Reports indicate that after the acquisition, Sony-imposed **crunch** was implemented to finish the game on time. The studio grew rapidly from ~150 to ~250 employees during this period.

### February 2024 — The wave of layoffs
Firesprite was among the hardest-hit studios in SIE's 900-person layoff wave:
- **Significant job cuts** (exact number undisclosed)
- **Twisted Metal live-service game cancelled**
- Creative director for Horizon Call of the Mountain among those laid off
- The studio was described by affected staff as "never recovering" from the cuts

### February 2024 — Eurogamer investigation
On February 29, 2024, Eurogamer published an investigation into Firesprite finding:
- **Chaotic and toxic work environment**
- **Crunch culture** imposed after Sony acquisition
- **High-profile exits** of senior talent
- Staff described a "sinking ship" atmosphere
- Management accused of poor communication and decision-making

### 2024-2025 — Continued exits
Founder Graeme Ankers departed the studio during 2024. The studio's staffing levels continued to decline through natural attrition.

### 2025 — "End of layoffs"
By July 2025, Firesprite confirmed the layoffs had concluded and the studio continued operating, albeit significantly smaller.

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Firesprite as of October 2026. Despite the toxic culture revelations and heavy layoffs, no public unionization drive has been reported.
