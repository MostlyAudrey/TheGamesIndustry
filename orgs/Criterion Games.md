# Criterion Games

**Guildford, UK-based studio, owned by [[orgs/Electronic Arts]].** Historically known for the **Burnout** and **Need for Speed** franchises. Since ~2022 has primarily supported the **Battlefield** franchise.

## Franchise shift

Criterion was the lead developer of **Need for Speed** from 2010-2019 (Hot Pursuit, Most Wanted, Rivals, Payback, Heat). After the release of NFS Unbound (December 2022), EA shifted the studio to Battlefield support, where it has focused on single-player narrative content for Battlefield 2042 and the next Battlefield title.
