# Koei Tecmo

**Japanese video game publisher and developer; creators of Dynasty Warriors, Ninja Gaiden, and Nioh.** Formed in 2009 through the merger of Koei (founded 1978) and Tecmo (founded 1967). Headquartered in Yokohama. Known for the "musou" (warriors) genre of large-scale historical action games.

## Corporate history and independence

| Year | Event | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| 1978 | Koei founded by Yoichi Erikawa | Historical strategy game pioneer |
| 1967 | Tecmo founded | Arcade and console developer |
| 2004 | Tomonobu Itagaki files lawsuit against Tecmo | Over unpaid bonus for Dead or Alive; Itagaki left in 2008 |
| 2009 | Koei and Tecmo merge | Tecmo was financially weakened after Itagaki's departure |
| 2009 | MBO executed | Management bought out external influence and took control |
| 2010 | Return to Tokyo Stock Exchange | Publicly traded again |
| 2025 | Reported as acquisition target | No deal materialized as of late 2026 |

The 2009 MBO is notable as Koei Tecmo "purchased itself" — Koei management orchestrated a buyout that effectively removed external influence (including representatives from rival Square Enix who had attempted to gain influence at Tecmo during its financial distress). The merged entity maintains independence to this day.

## Team Ninja division

Team Ninja is Koei Tecmo's premier internal action game development division:

| Director | Notable Works | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Tomonobu Itagaki | Dead or Alive, Ninja Gaiden (Xbox) | 1995-2008 |
| Yosuke Hayashi | Ninja Gaiden Sigma, Dead or Alive 5 | 2008-2016 |
| Fumihiko Yasuda | Nioh, Nioh 2, Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty | 2016-present |

After Itagaki's departure in 2008 (following a widely-publicized lawsuit claiming unpaid bonuses), Team Ninja restructured and rebuilt under new leadership. The division's output quality stabilized with Nioh (2017), which became a critical and commercial success.

## Labor overview

- **No union:** No union organizing activity documented at any Koei Tecmo entity
- **No layoffs:** No documented layoffs in the 2022-2026 period
- **2023 salary increase:** 7% average raise for permanent employees, following broader Japanese game industry trends
- **Rapid release cadence:** Koei Tecmo maintains a high-volume release schedule, particularly in the musou genre with multiple licensed collaborations (Zelda, Fire Emblem, Persona, One Piece)

## Capital significance

Koei Tecmo is one of the few remaining independent mid-tier Japanese publishers. The company's independence has attracted acquisition interest, but no deal has materialized. Koei Tecmo's consistent profitability from the low-cost/high-volume musou production model makes it a stable business.
