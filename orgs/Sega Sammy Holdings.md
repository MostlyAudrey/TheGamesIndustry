# Sega Sammy Holdings Inc.

**Japanese holding company formed by the 2004 merger of Sega and Sammy Corporation.** Controls a diversified entertainment conglomerate spanning video games (Sega, Atlus, Rovio), pachinko/pachislot machines (Sammy), arcade operations, toys, and anime (TMS Entertainment). Listed on Tokyo Stock Exchange (TSE: 6460).

**Current status (Oct 2026):** The group is undergoing significant restructuring — Sega's game business is consolidating European operations while the holding company diversified into online gaming platforms (GAN, Stakelogic) and mobile (Rovio). The Rovio acquisition has not generated profit as of 2026.

## Rovio acquisition
- **Announced:** April 2023
- **Completed:** August 18, 2023
- **Price:** $776 million (€706M) — all-cash tender offer
- **Entity:** Sega Europe acquired Rovio Entertainment Corporation (Espoo, Finland)
- **Purpose:** Accelerate mobile gaming presence via Angry Birds IP
- **Post-acquisition performance:** Reports indicate Rovio has not generated profit for Sega; accumulated losses estimated at $200M+ (per unverified industry reports)
