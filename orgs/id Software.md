# id Software

**Legendary first-person shooter developer, founded 1991.** HQ Richardson, TX with a Frankfurt, Germany office. Subsidiary of ZeniMax Media (acquired 2009), now part of Microsoft Gaming (via 2021 ZeniMax acquisition). Creators of DOOM and Quake — two of the most influential FPS franchises in gaming history.

## Labor status

id Software represents one of the most significant unionization stories in the post-Microsoft ZeniMax era. Workers organized a wall-to-wall union with CWA Local 6215 in December 2025, motivated by the broader industry instability and the closure of sister studios (Arkane Austin, Tango Gameworks) in 2024.

The union drive was enabled by the ZeniMax-specific neutrality agreement (valid until May 2026). Key bargaining priorities include AI protections, remote work guarantees, and expanded benefits.

**Union details:**
- **Union:** CWA Local 6215 (wall-to-wall)
- **Membership:** 165 of ~185 employees (Dec 2025)
- **Coverage:** Developers, artists, programmers, producers
- **Status:** Organizing toward first contract; Microsoft recognized voluntarily

## Workforce

Pre-July 2026 layoffs, id employed approximately 185 people (as of Dec 2025). After the 136-job cut in July 2026, the studio was left with roughly 49+ remaining staff in Texas. The Frankfurt office headcount was not separately disclosed.

## Key franchises

- **DOOM** (2016, DOOM Eternal 2020, DOOM: The Dark Ages upcoming)
- **Quake** (Quake Champions 2017, Quake 2 remastered 2023)
- **Rage** (2011, Rage 2 2019 — co-developed with Avalanche)
- **Wolfenstein** (original 1992)
