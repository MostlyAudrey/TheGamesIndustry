# Arkane Studios

**Acclaimed immersive sim developer with two historic studios — only Lyon survives.** Founded 1999 in Lyon, France. Opened Austin, Texas studio in 2006. Acquired by ZeniMax Media in 2010. Known for the Dishonored series, Prey, and Deathloop.

## Studio split and closure

Arkane existed as two studios under the ZeniMax umbrella — Arkane Austin and Arkane Lyon. Following the critical failure of Redfall (May 2023), Microsoft closed Arkane Austin entirely on May 7, 2024 as part of a broader ZeniMax restructuring. Arkane Lyon, which had released Deathloop to critical acclaim (2021) and was developing Marvel's Blade (announced Dec 2023), survived.

Arkane Lyon studio director Dinga Bakaba called the closure "a fucking gut stab" on social media, while confirming Lyon was safe.

## Labor status

Arkane Lyon operates under French labor law, which provides strong worker protections. There have been no confirmed union drives or CWA-style organizing at the studio.
