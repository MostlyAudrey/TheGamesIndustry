# Moon Active

## Overview

Moon Active is an Israeli mobile game developer best known for the hit casual game *Coin Master*. Founded in 2011 by Samuel Albin, the company is one of Israel's most valuable private tech companies (unicorn), with annual revenues exceeding $2 billion in both 2024 and 2025. The company employs approximately 2,200 people worldwide (as of early 2026), with roughly 1,400 in Israel.

In 2024, Moon Active acquired Lithuanian studio Melsoft for approximately $100 million.

## Labor Events

### First round — January 2025
In late January 2025, Moon Active conducted its first known round of layoffs, cutting "dozens" of staff (reports of between 50–100 people affected). The cuts primarily affected the Lithuanian office (Melsoft), though staff in Poland, Armenia, Ukraine, Turkey, and Israel were also impacted. [[sources/2025-01-28 — pocketgamer-moon-active-layoffs]] [[sources/2025-01-28 — gamesindustry-moon-active-layoffs]]

### Second round — January 2026
Approximately one year later, Moon Active announced a second round of layoffs affecting 110 employees worldwide (30 in Israel), representing 5% of the company's 2,200-employee workforce. The cuts included downsizing at the Melsoft studio in Lithuania. [[sources/2026-01-27 — globes-moon-active-110-layoffs]]

Notable pattern: Both layoff rounds occurred while Moon Active reported record or near-record revenues (~$2 billion annually), illustrating the broader industry trend of profitable mobile game companies cutting staff.

### Labor organizing
No known union organizing activity at Moon Active.
