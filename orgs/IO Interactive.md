# IO Interactive

**Danish video game developer and publisher** based in Copenhagen. Best known for the Hitman series (World of Assassination trilogy), Kane & Lynch, and the 2026 James Bond game 007 First Light.

## Independence turnaround

IO Interactive's most significant corporate event was its 2017 management buyout from Square Enix. Square Enix had deemed Hitman (2016) sales insufficient and ceased funding, expecting to find a buyer for the studio. Instead, IOI's management executed a buyout, retaining rights to the Hitman and Freedom Fighters franchises. This independence allowed the studio to:

1. Continue the World of Assassination trilogy on their own terms
2. Secure a James Bond license (Project 007, announced 2020)
3. Expand from ~200 to ~500+ employees across four studios
4. Release 007 First Light in 2026 to strong reception

## Structure & expansion

As of 2026, IO Interactive operates four studios (Copenhagen HQ, Istanbul, Barcelona, Brighton) and employs ~500+ people. CEO Hakan Abrak has led the studio since its independence. The studio is privately owned and has not accepted outside investment.

## Labor profile

No union activity documented. Danish labor law (the "flexicurity" model — flexible hiring/firing combined with strong social safety nets and active labor market policies) governs the studio's operations. No documented layoffs in the 2022-2026 period. The studio has been in a growth phase since independence.

## Scope note
IO Interactive is a Danish studio outside the US/Canada primary scope of Project Gamecraft. Included as a notable independent studio case study — the management buyout from Square Enix is one of the most successful independence stories in AAA game development.
