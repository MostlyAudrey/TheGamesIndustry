# Team Asobi

**Team Asobi** is a Tokyo-based game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for the Astro Bot series. The studio was formed as an internal R&D team within Japan Studio and was spun out as a standalone first-party studio in 2021.

## Structure

- Formed 2012 as internal R&D team within Sony's Japan Studio
- Became standalone first-party studio in June 2021 after Japan Studio restructuring
- Led by Nicolas Doucet (studio director and creative director)
- Based in Tokyo (Konan, Minato)
- Small team: ~65 as of 2024

## Labor History

Team Asobi is one of the **most stable studios** in Sony's portfolio. It has **no reported layoffs** in its history. The studio was not affected by the 2024 SIE layoffs (which primarily hit the Americas and EMEA regions).

The studio's small size, consistently high-quality output, and its role as the keeper of the Astro Bot franchise (now a key PlayStation mascot) have insulated it from restructuring.

### Japan Studio context
Team Asobi exists because of Japan Studio's effective closure (2021). When Sony restructured Japan Studio away from AAA development, the Team Asobi unit was preserved and elevated. The transition was handled without the public layoff announcements seen at Western studios — Sony reassigned employees rather than widely publicizing cuts.

### Crunch notes
- Nicolas Doucet has publicly emphasized work-life balance
- No major crunch reports associated with Team Asobi

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Team Asobi as of October 2026. Japanese game developers generally have low unionization rates.
