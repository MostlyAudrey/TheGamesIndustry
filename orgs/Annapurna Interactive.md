# Annapurna Interactive

Award-winning video game publisher under Annapurna Pictures. Known for *What Remains of Edith Finch*, *Stray*, *Outer Wilds*, *Neon White*, and *Cocoon*.

## Capital & Ownership
- Wholly owned division of Annapurna Pictures, which is owned by Megan Ellison (Oracle founder Larry Ellison's daughter).
- Not publicly traded; privately funded through Annapurna Pictures.

## Labor Record (2022–2026)

| Date | Event | Detail |
|------|-------|--------|
| Sep 12, 2024 | **Mass resignation of entire publishing team** | The entire Annapurna Interactive staff (~25 employees) resigned en masse after negotiations to spin off the gaming division into an independent entity broke down with Megan Ellison |
| Sep 2024 | Aftermath | Nathan Gary (president) and other leadership left; Annapurna rehired some former staff and moved to rebuild the division |
| Oct 2024 | New leadership | Former Epic Games executive Hector Sanchez hired to lead the restructured division |
| 2025 | mm2 acquisition | Annapurna acquired mm2 (French developer, *The Last Campfire*) and Remedy's *Control* IP film/TV rights |
| 2025 | *Control 2* co-publishing | Annapurna co-publishing *Control 2* with [[orgs/Remedy Entertainment]] for TV/movie universe integration |

**Labor organizing:** No union organizing reported at Annapurna Interactive. The September 2024 mass resignation was a collective bargaining leverage move by management-employees (not a union action).

## Headcount trajectory
- Pre-Sep 2024: ~25 (publishing team)
- Post-Sep 2024: rebuilt from scratch with new hires

## Notable games published
- *Stray* (2022, BlueTwelve Studio)
- *Neon White* (2022, Angel Matrix)
- *Cocoon* (2023, Geometric Interactive)
- *Thirsty Suitors* (2023, Outerloop Games)
- *Open Roads* (2024, Open Roads Team)

## Notes
- The 2024 mass resignation is one of the most dramatic singular labor events in games publishing — an entire division resigned at once over a structural disagreement with the owner.
- Annapurna later turned the crisis into an expansion: acquiring mm2, co-publishing *Control 2*, and building a film/TV production pipeline from game IP.
