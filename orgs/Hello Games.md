# Hello Games

**British video game developer and publisher** based in Guildford, Surrey. Privately held, founder-owned. Best known for the Joe Danger series and No Man's Sky.

## The No Man's Sky turnaround

Hello Games' defining narrative is the No Man's Sky turnaround. The game was announced in 2013 with enormous hype, promising a procedurally generated universe of 18 quintillion planets. When it launched in August 2016, many promised features were absent, leading to intense backlash, accusations of dishonesty, and a period where Hello Games went near-silent.

Rather than abandoning the title, the studio — still a tiny team of approximately 25 people — released continuous free updates over years, gradually adding multiplayer, base building, VR support, cross-play, and nearly all features originally promised. By the mid-2020s, No Man's Sky was widely regarded as one of gaming's great redemption stories, with "overwhelmingly positive" Steam reviews.

This turnaround was achieved with a remarkably small, stable team — the studio has never grown beyond approximately 50 employees, making it one of the most efficient successful game studios in the industry.

## Current projects

Hello Games announced Light No Fire at The Game Awards 2023 — another procedural survival game, this time set on a single Earth-sized planet rather than a universe. Sean Murray has been careful to temper expectations, learning from the No Man's Sky launch overhype.

## Labor profile

No documented union activity. No documented layoffs. The studio's stable, small-team structure has avoided the labor issues common at larger AAA studios. The team has consistently delivered major updates without requiring expansion or crunch-related controversy.

## Financial independence

Hello Games is privately held through Hello Games Holdings. The studio generated £28.07M revenue in FY2023 with net income of £20.85M — remarkably profitable for its size. No Man's Sky's ongoing revenue from new players across multiple platforms (including Nintendo Switch and VR) has provided sustained financial independence.

## Scope note
Hello Games is a UK-based developer outside the US/Canada primary scope of Project Gamecraft. Included as a notable independent studio case study and one of the industry's most remarkable turnaround stories.
