# London Venture Partners

Seed-stage VC exclusively focused on the video games industry. Widely recognized as one of the most successful seed investors in gaming history — portfolio companies have created approximately $18-30 billion in combined value.

**Key people:** David Gardner (co-founder, ex-EA).

**Notable portfolio:** [[orgs/Supercell]] (Clash of Clans, →Tencent; $10B+), [[orgs/Unity Technologies]] (engine, IPO →$30B+ peak cap), Playfish (social gaming, →EA, ~$400M), NaturalMotion (→Zynga, $527M), Futureplay (→Stillfront Group), Peak Games (→Zynga, $1.8B), Applifier (→Unity, became Unity Ads).

**Strategy:** Exclusively seed-stage. One of the earliest investors in game companies. Small, concentrated portfolio per fund. London-based but invests globally. Hands-on support: strategy, hiring, fundraising, publishing introductions. Not a huge AUM firm but extraordinary return track record.
