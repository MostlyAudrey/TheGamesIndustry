# Amazon Games

> The video game division of Amazon, formerly Amazon Game Studios (2012-2020), Amazon Games (2020-2025), now Amazon Games again. Headquarters in Seattle, WA. A division of Amazon Entertainment.

Amazon Games is the video game publishing and development division of Amazon. It operates internal studios (Relentless Studios, Amazon Games Orange County, San Diego, Bucharest) and publishes third-party titles like Lost Ark. It also operates Prime Gaming (formerly Twitch Prime) and the Amazon Luna cloud gaming platform.

## Labor History

Amazon Games has had a troubled development history, with multiple high-profile cancellations (Breakaway, Crucible, the Lord of the Rings MMO, and ultimately New World) and multiple layoff rounds.

### Layoff timeline

| Date | Event | Details |
|---|---|---|

### Amazon's total corporate layoff context

Amazon eliminated **over 27,000 corporate jobs** between late 2022 and 2023 ([[sources/2023-11-15 — aftermath-amazon-180-games-layoffs]]), and an additional **14,000+ in October 2025** ([[sources/2025-10-28 — bloomberg-amazon-14000-cuts-gaming-significant]]). The gaming division was affected in all of these rounds. Amazon reported near-record profit margins in Q3 2023 at the time of the November layoffs ([[sources/2023-11-15 — aftermath-amazon-180-games-layoffs]]).

### Union activity

**No known union organizing** at Amazon Games or any of its studios. Amazon has a well-documented history of anti-union activity across its logistics/warehouse operations (Amazon Labor Union, ALU) and at Whole Foods, but no gaming-specific labor organizing has been publicly reported.

## Game development history

- **Crucible** (2020): Class-based shooter launched May 2020, cancelled October 2020 — cited "inability to see a sustained future" ([[sources/2026-10-18 — wikipedia-amazon-game-studios]])
- **New World** (2021-2025): MMO launched September 2021 with 707k peak concurrent players. Development halted October 2025; servers to shut down in 2026. Amazon Games shifting focus away from MMOs and first-party AAA development ([[sources/2025-10-28 — bloomberg-amazon-14000-cuts-gaming-significant]])
- **Breakaway** (cancelled 2018): Team-based brawler integrated with Twitch
- **Lost Ark** (2022-present): Published by Amazon in NA/EU; Smilegate RPG developer

## Strategic pivot (2025-2026)

After the October 2025 layoffs, Amazon Games announced a shift away from first-party MMO and AAA development toward the Amazon Luna cloud gaming platform and publishing third-party titles. This represents a significant retreat from in-house game development ([[sources/2025-10-28 — bloomberg-amazon-14000-cuts-gaming-significant]]).
