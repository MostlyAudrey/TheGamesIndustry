# The Coalition

**First-party Xbox studio based in Vancouver, Canada, responsible for the Gears of War franchise.** Founded 2010 as Microsoft Studios Vancouver, rebranded to The Coalition in 2014 after Microsoft acquired the Gears of War IP from Epic Games. The studio has been one of Xbox's most stable first-party studios, described as a "flagship" and a "good child" by internal sources at Eurogamer.

**Relatively spared from Microsoft's 2024-2026 layoff waves**, but staff have grown increasingly fearful of post-launch cuts following the release of Gears of War: E-Day in October 2026. Story director Juan Vaca was laid off in September 2026, and multiple sources told Eurogamer that staff were quietly let go. No union organizing has been reported.
