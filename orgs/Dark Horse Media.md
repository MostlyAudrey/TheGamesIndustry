# Dark Horse Media

American comic book and game publishing company founded in 1986 by Mike Richardson. Best known for Dark Horse Comics, one of the largest independent comic publishers in the US. Also operates Dark Horse Games, a video game development/publishing division.

Acquired by Embracer Group in December 2022 for an undisclosed amount (~€40-50M estimated). Added significant IP portfolio including Hellboy, Sin City, 300, The Umbrella Academy, and Mass Effect comic series.

## Labor record

No union presence reported at Dark Horse. Oregon labor law. No layoffs reported.
