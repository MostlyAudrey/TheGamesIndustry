# NCSoft

## Overview

NCSoft (officially renamed NC Corporation in 2025) is a South Korean video game developer and publisher headquartered in Pangyo Techno Valley, Seongnam. Founded in 1997 by Kim Taek-jin, the company is one of Korea's largest game companies and a dominant force in the MMORPG space.

The company's flagship franchise is **Lineage**, which accounted for 70% of Q2 2024 revenue. NCSoft also develops and publishes Throne & Liberty, Guild Wars 2 (via ArenaNet), Aion, and Blade & Soul.

In 2024, the company announced a sweeping corporate split-off into multiple independent studios, accompanied by layoffs and project cancellations. The company changed its name from NCSoft to NC Corporation in 2025 following the restructuring.

## Financial Performance

- **2023:** Revenue decline driven by Lineage franchise slowdown; Guild Wars 2 a bright spot
- **Q2 2024:** Revenue ₩368.8B ($270.3M), operating profit ₩8.8B ($6.5M) — down 16% revenue, 75% operating profit YoY
- Lineage franchise accounted for 70% of Q2 2024 sales
- Multiple quarters of deteriorating financial performance triggered restructuring

## Labor and Employment

### Major Layoffs and Restructuring (October 2024)

In **October 2024**, NCSoft announced a sweeping corporate restructuring that included layoffs:

- **Date:** October 21, 2024 — NCSoft announced a corporate split-off into four wholly-owned subsidiaries
- **Trigger:** Co-CEOs Kim Taek-jin and Byung-Moo Park cited "deteriorating" financial situation and risk of becoming a "chronically loss-making company"
- **Voluntary retirement program:** Offered to employees as part of headcount reduction
- **Workforce reduction:** Plans to reduce headquarters from ~5,000 to ~4,500 employees
- **Project cancellations:**
  - **Project M** — Ultra-realistic interactive thriller announced in 2022, cancelled
  - **Minibus** — Cancelled
  - **Toguri Adventure** — Cancelled
  - **Battle Crush** — MOBA live-service game, shut down by end of 2024
- **Reassignments:** Some affected employees reassigned to other internal projects

### Corporate Split (February 2025)

The corporate split-off was approved by shareholders on **November 28, 2024** and took effect **February 1, 2025**:

Four wholly-owned private subsidiaries launched:

1. **Studio X** — Throne & Liberty development and support
2. **Studio Y** — Project LLL (open world shooter)
3. **Studio Z** — Project TACTAN (MMO RTS)
4. **NC AI** (formerly NC Research) — AI technology, generative AI platform VARCO

The restructuring moved game development out of the headquarters into decentralized standalone studios. NCSoft also created two additional spin-offs earlier in 2024:

- **NC QA Company** — Quality assurance
- **NC IDS Company** — Infrastructure and development services

### Union Organizing

NCSoft has an **active labor union** that has been vocal during the restructuring:

- **June 2024:** NCSoft's union joined with Nexon and Netmarble unions to **protest restructuring** across the game industry
- The NCSoft union specifically **urged management to retract the spinoff plan** and stop causing concerns over job security
- Union statement: "We are not disposable products, so we will not tolerate management's irresponsible and selfish behavior"
- The union attributed the company's financial decline to **lack of leaders who can direct development of attractive games**, rather than workforce issues
- The union threatened to "take every possible measure" to protect workers' rights, livelihoods, and future

### Historical Labor Context

- NCSoft introduced "flexible working hours" policy enabling employees to arrive between 7am-10am in 30-minute blocks (2018)
- The broader Korean game industry, including NCSoft, has been known for long working hours — software and gaming industry workers clock the longest hours in South Korea
- NCSoft was one of the first major Korean game companies to face organized unionization alongside Nexon

### Post-Restructuring Headcount

- After the split and layoffs, PitchBook reported NCSoft having **3,165 total employees** (early 2026)
- This is a significant reduction from the ~5,000 pre-restructuring headquarters headcount
- Note: Some employees were moved to subsidiary companies rather than laid off

## Lineage Franchise and Split

- The Lineage franchise (Lineage, Lineage 2, Lineage 2M, Lineage W) remained the company's cash cow, generating 70% of Q2 2024 revenue
- The corporate split was explicitly aimed at reducing headquarters' dependence on Lineage by creating independent studios to develop new IP
- NCSoft emphasized expanding core IP portfolio while introducing new IPs for the global market
- Journey of Monarch (latest Lineage series entry) released Q4 2024
- Despite the restructuring, NCSoft did not spin off Lineage as a separate entity — it remained a core asset of the parent company
