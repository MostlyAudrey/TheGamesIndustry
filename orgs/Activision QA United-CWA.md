# Activision QA United-CWA

**Largest certified union in US video game history at formation (March 2024).** Represents ~600 (now ~400) quality assurance testers across Activision Blizzard studios in California, Texas, and Minnesota. Voluntarily recognized by Microsoft under the 2022 Labor Neutrality Agreement.

## Current status
**No first contract after 2.5 years.** Strike authorized October 2026. Workforce has shrunk from ~600 to ~400 members while workload has not decreased. The Modern Warfare 4 launch window gives the union leverage.

## Key timeline
- **2024-03:** Formed (600 members); voluntarily recognized by Microsoft
- **2025-Fall:** Microsoft-CWA neutrality agreement expired
- **2026-10-16:** Strike authorization vote passed; next bargaining Oct 21-22
- **Ongoing:** AI automation a major concern for QA workers
