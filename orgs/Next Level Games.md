# Next Level Games

**Canadian video game developer and wholly owned Nintendo subsidiary.** Based in Vancouver, BC. Best known for Luigi's Mansion 3 (one of the Switch's best-selling titles) and the Mario Strikers series. Acquired by Nintendo in 2021 — one of Nintendo's rare studio acquisitions. Prior to acquisition, Next Level Games had been a second-party Nintendo partner exclusively developing for Nintendo platforms since 2014.

## Key facts

- **154 employees** (as of 2026)
- **No union** as of October 2026
- **No layoffs** documented since Nintendo acquisition (2021)
- Acquired by Nintendo in 2021 for an undisclosed sum
- Named one of **Canada's Top 100 Employers** multiple years
- Specializes in sports-action and physics-based games

## Acquisition context

Nintendo announced the acquisition on January 5, 2021, with completion on March 1, 2021 (Nintendo's 2021 annual report records the acquisition date as March 31, 2021). The transaction was triggered when "a number of owner-directors recently determined that the time is right for them to sell their shares, and NLG therefore began exploring potential sale transactions" (Nintendo official statement). All outstanding shares were purchased, transitioning the studio from second-party to first-party status. Co-founder Douglas Tronsgard retired from the studio by March 2022.

This was one of Nintendo's only studio acquisitions in the 2021-2026 period (along with the full buyout of Monolith Soft shares completed in December 2024).

## Labor note

Next Level Games operates as a non-union shop with no documented labor complaints or layoffs in the 2022-2026 period. The studio has been recognized as a top Canadian employer. As a Nintendo subsidiary, it is subject to British Columbia employment law, which does not have the same strong layoff protections as Japanese labor law but benefits from Nintendo's acquisition-driven growth phase.
