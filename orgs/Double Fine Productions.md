# Double Fine Productions

**American video game developer based in San Francisco, California.** Founded in 2000 by legendary game designer Tim Schafer. Known for creative, character-driven games including Psychonauts, Brütal Legend, and Broken Age. Acquired by Microsoft in 2019.

## History

Double Fine was founded by Tim Schafer after leaving LucasArts, where he directed classic adventure games Grim Fandango, Day of the Tentacle, and Full Throttle. The studio built a reputation for creative, narrative-driven games across multiple genres.

The original Psychonauts (2005) established the studio's signature style but was a commercial failure at launch, leading to financial difficulties. The studio found success through Kickstarter (Broken Age raised $3.3M in 2012) and diversified across publishing, documentary filmmaking, and the Fig crowdfunding platform.

Microsoft acquired Double Fine at E3 2019, with Tim Schafer remaining as studio head. The acquisition ensured completion of Psychonauts 2 (released August 2021 to widespread critical acclaim). The studio maintains a relatively independent culture within Xbox Game Studios, documented extensively through the 22-hour PsychOdyssey documentary series (2023, with a 90-minute update in July 2024).

## Labor

No union organizing has been reported at Double Fine Productions (as of 2026-10-18). The studio has maintained stable employment since Microsoft acquisition with no known layoffs. As a California employer, it is subject to state WARN Act requirements.
