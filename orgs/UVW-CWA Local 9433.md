# UVW-CWA Local 9433 — United Videogame Workers

**First industry-wide direct-join video game union in North America.** Launched at the 2025 Game Developers Conference (GDC) in San Francisco in partnership with the Communications Workers of America (CWA) and the American Federation of Musicians (AFM).

## Structure & model
- **Direct-join (pre-majority unionism):** workers join as individuals regardless of studio, employment status, or whether they're currently employed
- **Jurisdiction:** United States + Canada
- **Open to:** artists, writers, designers, QA testers, programmers, freelancers, contractors, indie developers, unemployed workers, and workers already organizing their workplaces
- **Linked to:** [[events/2025-03-19 — UVW-CWA founded]] and CODE-CWA (which has organized 6,500+ tech and game workers since 2020)

## Key actions
- **2025-03-19:** Launched at GDC 2025 with petition drive demanding job security and dignity for video game workers (as of 2025-03-19, [[sources/2025-03-19 — uvw-cwa-launch-press-release]])
- **2025-10-15:** Issued public statement opposing the EA buyout; called on regulators to scrutinize the deal; warned that ~$20B in debt could trigger studio closures and mass layoffs; emphasized organizing as the only guarantee of worker voice when ownership changes (as of 2025-10-15, [[sources/2025-10-15 — cwa-union-ea-buyout-statement]])

## Industry context (per UVW-CWA launch materials)
- **10,500** game industry jobs lost in **2023** (as of 2025-03-19, [[sources/2025-03-19 — uvw-cwa-launch-press-release]])
- **14,600** game industry jobs lost in **2024** (as of 2025-03-19, [[sources/2025-03-19 — uvw-cwa-launch-press-release]])
- **40,000** game workers lost jobs **since 2022** (as of 2025-10-15, [[sources/2025-10-15 — cwa-union-ea-buyout-statement]])
- >10% of surveyed devs reported being laid off in 2024 (GDC State of the Game Industry)
- Over 30 studios laid off their entire staff and were closed by parent companies including Microsoft and Sony
