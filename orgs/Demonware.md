# Demonware

**Activision's online services and netcode engineering studio, based in Dublin and Shanghai.** Founded in 2003, acquired by Activision in 2007. Demonware manages the online infrastructure for the Call of Duty franchise including matchmaking, data services, and anti-cheat systems.

## Labor and location context

As of October 2026, no known union organizing has occurred at Demonware. The studio's workforce spans two very different regulatory environments:

- **Dublin, Ireland:** Irish employment law provides stronger worker protections than US law (including statutory redundancy pay, consultation requirements for collective redundancies, and a strong unfair dismissals framework). However, formal trade union presence in the Irish game development sector is minimal. Dublin has a growing tech sector but game-specific unions are virtually nonexistent.

- **Shanghai, China:** Chinese labor law provides certain protections but independent unions are effectively prohibited. All official unions are under the All-China Federation of Trade Unions (ACFTU), which is state-controlled. No independent worker organizing is possible.

Demonware's technical focus (online services, not game development) and international structure make it an unlikely candidate for the kind of wall-to-wall union organizing seen at US-based Activision QA and Blizzard studios.
