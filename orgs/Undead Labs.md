# Undead Labs

**Seattle-based game developer founded in 2009 by Jeff Strain, known for the State of Decay franchise.** Acquired by Microsoft in 2018. Undead Labs was owned by Xbox Game Studios until September 2026, when it separated to become an independent employee-owned studio — part of a broader Xbox downsizing pattern.

**A dramatic story of studio survival through downsizing.** Undead Labs endured multiple layoff waves (2024, 2025, 2026), reports of potential closure, and ultimately achieved independence from Microsoft — but at the cost of "significant" layoffs. The studio shipped State of Decay with just 20 people and State of Decay 2 with 65; State of Decay 3 was reportedly "double that size or greater" before the cuts. No union organizing has been reported.
