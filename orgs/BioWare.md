# BioWare

**US + Canada studio, owned by [[orgs/Electronic Arts]].** Known for **Mass Effect**, **Dragon Age**, **Star Wars: The Old Republic**.

## Labor-flow
- **2023-08:** **50** employees laid off; included veteran writer Mary Kirby and technical director Jon Renish (as of 2023-08-24, [[sources/2025-01-30 — pcgamer-bioware-veterans-laid-off]])
- **2025-01:** Restructuring — at least ~**24** laid off; headcount dropped from >200 to **<100**; senior devs posted publicly they were laid off while EA claimed they were being 'reassigned' (as of 2025-02-02, [[sources/2025-02-02 — pcgamer-bioware-under-100]]; as of 2025-04-29, [[sources/2025-04-29 — gamedeveloper-respawn-hundreds-layoffs]])
- **Net flow:** ~74+ cut (2023-2025), headcount collapsed from >200 to <100; no documented hiring sprees (as of 2026-10-16)

## Key decisions
- **2023-08:** ~50 laid off in 'restructuring' — workforce reduced (as of 2023-08-24, [[sources/2025-01-30 — pcgamer-bioware-veterans-laid-off]])
- **2025-01:** Studio gutted — <100 employees remain; focused on next Mass Effect (as of 2025-02-02, [[sources/2025-02-02 — pcgamer-bioware-under-100]])
- **2024-10:** Dragon Age: The Veilguard released; later cited as 'underperforming' (as of 2025-04-29, [[sources/2025-04-29 — gamedeveloper-respawn-hundreds-layoffs]])
