# Motive Studio

**Montreal-based EA studio, HQ in downtown Montreal.** Founded in 2015. Developer of **Star Wars: Squadrons** (2020), the **Dead Space remake** (2023), and the upcoming **Iron Man** game. Joined the Battlefield development team in April 2024.

## Growth trajectory

Unlike most EA studios in 2023-2026, Motive has been in an active **growth phase**:
- Hiring 100+ developers in 2024-2025 across Iron Man and Battlefield
- Expanding Montreal presence
- Running dual-project model (Iron Man + Battlefield support)

## Labor context — Montreal

Montreal's video game industry employs ~19,000+ people across studios including Ubisoft Montreal, EA Motive, Behaviour Interactive, Square Enix Montreal (closed), and others. Despite Quebec's $477M (2025) multimedia tax credit program, the province has seen **nearly 500 video game job cuts across all Montreal studios in 2024-2025** (per Ctrl Alt Deliberate / Betakit).

Montreal is a target market for UVW-CWA organizing ([[orgs/UVW-CWA Local 9433]]).
