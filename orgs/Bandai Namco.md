# Organization: Bandai Namco

## Metadata
- **Full Name:** Bandai Namco Holdings Inc.
- **Founded:** 2005 (merger of Bandai and Namco)
- **Headquarters:** Tokyo, Japan
- **Ticker:** TYO:7832
- **Key Subsidiaries:** Bandai Namco Entertainment (BNE), Bandai Namco Studios, Bandai Namco Filmworks (BNFW), Bandai Namco Experience (BNEX), Reflector Entertainment
- **Industry:** Video games, toys, arcade, anime/music

## Structure
Bandai Namco Holdings oversees five main business segments: Digital (video games via BNE), Toys & Hobby (Bandai), Film & Music (Bandai Namco Filmworks), Amusement (Bandai Namco Experience), and IP Creation.

## Labor Events

### 2024: "Expulsion Rooms" (Oidashi Beya) Scandal
- **Date:** October 2024 (reported by Bloomberg)
- Approximately **200 out of 1,300 Bandai Namco Studios employees** were moved to "expulsion rooms" (oidashi beya) — given no tasks and pressured to resign.
- Nearly **half (~100) had voluntarily resigned** by the time of reporting.
- Bandai Namco denied the practice, stating it was a standard reassignment process.
- This followed the cancellation of "several" game titles including One Piece, Naruto, and a Nintendo-licensed project.
- (Sources: Bloomberg, GamesIndustry.biz, PC Gamer, Windows Central — Oct 15-16, 2024)

### 2024-2025: Silent Workforce Reduction at Bandai Namco Studios
- Japan Pension Service database shows a **decrease of 117 insured employees** at Bandai Namco Studios between April 2024 and February 2025.
- Represents a continuation of the workforce reduction identified in the "expulsion room" reporting.
- (Sources: Automaton, Game Developer, Game Rant — Feb 2025)

### Reflector Entertainment Layoffs (Subsidiary)
- **November 2024:** Reflector Entertainment (Canadian subsidiary, developer of Unknown 9: Awakening) laid off ~18% of its workforce after the game's commercial underperformance.
- **January 2025:** Second round of layoffs; remaining staff consolidated into a single project; their next original game cancelled.
- (Sources: Game Developer, ResetEra — Nov 2024, Jan 2025)

### Blue Protocol Shutdown
- **August 2024:** Bandai Namco announced the MMO **Blue Protocol** would shut down in Japan on **January 18, 2025**, and the Western release was cancelled entirely.
- (Sources: GamesIndustry.biz, TheGamer — Aug 2024)

### Game Cancellations (Oct 2024)
- Multiple unrevealed titles cancelled, including One Piece, Naruto, and a **Nintendo co-developed game**.
- Project cancellations closely tied to the "expulsion room" workforce reduction.
- (Sources: Bloomberg, ScreenRant, Metro — Oct 2024)

### 2026: Major Corporate Restructuring
- **Announced March 1, 2026**, effective **April 1, 2026**.
- Restructure covers all major divisions: Bandai Namco Holdings, Bandai (toys), Bandai Namco Entertainment (games), Bandai Namco Filmworks, Bandai Namco Experience.
- Executive reshuffle at departmental head level.
- **Sony investment** involved in the restructuring.
- (Sources: Anime News Network, Rock Paper Shotgun, Final Weapon — Mar 2026)

## Union Organizing
- **No evidence** of union formation or union organizing activity at Bandai Namco in Japan during 2022-2026.
- Labor practices in Japan occur within the standard enterprise union framework; no independent game developer union identified.
- (Conclusion based on search results — none found)

## Elden Ring Team / FromSoftware Relationship
- Bandai Namco has historically co-published and co-owned Elden Ring IP with FromSoftware.
- **February 2024:** Reports indicated the Elden Ring IP rights transferred fully to FromSoftware, ending co-ownership structure.
- No layoffs specific to the Elden Ring team (development is handled by FromSoftware, not directly by Bandai Namco).
- FromSoftware continues to operate independently; Bandai Namco remains a publishing partner.
- (Sources: Metro — Feb 2024)

## Notes
- Unlike many Western publishers, Bandai Namco's workforce reductions were accomplished primarily through attrition and "oidashi beya" pressure tactics rather than mass layoffs — a common pattern in Japanese corporate downsizing.
- The 2026 restructuring with Sony investment represents a significant strategic shift for the group.
