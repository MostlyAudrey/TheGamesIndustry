# Turn 10 Studios

**First-party Xbox racing studio based in Redmond, Washington, founded in 2001 by Alan Hartman.** Responsible for the Forza Motorsport simulation racing series. Forza Horizon is developed by sister studio Playground Games (also based in the UK). 

**Relatively stable through most of Microsoft's gaming cuts**, Turn 10 lost its founder Alan Hartman to retirement in October 2024 (after 36 years at Microsoft). Craig Duncan, who left Rare's studio head role, assumed oversight as the new head of Xbox Game Studios. The studio faced layoff rumors in mid-2025, with roughly 70 positions reportedly cut in July 2025 as part of broader Microsoft reductions. A former developer stated the Forza Motorsport team was "no more," though the Forza brand continues. No union organizing has been reported.
