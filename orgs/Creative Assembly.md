# Creative Assembly Ltd

**UK-based video game developer, wholly owned by Sega Corporation.** Best known for the Total War strategy series and Alien: Isolation. The studio suffered extensively in Sega's European restructuring following the cancellation of its live-service extraction shooter Hyenas in September 2023.

## Headcount trajectory

Creative Assembly's workforce has experienced a dramatic decline from its 2023 peak:

| Period | Employees | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 Q2 (peak) | ~1,920 | — |
| 2023 Q4 | ~1,452 | -468 from peak |
| 2024 Q1 | ~1,290 | -162 |
| 2024 Q3 (low) | ~981 | -309 |
| 2025 Q1 | ~1,008 | +27 |
| 2026 Q1 | ~1,090 | +82 |

*Data source: Revelio Labs (August 2026)*

## Layoff timeline

### September 2023 — Hyenas cancellation
- Sega cancelled Hyenas, a live-service extraction shooter CA had been developing for years
- The entire Hyenas development team faced redundancy
- Sega also cancelled multiple unannounced games
- CA confirmed it would begin redundancy consultation: "we have always aimed to operate as a 'people-first' studio; that is foundational to our values and culture"

### March 2024 — Sega Europe-wide cuts
- 240 jobs lost across Sega Europe; majority at Creative Assembly
- Smaller numbers at Sega Hardlight and Sega Europe office
- Relic Entertainment sold to external investor
- Sega blamed "rapidly changing financial landscape" and "reactionary decline from the stay-at-home demand in Covid-19"

### Financial impact
- CA reported £3.4M loss before tax for year ending March 2024 (from £10.6M profit in 2023)
- Cost attributed to restructuring and redundancies
- Sega's European operations recorded significant losses

## Studio structure

### Main studio (Horsham, UK)
- Develops Total War series, Alien: Isolation
- 71% of CA's workforce in Northern Europe (primarily UK)
- 0 active job postings in 2026 per Revelio Labs

### CA Sofia (Bulgaria)
- Founded 2001 as Black Sea Studios; acquired by Crytek (2008); sold to Sega (2017) and renamed Creative Assembly Sofia
- Contributed to Total War: Warhammer III DLC (Tides of Torment)
- ~27% of CA workforce in Eastern Europe per Revelio Labs (including Sofia)
- Status: operational as of 2026; supported Warhammer III content

## Union status
**No known union organizing at Creative Assembly UK or Sofia.** The Sega group's only union is AEGIS-CWA at Sega of America (Irvine, CA).
