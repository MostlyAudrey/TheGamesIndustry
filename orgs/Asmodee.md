# Asmodee

French board game publisher, one of the largest in the world. Owns major board game IPs including Catan, Ticket to Ride, Dobble/Spot It!, Splendor, and Pandemic. Also operates digital board game adaptations.

Acquired by Embracer Group in 2021 for €2.75B in cash and shares — one of Embracer's largest acquisitions. In the 2024 Embracer split, Asmodee Group became one of the three independent companies (alongside Coffee Stain & Friends, and Middle-earth Enterprises & Friends).

## Labor record

French labor law provides strong worker protections. No unionization drives reported.
