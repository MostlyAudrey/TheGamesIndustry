# Bend Studio

**Bend Studio** is a Bend, Oregon-based game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for Days Gone (2019). The studio has a tumultuous recent history: its Days Gone sequel was rejected, a live-service pivot collapsed, and the resulting layoffs cut 30% of its workforce.

## Ownership & Structure

- **Sony Interactive Entertainment** acquired Bend's predecessor in 2008
- Operates from Bend, Oregon
- Studio head: Kevin McAllister

## Labor Timeline

### 2019 — Days Gone release
After nearly 6 years of development, Days Gone shipped to mixed reviews but strong commercial performance. Developer accounts described a challenging development with multiple pivots.

### 2021 — Sequel rejection and creative departure
- Sony reportedly rejected a Days Gone sequel pitch
- Creative director John Garvin publicly criticized the rejection, blamed low Metacritic scores
- Garvin was removed/departed from the studio
- Studio morale suffered; key talent departed

### 2023 — Live-service pivot
Bend Studio shifted to developing an unannounced live-service open-world game, part of Sony's broader live-service strategy.

### January 2025 — Project cancellation
Sony canceled Bend's unannounced live-service project as part of the broader retreat from live-service games following Concord's failure.

### June 2025 — 30% layoffs (~40 jobs)
- ~40 of ~120 staff laid off
- Sony confirmed Bend "is working on a new project" and remains "confident in its future"
- The studio survived (unlike Bluepoint), but was significantly reduced

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Bend Studio as of October 2026. No public unionization drive has been reported.

## Edge Notes
- Bend is a cautionary tale about the live-service pivot's human cost: a studio that built a successful single-player franchise, was forced into live-service development, then lost 30% of its workforce when the strategy reversed
- Unlike Bluepoint Games (closed entirely), Bend survived due to its senior leadership and smaller operational footprint
- The studio is now developing a new single-player open-world project
