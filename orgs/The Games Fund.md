# The Games Fund

Early-stage VC fund focused on video games. Launched April 2021 by veterans of MY.GAMES corporate venture arm (MGVC). Targets seed-stage game developers, gaming technologies, and services.

**Key people:** Ilya Karpinskiy (founder, ex-MGVC), Maria Kochmola (co-founder, managing partner), Ilya Eremeev (co-founder).

**Notable portfolio:** Mundfish (Atomic Heart), Sweet Bandits Studios, Playgama, Hypemasters. 35+ studios invested total, 6 acquired by MY.GAMES.

**Strategy:** Leads seed/Series A rounds. Deep Eastern European game dev connections (Cyprus, Poland, Ukraine, Baltics) combined with US market access. One of the few dedicated Eastern Europe-focused games VCs.
