# Square Enix Holdings Co., Ltd.

**Japanese video game publisher and developer, HQ Shinjuku, Tokyo.** Major franchises: Final Fantasy (incl. FFXIV MMO), Dragon Quest, Kingdom Hearts, Nier, Octopath Traveler, Bravely Default, Mana, SaGa.

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Undergoing radical restructuring — selling off Western studios (2022), cutting Western publishing staff (2024-2025), closing overseas development, consolidating everything in Japan. FFXIV Dawntrail (Jul 2024) initially boosted MMO revenue but reception was poor and engagement/income declined sharply by 2026.

## Corporate structure

Square Enix operates through a "Creative Business Unit" system:
- **CBU1** — Final Fantasy (XIV, XVI, VII Remake, Kingdom Hearts)
- **CBU2** — Dragon Quest, Nier
- **CBU3** — Octopath Traveler, Bravely Default
- **Square Enix External Studios** — partnerships with Western developers (Life is Strange developer Deck Nine, Just Cause developer Avalanche)
- **Square Enix Europe** — London-based publishing, marketing, localization — severely impacted by 2025 layoffs
- **Square Enix America** — Los Angeles-based publishing — impacted by 2024 and 2025 cuts

## Western studio divestment history
- **Aug 2022:** Sold Crystal Dynamics (Tomb Raider), Eidos-Montréal (Deus Ex), Square Enix Montréal to Embracer Group for $300M. Included IPs: Tomb Raider, Deus Ex, Thief, Legacy of Kain, and 50+ others.
- **2024-2025:** Remaining Western operations reduced from development to publishing-only; overseas development studios closed in 2025 restructuring.
- **Post-2025:** Square Enix's Western presence is publishing/marketing only, with Life Is Strange, Outriders, Just Cause franchises managed through external partnerships.

## FFXIV financial and engagement decline
- **FY2024/3 (Apr 2023-Mar 2024):** MMO segment strong, Endwalker expansion cycle
- **FY2025/3 (Apr 2024-Mar 2025):** Dawntrail launch (Jul 2024) boosted net sales and operating income in FY2024 report
- **Aug 2025 quarterly:** FFXIV engagement declined; sales -23% ($19.6M), operational profits down -45% ($20.3M)
- **FY2026 (May 2026):** MMO revenue -31% YoY; no major expansion in period; attributed to Dawntrail being poorly received and subscriber decline

## Union organizing and labor context

### Japan
- **No known union organizing** at Square Enix Japan. Japanese game publisher unions are extremely rare.
- Japanese labor law (doctrine of abusive dismissal) protects regular employees from arbitrary layoffs, which partially explains why cuts fall disproportionately on Western offices.
- Square Enix benefits from the broader Japanese employment framework where companies must prove near-bankruptcy to justify mass layoffs of regular employees (per [[sources/2024-07-08 — verge-japan-avoided-layoffs]]).

### US/UK
- No reported union organizing at Square Enix America (Los Angeles) or Square Enix Europe (London).
- The 2025 layoffs impacted "nearly all areas" of the Western business — IT, marketing, publishing, sales, QA, business planning — but no collective action or union response has been reported.
- UK employment law requires a consultation period for proposed redundancies (ongoing as of Nov 2025).

### Generative AI and labor
- Square Enix stated in Nov 2025 that it expects **70% of QA and debugging work** to be handled by generative AI by the end of 2027 ([[sources/2025-11-06 — ign-squareenix-mass-layoffs]]).
- The company has said it will be "aggressive in applying AI" across development and publishing (per IGN).
- This AI push coincides with layoffs affecting QA staff in Western offices.
