# Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia (PIF)

**Saudi sovereign wealth fund.** The largest single capital source in the EA take-private, and the operator of **Savvy Games Group** — a US/CA-relevant gaming platform.

## Capital node
- **AUM:** **$930bn** (as of 2026-03-23, Invest Riyadh; *verify with PIF primary*).
- **Gaming mandate:** **$38B** to invest in gaming by **2030** (as of 2023-04-03, Bloomberg via [[sources/2025-09-29 — latimes-ea-55b-take-private]]).
- **EA stake:** PIF was already the **largest insider stakeholder** and will **roll over its existing 9.9% stake** into the take-private (as of 2025-09-30, [[sources/2025-09-30 — nbc-ea-52-5b-pe-buyout]]).
- **Savvy Games Group portfolio (US/CA-relevant):**
  - [[orgs/Scopely]] (Culver City, CA) — acquired 2024-2025; holds Niantic's games portfolio incl. *Pokémon Go*
  - [[orgs/ESL FACEIT Group]] (esports, US presence) — acquired 2022
  - [[orgs/Steer Studios]]
- **Other gaming:** "minority stakes in most scaled public video gaming publishers" since 2022 (as of 2025-09-30, Marok/NBC).
