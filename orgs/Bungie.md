# Bungie — Games Industry Power Map Entry

## Summary

Bungie is a Bellevue, WA-based AAA game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for creating the Halo, Destiny, and Marathon franchises. The studio went from independent (post-Microsoft 2007) through a ~$3.6B Sony acquisition (2022) to a severe headcount collapse from ~1,400 pre-2023 to ~500 (est.) by 2026 — a ~64% reduction driven by three devastating layoff rounds, a failed Marathon reboot, the end (and subsequent reversal) of Destiny 2 live service, and two CEO changes in less than a year.

## Ownership & Structure

- **Sony Interactive Entertainment** acquired Bungie for ~$3.6B (announced Jan 31, 2022; closed July 15, 2022)
- At acquisition, Bungie was promised independence: "continue to independently publish and creatively develop" as a multi-platform studio
- After the 2024 layoffs, ~155 employees were being integrated into SIE, eroding the independence model
- By 2026, Sony recorded a $765M impairment/writedown on the acquisition

## Labor Timeline

### October 2023: First layoff round (~100, ~8%)
- Triggered by Destiny 2 revenue falling 45% below projections
- Lightfall expansion "quality miss" identified
- Two game releases delayed (Final Shape, Marathon)
- Among those laid off: chief legal officer Don McGowan

### July 2024: Second layoff round (220, 17%)
- CEO Parsons admitted "we began running in the red"
- Additional 155 staff transferring to Sony SIE roles
- ~40 staff spun out into a new Sony-owned studio
- Combined with Oct 2023: ~40% of workforce gone in 12 months
- Reputational crisis: Parsons's $2.4M classic car spending revealed

### August 2025: Pete Parsons steps down
- Replaced by Justin Truman (Bungie veteran)

### March 2026: Marathon launches
- Extraction shooter reboot; 88K Steam peak concurrent
- By September: 96% player base decline

### May 2026: Destiny 2 live service ends
- Final update: Monument of Triumph (June 2026)
- Studio refocuses on Marathon

### June 2026: Largest layoff round (292+ WARN, ~400 est.)
- "Most of the Destiny team and some Marathon team members" cut
- Justin Truman steps down after ~10 months
- Poria Torkan (former VP Ops) becomes studio head
- Co-founder Jason Jones reportedly among those laid off
- Sony takes $765M writedown

### September 2026: Strategy reversal
- Bungie reverses Destiny 2 shutdown; un-vaults content
- Marathon roadmap: PvE mode, social space
- New leadership acknowledges failures
- Studio estimates: ~500 employees

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Bungie as of October 2026. Key developments:

- **Oct 2023 layoffs**: Former CLO Don McGowan became a public advocate for industry-wide unionization
- **July 2024 layoffs**: Employees publicly called for unionization on social media; former developers amplified calls
- **CWA statements**: Communications Workers of America issued statements following Bungie layoffs
- **UVW-CWA**: Industry-wide union launched March 2025; open to Bungie workers
- No NLRB certification election filings found for Bungie-specific unit

## Edge Notes

- Bungie is a rare case of a studio shrinking by >60% under a parent while the parent takes a billion-dollar writedown
- The independence promise ("multi-platform, creative autonomy") was effectively dead by 2026
- The studio now appears to be in a "make or break" phase under Poria Torkan's leadership
- Marathon Season 3 (late 2026) is widely seen as make-or-break for the reboot
