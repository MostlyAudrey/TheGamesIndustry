# March Capital

Venture growth firm headquartered in Santa Monica, California. Invests globally since 2014. Over $1.65B across four funds. Primary focus is AI-enabled enterprise software, but has notable gaming track record.

**Key people:** Sumant Mandal (co-founder, managing director), Jamie Montgomery (co-founder), Gregory Milken (managing director), Pär-Jörgen Pärson (GP).

**Notable games portfolio:** [[orgs/Epic Games]] (Fortnite), Mojang/Minecraft (→Microsoft, $2.5B), Among Us/Innersloth.

**Strategy:** "Early-growth" VC — leads rounds at inflection point between early and growth stage. Global mandate with strong North America focus. High-conviction portfolio (25+ companies across 4 funds). Generalist with strong gaming track record.
