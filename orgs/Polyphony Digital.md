# Polyphony Digital

**Polyphony Digital** is a Tokyo-based racing game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for the Gran Turismo series, one of gaming's most successful and long-running franchises.

## Structure

- Founded as an internal group within Sony's Japan Studio (Polys Entertainment)
- Became independent studio (Polyphony Digital) after Gran Turismo's success in 1998
- Led by founder Kazunori Yamauchi since inception
- One of Sony's smallest first-party studios by headcount (~200-300)

## Labor History

Polyphony Digital is notable as **one of the most stable studios** in the entire game industry. It has never reported layoffs in its decades-long history. The studio was not affected by SIE's February 2024 layoffs or any subsequent Sony restructuring.

The studio maintains a small, focused team relative to the scale of the Gran Turismo franchise, with 200-300 developers maintaining one of PlayStation's most profitable franchises.

### Crunch notes
- Like most Japanese studios, overtime/crunch is a known cultural norm, but no major whistleblower reports specific to Polyphony Digital

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Polyphony Digital as of October 2026. Japanese labor unions in the game industry are rare.

## Edge Notes
- The studio is unusual in being effectively a single-franchise developer
- Kazunori Yamauchi remains president at age ~58, showing unusual leadership continuity
- Gran Turismo 8 is in development; the studio was hiring as of 2026
- Polyphony Digital is considered the safest studio in the Sony portfolio from a job security standpoint
