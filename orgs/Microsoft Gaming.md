# Microsoft Gaming

**Largest game publisher by market capitalization.** HQ Redmond, WA. CEO Satya Nadella (MSFT), Gaming CEO Phil Spencer. Acquired ZeniMax/Bethesda (~$7.5B, 2021) and Activision Blizzard (~$69B, 2023).

**Central labor case study:** The publisher with the most unionized workers in North American games (~4,000+ CWA members), enabled by a 2022 Labor Neutrality Agreement that the CWA traded for support of the Activision acquisition. The relationship has deteriorated significantly since the neutrality agreement expired in Fall 2025.

## Labor timeline

| Date | Event | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 2021-12 | Raven Software strike | ~12 QA testers laid off, sparked union drive |
| 2022 | Microsoft-CWA neutrality agreement | Microsoft pledged neutrality; CWA supported Activision acquisition |
| 2023-01 | ZeniMax Workers United-CWA formed | ~300 QA workers; first union under Microsoft; night (MD + TX) |
| 2023-10 | Activision Blizzard acquisition closes | $69B deal |
| 2024-01 | 1,900 layoffs (~9% of gaming) | Primarily Activision Blizzard; CWA members exempted |
| 2024-03 | Activision QA United-CWA formed | ~600 QA workers — largest impartial game union at time; voluntarily recognized |
| 2024-05 | Studio closures | Arkane Austin, Tango Gameworks, Alpha Dog shuttered |
| 2024 | WoW wall-to-wall union (~500) | Blizzard workers continue organizing momentum |
| 2024-11 | ZeniMax Workers United strike | One-day walkout over remote work and outsourcing |
| 2025-07 | ~9,000 Microsoft layoffs (not all gaming) | ZeniMax impacted |
| 2025-Fall | Neutrality agreement expires | Labor relations deteriorate afterward |
| 2025-2026 | Multiple Blizzard units unionize | Overwatch (200), Story/Franchise (160), Diablo (450), Platform/Tech (400+) |
| 2026-07 | 3,200 additional gaming layoffs announced | Half immediate (hundreds of CWA members); rest by end of 2027 |
| 2026-08 | 'Save Our Devs' rallies | CWA rallies across US and Canada |
| 2026-09 | 1,900 Blizzard workers win first contract | Recall rights (14 mo), severance formula, AI language, remote work guarantee |
| 2026-10-16 | Activision QA authorizes strike | No first contract after 2.5 years; workforce 600→400; Modern Warfare 4 launch |
