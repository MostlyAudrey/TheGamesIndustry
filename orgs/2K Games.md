# 2K Games

**Novato, CA-based publishing label of [[orgs/Take-Two Interactive]].** Established 2005. Home to major franchises including NBA 2K, Borderlands, Civilization, XCOM, WWE 2K, and BioShock. Multiple development studios under the 2K umbrella.

## Take-Two cuts context

2K has been the primary label affected by Take-Two's cost-cutting:
- **April 2024:** 579 jobs cut across Take-Two — predominantly at 2K and Private Division
- **July 2025:** Cat Daddy Games (2K's Seattle mobile studio) laid off staff
- **September 2025:** Firaxis Games (flagship 2K strategy studio) laid off staff after Civilization VII launch
