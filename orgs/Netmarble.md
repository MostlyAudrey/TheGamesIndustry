# Netmarble

## Overview

Netmarble is one of South Korea's largest mobile game publishers, founded in 2000. Tencent is a major shareholder (~17.5% via Han River Investment). The company also operates the Marblex blockchain gaming platform.

The company's flagship titles include *Lineage 2 Revolution*, *Marvel Future Fight*, and *Ni no Kuni: Cross Worlds*. Netmarble has a complex subsidiary structure including Netmarble F&C (developer subsidiary), Netmarble Neo, and others.

## Labor Events

### Union formation (May–June 2024)
Netmarble employees formed a labor union in May 2024, declaring that the company was undergoing an "invisible restructuring." The union's inaugural declaration cited:
- A decrease in employee headcount over two years
- Forced resignations with only a month's severance
- Subsidiary closures
- Contract-worker layoffs with remaining contract periods abandoned
- Team dismantlements for "incomprehensible reasons"
- Frozen salaries

In June 2024, the Netmarble union joined a coordinated protest with Nexon and NCSoft unions against the industry-wide restructuring wave [[sources/2024-05-07 — mk-netmarble-union]] [[sources/korea-times-nexon-ncsoft-netmarble-unions-2024]].

### Metaverse World liquidation (January 2024)
Netmarble liquidated its metaverse subsidiary Metaverse World, laying off all ~70 employees (press reported 70; Netmarble did not confirm the number) [[sources/2024-01-23 — gamedeveloper-netmarble-metaverse-world]].

### Netmarble F&C layoffs (January 2025)
Netmarble F&C, a developer subsidiary, laid off ~20 people — approximately 25% of the *Demis Re:Born* development team — in early January 2025 [[sources/2025-01-07 — insider-netmarble-fc-layoffs]].

## Capital Structure

Tencent holds ~17.52% of Netmarble via Han River Investment Pte. Ltd., giving the Chinese giant significant influence over the Korean publisher's strategy.
