# Treyarch

**Call of Duty developer best known for the Black Ops series.** Founded in 1996 by Peter Akemann and Doğan Köslü, acquired by Activision in 2001. Treyarch develops Call of Duty on a three-year rotation alongside Infinity Ward and Sledgehammer Games.

## Labor history

Treyarch is the subject of the landmark 2019 Kotaku report "The Human Cost of Call of Duty: Black Ops 4" which documented developers working 100-hour weeks during crunch periods. QA testers described "sweatshop" conditions ([[sources/2019-04-20 — pcgamer-treyarch-sweatshop-qa]]). Despite the well-documented crunch culture documented at Treyarch, there is no known union organizing at the studio as of October 2026. It was not included in the Activision QA United-CWA unit which covered QA across Activision's Austin, Eden Prairie, and El Segundo locations.

- Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War (2020)
- Call of Duty: Black Ops 6 (2024)
