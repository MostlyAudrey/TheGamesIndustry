# Sega Corporation

**Japanese video game publisher and developer, HQ Shinagawa, Tokyo.** Major franchises: Sonic the Hedgehog, Total War (CA), Like a Dragon / Yakuza (RGG Studio), Persona / Shin Megami Tensei (Atlus), Football Manager, Phantasy Star Online 2.

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Significant restructuring of European operations (240+ jobs cut 2023-2024). Creative Assembly suffered major setback with Hyenas cancellation (2023) and financial losses (£3.4M loss FY2024). Relic Entertainment sold off (2024). **Largest multi-department union in US gaming (AEGIS-CWA) formed at Sega of America Irvine (July 2023)** — currently in contentious bargaining with accusations of union-busting and offshoring.

## Studio breakdown

### Creative Assembly (Horsham, UK) — Total War
- **Franchises:** Total War series (Rome, Medieval, Warhammer, Three Kingdoms), Alien Isolation, Halo Wars 2
- **2023-09:** Hyenas (live-service extraction shooter in development for years) canceled by Sega; entire dev team faced redundancy
- **2023-09:** Redundancy consultation begun for "some areas of UK operations"
- **2024-03:** Majority of 240 Sega Europe layoffs hit CA
- **Financial:** Slipped to £3.4M loss before tax for year ending Mar 2024 (from £10.6M profit in 2023); attributed to restructuring and redundancies
- **No known union organizing at Creative Assembly UK**

### Sega Hardlight (Leamington Spa, UK) — Sonic mobile
- Developer of Sonic Dream Team (Apple Arcade)
- "Small number" of job losses in March 2024 cuts
- No known union activity

### Relic Entertainment (Vancouver, Canada) — ***SOLD March 2024***
- Developer of Company of Heroes, Age of Empires 4 (co-dev), Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War
- **May 2023:** 121 staff laid off
- **March 2024:** Sold to external investor; became independent studio
- Sega had acquired Relic from THQ bankruptcy auction in 2013 for $26.6M

### Sega of America (Irvine, CA)
- Publishing, marketing, localization, QA
- **Largest multi-department union in US gaming:** AEGIS-CWA formed July 2023 (91-26 vote), covering 200+ workers across marketing, sales, product development, QA, localization
- AEGIS-CWA is the first union at a Japanese-owned game publisher in the US
- Active labor dispute: Sega proposed phasing out all temporary workers by Feb 2024; union filed ULP accusing Sega of direct dealing and retaliation
- **2025 ULP:** CWA accused Sega of forcing employees into a meeting where they were told jobs would be offshored to Japan and Europe

### Atlus (Tokyo, Japan)
- Developer of Persona, Shin Megami Tensei, Catherine
- Acquired by Sega Sammy in 2013 (via Index Corporation acquisition)
- 15% salary increase for employees in 2023 (per Kotaku)
- No known union organizing

### RGG Studio (Tokyo, Japan) — Like a Dragon / Yakuza
- Developer of Yakuza / Like a Dragon series, Judgment
- No known union activity or layoffs

### Sega Japan (general)
- **Feb 2023:** Sega Sammy announced 30% salary increase for Japan-based employees — "to create a more comfortable working environment"
- Protected by Japanese labor law (doctrine of abusive dismissal) — no mass layoffs of regular Japan-based employees reported
- No known union organizing at Sega Japan

## Union organizing (AEGIS-CWA)
- **First announced:** April 24, 2023 (Sega of America workers filed for union election)
- **Certified:** July 10, 2023 — NLRB announced 91-26 vote in favor
- **Size:** 200+ workers — largest multi-department union in US gaming industry
- **Coverage:** Marketing, sales, product development, QA, localization, and other departments at Sega of America Irvine office
- **Affiliation:** Communications Workers of America (CWA) via CODE-CWA

### Labor disputes
- **Nov 2023:** Sega proposed "phase out all temporary workers by February 2024" — many in QA and localization, ~80 unionized employees (~40% of unit)
- AEGIS-CWA filed Unfair Labor Practice charge — accused Sega of direct dealing (negotiating with employees instead of the union) and breaching status quo
- **July 2025:** Second ULP filed by CWA — accused Sega of forcing employees into meeting where they were told jobs would be offshored to Japan and Europe

### Key comparisons
- Largest multi-department union, unlike studio-specific unions (ZeniMax QA, Activision QA)
- Unique because it encompasses employees from all types of roles, not just QA or a single department
- First union at a Japanese-owned game publisher in the US

### Japan-side labor context
- Sega Sammy raised Japan salaries 30% in 2023 — potentially a response to union activity in the US and broader Japanese labor shortage
- Japanese labor law makes mass layoffs of regular employees extremely difficult; this may explain why Sega's layoffs are concentrated in Europe and why the US union accused the company of offshoring jobs to Japan
