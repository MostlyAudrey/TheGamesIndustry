# Ninja Theory

**British video game developer based in Cambridge, England.** Founded in 2000, known for cinematic action-adventure games including the Hellblade series. Acquired by Microsoft in 2018 as part of Xbox Game Studios.

## History

Ninja Theory was founded in 2000 by Tameem Antoniades, Nina Kristiansen, and others, building a reputation for cinematic, story-driven action games. The studio gained prominence with Enslaved: Odyssey to the West (2010) and DmC: Devil May Cry (2013). In 2014, the studio announced Hellblade as a self-published, smaller-scale project focusing on mental health themes.

Hellblade: Senua's Sacrifice (2017) was a critical and commercial success, praised for its depiction of psychosis and standout performance by Melina Juergens. Microsoft acquired the studio in 2018.

## Financials

As a UK private company, Ninja Theory files publicly with Companies House. For FY22/23 (ended June 30, 2023), the studio reported £23.99 million ($31.2M) revenue, up 15.8% YoY, and £2.97 million ($3.86M) profit after taxes. The studio had £100.4 million ($130M) in net assets. Revenue was 96.5% intercompany recharge from Microsoft [[sources/2024-05-21 — windowscentral-ninja-theory-hellblade-2-launch]].

Post-Hellblade II (FY23/24), the studio reported £22m turnover and £5m profit, with the studio declaring the game a "successful release" despite mixed retail performance.

## Labor

No union organizing or labour disputes have been reported at Ninja Theory (as of 2026-10-18). The studio has not had known layoffs since acquisition. UK labour law provides collective redundancy consultation.
