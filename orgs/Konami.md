# Organization: Konami

## Metadata
- **Full Name:** Konami Group Corporation
- **Founded:** 1969 (incorporated 1973)
- **Headquarters:** Tokyo, Japan
- **Ticker:** TYO:9766
- **Key Subsidiaries:** Konami Digital Entertainment, Konami Gaming Inc., Konami Osaka Studio
- **Industry:** Video games, pachinko/pachislot, fitness/sports, trading cards (Yu-Gi-Oh!), anime
- **Notable Franchises:** Metal Gear Solid, Silent Hill, Castlevania, Pro Evolution Soccer / eFootball, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Contra, DDR

## Structure
Konami Group Corporation is the holding company for Konami Group. Its gaming-related businesses operate primarily under **Konami Digital Entertainment** (KDE). The company also has significant operations in pachinko/pachislot machines (Konami Amusement), gaming machines for casinos (Konami Gaming Inc.), and fitness/sports (Konami Sports Club).

## Labor Events

### No Major Layoffs or Labor Disputes (2022-2026)
- **No reported layoffs** at Konami Group in Japan between 2022 and 2026.
- Konami's 2021 restructuring (consolidation of production divisions) was completed before this period and did not result in significant job losses.
- No strikes, union disputes, or labor litigation identified in this timeframe.
- The company has taken a **conspicuously opposite approach** to the broader industry trend of mass layoffs.

### Salary Increases — Four Consecutive Years
- **March 2026:** Konami raised base salaries for all full-time employees in Japan for the **fourth consecutive fiscal year**.
- New graduate starting salary raised to **¥305,000/month** (approx. $2,000 USD).
- Monthly increase of **¥5,000** for current employees.
- The company's stated position: *"Human resources are the source of our competitiveness."*
- Previous increases occurred in FY2023, FY2024, FY2025 as well.
- (Sources: Konami press release Feb 19, 2025; Game Developer; Automaton West — Feb 2025)

## Post-Kojima Restructuring Context
- The major post-Kojima restructuring occurred in **2015-2021**: departure of Hideo Kojima, cancellation of Silent Hills, pivot from AAA console toward mobile/pachinko.
- **January 2021:** Konami restructured internal production divisions, consolidating them — but **did not dissolve its game development teams** as widely rumored.
- (Source: GamesIndustry.biz, IGN — Jan 2021)

## Resurgence in Game Development (2022-2026)

### Osaka Studio Opening (April 2023)
- **Konami Osaka Studio** opened on **March 27, 2023**, as a "major production center" for big-budget game development.
- Located at Osaka Umeda Twin Towers South business centre.
- Equipped with motion capture technology and facilities for AAA development.
- The studio represents a **deliberate reinvestment in console game production**.
- (Sources: GameSpot, VGC, Push Square — Apr 2023)

### Next-Generation R&D Center (Tokyo)
- Konami is constructing a "next-generation R&D center" in Tokyo, **due for completion in 2025**.
- Signals long-term commitment to internal game development capabilities.
- (Source: Push Square — Apr 2023)

### Major Game Development Pipeline
- **Metal Gear Solid Delta: Snake Eater** — In development by Konami (with Virtuos), targeting release. A full remake of MGS3 on Unreal Engine 5.
- **Silent Hill 2 Remake** — Developed by Bloober Team under Konami's oversight, released October 2024.
- **eFootball** — Ongoing live-service football franchise, replacing Pro Evolution Soccer.
- **Yu-Gi-Oh!** — Continued trading card game and digital products.
- Multiple other Silent Hill projects (Townfall, f) announced in various stages.
- (Sources: Various — 2023-2025)

### Workforce Growth
- After declining slightly between 2022 and 2024 (−2.1% cumulative), headcount **grew by 2.6% in 2025** (+118 employees).
- The growth aligns with the ramp-up of AAA game production at the Osaka Studio and R&D center.
- (Source: Revelio Labs)

## Notes
- Konami stands out as a **counter-example** to the 2022-2026 gaming industry layoff cycle. While most major publishers conducted mass layoffs, Konami raised salaries four years in a row and invested in new studio infrastructure.
- The company's post-2015 pivot away from AAA console games appears to be **reversing**, with the Osaka Studio opening, R&D investments, and the MGS Delta / Silent Hill 2 projects signaling a return to high-budget game development.
- No significant labor controversies identified in this period.
