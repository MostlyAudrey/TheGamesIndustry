# Insomniac Games

**Insomniac Games** is a Burbank, CA-based AAA game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for the Spyro, Ratchet & Clank, and Marvel's Spider-Man series. Acquired by Sony in 2019, the studio operates dual locations (Burbank, CA and Durham, NC).

## Ownership & Structure

- **Sony Interactive Entertainment** acquired Insomniac in 2019 (reportedly ~$229M)
- Operates two studios: Burbank (flagship) and Durham, NC (opened 2020, focused on Wolverine)
- Founder Ted Price ran the studio for 31 years before retiring in 2025

## Major Labor Event: December 2023 Ransomware Attack

The **Rhysida ransomware gang** breached Insomniac's systems in November 2023, with the hack becoming public on December 12, 2023. The group stole and published **1.67 TB of data** including:
- **Employee personal information** (passport scans, driver's licenses, Social Security numbers)
- **HR documents** (disciplinary reports, internal investigations)
- **Game development assets** (Spider-Man 3, Wolverine, X-Men, Ratchet & Clank content)
- **Recorded meeting videos**
- **Employee headcount and salary data**

The data was used to demand a ransom. When Insomniac/Sony refused to pay, the data was leaked publicly. Sony confirmed the breach and said it was investigating.

**Impact on employees:** The leak put hundreds of developers at risk of identity theft and doxxing. The HR documents exposed disciplinary records and personal information. Polygon described the leak as a "catastrophe" that went "way beyond leaked games."

## February 2024 layoffs
Insomniac was one of several studios affected by SIE's 900-person layoff wave. The exact number of cuts at Insomniac was not disclosed.

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Insomniac Games as of October 2026. Despite the severe privacy breach (Dec 2023) and layoffs (Feb 2024), no public unionization drive has been reported.

## Edge Notes
- The ransomware attack is one of the most severe data breaches in video game industry history
- Insomniac is notable as a studio that has remained non-union despite being at the center of two significant labor-adjacent events
- Unlike many first-party Sony studios, Insomniac was not heavily affected by the live-service pivot fallout (Spider-Man and Wolverine are single-player)
