# Bohemia Interactive

**Czech video game developer and publisher** based in Prague. Privately held, founder-owned. Known for military simulation games — the Arma series, Operation Flashpoint, and DayZ.

## History

Bohemia Interactive was founded in May 1999 by brothers Marek and Ondřej Španěl along with Slavomír Pavlíček. The studio's first game, Operation Flashpoint: Cold War Crisis (2001), was a critical success and won Best Debut at the Game Developers Choice Awards. 

The studio suffered a near-fatal financial crisis after the problematic Xbox port of Operation Flashpoint: Elite (2005), but was saved by simulation contracts from the US Marine Corps — leading to the creation of Bohemia Interactive Simulations (BISim), which later became a standalone entity.

The standalone DayZ (2018), based on a viral mod for Arma 2 created by Dean Hall, became a significant commercial success and established a long-running survival game franchise.

## Structure

Bohemia Interactive has over 500 employees as of 2025. The company remains privately held by its founders. The studio has survived multiple near-death experiences and demonstrated significant resilience.

## Labor profile

No documented union activity. No documented layoffs in the 2022-2026 period. The studio operates under Czech labor law. When THQ Nordic acquired the Bratislava and Brno teams in 2020, employees transferred rather than being laid off.

## Scope note
Bohemia Interactive is a Czech developer outside the US/Canada primary scope of Project Gamecraft. Included as a notable private, founder-owned European studio with significant simulation game franchises.
