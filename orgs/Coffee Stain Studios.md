# Coffee Stain Studios

Swedish video game developer, based in Skövde. Best known for Goat Simulator (2014) and Satisfactory (2024, 1.0 release). Founded by six students from the University of Skövde's game development program.

Acquired by Embracer Group (then THQ Nordic AB) in 2018 for ~$50M (220M SEK). Became the namesake of Embracer's "Coffee Stain & Friends" operating group. In the 2024 Embracer split, Coffee Stain & Friends became one of the three independent companies.

## Labor record

Swedish labor law provides strong worker protections through collective bargaining agreements. No unionization drives reported. No layoffs.
