# Sucker Punch Productions

**Sucker Punch Productions** is a Bellevue, WA-based AAA game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for the Sly Cooper, inFAMOUS, and Ghost series. The studio is known for its relative stability within Sony's first-party portfolio, with no reported layoffs in its history as a Sony-owned studio.

## Ownership & Structure

- **Sony Interactive Entertainment** acquired Sucker Punch in 2011
- Operates from Bellevue, WA (two floors in downtown Bellevue)
- ~180 employees as of 2024

## Labor History

Sucker Punch is notable among Sony's first-party studios for **not having experienced any reported layoffs** during the 2024-2026 industry downturn. The studio was not listed among the studios affected by Sony's February 2024 layoffs of 900 workers, and has not reported any subsequent reductions.

The studio has not been publicly accused of systemic crunch, though like most AAA studios, some overtime was likely during Ghost of Tsushima's final development months.

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Sucker Punch as of October 2026. No public unionization activity has been reported.

## Edge Notes
- One of the most stable studios in the Sony first-party portfolio
- Ghost series is now Sucker Punch's flagship franchise; the studio successfully pivoted from Sly Cooper → inFAMOUS → Ghost
- No known live-service aspirations for Ghost of Yōtei (single-player focus kept the studio off the live-service roller coaster)
- Studio was not impacted by the 2024-2026 Sony restructuring wave
