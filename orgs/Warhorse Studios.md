# Warhorse Studios

**Czech video game developer** based in Prague, wholly owned by Plaion (Embracer Group). Best known for the Kingdom Come: Deliverance series.

## History

Warhorse Studios was founded in July 2011 by Dan Vávra (formerly of 2K Czech, writer on Mafia and Mafia II) and Martin Klíma (formerly of Bohemia Interactive). The studio's debut title, Kingdom Come: Deliverance, was partially crowdfunded (raising $1.1M on Kickstarter) and released in February 2018 to critical acclaim and commercial success — selling 6 million units by February 2024.

The studio was acquired by Koch Media (renamed Plaion) in February 2019 for €42.8 million, becoming part of the Embracer Group. The sequel, Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, launched in February 2025 and sold 1 million copies in its first day.

In May 2026, Warhorse announced it was developing two open-world RPGs simultaneously — another Kingdom Come adventure and a Lord of the Rings open-world RPG, the latter representing a major franchise license acquisition.

## Labor profile

No documented union activity. No documented layoffs. The studio has been in consistent growth mode from 131 employees (2019) to 250 (2024). Czech labor law applies.

## Scope note
Warhorse Studios is a Czech developer outside the US/Canada primary scope of Project Gamecraft. Included as a notable Embracer Group subsidiary and for its independent-to-acquisition trajectory.
