# ZeniMax Workers United-CWA

**First union formed under the Microsoft banner.** Supermajority of ~300 ZeniMax QA workers voted to organize in January 2023 in Maryland and Texas. Largest union within Microsoft and US game industry at the time.

## Key actions
- **2023-01:** Formed; Microsoft voluntarily recognized
- **2023-Q4:** Microsoft incorporated contract workers into union; some converted to full-time
- **2024-11-13:** One-day strike over remote work policy and QA outsourcing; CWA had filed unfair labor practice charge
- **2025:** Opened grievances over how layoffs were executed at ZeniMax
- **2026-07:** Hundreds of CWA members at Bethesda/ZeniMax impacted by Microsoft 3,200 layoff announcement

## Contract status
**Has a first contract** (one of few Microsoft unions to have one). Includes:
- 60 days bargaining for laid-off workers
- Improved severance
- Grievance and arbitration process
- **No-strike, no-lockout clause** (limits ability to walk out but protects against lockouts)
