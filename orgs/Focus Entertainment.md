# Focus Entertainment

French video game publisher focused on AA and niche indie titles. Known for *A Plague Tale*, *Evil West*, *Atomic Heart*, and the *Surge* and *GreedFall* series.

## Capital & Ownership
- Publicly traded (Euronext Paris).
- Historically independent; no majority shareholder.

## Labor Record (2022–2026)

| Date | Event | Detail |
|------|-------|--------|
| FY2023 | Financial performance | Revenue growth through strong *A Plague Tale: Requiem* launch |
| Dec 2023 | Strategic pivot | Shift toward "fewer, bigger" titles; some internal reorganization |
| Jun 2024 | Studio closures / restructuring | Deck13 (acquired 2020) was integrated into Focus; small number of departures |
| 2025 | No mass layoffs reported | Continued selective development funding |

**Labor organizing:** No formal union organizing reported at Focus Entertainment. French labor law provides works council (*comité social et économique*) representation.

## Headcount trajectory
- Stable at ~350 employees (publisher + owned studios).

## Studio portfolio (owned)
- Deck13 (German developer, *Atlas Fallen*, *The Surge* series) — acquired 2020
- Douze-Dixièmes (French developer, founded by ex-Amplitude)

## Notes
- Focus is a relatively stable publisher compared to peers — no mass layoffs 2022–2026.
- Business model is AA curation, which limits exposure to AAA budget blowouts.
- Lost publishing partnership with Saber Interactive (2023) after Embracer acquisition.
