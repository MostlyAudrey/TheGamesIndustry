# Rockstar Games

**Flagship label of [[orgs/Take-Two Interactive]]**, headquartered in New York with development studios worldwide (London/Edinburgh led). Developer of the **Grand Theft Auto** franchise — the most profitable entertainment product in history — and **Red Dead Redemption**.

## Labor significance (the major story)

Rockstar is the most significant **union organizing battleground** at Take-Two as of 2026. After decades of famous crunch culture (detailed in Kotaku's 2018 report on RDR2's 100-hour weeks), Rockstar has faced active organizing via the **IWGB** (Independent Workers' Union of Great Britain) since the **October 30, 2025 mass termination of 31-34 UK employees**.

Key developments:
1. **October 2025:** 31-34 UK employees fired in one day. IWGB accused Rockstar of union-busting.
2. **May 2026:** Remaining workers formed the **Rockstar Games Workers Union** (IWGB).
3. **June 2026:** First meeting with management — union called it "a step in the right direction."
4. **October 2026:** UK employment tribunal ongoing; Rockstar denies union surveillance claims.

### Take-Two's union holdout
Parent company [[orgs/Take-Two Interactive]] remains the **largest Western publisher without any unionized studio or bargaining unit** as of October 2026. The Rockstar IWGB campaign is the closest any Take-Two studio has come to formal union recognition.

## Notable: no mass layoffs

Unlike most major publishers, Rockstar conducted **no mass layoff rounds** during the 2023-2026 industry contraction. Parent company Take-Two cut 5% (579+) in April 2024 and 30+ at Firaxis in March 2023 — but these cuts **did not include Rockstar** at any significant scale. The only Rockstar workforce reduction was the contested October 2025 termination of 31-34 employees.
