# Atlus

**Japanese video game developer best known for the Persona and Shin Megami Tensei series.** Subsidiary of [[orgs/Sega Corporation]] since 2013. Headquartered in Shinagawa, Tokyo.

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Studio is productive and growing post-Metaphor: ReFantazio (2024) success. Underwent salary increases in 2024. No documented layoffs or union organizing.

## Structure

Atlus operates as a wholly owned subsidiary of Sega Corporation (which is itself a subsidiary of Sega Sammy Holdings). The company maintains its brand identity and operates three internal creative departments:

- **Creative Dept. 1:** The original Megami Tensei development team; also handles Etrian Odyssey
- **P-Studio (Creative Dept. 2):** The Persona series team; led by producer Kazuhisa Wada
- **Studio Zero (Creative Dept. 3):** Founded 2016; led by Katsura Hashino (director of Persona 3-5); developed Catherine, Metaphor: ReFantazio

## Salary and labor
- **2024-04:** Atlus raised average annual salary by 15%, following Sega Sammy's broader 30% increase for Japan employees in 2023
- The salary increase was publicly framed as "talent investment"
- **No union organizing:** No union activity documented at Atlus Japan
- **No layoffs:** No documented layoffs of permanent employees in 2022-2026 period

## Notable releases (2022-2026)
- Persona 5 Royal (multi-platform re-release, Oct 2022) — 7M+ sold worldwide
- Persona 3 Reload (Feb 2024) — remake of P3
- Metaphor: ReFantazio (Oct 2024) — new IP from Studio Zero; critically acclaimed
- Persona 5: The Phantom X (mobile, China/Japan)
- Various Shin Megami Tensei related releases
