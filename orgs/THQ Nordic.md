# THQ Nordic

Austrian video game publisher. Originally established in 2011 as Nordic Games GmbH, acquiring the THQ trademark and back catalog after THQ's 2013 bankruptcy. Renamed THQ Nordic in 2014. Became the initial listing vehicle for what grew into Embracer Group.

Focuses on remasters, re-releases, and mid-market publishing. Handles the Darksiders, Destroy All Humans!, SpongeBob SquarePants, and Gothic series. Also owns numerous IPs from the original THQ catalog.

## Embracer context

THQ Nordic's original listing (THQ Nordic AB, later Embracer Group AB) was the foundation for the entire Embracer acquisition spree. The publishing arm itself operates independently under Embracer's Publishing & Distribution segment.

## Labor record

No reported union presence. Austrian labor law provides works council rights for companies with 5+ employees. No layoffs reported.
