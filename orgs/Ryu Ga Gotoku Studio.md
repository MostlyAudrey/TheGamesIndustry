# Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio

**Japanese video game developer and internal division of [[orgs/Sega Corporation]].** Best known for the Yakuza / Like a Dragon series. Headquartered in Tokyo. Founded in 2004 by Toshihiro Nagoshi.

**Current status (Oct 2026):** One of Sega's most prolific and successful internal studios. Released Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth (2024) to critical and commercial success. Announced Virtua Fighter revival. Studio output is notably high — multiple full-scale releases per generation — sustained by a team of ~300.

## Structure

RGG Studio operates as an **internal development division of Sega Corporation** (originally Sega CS1 Team). Unlike [[orgs/Atlus]], which is a legally separate subsidiary, RGG is a direct division of Sega with no separate corporate entity.

## Labor context
- **No union:** No known union organizing at RGG Studio
- **No layoffs:** No documented layoffs. The studio maintains stable employment for its ~300 staff
- **Efficient development:** Known for using Dragon Engine and asset reuse across titles, enabling rapid release cadence without excessive crunch — at least relative to Western AAA studios
- **Nagoshi departure (2021):** Founder Nagoshi left to form Nagoshi Studio under NetEase; studio continuity maintained by Yokoyama and remaining senior staff
- Studio was **not affected** by Sega's 2024 European layoffs (Japan-based)

## Development approach
- Uses internally developed Dragon Engine
- Relies on reusable assets (maps, animations, systems) across entries — enabling one major release every 12-18 months
- This efficiency has allowed RGG Studio to avoid the kind of headcount growth or contraction seen at peer studios
