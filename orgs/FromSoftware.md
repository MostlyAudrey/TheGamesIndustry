# FromSoftware

**Renowned Japanese game developer; creators of Elden Ring, Dark Souls, Bloodborne.** Headquartered in Shinjuku, Tokyo. President Hidetaka Miyazaki since 2014. Founded 1986, entered games 1994. Acquired by Kadokawa in 2014.

## Ownership structure (as of late 2026)

| Shareholder | Stake | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[orgs/Kadokawa Corporation]] | **69.66%** | Majority owner; media conglomerate |
| Tencent (Sixjoy HK) | **16.25%** | Acquired Aug 2022 |
| Sony Interactive Entertainment | **14.09%** | Acquired Aug 2022; also ~10% owner of Kadokawa |

*(as of 2026-10, [[sources/2026-10-05 — revenuememo-fromsoftware-ownership-2026]])*

## Capital chain significance

FromSoftware sits at the center of a strategic contest between Sony and Tencent. Both hold direct minority stakes in the studio (2022), and both have invested in parent Kadokawa (Sony ~10%, largest shareholder from Jan 2025; Tencent ~7.97% from Mar 2025). Sony's dual position (direct studio stake + parent-level influence) gives it significant indirect control despite not owning a majority.
