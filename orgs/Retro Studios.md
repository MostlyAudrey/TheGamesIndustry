# Retro Studios

**American first-party Nintendo developer based in Austin, Texas.** Best known for the Metroid Prime series (which revived the Metroid franchise in 3D) and the Donkey Kong Country Returns series. Founded in 1998 by Jeff Spangenberg (founder of Iguana Entertainment) with Nintendo backing, and became a wholly owned Nintendo subsidiary in 2002.

## Key facts

- **~200 employees** (estimated; exact figure not publicly disclosed)
- **No union** as of October 2026
- **No mass layoffs** in the 2022-2026 period
- President and CEO Michael Kelbaugh has been in role since April 2003 — one of the longest-tenured studio heads in the industry
- Currently developing **Metroid Prime 4: Beyond** (announced; release TBD)

## Notable games

- Metroid Prime (2002) — GameCube
- Metroid Prime 2: Echoes (2004) — GameCube
- Metroid Prime 3: Corruption (2007) — Wii
- Donkey Kong Country Returns (2010) — Wii
- Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (2014) — Wii U / Switch
- Metroid Prime Remastered (2023) — Switch
- Metroid Prime 4: Beyond (announced) — Switch

## Labor note

Retro Studios is a non-union shop with no documented workplace complaints in the 2022-2026 period. The studio experienced heavy crunch during the original Metroid Prime development (reported 80-100 hour weeks), but no similar reports have surfaced for its more recent projects. Under Nintendo's ownership umbrella, Retro benefits from the parent company's aversion to layoffs, and the studio has maintained stable employment throughout the 2022-2026 industry downturn.

## Key decisions

- 2002-05-02: Nintendo secured $1M in Retro stock; reclassified as first-party developer
- 2003-04: Steve Barcia left; Michael Kelbaugh appointed president (serves to date)
- 2010: Shifted from Metroid to Donkey Kong Country Returns
- 2019: Announced development of Metroid Prime 4 following restart from Bandai Namco
- 2023-02: Released Metroid Prime Remastered
- 2024-ongoing: Development of Metroid Prime 4: Beyond
