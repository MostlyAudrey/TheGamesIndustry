# Square Enix Holdings Co., Ltd.

**Japanese holding company overseeing the Square Enix Group.** Formed by the 2003 merger of Square (Final Fantasy) and Enix (Dragon Quest). Headquartered in Shinjuku, Tokyo. Listed on Tokyo Stock Exchange (TYO: 9684).

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Undergoing radical restructuring — sold Western studios (Crystal Dynamics, Eidos-Montréal) to Embracer in 2022, cutting Western publishing staff 2024-2025, closing overseas development, consolidating development in Japan. HD game titles (Final Fantasy XVI, FFVII Rebirth) underperformed commercially post-2023.

## Corporate structure

Square Enix Holdings is the pure holding company that owns the Square Enix Group's operating entities:

- **[[orgs/Square Enix]]** — The operational game development and publishing subsidiary (the entity most commonly referred to as "Square Enix"). Operates through Creative Business Units.
- **Square Enix Europe** — London-based publishing, marketing, localization
- **Square Enix America** — Los Angeles-based publishing
- **Taito Corporation** — Arcade and mobile game subsidiary
- **Various regional publishing subsidiaries** across Asia

## HD game difficulties (post-2023)

| Game | Release | Performance |
|---|---|---|
| Final Fantasy XVI | June 2023 | Sales below expectations; high-budget HD title with narrow platform base |
| Final Fantasy VII Rebirth | Feb 2024 | Sales below expectations; failed to meet internal forecasts |
| Foamstars | Feb 2024 (PS+) | Underperformed as live-service title |
| Various HD games | 2023-2025 | Multiple titles failed to meet sales targets |

The company acknowledged in its 2024 annual report that HD game profitability was a concern. The "Reboots and Awakens" plan explicitly addressed the need to improve quality and profitability of HD titles.

## FINAL FANTASY XIV
- **Profitable MMO segment** — FFXIV remains the company's most reliable revenue source
- **Dawntrail expansion** (July 2024) — initially boosted MMO revenue but was poorly received critically; subscriber engagement declined
- **MMO revenue fell 31% YoY** by May 2026 as no new expansion cycle was in period
- FFXIV's profitability has partially offset HD game losses but declining engagement is a concern

## Western studio divestment history
- **Aug 2022:** Sold Crystal Dynamics (Tomb Raider), Eidos-Montréal (Deus Ex), Square Enix Montréal to Embracer Group for $300M
- **2024-2025:** Remaining Western operations reduced from development to publishing-only
- **2025-11-06:** Mass layoffs across nearly all areas of Western business; overseas dev studios closing

## Union and labor context
- **No known union organizing** at Square Enix Japan. Japanese game publisher unions are extremely rare.
- No mass layoffs in Japan — Japanese labor law (doctrine of abusive dismissal) protects regular employees
- **Generative AI push:** Company stated 70% of QA work to be handled by generative AI by end of 2027 (as of 2025-11-06)
- Cuts fall disproportionately on Western offices where employment law allows layoffs
