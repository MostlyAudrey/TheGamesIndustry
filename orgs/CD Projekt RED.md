# CD Projekt RED

Polish AAA game developer, the primary development subsidiary of CD Projekt S.A. Known for The Witcher series and Cyberpunk 2077. Approximately 1,250 employees across Warsaw, Kraków, and Wrocław offices.

## Labor record

| Year | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 2022 | No mass layoffs | Stable headcount throughout industry downturn |
| 2023 | No mass layoffs | Continued growth — hiring for multiple projects |
| 2024 | No mass layoffs | Cyberpunk 2.0 + Phantom Liberty success stabilized finances |
| 2025 | Works council established | Formal employee representation body |

**Notable:** CD Projekt RED is one of the few AAA studios of its size that avoided mass layoffs entirely during the 2023-2024 industry bloodbath. The Cyberpunk 2077 recovery (2.0 update + Phantom Liberty expansion) generated significant revenue and stabilized long-term employment.

## Union presence

CD Projekt RED does not have a traditional union. However, in 2024-2025, employees established an **internal works council** (rada pracowników) under Polish labor law. Some developers are members of the **Polish Gamedev Workers Union** (OGIW — Ogólnopolski Związek Zawodowy Pracowników Branży Gier), a broader industry union established in 2023.
