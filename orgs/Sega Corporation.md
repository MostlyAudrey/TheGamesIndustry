# Sega Corporation

Japanese video game company, the operational subsidiary of Sega Sammy Holdings. Headquartered in Tokyo. One of the most storied names in gaming — transitioned from console manufacturer (Genesis/Mega Drive, Saturn, Dreamcast) to third-party publisher/developer.

## Structure

Sega operates through several internal divisions and wholly-owned subsidiaries:

- [[orgs/Sonic Team]] — Sonic the Hedgehog
- [[orgs/Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio]] — Yakuza/Like a Dragon
- [[orgs/Atlus]] — Persona, Shin Megami Tensei
- [[orgs/Creative Assembly]] — Total War (UK)
- Sega of America — US publishing
- Sega Europe — EU publishing (Relic sold Jan 2024, CA remains)
- Rovio Entertainment — acquired Aug 2023 ($776M)

## Union presence

No formal union at Sega Japan. Creative Assembly (UK) falls under UK labor law. No unionization drives reported at Sega's Japanese studios. The Japanese game industry has very low union density overall.
