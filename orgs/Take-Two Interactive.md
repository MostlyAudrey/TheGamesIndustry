# Take-Two Interactive

**US video game holding company**, HQ **New York, NY**. Founded **1993** by Ryan Brant. One of the three largest Western game publishers alongside EA and Microsoft Gaming. Publishes through **Rockstar Games** (GTA, Red Dead Redemption), **2K** (NBA 2K, Borderlands, Civilization, WWE 2K, PGA Tour 2K), and **Zynga** (mobile/social games).

## Capital structure
- **Publicly traded** on Nasdaq (TTWO)
- **Market cap:** ~$41B (as of April 2025)
- **Major institutional holders:** includes [[orgs/Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia]] (3.9M shares acquired March 2021)

## Key events (2022-2026 labor)

### Zynga acquisition (May 2022)
Take-Two acquired **Zynga** for **$12.7B** in the largest deal in company history, adding ~6,900 mobile/social gaming employees. This more than doubled Take-Two's workforce.

### March 2023 layoffs
First round of post-Zynga cuts: **30 at Firaxis Games** (Civilization, Marvel's Midnight Suns) and an unspecified number at **Private Division**.

### April 2024 — 5% workforce cut
**579 employees** laid off company-wide (5% of workforce). Multiple unannounced projects cancelled. The largest single layoff round in Take-Two's history.

### Studio closures — Roll7 and Intercept Games (May-November 2024)
- **Intercept Games** (Seattle, WA): KSP2 developer; closed June 28 per WARN notice
- **Roll7** (UK): OlliOlli World and Rollerdrome developer; fully wound down
- Both studios were under the Private Division label
- CEO Zelnick initially denied closures ("we didn't shutter those studios") but Tak-Two later confirmed them

### Private Division sale (November 2024)
Take-Two sold the indie publishing label to an **unnamed buyer** after most staff had already been laid off. Zelnick acknowledged the label's hits "were not big in the context of our core intellectual properties."

### SAG-AFTRA strike (2024-2025)
Take-Two was a struck company in the industry-wide SAG-AFTRA video game strike (July 2024 - July 2025) over AI protections.

## Union organizing
**No studio-level union organizing detected** at any Take-Two owned studio (2K, Rockstar, Zynga, Gearbox) as of 2026-10-17. Unlike competitors Activision Blizzard (Microsoft) which saw extensive CWA QA union wins, or EA which saw the Keywords Edmonton United unionization, Take-Two's workforce has **not** had any public unionization campaigns, elections, or voluntary recognitions.

This makes Take-Two a notable holdout — the largest Western publisher without any unionized studio or bargaining unit as of October 2026.
