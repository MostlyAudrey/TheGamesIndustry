# Devolver Digital

Indie game publisher known for a distinctive, irreverent brand. Portfolio includes *Hotline Miami*, *Enter the Gungeon*, *Cult of the Lamb*, *The Stanley Parable*, and *Trek to Yomi*.

## Capital & Ownership
- Publicly traded on the London Stock Exchange (since 2021).
- Listed via reverse merger; major shareholders include founders (Fork Parker, Mike Wilson, Harry Miller, Rick Stults, Nigel Lowrie), with significant employee ownership.
- Cross-investment structure with [[orgs/Paradox Interactive]] (Paradox holds ~5% of Devolver).

**Labor organizing:** No union organizing reported at Devolver Digital. The company has an unusually flat, employee-friendly culture with significant equity distribution.

## Headcount trajectory
- 2022: ~75
- 2025: ~90 (modest growth despite market headwinds)

## Publishing model
- Devolver does not own its development studios; it partners with indie teams on a game-by-game basis.
- This asset-light model means no studio closures or developer layoffs from Devolver directly.

## Notes
- Devolver remains one of the most stable employers in games publishing — no mass layoffs, no union campaigns, consistent headcount.
- The company's employee-ownership structure may contribute to labor stability.
- Revenue has been volatile post-IPO but the company has avoided layoffs through selective publishing.
