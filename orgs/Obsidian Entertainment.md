# Obsidian Entertainment

**American video game developer based in Irvine, California.** Founded in 2003 by former Black Isle Studios staff. Known for deep narrative RPGs including Fallout: New Vegas, Pillars of Eternity, and The Outer Worlds. Acquired by Microsoft in 2018.

## History

Obsidian Entertainment was founded in 2003 after the closure of Black Isle Studios. The studio built its reputation on deep narrative RPGs: Neverwinter Nights 2 (2006), Fallout: New Vegas (2010 — still considered one of the best Fallout games), and the Kickstarter-funded Pillars of Eternity (2015) that helped spark the CRPG renaissance.

The studio faced financial struggles in its independent era, including the cancellation of a project by publisher Sega and a legal dispute with Bethesda over New Vegas royalties. Microsoft's acquisition in November 2018 provided financial stability.

## Post-Acquisition

Under Xbox Game Studios, Obsidian has expanded significantly. CEO Feargus Urquhart stated in February 2025 that the studio has grown significantly, running multiple simultaneous projects [[sources/2025-04-15 — rpgmaker-obsidian-growing-headcount]]. The studio released Grounded (2020), Pentiment (2022), and Avowed (February 2025) — the latter delayed from late 2024 to give "players' backlogs breathing room" [[sources/2024-02-18 — eurogamer-obsidian-avowed-delay]]. The Outer Worlds 2 remains in development.

In September 2026, Obsidian was transferred from Xbox Game Studios to the ZeniMax reporting structure, placing it alongside Bethesda Game Studios and id Software.

## Labor

No union organizing has been reported at Obsidian Entertainment (as of 2026-10-18). The studio has not had known layoffs since Microsoft acquisition. As a California employer, it is subject to state WARN Act requirements.
