# Ubisoft Massive

Swedish game developer based in Malmö. Founded 1997, acquired by Ubisoft 2008. Known for The Division franchise, Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora, and underlying tech support for major Ubisoft projects including Star Wars Outlaws.

## Labor record

Swedish labor law provides strong worker protections through collective bargaining agreements (kollektivavtal). No major layoffs reported specifically at Massive, though Ubisoft global reductions affected the Swedish studios less than Montreal/Paris.

## Union presence

Massive operates under Swedish collective bargaining law (MBL). No unionization drives reported — the Swedish model provides widespread collective agreement coverage regardless of formal union election.
