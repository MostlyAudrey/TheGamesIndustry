# 4A Games

**Ukrainian-Maltese video game developer** based in Sliema, Malta with a studio in Kyiv, Ukraine. Best known for the Metro series (Metro 2033, Metro: Last Light, Metro Exodus). Founded in 2006 by former GSC Game World developers who worked on S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl.

## History

4A Games was founded by three developers — Andrew Prokhorov, Oles Shyshkovtsov, and Alexander Maximchuk — who left GSC Game World after disagreements over compensation and working conditions. Their first game, Metro 2033 (2010), was an adaptation of Dmitry Glukhovsky's novel and established the studio's reputation for atmospheric, narrative-driven first-person shooters.

The studio survived THQ's 2013 bankruptcy and moved its headquarters to Malta in 2014 for easier EU operations while retaining its Kyiv studio.

## Ownership structure

- **Acquired by Saber Interactive** (Embracer Group) in August 2020 for ~$36M
- **Retained by Embracer Group** when Saber was sold to Beacon Interactive in March 2024
- **Publishing rights** for the Metro series belong to Embracer subsidiary Plaion (formerly Koch Media/Deep Silver)

## Wartime operations

4A Games has continued development through the Russian invasion of Ukraine (2022-present). Saber offered Kyiv employees relocation abroad. The studio announced Metro 2039 in April 2026.

In February 2025, the original Kyiv studio was split — part rebranded as Reburn (independent studio), while the remainder continues as 4A Games Limited.

## Labor profile

No documented union activity. No documented layoffs in the 2022-2026 period. The studio's most significant labor issue was the original founders' departure from GSC Game World over unpaid royalties — the founding mission was to treat employees better than GSC had.
