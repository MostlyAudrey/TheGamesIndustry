# Mojang Studios

**Swedish video game developer** based in Stockholm, wholly owned by Microsoft. Developer of Minecraft, the best-selling video game of all time with over 300 million copies sold.

## History

Founded by Markus "Notch" Persson in 2009, Mojang grew rapidly as Minecraft became a global phenomenon. Microsoft acquired the studio in November 2014 for $2.5 billion — a deal that made Persson a billionaire and led to his departure (along with co-founders Jakob Porsér and CEO Carl Manneh). The studio was rebranded from Mojang AB to Mojang Studios in May 2020.

## Structure

Mojang Studios employs approximately 600 people across five locations (Stockholm HQ, London, Shanghai, Tokyo, Redmond). Maria Angelidou-Smith serves as CEO. The studio reports to Microsoft's Xbox division (as of July 2026, restructured from Xbox Game Studios).

## Labor profile

No union activity documented. Mojang operates under Swedish labor law, which includes strong worker protections and a tradition of collective bargaining. The studio has had no documented layoffs in the 2022-2026 period, maintaining consistent headcount.

Mojang Studios is a Swedish developer outside the US/Canada primary scope of Project Gamecraft. Included for its significance as the developer of the best-selling game of all time and for the Microsoft ownership link.
