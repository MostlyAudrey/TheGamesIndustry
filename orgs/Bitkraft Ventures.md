# Bitkraft Ventures

Leading VC firm focused on gaming and emerging technology. Operates six funds (four equity, two crypto) with $1.05B total AUM. Most active VC investor in gaming globally in 2020, 2021, and 2022 per PitchBook.

**Key people:** Jens Hilgers (founding partner — ex-Goodgame Studios, ESL co-founder), Malte Barth (founding partner).

**Notable portfolio:** [[orgs/Epic Games]] (crown jewel), Mythical Games (blockchain gaming platform — lead investor), Overwatch League (Blizzard/Activision esports league), ProbablyMonsters (AAA incubator), Genvid Technologies.

**Funds:** Venture Fund I ($150M), Venture Fund II (~$275M), Venture Fund III ($275M, launched Apr 2024), Crypto Fund I, Crypto Fund II.

**Strategy:** Multi-fund structure (equity + crypto). Invests across stages seed to growth. Global mandate. Pioneer in gaming/crypto convergence. Dedicated blockchain gaming funds alongside traditional equity gaming funds. Synthetic Reality thesis (gaming, AR/VR, AI, edutainment). Largest dedicated gaming VC by AUM.
