# Media Molecule

**Media Molecule** is a Guildford, UK-based game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for the LittleBigPlanet series and the game creation platform Dreams. Known for its creative, experimental approach to game design.

## Ownership & Structure

- **Sony Interactive Entertainment** acquired Media Molecule in 2010
- Based in Guildford, UK
- Studio director: John Beech (as of 2024)

## Labor Timeline

### January 2023 — Dreams live service ends
Sony announced it would end Dreams live service support. No more curated content updates. The Dreams community continued to create, but the studio effectively stopped supporting the game.

### October 2023 — Layoffs (~20 staff, ~15%)
- Headcount reduced from 135 to 115
- SIE confirmed "significant strategic changes" at the studio
- Studio transitioning to focus on a new project
- The layoffs affected planned community and curation support for Dreams
- Dreams continued to be available, but with reduced support

### Key context
Media Molecule's layoffs were notably separate from the larger SIE 900-person round in February 2024 — the studio's cuts came months earlier as it wound down Dreams support and pivoted to a new project. Media Molecule was reportedly considered for possible closure (per industry analyst Nathan Brown), but survived.

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Media Molecule as of October 2026. The UK has game-specific union organizing (IWGB/Game Workers Unite presence) but no public drive at this studio.
