# ZeniMax Online Studios (ZOS)

**Developer of The Elder Scrolls Online and a now-cancelled second MMORPG (Project Blackbird).** Founded 2007 by Matt Firor. HQ in Hunt Valley, Maryland with a Budapest office. Subsidiary of ZeniMax Media (now Microsoft Gaming).

## Labor status

ZOS is one of the most heavily unionized game studios under Microsoft. Two unions represent workers:

1. **ZeniMax Workers United-CWA** (Jan 2023): ~300 QA testers across ZeniMax, including ZOS QA. First union formed under Microsoft.
2. **ZOS United-CWA** (Dec 2024): 461 developers, designers, engineers, graphic artists. One of the largest game developer union units.

The union presence did not prevent severe layoffs. ZOS lost 275 employees in a 12-month span (July 2025-July 2026), representing a 60%+ staff reduction.

## Key products

- **The Elder Scrolls Online** (2014) — Still live, receiving regular content updates
- **Project Blackbird** (cancelled Jul 2025) — Unannounced MMORPG; cancellation led to Matt Firor's resignation
- **Roundhouse Studios** (absorbed into ZOS May 2024) — Former Human Head / Roundhouse team
