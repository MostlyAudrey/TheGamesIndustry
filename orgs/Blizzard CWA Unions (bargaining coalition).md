# Blizzard CWA Unions (bargaining coalition)

**The largest union contract victory in North American video game history.** ~1,900 workers across 6 Blizzard Entertainment bargaining units won their first collective bargaining agreement in early September 2026.

## How it happened
The organizing wave started with Raven Software QA workers in December 2021. Microsoft signed a Labor Neutrality Agreement with CWA in 2022 to secure union support for the Activision Blizzard acquisition. This enabled a wave of organizing that eventually covered thousands of Blizzard workers across multiple game teams.

## Contract terms (landmark)
- **Recall rights:** 14 months, across ALL Blizzard bargaining units
- **Severance:** 4 weeks + 1 week per 6 months tenure (max 39 weeks)
- **Just cause** and grievance/arbitration process
- **Remote work guarantee** for existing remote workers (~25%)
- **AI language:** Company must discuss, evaluate, and bargain over AI impact — one of first such provisions in a major US tech union contract
- **Outsourcing protections:** Job security against contractor replacement
- **Successorship:** Contract survives sale of unionized units
- **Paid May 1** (International Workers' Day)

## Units that still need contracts
After this win, the Activision QA United-CWA (~400 members) still lacks a first contract after 2.5 years and authorized a strike in October 2026.
