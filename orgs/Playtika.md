# Playtika

## Overview

Playtika is an Israeli mobile gaming company best known for its casino/social slot machine games. Founded in 2010 by Robert Antokol, the company went public on NASDAQ in 2021. Playtika employs approximately 3,500 people globally (as of 2026), with about 1,000 in Israel.

Playtika's core business is mobile casino games including *Slotomania*, *Bingo Blitz*, and *House of Fun*. The company has pursued an aggressive AI-and-automation strategy, citing the need to "leverage AI and automation to do more with less."

## Labor Events

### January 2024 layoffs (10% workforce)
In January 2024, Playtika cut approximately 10% of its workforce, laying off 300–400 employees. The cuts primarily affected staff outside of Israel; the company's 1,100 Israeli employees were largely spared. The company shut down several Central and Eastern European offices. Playtika also terminated the roles of chief revenue officer and chief operating officer. [[sources/2024-01-11 — gamesindustry-playtika-layoffs]]

### January 2026 layoffs (15% workforce ~500 jobs)
In January 2026, Playtika announced a second, larger round of layoffs: approximately 500 employees, or 15% of its global workforce. The company stated it was responding to "market reality changes" and shifting to leaner teams that rely on AI. The layoffs were to be executed during Q1 2026, with $12–15 million in restructuring costs recognized. CEO Robert Antokol sent a letter to staff outlining the cuts. [[sources/2026-01-14 — globes-playtika-layoffs]] [[sources/2026-01-15 — gamesindustry-playtika-layoffs-2026]]

### Labor organizing
No known union organizing activity has been reported at Playtika. The company's heavy reliance on AI/automation to replace human roles suggests management opposition to collective bargaining.
