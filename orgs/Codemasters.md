# Codemasters

**Southam, UK-based racing game developer, owned by [[orgs/Electronic Arts]].** One of the longest-running game developers in the UK (founded 1986). Specializes in licensed racing simulations. Acquired by EA in 2021 for $1.2B.

## Racing portfolio under EA

Since the EA acquisition, Codemasters has produced:
- Annual **F1** titles (F1 22, F1 23, F1 24, etc.)
- **EA Sports WRC** (2023; WRC license)
- Commitment to future F1 titles post-WRC cancellation
