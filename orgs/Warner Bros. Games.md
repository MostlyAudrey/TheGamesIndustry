# Warner Bros. Games

Formerly **Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment** (WBIE). Video game publishing division of Warner Bros., founded 2004.

## Discovery Merger Impact (2022)

WarnerMedia was spun off by AT&T and merged with Discovery Inc. in April 2022 to form **Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD)**. The new entity carried ~$53B in gross debt as of June 2022 (as of 2022-06, Variety). CEO David Zaslav implemented aggressive cost-cutting across all divisions, including games.

Zaslav's strategy for the games division was to shift toward **live-service titles** and focus on only four core franchises: **Harry Potter, Mortal Kombat, DC (especially Batman), and Game of Thrones** (as of 2023-11, Variety). This strategy directly led to the live-service Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League and MultiVersus — both of which became the division's biggest financial disasters.

## Financial Disasters (2024)

- **Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League** (Rocksteady, launched Feb 2024) — failed commercially and critically; WB impaired **$200M** in Q1 2024. CFO Gunnar Wiedenfels: "the disappointing Suicide Squad release this past quarter, which we impaired, leading to a $200 million impact to EBITDA" (as of 2024-05-09, [[sources/2024-05-09 — ign-suicide-squad-200m-writedown]]).
- **MultiVersus** (Player First Games) and **Harry Potter: Quidditch Champions** (Unbroken Studios) — failed to meet targets; WB took an additional **$100M+ impairment** in Q3 2024. Total games writedown for 2024: **> $300M** (as of 2024-11-07, [[sources/2024-11-07 — ign-multiversus-100m-writedown]]).
- Games revenue dropped **41% year-over-year** in Q2 2024, and declined $400M+ in Q1 2024 compared to Hogwarts Legacy's blockbuster Q1 2023.

## Studio Closures (2025)

On February 25, 2025, Warner Bros. Games closed three development studios:
- **[[orgs/Monolith Productions]]** — creator of *Shadow of Mordor* / *Shadow of War*; 201-500 employees. Cancelled the announced **Wonder Woman** game (revealed at The Game Awards 2021).
- **[[orgs/Player First Games]]** — developer of *MultiVersus*; 11-50 employees. MultiVersus was delisted May 2025.
- **[[orgs/WB Games San Diego]]** — mobile/support studio; 51-200 employees.

JB Perrette, head of games and streaming: *"The quality of too many of our new releases has really missed the mark... We need to make some substantial changes to our portfolio/team structure if we are to commit the necessary resources to get back to a 'fewer but bigger franchises' strategy."* (as of 2025-02-25, [[sources/2025-02-25 — gamesindustry-wb-three-studios-closed]])

## Leadership

David Haddad, president of WB Interactive Entertainment since 2013, announced his departure in January 2025 and exited in April 2025 (as of 2025-01-23, [[sources/2025-01-23 — gamesindustry-haddad-exit]]).

## Union Organizing

As of late 2026, **no Warner Bros. Games studio has successfully unionized**. However:

- The **Communications Workers of America (CWA)** specifically targeted WB Games studios after the AT&T/Time Warner merger, including NetherRealm and Rocksteady (as of 2024-09-04, [[sources/2024-09-04 — thewrap-wbgames-union-history]]).
- The **Game Workers Alliance (GWA)** raised labor awareness within WB's orbit during the AT&T era, laying groundwork for CWA's expanded games-industry organizing.
- The broader industry union **United Videogame Workers-CWA Local 9433** (UVW-CWA), launched March 2025, provides a direct-join infrastructure that WB Games workers could potentially use.
- The combination of massive layoffs, studio closures, and the Paramount Skydance merger (Oct 2026) creates conditions conducive to organizing, though no formal campaigns have been publicly announced.

## Skydance Era (Oct 2026)

On October 6, 2026, **Paramount Skydance** completed its $111B acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery. Within 24 hours, **Paramount Games Studio and WB Games** were merged into a single unit under Skydance, led by Tony Driscoll. The merged entity folds in NetherRealm (Mortal Kombat), Rocksteady, TT Games, Avalanche Software, and WB Montreal alongside Paramount's game assets.
