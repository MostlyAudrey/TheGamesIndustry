# Electronic Arts (EA)

**US games publisher, HQ Redwood City, CA. Founded 1982 by Trip Hawkins.** Anchor node of the power map: subject of the **largest leveraged buyout in history** (agreed **2025-09-29**, ~**$52.5B**, expected to close **Q1 2027**).

## Capital node (the money chain)
- **Acquirers (consortium):** [[orgs/Silver Lake Partners]] + [[orgs/Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia]] + [[orgs/Affinity Partners]].
- **Price:** **$210/share** (25% premium over the $168.32 close); **~$52.5B** (NBC/AP) / **~$55B** (LA Times headline).
- **PIF** rolls over its existing **9.9%** insider stake into the deal.
- **Status:** pending shareholder + regulatory approval; **expected to close Q1 2027**.

## Leadership + tenure (big decisions under Wilson)
- **CEO: [[people/Andrew Wilson — CEO EA]]** (since **2013**). Under his tenure:
  - **2024:** planned to cut **5%** of workforce.
  - **2025-05:** closed **several hundred positions**, including **~100 at Respawn (L.A.)**.
  - **2025-09-29:** agreed to the take-private.
  - Revenue **stagnant $7.4–7.6B** over the last 3 fiscal years.

## Studios (the labor surface)
- **BioWare** (Edmunds, CA + Montreal, CA)
- **Maxis** (Edmunds, CA)
- **EA Sports** (multiple US studios)
- **Respawn** (L.A., CA)
- **DICE** (Gothenburg, SE + US)
