# Saber Interactive

## Overview

Saber Interactive is a developer and publisher known for porting and co-developing titles. Founded in 2001, it was acquired by Embracer Group in 2020. In March 2024, Embracer sold Saber to **Beacon Interactive** (owned by Saber co-founder Matthew Karch) for $247 million, transferring 2,950 employees (21% of Embracer's workforce) [[sources/2024-03-14 — vgc-embracer-saber-sale]].

The company operates studios across North America, Eastern Europe (primarily Russia), and elsewhere. As of late 2026, Saber's CEO stated the company has "no real development in North America any more."

### AI controversy (August 2026)
Saber CEO Matthew Karch publicly stated he "would frankly in retrospect have been happy to replace [a writer] with AI," sparking backlash.

## Labor Events

### New World Interactive layoffs (December 2023)
New World Interactive, a Saber subsidiary developing *Insurgency: Sandstorm*, underwent layoffs in December 2023 as part of Embracer's restructuring program. [[sources/2024-12-05 — vgc-new-world-interactive-layoffs]]

### Sale-related workforce transfer (March 2024)
The sale of Saber to Beacon Interactive transferred 2,950 employees. The event itself was a transfer, not a layoff, but some job losses may have occurred during restructuring of the Russian operations and North American wind-down.

No known union organizing activity at Saber Interactive.
