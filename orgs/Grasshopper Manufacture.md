# Grasshopper Manufacture

**Japanese video game developer; cult-favorite action game studio known for the No More Heroes series.** Founded in 1998 by Goichi Suda ("Suda51"). Headquartered in Tokyo. Acquired by NetEase in 2021.

## Suda51 and Grasshopper's creative identity

Goichi Suda, popularly known as Suda51, is one of the most distinctive creative voices in Japanese game development. Grasshopper's games are known for:

- **Stylized violence and pop art aesthetics** — punk, exploitation B-movie visual language
- **Meta-narratives** — games that comment on video game culture
- **Cult status** — Grasshopper games typically achieve cult followings rather than mainstream commercial success
- **Licensed aesthetic mashups** — collaborations with artists, musicians, and other creators

## Ownership history and NetEase acquisition

| Period | Owner | Key Context |
|---|---|---|
| 1998-2013 | Independent | Built reputation with Killer7, No More Heroes on limited budgets |
| 2013-2021 | Marvelous Inc. (via GungHo) | Budget-constrained; fewer high-profile releases |
| 2021-present | NetEase | Financial stability, Suda51 retains autonomy |

The NetEase acquisition in 2021 was notable because:
- NetEase publicly promised creative independence for Grasshopper
- The deal was part of NetEase's broader Japanese studio acquisition strategy (alongside Nagoshi Studio, GPTRACK50, etc.)
- Suda51 stated the acquisition would allow Grasshopper to make "even more extreme games" without worrying about budget
- As of late 2026, Grasshopper had not released a major new title under NetEase, though development was ongoing

## Key quote: Suda51 on NetEase acquisition (2021)

> "This is not a so-called 'absorption' merger, but rather that [Grasshopper] will continue to operate normally as an organization while being under the umbrella of NetEase. There is absolutely no change in the creative side."
