# Krafton Inc.

## Overview

Krafton Inc. (stylized KRAFTON) is a South Korean video game holding company and publisher headquartered in Seongnam, South Korea. It was founded on November 5, 2018 as Krafton Game Union by Bluehole to serve as the parent company unifying Bluehole and its subsidiaries under a single brand. The company is best known for publishing **PUBG: Battlegrounds**, one of the best-selling video games of all time, and owns a growing portfolio of global development studios.

The company's name "Krafton" is derived from "craft" + "ton" (as in an important place/unit). The former trade name "Krafton Game Union" sometimes causes confusion with labor unions — it is **not** a labor union but a corporate holding structure.

## Financial Performance

- 2022: Operating revenue ~1.5 trillion KRW
- 2023: Record revenue, 11 studio investments made
- 2024: Three consecutive quarters of record revenue; ~2.1 trillion KRW ($1.4B) in Jan-Oct 2024
- 2024 Q3 record quarterly profit of $717 million (approx 1 trillion KRW)
- Shares up >70% since start of 2024
- Market cap: ~$9B (2025)

## Labor and Employment

### Voluntary Resignation Program (November 2025)

In **November 2025**, Krafton launched a controversial **voluntary resignation program** weeks after declaring itself an "AI-first company." Key details:

- Declared "AI-first company" in October 2025, planning to invest over 130 billion won ($88M) in agentic AI infrastructure beginning 2026
- On November 4, 2025 earnings call, CFO Bae Dong-geun announced a **company-wide hiring freeze** except for AI-related and original IP development roles
- On November 12, 2025, voluntary resignation program announced with tenure-based buyouts:
  - 6 months' salary for up to 1 year of service
  - 12 months for up to 2 years
  - 18 months for up to 5 years
  - 24 months for 5+ years
- Krafton publicly maintained the program is "not a layoff plan" but a "growth direction" support program
- Widely reported in gaming press as **AI-driven workforce reduction**; sparked calls for boycott by players

### Union Organizing

There is **no known formal labor union** organized specifically at Krafton as of late 2025. Unlike Nexon and NCSoft, Krafton has not faced public unionization efforts. The company name "Krafton Game Union" (its former trade name) is a corporate branding term, not a labor union.

### Headcount & Restructuring

- Krafton maintained relatively stable headcount through 2022-2024
- The company expanded through acquisitions rather than large-scale hiring
- November 2025: Hiring freeze except AI teams and original IP development
- The voluntary resignation program was expected to reduce workforce without formal layoffs

## Acquisitions and Global Expansion

Krafton has pursued an aggressive M&A strategy:

**2022:**
- 5 studio investments
- Acquired **Unknown Worlds Entertainment** (Subnautica) in October 2021 (finalized earlier)

**2023:**
- 11 investments in game studios (minority stakes)
- Strategic partnership with **Ironmace** for Dark and Darker Mobile
- Spent $680M on minority stakes across 2022-2023

**2024:**
- **Tango Gameworks** — Acquired from Microsoft on August 1, 2024 (announced August 12); saved the Hi-Fi Rush developer after Microsoft's studio closure; ~50 of 105 original staff rehired; included Hi-Fi Rush IP
- 13 studio investments in first three quarters alone
- IP licensing agreement with **Pocketpair** for Palworld Mobile
- Total investment: $680M across 2022-2024

**2025:**
- **Eleventh Hour Games** — Full acquisition of Last Epoch developer; initial consideration $96M
- **Nautilus Mobile** — Controlling stake for $14M (Indian mobile game studio)
- **ADK** — Full acquisition
- Planned another $200B KRW ($136M) for 10+ studio investments in 2025

## AI Investments

- Invested over 1 trillion KRW ($680.8M) from 2021 to September 2024 on AI talent and R&D
- 80 engineers focused on deep learning and AI (2025)
- Plans to add 35 more AI engineers in 2025
- Developed CPC (Co-playable Character) AI model using NVIDIA ACE
- CPC NPCs planned for PUBG and inZOI

## Labor Controversies

As of 2025, Krafton has not been the subject of major labor controversies compared to other Korean game publishers. The primary labor concern is the **voluntary resignation program** (2025) widely viewed as a mechanism to reduce headcount under the cover of AI transformation while the company posts record profits.

The program drew widespread criticism from the gaming community and press, with many commentators noting the irony of layoff-adjacent restructuring during a period of financial success.
