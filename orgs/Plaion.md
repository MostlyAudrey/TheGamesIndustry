# Plaion

German video game publisher and media company, headquartered in Munich. Founded in 1994 as Koch Media, rebranded to Plaion in 2022. Owns the Deep Silver label, known for the Metro series (via 4A Games), Dead Island, Saints Row, and Kingdom Come: Deliverance (via Warhorse Studios).

Acquired by Embracer Group in 2018 for an undisclosed amount. Operates as one of Embracer's largest operating groups. Also includes distribution and video production arms.

## Labor record

German labor law mandates works councils (Betriebsrat) for companies with 5+ employees. No unionization drives reported. No mass layoffs reported prior to Embracer restructuring.
