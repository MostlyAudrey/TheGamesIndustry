# Kowloon Nights

Video gaming investment fund established in 2017. Unlike traditional VC, provides project-based funding to talented teams for PC and console premium games. Takes a hands-off approach during development, empowering creative teams while providing financial support.

**Key people:** Alexis Garavaryan (co-founder, CEO — ex-Ubisoft, ID@Xbox, Tencent), Jay Chi (co-founder, founding partner). Small team of 5.

**Notable portfolio:** House House (Untitled Goose Game), [[orgs/Annapurna Interactive]], Redhill Games (Finland), indieBI (game analytics).

**Strategy:** Non-dilutive or low-dilution project financing. Revenue-share based returns. Developer-friendly — studios retain IP and creative control. European studio focus (Finland, Poland). Fund vehicle: Fairchild Initiative. More akin to production financing than traditional VC.
