# Nexon

## Overview

Nexon Co., Ltd. is a South Korean–Japanese video game developer and publisher headquartered in Tokyo, Japan (for the listed holding company), with major operations in Seongnam, South Korea. Founded in 1994, Nexon pioneered the free-to-play, microtransaction-based MMO model. It is one of the world's largest gaming companies by revenue.

Nexon is known for **MapleStory** (2003–present), one of the longest-running and most profitable MMORPGs globally, and **Dungeon & Fighter** (DNF), the highest-grossing game of all time by lifetime revenue.

## Financial Performance

- 2023 Revenue: ~3.2 trillion KRW (~$2.4B)
- 2024: Operating profit impacted by portfolio review and project cancellations
- 2026: Plans workforce redeployment, rules out layoffs (March 2026)
- Core markets: Korea, China, Japan, North America

## Labor and Employment

### Union Organizing — "Starting Point"

Nexon is historically significant in the Korean game industry as the **first major game company to have its own labor union**.

- **2016-2017:** Employees began organizing in response to toxic crunch culture, job insecurity, and opaque management practices at Nexon Korea
- **September 3, 2018:** Nexon's labor union "Starting Point" (시작점) officially formed under the Korean Confederation of Trade Unions (KCTU). This was the **first labor union in the Korean game industry** at a major publisher
- The union's name symbolized a new beginning for workers' rights in Korean gaming

### 2019 — First Demonstration

- Over **600 unionized Nexon employees** protested outside Nexon headquarters in Seongnam
- Demanded job security amid company-wide reorganization and project cancellations
- Union head Bae Soo-chan stated workers suffered from "constant job insecurity" when projects ended

### 2022 — Project Suspensions

- Nexon suspended four development projects
- Around 100 workers put on standby
- Union held demonstration demanding job security

### 2023 — Major Rally and Conflict

- **October 2023:** ~600 unionized employees demonstrated over project cancellations and general unrest
- The rally followed months of brewing tension at the company
- **December 2023:** Nexon workers indicated intention to **leave the KCTU** over a dispute about the umbrella union's stance on feminism
- Nexon workers opposed the KCTU's support of feminist groups that criticized the company's countermeasures against internal gender issues
- This highlighted internal fractures between the company union and the broader labor movement

### 2024 — Pay Negotiations and Neople Dispute

- **January 2024:** Nexon Korea agreed to annual wage negotiations with Starting Point
- **Mid-2024:** Neople (Nexon subsidiary developing Dungeon & Fighter) union threatened to strike after wage talks broke down
- Neople union: "Between 2019 and 2023, Neople's operating profit reached 3.8 trillion won ($2.7 billion)"
- Nexon management offered wage increases similar to other affiliates despite Neople's outsized profits
- Union demanded 9.24% wage increase; management cited 11% drop in annual operating profit as counterargument

### Layoffs and Restructuring

**Nexon America Layoffs 2023-2024:**
- **2023:** Nexon America restructured to "streamline operations and reset the organization"; small number of layoffs
- **September 2024:** Nexon America laid off a "small number" of staff as part of reorganization
- Restructuring involved forming a **Western Development Group** in Los Angeles
- Concurrently, the company was hiring for the new LA-based development team
- Part of a "hyperlocalization" strategy for Western markets

**Nexon Global Workforce Plans:**
- **March 2026:** Nexon Co. ruled out layoffs or forced restructuring at a Capital Market Briefing in Tokyo
- Instead announced workforce **reallocation/redeployment** as part of efficiency drive
- No major Korea-wide layoffs equivalent to NCSoft or Krafton have occurred at Nexon

### Mintrocket Full Acquisition

- Nexon fully acquired subsidiary **Mintrocket** (Dave the Diver developer) in 2024
- The acquisition was framed as accelerating development processes rather than workforce reduction

## MapleStory-Related Controversies

### MapleStory Loot Box Scandal (2024)

While not strictly a labor issue, the MapleStory controversy affected company reputation, employee morale, and regulatory burden:

- **January 2024:** Korea Fair Trade Commission fined Nexon **11.6 billion won (~$8.9M)** — a record fine
- Nexon had **secretly altered drop rates** of coveted items in MapleStory's gacha/cube system without informing players
- The manipulation occurred over multiple years before discovery
- **Class action lawsuit** filed by over 1,000 players seeking damages
- Nexon contested the fine through legal channels
- The scandal became one of the most high-profile consumer protection cases in Korean gaming history

## Additional Labor Context

- Nexon has been historically known for **intense crunch culture**, contributing to unionization efforts
- The Korean game industry overall has notoriously long working hours — software/game industry workers clock the longest hours in South Korea
- Nexon's union was seen as a "tow truck" for improving labor conditions across the industry (per the union's 2018 founding statement)
