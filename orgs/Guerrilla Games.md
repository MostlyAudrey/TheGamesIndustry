# Guerrilla Games

**Guerrilla Games** is an Amsterdam-based AAA game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for the Killzone series and, more recently, the Horizon franchise which has become a major pillar of Sony's first-party output.

## Ownership & Structure

- **Sony Interactive Entertainment** acquired Guerrilla in 2005
- Studio director: Jan-Bart van Beek (former Hermen Hulst, who became head of all PlayStation Studios)
- Operates solely from Amsterdam, Netherlands

## Labor Timeline

### February 2024 — ~40 jobs cut (10%)
Guerrilla was one of several first-party studios hit by SIE's 900-person layoff. Dutch outlet AD.nl reported that approximately 10% of the studio's workforce was let go — about 40 of over 400 employees. This was confirmed by multiple outlets including Eurogamer, Push Square, and VGC.

### Other labor notes
- No major crunch reports on Horizon Forbidden West (Sony publicly stated it improved work-life balance since Killzone era)
- Guerrilla operates under Dutch labor law which provides stronger worker protections than US jurisdictions
- Multiple Horizon projects are in development simultaneously (Horizon 3, multiplayer Horizon)

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Guerrilla Games as of October 2026. While the Netherlands has strong labor protections and workers can join existing unions, no game-specific union certification has been reported at the studio.

## Edge Notes
- Guerrilla has expanded significantly since Horizon Zero Dawn, going from ~250 to ~400+ employees
- The multiplayer Horizon game represents the studio's first live-service attempt
- Hermen Hulst's promotion to SIE co-CEO (June 2024) means the studio has direct line to top leadership
