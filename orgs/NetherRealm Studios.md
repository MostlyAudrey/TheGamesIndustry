## History

**Midway origins:** When Midway Games filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2009, Warner Bros. acquired "substantially all of Midway's assets" for $49M, including the Mortal Kombat IP. The Chicago studio was retained and initially named WB Games Chicago, then reincorporated as NetherRealm Studios on April 20, 2010.

**Fighting game dominance:** Under Ed Boon (Mortal Kombat co-creator), NetherRealm established itself as one of the premier fighting game developers in the world, alternating between Mortal Kombat and Injustice titles. Each mainline release has been a commercial and critical success.

**Mobile division:** NetherRealm developed mobile versions of several WB properties. In July 2024, the mobile gaming division was shut down with at least 50 employee layoffs. Mortal Kombat: Onslaught mobile game shut down in October 2024.

**Stability:** NetherRealm is considered the most stable studio in the WB Games portfolio. It was explicitly named as one of the four core IPs/franchises WB Games will focus on going forward (June 2025 restructuring).

## Controversies

- **Toxic workplace allegations (2019):** Former employees described a toxic workplace with gender discrimination and severe crunch during the development of Mortal Kombat 11. The studio released a statement saying they take the allegations seriously and gave employees the weekend off.
- **Violence impact (2019):** Kotaku reported on mental health toll of working on excessively violent games, with developers viewing real murder/animal slaughter videos as reference material, leading to PTSD and insomnia.

## Financial

- NetherRealm is consistently profitable for Warner Bros.
- Mortal Kombat 11 sold over 15 million copies.
- Mortal Kombat 1 (2023) launched to strong sales.
- The mobile division closure suggests cost-cutting, but core console development continues.

## Future

NetherRealm was highlighted as a key part of WB Games' new strategy focusing on four core IPs (DC includes Injustice). Likely to continue alternating between Mortal Kombat and Injustice titles.
