# CD Projekt

**Polish video game company, HQ Warsaw.** Developer of The Witcher series and Cyberpunk 2077. One of the few major game companies that did **not** participate in the 2023-2026 mass layoff wave — headcount grew during this period.

> **Note:** US + Canada only per project scope; CD Projekt is included as a comparison/labor example for the campaign-drama pipeline.

## Crunch culture

CDPR has one of the most documented crunch histories in the industry:

1. **The Witcher 3 (2015):** Extensive mandatory overtime during development.
2. **Cyberpunk 2077 (2020):** Marcin Iwiński promised "no mandatory overtime" in May 2019, reiterated in June 2019. In September 2020 — **three months before launch** — the company ordered mandatory 6-day work weeks. Developers later reported 13-hour days during peak production. The game still launched in a critically broken state (especially on last-gen consoles).
3. **Post-launch reforms:** After widespread criticism, Iwiński apologized in a video update and CDPR re-committed to "avoiding crunch on all of our future projects is one of our top priorities."
4. **Phantom Liberty (2023):** CDPR claimed **no mandatory crunch** during development. The game released on September 26, 2023. Multiple press sources reported that the commitment held, making it CDPR's first major release without forced overtime.
5. **The Witcher 4 (announced 2022, in development):** Director Jason Slama publicly promised "not on my watch" regarding crunch. Whether this holds during the final push remains to be seen.

## Labor organization

In **October 2023**, CDPR employees formed the **Związek Pracowników Branży Gier** (Game Industry Workers Union) — the first labor union in the Polish video game industry. Key features:

- Operates under **OZZ Inicjatywa Pracownicza** (Workers' Initiative), a nationwide independent union federation
- Not limited to CDPR — open to **all Polish game industry workers**
- Monthly membership fee: **16 PLN**
- Focus on **worker education** about legal rights, employment security, fair treatment, and safe working conditions
- CDPR management reportedly **did not react negatively** to the union's formation
- The union became public after documentation was formally submitted to HR

The timing — forming just ahead of Phantom Liberty's launch — is notable given CDPR's simultaneous "no crunch" messaging.
