# King (King.com Limited)

**Swedish mobile game giant — creator of Candy Crush Saga (2012), one of the most successful free-to-play games of all time.** ~2,000 employees across ~10 studios/offices. HQ Stockholm + London.

Acquired by Activision Blizzard for $5.9B (2016), now part of Microsoft Gaming since October 2023.

## The Candy Crush Machine

Candy Crush Saga is one of the most financially successful free-to-play games ever:
- Peak revenue: ~$1.5B+/year
- Still generates significant recurring revenue (~$1B/year)
- 10+ years of live operations
- Flagship of King's "saga" franchise family

King's business model: hyper-casual match-3 mechanics with freemium monetization. The company has successfully operated the Candy Crush franchise as a long-term live service property with seasonal events, new levels, and cross-promotion.

## Labor Profile

King's labor situation is notable for being an **active union formation case study** within the Microsoft Gaming ecosystem:

**Union formation:**
- October 2024: Stockholm employees formed a Fackklubb (union club) with Unionen, Sweden's largest white-collar union
- Catalyst: Removal of on-site private doctor benefit after Microsoft acquisition
- Growth: From ~10 members to 217 by January 2025 (potentially majority of Stockholm office)
- CBA negotiations began but stalled

**Current status (Sep 2026):**
- CBA not yet signed — King management alleged to have "shut down" negotiations over benefits
- Strike authorized by Candy Crush developers
- Blizzard Entertainment ratified its first union contracts the same week (providing contrast)
- Swedish labor model favors negotiation; strikes are rare in Sweden

**Context within Microsoft:**
- Microsoft's CWA neutrality agreement (2022, expired 2025) did not apply to Sweden
- Swedish labor law follows a different model (collective bargaining agreements versus NLRB elections in US)
- King is now the most significant holdout within the Microsoft Gaming union landscape alongside Activision
