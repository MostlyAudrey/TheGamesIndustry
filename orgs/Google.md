# Google

> Gaming at Google consists primarily of the failed Stadia cloud gaming platform. HQ: Mountain View, CA.

Google's gaming ambitions began with the acquisition of game streaming technology and culminated in the launch of Stadia in November 2019. The service was shut down in January 2023 after failing to gain market traction.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|

### Stadia Games & Entertainment (SG&E) shutdown — February 2021

Google had hired **~150 game developers** to staff two internal studios: one in Montreal and one in Los Angeles. Neither studio released a game. In February 2021, Google abruptly closed both studios, citing a decision to stop investing in exclusive content development. The decision came just a week after Stadia GM Phil Harrison had praised the team's progress ([[sources/2021-02-26 — wired-stadia-games-entertainment-collapse]]).

### Stadia service shutdown — January 2023

Google announced the Stadia shutdown in September 2022, with the service ending on January 18, 2023. Google provided full refunds for hardware and software purchases. Some Stadia team members were reassigned to other Google divisions; others were laid off ([[sources/2022-09-29 — wired-stadia-shutdown-announcement]]).

## Union activity

**No known union organizing** at Google's gaming operations. Google/Alphabet has had some labor organizing (Alphabet Workers Union, CWA-affiliated, ~1,400 members across Google), but none specifically in the gaming division.
