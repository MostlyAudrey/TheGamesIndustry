# Bethesda Softworks

**Legacy publisher founded 1986; acquired by Microsoft 2021 as part of ZeniMax Media ($8.1B).** HQ Rockville, MD. President Jill Braff. Publishes The Elder Scrolls, Fallout, Starfield, Doom, Wolfenstein, Quake, and Dishonored franchises.

**Central labor case study:** Under independent ZeniMax ownership (pre-2021), Bethesda had a reputation as a stable, no-layoff employer where employees expected to retire. Post-Microsoft acquisition, it has endured repeated layoffs and studio closures while simultaneously experiencing the largest unionization wave among any Microsoft game publisher, with five CWA units formed across ZeniMax/Bethesda/studios since 2023.

## Pre-Microsoft Era (1986–2021)

Bethesda operated under ZeniMax Media as a stable publisher with no known layoffs during the 2018–2020 period. Employees interviewed in 2026 described a culture of job security: "We didn't have layoffs before, and now all of a sudden we have yearly layoffs" (lead character artist Alex Nguyen, per WCCFTech Aug 2026). At acquisition (2020), ZeniMax employed 2,300+ people across multiple studios.
