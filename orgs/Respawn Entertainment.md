# Respawn Entertainment

**L.A. studio, owned by [[orgs/Electronic Arts]].** Known for **Apex Legends** (2019), **Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order** (2019), **Star Wars Jedi: Survivor** (2023).

## Labor-flow
- **2024-03:** **23** positions cut, mostly from the Apex Legends team (as of 2024-03-21, [[sources/2024-03-21 — ign-respawn-23-layoffs]])
- **2025-04/05:** ~**100** Respawn employees impacted; EA eliminated **300-400 positions company-wide**; two incubation projects canceled including Apex extraction shooter (as of 2025-04-29, [[sources/2025-04-29 — gamedeveloper-respawn-hundreds-layoffs]])
- **Net flow:** ~123+ cut (2024-2025), no documented hiring sprees (as of 2026-10-16)

## Key decisions
- **2024-02:** EA 5% workforce reduction — 23 Respawn jobs cut; unannounced Star Wars FPS canceled (as of 2024-03-21, [[sources/2024-03-21 — ign-respawn-23-layoffs]])
- **2025-04:** Two incubation projects canceled (code-named "R7" = Apex extraction shooter); EA 300-400 positions eliminated company-wide (as of 2025-04-29, [[sources/2025-04-29 — gamedeveloper-respawn-hundreds-layoffs]])
