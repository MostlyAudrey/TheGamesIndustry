# Quantic Dream

French narrative-driven game studio, known for *Heavy Rain*, *Beyond: Two Souls*, *Detroit: Become Human*. Acquired by NetEase in August 2022 for undisclosed amount.

## Capital & Ownership
- NetEase acquired 100% of Quantic Dream in August 2022, after previously holding a minority stake (2019).
- Studio operates as a wholly owned subsidiary of [[orgs/NetEase]].
