# Kadokawa Corporation

**Japan's largest media conglomerate; parent of FromSoftware, Spike Chunsoft, Acquire.** Founded 1945 as Kadokawa Shoten (publishing), merged with Dwango (Niconico video platform) in 2014.

## Ownership of FromSoftware
- Kadokawa owns 69.66% of FromSoftware (majority)
- Sony owns 14.09% of FromSoftware directly
- Tencent (Sixjoy HK) owns 16.25% of FromSoftware directly
- *(as of 2026, [[sources/2026-10-05 — revenuememo-fromsoftware-ownership-2026]])*

## Labor overview

Kadokawa's labor profile reflects the dual structure of Japanese creative industries:
- **Permanent employees** (Japan): Protected by strict labor law; zero involuntary mass layoffs. The 2026 voluntary retirement program is the only significant headcount reduction.
- **Freelancers** (publishing/creative): Systemic issues with verbal contracts, delayed payments, and below-market rates — documented by JFTC findings in 2024, 2025, and 2026 across Kadokawa and its subsidiaries.
- **Game development subsidiaries** (FromSoftware, Spike Chunsoft, etc.): Below-industry wages at FromSoftware documented in 2022; 11.8% salary increase announced Oct 2024.

## Capital significance

Kadokawa sits at an important intersection of gaming, anime, and publishing in Japan. The Sony investment (2024-2025) and Tencent's increasing stake (2025) make Kadokawa a contested asset among global entertainment giants. The arrival of activist investor Oasis Management (8.86%, Jun 2026) signals potential pressure for restructuring or asset sales.
