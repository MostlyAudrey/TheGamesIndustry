# High Moon Studios

**San Diego-based Activision studio focused on Call of Duty support.** Founded as Sammy Studios in 2002, acquired by Vivendi Games in 2006 and renamed High Moon Studios. After the Activision Blizzard merger, the studio was integrated into Activision's Call of Duty development pipeline.

## Labor status

As of October 2026, no known union organizing has occurred at High Moon Studios. The studio was not included in the [[orgs/Activision QA United-CWA]] unit which covered QA testers in Austin, Eden Prairie, and El Segundo — not San Diego. No wall-to-wall or studio-specific union activity has been reported.

High Moon previously developed licensed titles (Transformers: War for Cybertron, Deadpool) before being transitioned to Call of Duty support work in 2018. The studio primarily supports other Activision studios on Call of Duty content.

## Key games
- Transformers: War for Cybertron (2010)
- Transformers: Fall of Cybertron (2012)
- Deadpool (2013)
- Call of Duty support work (2018-present)
