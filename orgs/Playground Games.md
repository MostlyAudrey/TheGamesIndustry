# Playground Games

**British video game developer based in Leamington Spa, England.** Founded in 2010 by veteran racing game developers from Codemasters and Bizarre Creations. Best known for the Forza Horizon open-world racing series and now developing the Fable reboot.

## History

Playground Games was founded in 2010 by Gavin Raeburn, Trevor Williams, and Ralph Fulton, all former Codemasters staff. The studio's first project, Forza Horizon (2012), was developed in ~2 years and established the franchise as a premier open-world racing series. Microsoft acquired the studio in 2018 as part of expanding Xbox Game Studios.

The studio has steadily expanded: opening a second Leamington Spa team in 2020 (Forza Horizon support), a third studio for Fable development, and a fourth by 2024. Financial statements confirm headcount grew from 381 in 2023 to 455 by the end of 2024 [[sources/2024-10-12 — ign-forza-horizon-6-rumors]].

## Current Projects

- **Fable reboot** — First mainline Fable game since Fable III (2010). Announced July 2020, first in-game trailer June 2023. Built on ForzaTech engine. Directed by Ralph Fulton. Releasing February 23, 2027 on Xbox Series X/S, Windows, and PlayStation 5.
- **Forza Horizon 6** — Next entry in the open-world racing franchise. In development at the Forza Horizon team. Rumored to also release on PlayStation 5.

## Labor

No union organizing or labour disputes have been reported at Playground Games (as of 2026-10-18). The studio has not had any known layoffs since its founding. UK labour law provides collective redundancy consultation protections.
