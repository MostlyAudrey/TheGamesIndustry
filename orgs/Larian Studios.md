# Larian Studios

**Belgian independent AAA RPG developer — the Baldur's Gate 3 success story.** Founded 1996 by Swen Vincke. Private company majority-owned by Vincke and his wife. Tencent holds a minority non-controlling stake. 530 employees across 7 global studios.

A rare counter-example in the modern AAA industry: independent, profitable, no layoffs, no union (but no anti-union posture either), and founder with strong ethical public voice.

## Baldur's Gate 3 — The Financial Breakthrough

In 2022, Larian posted a €7.6 million loss. In 2023, after BG3's August launch:

- **Revenue:** €427 million (~$447M) — up from ~€30M in 2022
- **Profit:** €249 million (~$260M) pre-tax
- **Sales:** 15M copies by end 2024 → 20M by end 2025

This gave Larian extraordinary financial independence. Swen Vincke described being in a "luxurious position" to "pick our own destiny and our own path."

## Independence Strategy

Larian illustrates a unique model in modern AAA:

- **Rejected buyout offers** from major publishers after BG3's success
- **Tencent minority stake** is non-controlling; Vincke says Tencent "does not have a say in how his company operates"
- **Walking away from D&D:** In March 2025, Larian announced it would not make another Baldur's Gate or D&D game, returning to its own Divinity IP — a rare choice to leave a lucrative licensed franchise
- **Self-publishing:** Larian self-publishes, keeping full revenue and creative control
- **No layoffs:** In an industry that shed 30,000+ jobs in 2023-2025, Larian had none
- **530 employees** across 7 studios to support AAA production scale

## Labor and Culture

- **No union** — but no anti-union reputation either; more a product of Belgian/Swedish labor models and small-studio culture that scaled
- **Anti-crunch stance:** Larian has been vocal about minimizing crunch, though Vincke has acknowledged some crunch on BG3 was unavoidable
- **No layoffs** in company history
- **Diverse leadership:** Vincke has been unusually transparent about compensation, studio finances, and decision-making
- **Tencent stake:** Vincke described it as "essential" for confidence but not controlling

## Current Projects (as of Oct 2026)

- **New Divinity title** (announced at The Game Awards 2025) — in development
- **D&D project cancelled** — Vincke said "the hearts of the developers" weren't in it
- No AI content: "Everything is human actors"
