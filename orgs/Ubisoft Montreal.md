# Ubisoft Montreal

Largest Ubisoft studio globally, with approximately 4,000 employees pre-2024 reductions. Founded 1997. Primary developer of the Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, and Watch Dogs franchises, with ongoing support for Rainbow Six Siege.

## Union presence

Ubisoft Montreal has active **STJV** (Syndicat des Travailleurs et Travailleuses du Jeu Vidéo) presence — the largest game workers' union in Quebec. In February 2025, approximately 600 staff walked out in a one-day strike over return-to-office mandates and broader workplace conditions.
