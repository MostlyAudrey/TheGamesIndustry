# Konvoy Ventures

Thesis-driven VC based in Denver, specializing in platforms and technologies at the frontier of gaming and interactive entertainment. Manages ~$270M across three funds. Best known for publishing the annual Games VC Report, a widely-cited industry report on games investment trends, M&A, and fundraising.

**Key people:** Josh Chapman (co-founder, managing partner), Jason Chapman (co-founder, managing partner).

**Notable portfolio:** [[orgs/Epic Games]], Mythical Games (blockchain/NFT gaming platform), ProbablyMonsters (AAA incubator), Axie Infinity/Sky Mavis (blockchain pioneer), Gamefam (Roblox dev network), Carry1st (African mobile gaming), Ready Player Me.

**Strategy:** Pre-seed, seed, Series A. Global mandate (US, Europe, Africa, Asia). 50+ portfolio companies. Fund III ($150M) actively deploying.
