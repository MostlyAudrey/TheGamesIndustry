# Ghost Ship Games

Danish video game developer based in Copenhagen. Best known for Deep Rock Galactic (2020), a cooperative first-person shooter featuring procedurally generated caves and dwarven miners. Created by a team with experience from IO Interactive, IO Interactive, and other Danish studios.

Acquired by Embracer Group in 2021 via the Coffee Stain & Friends operating group. Deep Rock Galactic was a critical and commercial success, selling over 5 million copies by 2024.

## Labor record

Danish labor law provides strong worker protections. No union presence reported. No layoffs. Known for transparent development and strong community relations.
