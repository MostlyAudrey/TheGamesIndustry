# Gearbox Entertainment

## Overview

Gearbox Entertainment is a Texas-based video game developer and publisher best known for the *Borderlands* franchise. It was acquired by Embracer Group in April 2021 for up to $1.3 billion. In March 2024, Embracer sold Gearbox to **Take-Two Interactive** for $460 million, placing it under the 2K label. [[sources/2024-11-07 — ign-multiversus-100m-writedown]] marks part of the broader deal context.

CEO: Randy Pitchford (founder, since 1999).

## Labor Events

### Post-sale layoffs (March 2024)
Hours after the Take-Two acquisition was announced, multiple Gearbox employees (including Director of Online Engagement Chris Harada) reported being laid off. Gearbox confirmed "a number of staff have been impacted" but did not provide numbers. [[sources/2024-03-28 — gamedeveloper-gearbox-layoffs]] [[sources/2024-04-03 — gamesindustry-gearbox-layoffs]]

### Lost Boys Interactive layoffs (January 2024)
Lost Boys Interactive, a Gearbox-owned studio that employed over 400 people, was hit by "sizable" layoffs in January 2024. A spokesperson confirmed the cuts. [[sources/2024-01-12 — verge-lost-boys-layoffs]]

### Lost Boys Interactive mass layoffs (October 2025)
Lost Boys Interactive experienced another round of mass layoffs in October 2025, with former employees describing it as "massive" and stating a "sizable portion" of the company was let go. [[sources/2025-10-27 — aftermath-lost-boys-layoffs]]

### Post-Take-Two layoffs (August 2025)
Gearbox confirmed further staff impacts following the full integration into Take-Two, part of broader Take-Two cost-cutting. [[sources/2025-08-16 — gameshub-gearbox-layoffs]]

No known union organizing activity at Gearbox.
