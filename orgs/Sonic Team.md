# Sonic Team

**Japanese video game developer and internal division of [[orgs/Sega Corporation]].** Primary developer of the Sonic the Hedgehog series. Known for Sega CS2 (Consumer Research and Development Dept. 2), and also Sonic Team USA (San Francisco).

**Current status (Oct 2026):** Active development on multiple Sonic titles. Sonic Frontiers (2022) and Sonic X Shadow Generations (2024) commercially successful. Studio remains small for a flagship franchise, relying on external co-developers (Arzest, Dimps, HARDlight) for certain projects. No documented layoffs or union organizing.

## Structure

Sonic Team operates as an internal R&D division of Sega Corporation:
- **Sonic Team (Japan)** — Core development (Tokyo)
- **Sonic Team USA (San Francisco)** — Previously a separate entity (Sega Studio USA); handles some development and support

The division is small relative to the franchise's global significance. Core Sonic Team headcount is estimated at 50-60 employees, supplemented by:
- External co-developers (Arzest, Dimps)
- Sega Hardlight (UK) — Sonic mobile titles
- Various outsourcing partners

## Notable releases (2022-2026)
- **Sonic Frontiers (2022)** — Open-zone design; commercially successful 
- **Sonic Superstars (2023)** — 2D sidescroller; co-developed with Arzest
- **Sonic X Shadow Generations (2024)** — Remaster + new Shadow the Hedgehog campaign
- **Sonic Dream Team (2023)** — Apple Arcade mobile title (developed by Sega Hardlight)
- Various mobile and spin-off titles

## Labor context
- **No union:** No union organizing at either Sonic Team Japan or Sonic Team USA
- **No layoffs:** Sonic Team was not affected by Sega's 2024 European layoffs (240 jobs) since the studio is Japan/US-based
- **Sonic Team USA:** Employs fewer than 50 staff in San Francisco; no reported labor issues
- The small team size is a notable contrast to the franchise's status as Sega's flagship IP

## Sega film universe expansion
- Paramount's Sonic films (2020, 2022, 2024) have significantly boosted the franchise's profile
- Sonic Team has provided consultation/oversight but development is handled by Sega's licensing division
- The film success has not translated to significant headcount growth at Sonic Team
