# Pearl Abyss Corp

**South Korean video game publisher and developer, headquartered in Anyang.** Best known for Black Desert Online, the flagship MMORPG with significant global presence. Owns CCP Games (EVE Online) and is developing Crimson Desert, DokeV, and Plan 8.

## Labor events 2022-2026

Pearl Abyss has had relatively modest labor events compared to large Western publishers:

### Factorial Games closure (Q1 2023)
- Pearl Abyss acquired Factorial Games, a Korean mobile game studio, in May 2021 for ₩20 billion
- Factorial Games launched Super String in May 2021; the game shut down on March 28, 2023
- Pearl Abyss subsequently closed the studio in Q1 2023
- This contributed to a 6.7% headcount reduction (1,452→1,355) in Q1 2023
- Developer headcount dropped more severely: 9.5% (876→793)
- Pearl Abyss attributed the reduction to departing interns and the studio closure

### CCP Games layoffs (March 2023)
- CCP Games, Pearl Abyss's Icelandic subsidiary (EVE Online), laid off 13 employees in March 2023
- The layoffs resulted from restructuring that created duplicate positions
- CCP was reportedly hiring for open roles simultaneously

### No union activity
No known union organizing at Pearl Abyss or its subsidiaries. South Korea's game industry has seen limited unionization compared to the US/EU.

## Upcoming titles
