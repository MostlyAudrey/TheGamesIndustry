# Haven Studios

**Montreal-based game developer** and wholly-owned subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Founded in March 2021 by industry veteran Jade Raymond (former Ubisoft Toronto founder, EA Motive founder, Google Stadia VP).

## History

Haven was formed in the wake of Google's 2021 closure of its internal game development teams. Raymond left her position as Stadia's development boss and, along with five co-founders and approximately 20 ex-Google employees, launched the studio with Sony's backing. The studio operated independently for approximately 12 months before Sony acquired it in March 2022.

The studio has not yet shipped a title as of October 2026. It is working on an "original new IP" for PlayStation.

## Leadership

Jade Raymond served as CEO from founding until May 2025, when she departed the studio. No replacement CEO was publicly announced as of late 2026.

## Labor profile

No significant labor events documented. Haven has the profile of a well-funded, growing first-party studio under Sony's umbrella. The studio has no reported layoffs and no union organizing activity. At ~100 employees with an unreleased title, labor organizing surface is minimal.
