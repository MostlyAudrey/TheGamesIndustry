# Santa Monica Studio

**Santa Monica Studio** is a Los Angeles, CA-based AAA game developer and subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Best known for the God of War series. It is one of the oldest and most prestigious first-party Sony studios.

## Ownership & Structure

- Subsidiary of Sony Interactive Entertainment (founded in-house 1999)
- Studio head: Yumi Yang; Creative director: Cory Barlog
- Based in Los Angeles, CA

## Labor History

Santa Monica Studio is one of the few major AAA studios that has **not experienced any reported layoffs** in the 2023-2026 game industry downturn. The studio was not affected by SIE's 900-person February 2024 layoff wave, and has not reported any subsequent reductions.

The studio was actively hiring in 2024 (24 open positions) for its next God of War project, and continued hiring through 2026.

### Crunch notes
- God of War (2018) and God of War Ragnarök (2022) both had periods of intensive development, but no major whistleblower reports of systemic crunch

## Union Organizing

There is **no formal union** at Santa Monica Studio as of October 2026. No public unionization activity has been reported.

## Edge Notes
- One of the few Sony studios completely untouched by the 2024-2026 restructuring and layoff waves
- The studio works closely with external partners (outside creative studios contracted to support God of War games)
- Santa Monica Studio also functions as an incubator — it has helped support external studios like Bluepoint on God of War projects
- The loss of Bluepoint Games (closed Feb 2026) — which was working on a live-service God of War spin-off — did not affect Santa Monica Studio's core team
