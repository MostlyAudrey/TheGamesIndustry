# CCP Games

Icelandic game developer, founded 1997. Best known for EVE Online, the long-running space MMO. Acquired by Korean publisher Pearl Abyss in 2018 for $425M. Approximately 250 employees in Reykjavík and London.

## Union presence

Iceland has strong labor protections but no dedicated game industry union reported at CCP. No unionization drives reported.
