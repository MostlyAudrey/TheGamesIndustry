# Rare Ltd

**Iconic British game developer, founded 1985 by Tim and Chris Stamper.** Acquired by Microsoft in 2002. HQ Twycross, Leicestershire, UK. Known for classic Nintendo-era titles (Donkey Kong Country, GoldenEye, Banjo-Kazooie) through the 1990s, and more recently for the live-service pirate game Sea of Thieves (2018).

**Unusual among Xbox first-party studios for avoiding mass layoffs** through the 2024-2026 Microsoft gaming cut waves. However, its long-gestating project Everwild was cancelled in July 2025. Studio head Craig Duncan departed in October 2024 to lead Xbox Game Studios. In September 2026, as part of a major Xbox restructuring, Rare was transferred to the Activision publishing umbrella along with the Halo franchise and World's Edge. No union organizing has been reported.
