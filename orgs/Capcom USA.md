# Capcom USA

**American subsidiary of [[orgs/Capcom]] Co., Ltd., handling US sales, marketing, localization, and community management.** Headquartered in San Francisco, California. Established 1985 as Capcom's first international subsidiary.

## Role within Capcom Group

Capcom USA is a sales, marketing, and localization arm — not a development studio. Key operational areas include:

- **Marketing:** North American promotional campaigns for Resident Evil, Street Fighter, Monster Hunter, and other Capcom franchises
- **Localization:** English-language adaptation of Capcom games
- **Esports:** Operation of the Capcom Pro Tour (Street Fighter competitive circuit) and Street Fighter League
- **Community management:** Social media, events, and fan engagement
- **Licensing and brand management:** Third-party licensing and IP protection in North America

## Labor

- **No union:** No union organizing activity documented at Capcom USA
- **No layoffs:** No documented layoffs at Capcom USA in the 2022-2026 period
- **SAG-AFTRA action (2026):** Capcom was targeted by SAG-AFTRA (the US actors' union) for a "Do Not Work" order regarding Mega Man voiceover casting. This action concerned use of non-union voice actors for Capcom projects and is an external labor action, not internal employee organizing.
