# Arrowhead Game Studios

**Swedish video game developer** based in Stockholm. Founded in 2008 by a group of students from Luleå University of Technology. Known for cooperative multiplayer games — Magicka, the original Helldivers, and the phenomenally successful Helldivers 2 (2024).

## Helldivers 2 success

Helldivers 2, released February 2024, was one of the biggest game launches of the year. A third-person cooperative shooter published by Sony Interactive Entertainment, it sold over 12 million copies within months, becoming Sony's fastest-selling PC game and a major live-service success story. The game's success transformed Arrowhead from a small studio (~30 pre-launch) to a rapidly expanding organization (~100-158).

## Labor profile

No documented union activity. Arrowhead operates under Swedish labor law, which includes strong worker protections and a tradition of collective bargaining. The Swedish game industry has seen unionization at several major studios — Paradox Interactive signed a CBA with Swedish unions in 2020, creating a model that other Swedish studios can follow. As of 2026, no specific union election has been filed at Arrowhead.

## Scope note
Arrowhead Game Studios is a Swedish developer outside the US/Canada primary scope of Project Gamecraft. Included for its significance as a major industry success story and as a case study in Swedish labor law context.
