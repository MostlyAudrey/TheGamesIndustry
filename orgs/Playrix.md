# Playrix

## Overview

Playrix is a mobile game developer and publisher founded in Russia in 2004 by brothers Igor and Dmitri Bukhman. The company is now headquartered in Dublin, Ireland. Playrix is best known for casual hit titles *Homescapes*, *Gardenscapes*, *Fishdom*, and *Township*. It is consistently among the top grossing mobile game publishers globally.

## Labor Events

### Russia/Belarus exit and relocation (2022–2025)
Following Russia's invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, Playrix announced in October 2022 that it was closing all offices and operations in Russia and Belarus. The company initiated a massive relocation of approximately 1,500 employees to Ireland, Cyprus, Portugal, Serbia, Armenia, Kazakhstan, and Turkey. [[sources/2022-10-19 — mobilegamer-playrix-russia-relocation]]

The relocation process continued through 2023 and 2024. As of September 2023, all offices in Russia and Belarus were reported closed, employees relocated, and some staff separated. [[sources/2023-12-27 — gamedeveloper-playrix-exit]]

The company completed its full withdrawal from Russia and Belarus in mid-2025. [[sources/2025-06-23 — hitmarker-playrix-pulls-out]]

### Nature of workforce change
Playrix's workforce changes differ from typical layoffs — the primary driver was geopolitical relocation rather than cost-cutting. Some staff chose not to relocate and were likely separated. The company maintained its employee count in other jurisdictions.

### Labor organizing
No known union organizing activity at Playrix.
