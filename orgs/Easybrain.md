# Easybrain

Cyprus-based mobile game developer known for Sudoku.com and other puzzle games. One of Embracer Group's largest mobile gaming subsidiaries.

Acquired by Embracer Group in February 2021 for up to $640M (€530M), one of Embracer's largest deals. At the time, Easybrain had over 500 employees and was highly profitable. In November 2024, Embracer sold Easybrain for an undisclosed amount as part of the post-breakup portfolio optimization.
