## Silver Lake Relationship

### SLP VII Commitment
- **Amount: $100 million** to Silver Lake Partners VII, L.P.
- **Documented in**: NJ State Investment Council Agenda / Investment Memorandum (PDF)
- **Date**: 2024 (per NJ Treasury filing)
- **Status**: Proposed and approved investment
- **Source**: [NJ.gov official filing](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/doinvest/pdf/AlternativeInvestments/PrivateEquity/SilverLakePartnersVII.pdf) — "The Division of Investment (the 'Division') is proposing an investment of up to $100 million"

### Prior Funds
- **Silver Lake Partners VI** (2020): $100 million commitment (per [NJ Agenda filing](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/doinvest/pdf/AlternativeInvestments/PrivateEquity/AgendaItem6b-SilverLakeFundVI.pdf))
- **Silver Lake Partners IV** (2013): $200 million commitment (per [NJ filing](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/doinvest/pdf/AlternativeInvestments/PrivateEquity/SilverLakePartnersIV.pdf))
- **Silver Lake Partners III** (2007): $100 million commitment

### Repeat Investor Pattern
NJ Division of Investment has consistently invested in Silver Lake flagship funds since at least 2007 (SLP III), committing at least $500M cumulatively across four fund vintages. This makes NJ DOI one of Silver Lake's most loyal and longest-running US public pension LPs.
