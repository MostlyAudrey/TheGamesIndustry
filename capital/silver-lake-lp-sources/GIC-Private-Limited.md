## Silver Lake Relationship

### Relationship Type
GIC is primarily a **co-investment partner** with Silver Lake on specific transactions, rather than a traditional limited partner in Silver Lake's flagship funds. However, GIC likely also participates in the funds.

### Co-Investment: Ancestry (2018)
- **Date**: June 2018
- **Deal**: Silver Lake and GIC announced strategic investments in Ancestry
- **Enterprise value**: ~$2.6 billion
- **Structure**: Both firms acquired substantial equity stakes from existing equity holders
- **Source**: [PRNewswire — "Silver Lake and GIC Announce Strategic Investments in Ancestry"](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/silver-lake-and-gic-announce-strategic-investments-in-ancestry-300244801.html)

### Co-Investment: Zuora Acquisition (2024-2025)
- **Date**: Announced October 2024, completed February 14, 2025
- **Deal**: Take-private acquisition of Zuora, Inc.
- **Enterprise value**: ~$1.7 billion
- **Structure**: Silver Lake in partnership with an affiliate of GIC Pte
- **Silver Lake's existing stake**: Had invested $400M in Zuora since 2022
- **Sources**:
  - [Silver Lake / GIC — Zuora completion press release](https://www.silverlake.com/silver-lake-and-gic-complete-acquisition-of-zuora/)

### SLP VII Status
- GIC is listed as a **confirmed co-investor** partner of Silver Lake
- It is possible (but not confirmed through public filings) that GIC is also a limited partner in SLP VII
- GIC's primary relationship with Silver Lake is deal-by-deal co-investment rather than fund commitment

## Games Industry Connections
GIC's games industry exposure comes through:
- **Ancestry** — Family history/genealogy platform (not gaming)
- **Zuora** — Subscription billing platform used by many SaaS and gaming companies
- The co-investment partnership with Silver Lake gives GIC indirect exposure to Silver Lake's broader gaming portfolio
