### Relationship Structure
Unlike traditional LPs who commit to a specific fund, Mubadala's relationship with Silver Lake operates on three levels:

1. **25-Year Capital Commitment ($2B)** — A unique long-dated mandate allowing Silver Lake to deploy capital flexibly over a 25-year horizon without traditional fund life constraints
2. **Equity Stake in Silver Lake Management** — Mubadala acquired a minority stake in the management company itself, aligning interests at the firm level
3. **SLP VII LP** — Mubadala is also likely an LP in SLP VII as part of its broader relationship, though the $2B 25yr strategy is a separate vehicle

### Recent Updates
- **May 2026**: Mubadala and Silver Lake reaffirmed the partnership with a refreshed announcement on Mubadala's website regarding the unique long-term investment strategy
