## Silver Lake Relationship

### SLP VII Status
- **Confirmed LP** in Silver Lake Partners VII
- Commitment amount: Not publicly disclosed
- NYSCRF is a long-standing LP across multiple Silver Lake funds

### Historical Involvement
- **Fee waiver controversy (2012)** — Reuters reported that the $150.6 billion NYSCRF was among the investors that "has long agreed to management fee waivers" with Silver Lake Partners and other PE firms (Apollo, Bain, KKR, TPG). NY AG Eric Schneiderman subpoenaed Silver Lake and others over the practice of converting management fees into fund investments.
- **Prior fund participation** — NYSCRF has been an LP in earlier Silver Lake flagship funds
- **In-state PE program** — NYSCRF runs an in-state private equity investment program (documented in OSC reports from 2009), though Silver Lake investments would be part of its main PE allocation

### Investment Approach
Per the Comptroller's office (2026), the CRF "invests in private equity partnerships directly and through separately managed accounts." Silver Lake investments would fall under the direct partnership model.

## Games Industry Connections
No direct games industry investments identified. NYSCRF's Silver Lake relationship provides indirect exposure to Silver Lake's tech and gaming portfolio companies.
