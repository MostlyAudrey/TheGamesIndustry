## Silver Lake Relationship

### SLP VII Commitment
- **Confirmed LP** in Silver Lake Partners VII
- **Amount**: Not publicly disclosed (Alternatives Watch reports "unspecified amount")
- **Status**: Active

### Co-Investment: Qualtrics Acquisition
CPP Investments' most significant Silver Lake partnership came through the **Qualtrics acquisition**:

- **Date**: Announced March 2023, completed June 28, 2023
- **Deal size**: ~$12.5 billion all-cash transaction
- **Structure**: Silver Lake and its co-investors, together with CPP Investments, acquired 100% of Qualtrics outstanding shares from SAP and public shareholders
- **CPP role**: Co-investor alongside Silver Lake (not through the fund, but as a direct co-investment partner)
- **Sources**: [CPP Investments press release](https://www.cppinvestments.com/newsroom/qualtrics-to-be-acquired-by-silver-lake-and-cpp-investments-for-12-5-billion/); [Qualtrics press release](https://www.qualtrics.com/news/qualtrics-to-be-acquired-by-silver-lake-and-cpp-investments-for-12-5-billion/)

### Prior Fund Commitments
CPP Investments has committed to Silver Lake limited partnerships historically:
- Per CPP Investments' website: "The commitments to Hellman & Friedman and **Silver Lake Partners** bring the CPP Investment Board's private equity commitments to a total of $6.3 billion committed to 46 limited partnerships managed by 38 private equity firms."

### Relationship Scope
CPP Investments engages with Silver Lake through multiple channels:
1. **Fund LP** — In Silver Lake flagship funds (including SLP VII)
2. **Co-investor** — Direct co-investments alongside Silver Lake (Qualtrics)
3. **Long-term partner** — North American institutional relationship

## Games Industry Connections
Through the Qualtrics co-investment, CPP Investments has indirect ties to Silver Lake's enterprise software portfolio. Qualtrics is an experience management platform used by gaming companies for customer feedback, though not a gaming company itself.
