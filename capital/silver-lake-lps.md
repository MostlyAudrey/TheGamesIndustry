## Co-Investment Vehicle & Separately Managed Accounts

Silver Lake also manages:
- **Silver Lake Strategic Investors VI** — a separate fund vehicle with only 4 LPs, including CalPERS (per PitchBook)
- **Co-investment vehicle alongside SLP VII** — allows LPs to co-invest directly in deals
- **Separately managed accounts (SMAs)** for large sovereign/pension LPs, including potential ADIA SMA (~$1B)
- **25-year long-term investment strategy** launched with Mubadala ($2B commitment, 2020/2023)

## Prior Fund LP Base

SLP VI (2021, $20B) had **76 LPs** per PitchBook. Many LPs likely rolled from SLP VI into SLP VII. Notable SLP VI LPs included:
- Teacher Retirement System of Texas ($200M)
- Arizona State Retirement System
- New Jersey Division of Investment
